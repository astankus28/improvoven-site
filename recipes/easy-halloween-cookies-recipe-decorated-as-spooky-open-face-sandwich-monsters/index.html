Easy Halloween Cookies Recipe Decorated as Spooky Open-Face Sandwich Monsters

Published October 15, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you searched 'easy Halloween cookies recipe decorated' and landed here — stay with me, because these open-face sandwich 'cookies' are the spooky midday lunch you didn't know you needed. They look like decorated Halloween cookies but they're actually savory, creamy, and totally shareable — think cream cheese-slathered rounds topped with veggies and deli meat arranged into little monster faces. Perfect for Halloween lunch boxes, school parties, or just making Tuesday feel like a holiday.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
15 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 8 large round crackers or small round bread slices (anything circular works — rye rounds, slider buns cut flat, or sturdy crackers)
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 teaspoon hot sauce (like Tabasco or your favorite)
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 4 slices deli turkey or ham, torn into pieces
  • 1 small cucumber, sliced into thin rounds (for eyes and decoration)
  • 1 small carrot, cut into thin sticks and small rounds
  • 8 black olives, sliced (for eyes, pupils, and mouths)
  • 1/4 cup roasted red peppers or cherry tomatoes, sliced (for mouths and fangs)
  • A few sprigs of fresh parsley or cilantro (for hair or monster fur)
  • Optional: a few drops of green food coloring stirred into cream cheese for a spooky swamp monster look

Instructions

  1. 1Mix the cream cheese with hot sauce, garlic powder, salt, and pepper in a small bowl until smooth and spreadable. If you want to go full Halloween, stir in a tiny drop of green food coloring here — it turns the whole thing into swamp monster territory and kids absolutely lose their minds over it.
  2. 2Lay your round crackers or bread rounds out on a cutting board or plate — these are your 'cookie' canvases. Spread a generous layer of the seasoned cream cheese on each one, going all the way to the edges.
  3. 3Now comes the fun part — building your monsters. Drape a torn piece of deli turkey or ham loosely over the cream cheese as the base 'face.' It doesn't need to be neat — wrinkled and messy actually looks more monstrous.
  4. 4Use two olive slices as eyes. Add a small cucumber round under each olive to make them pop like eyeballs on stalks. Press a sliced cherry tomato or strip of roasted red pepper into a jagged smile or fang shape near the bottom.
  5. 5Tuck a few small carrot sticks into the top edge of the cream cheese like antennae or horns. Press a few parsley or cilantro sprigs in for wild monster hair. Stack a second olive slice off-center on one eye to make it look like a winking freak — full character.
  6. 6Repeat with all your rounds and serve immediately on a plate or pack into a lunch container with the toppings in a small bag so they stay crispy. These hold up fine for a couple hours in the fridge, cream cheese and all — just keep them covered.
💡 Improv Tip

The cream cheese is your glue AND your flavor base — don't skip seasoning it. A little hot sauce, garlic powder, even a squeeze of lime if you're feeling Miami about it, makes the difference between 'cute snack' and 'I want three more of these.' And honestly? Adults eat just as many as the kids.
