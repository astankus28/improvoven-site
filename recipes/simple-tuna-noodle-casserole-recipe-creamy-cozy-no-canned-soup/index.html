Simple Tuna Noodle Casserole Recipe (Creamy, Cozy, No Canned Soup)

Published July 21, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple tuna noodle casserole recipe skips the condensed soup shortcut and makes a real, from-scratch cream sauce that takes maybe five extra minutes and tastes a hundred times better. It's the kind of weeknight dinner that feels like a hug — bubbly, golden on top, loaded with egg noodles and flaky tuna, and built almost entirely from pantry staples. If you've got canned tuna, a box of noodles, and some frozen peas, you're already halfway there.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
30 mins
Total
45 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 12 oz wide egg noodles
  • 3 cans (5 oz each) tuna in water, drained
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups whole milk (or 2%)
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese, divided
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more for pasta water
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs (panko or regular)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or melted butter (for topping)
  • Optional: 1/2 cup diced celery, added with the onion

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 375°F. Bring a big pot of salted water to a boil and cook the egg noodles for about 2 minutes less than the package says — they'll finish cooking in the oven, and you don't want mush. Drain them and set aside.
  2. 2While the noodles cook, melt the butter in a large oven-safe skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced onion (and celery if you're using it) and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until softened and just starting to turn golden. Add the garlic and cook another 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. 3Sprinkle the flour over the onion and stir it in — it'll look a little pasty and that's exactly right. Cook that mixture for about 1 minute, stirring constantly, to knock out the raw flour taste.
  4. 4Pour in the chicken broth first, whisking as you go to smooth out any lumps, then add the milk. Bring it up to a gentle simmer over medium heat, stirring frequently. In about 3 to 4 minutes it will thicken into a proper cream sauce. If it gets too thick, just splash in a little more milk.
  5. 5Take the pan off the heat. Stir in the Dijon mustard, Worcestershire sauce, onion powder, salt, pepper, and 3/4 cup of the shredded cheddar. Stir until the cheese is fully melted into the sauce.
  6. 6Add the drained tuna, frozen peas, and cooked egg noodles to the sauce. Fold everything together gently so the noodles are well coated and the tuna stays in nice chunky pieces — you don't want it to turn into paste.
  7. 7In a small bowl, toss the breadcrumbs with the olive oil or melted butter and the remaining 1/4 cup of cheddar. Scatter the topping evenly over the casserole.
  8. 8Bake uncovered for 20 to 25 minutes, until the top is golden and the edges are bubbling. Let it rest for 5 minutes before serving — this helps it set up so it scoops cleanly instead of sliding around the plate.
💡 Improv Tip

The secret to a creamy casserole that doesn't dry out is slightly undercooking your noodles before they go in the oven — they'll absorb sauce as they bake and if they're already fully cooked you'll end up with a dry, sticky brick. Two minutes under the package time is the sweet spot. Also, tuna packed in water works great here, but if you happen to have tuna in olive oil, use it — the sauce gets even richer.
