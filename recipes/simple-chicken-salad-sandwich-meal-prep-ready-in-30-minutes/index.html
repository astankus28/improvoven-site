Simple Chicken Salad Sandwich Meal Prep (Ready in 30 Minutes)

Published October 28, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple chicken salad sandwich meal prep is the kind of thing you make on Sunday and feel genuinely smug about all week long. Creamy, tangy, and loaded with crunch from celery and red onion, it comes together fast and keeps beautifully in the fridge for days. No sad desk lunches allowed.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts (or thighs — both work great)
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 2 stalks celery, finely diced
  • 1/4 cup red onion, finely diced
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon onion powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 8 slices of your favorite sandwich bread, or 4 croissants or rolls
  • Optional: a handful of halved red grapes or a tablespoon of sweet pickle relish for a little something extra
  • Optional for serving: lettuce leaves, sliced tomato, or thin cucumber rounds

Instructions

  1. 1Poach the chicken: Place chicken breasts in a medium saucepan and cover with cold water by about an inch. Add a big pinch of salt, a few peppercorns if you have them, and bring to a gentle boil over medium-high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to a low simmer, cover, and cook for 13 to 15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and no longer pink in the center. Pull out the chicken and let it rest on a cutting board for 5 minutes.
  2. 2Shred or chop the chicken: Once rested, use two forks to shred the chicken into chunky, satisfying pieces — or just chop it into small cubes if you prefer that texture. Either way works beautifully. Let it cool for another few minutes so it doesn't melt your dressing.
  3. 3Make the dressing: In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the mayo, Dijon mustard, and apple cider vinegar until smooth. Add the garlic powder, onion powder, a good pinch of salt, and several cracks of black pepper. Give it a taste — it should be creamy, a little tangy, and well-seasoned on its own.
  4. 4Mix everything together: Add the shredded chicken, diced celery, and red onion to the bowl. Stir until everything is evenly coated. If you're going the grape or relish route, fold those in now too. Taste one more time and adjust salt, pepper, or a little extra vinegar if you want more brightness.
  5. 5Meal prep for the week: Transfer the chicken salad to an airtight container and refrigerate. It keeps well for up to 4 days. When you're ready to eat, pile it generously onto your bread of choice, top with lettuce and tomato if you've got them, and call lunch done. Build the sandwiches fresh each day so the bread doesn't get soggy — the salad itself is what you're prepping ahead.
  6. 6Serving tip: This is also fantastic scooped into lettuce cups, stuffed into a halved avocado, or spooned over crackers if bread isn't your thing that day.
💡 Improv Tip

The number one chicken salad mistake is using warm chicken — it makes the mayo go greasy and sad. Let it cool completely before mixing, and your texture will be so much better. Also, don't skip tasting the dressing before it hits the chicken. That's your only real chance to dial it in.
