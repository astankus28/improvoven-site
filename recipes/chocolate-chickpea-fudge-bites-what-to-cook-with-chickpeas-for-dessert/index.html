Chocolate Chickpea Fudge Bites (What to Cook with Chickpeas for Dessert)

Published October 22, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've ever wondered what to cook with chickpeas beyond savory dishes, these no-bake chocolate fudge bites are about to blow your mind — in the best way. Blended smooth with cocoa powder, peanut butter, and a touch of honey, chickpeas pull off a secretly creamy, protein-packed fudge that tastes like a candy bar had a responsible older sibling. They're Miami-approved, freezer-friendly, and nobody — I mean nobody — will guess what's actually in them.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
1 hr 15 mins (includes chill time)
Serves
16 bites

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1/3 cup natural peanut butter (or sunflower seed butter to keep it nut-free)
  • 3 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 3 tablespoons honey or agave syrup
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper (optional — gives it that Miami heat, trust me)
  • 2 tablespoons mini chocolate chips, plus more for topping
  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil (helps the bites set firm)
  • Flaky sea salt, for finishing (optional but so worth it)

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your drained chickpeas as dry as you can with a clean kitchen towel or paper towels — the drier they are, the fudgier your bites will be. Don't skip this step. Wet chickpeas = sad, grainy fudge.
  2. 2Add the chickpeas, peanut butter, cocoa powder, honey, vanilla, sea salt, cinnamon, and cayenne (if using) to a food processor. Blend for about 2 minutes, scraping down the sides halfway through, until the mixture is completely smooth and looks like a thick brownie batter. Taste it — adjust honey if you want it sweeter, add more cocoa if you want deeper chocolate flavor.
  3. 3Add the coconut oil and blend another 30 seconds to combine. The coconut oil is what helps these babies firm up in the fridge, so don't leave it out.
  4. 4Stir in the mini chocolate chips with a spatula — fold them in by hand so they stay whole and give you little chocolate pockets in every bite.
  5. 5Line a small baking sheet or plate with parchment paper. Using a tablespoon or a small cookie scoop, scoop out portions of the mixture and roll them into balls with lightly damp hands (the damp hands keep it from sticking). You should get about 16 bites.
  6. 6Press a few extra mini chocolate chips on top of each bite, and if you're feeling fancy, hit them with a pinch of flaky sea salt. That sweet-salty thing is everything.
  7. 7Refrigerate for at least 1 hour until firm, or pop them in the freezer for 20 minutes if you need them fast. Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to a week — or the freezer for up to a month.
💡 Improv Tip

If your food processor is struggling to get the chickpeas totally smooth, add 1 to 2 teaspoons of water, one at a time, to help it along — but go slowly. You want thick and fudgy, not loose and scoopable. Also, rolling the bites with slightly damp hands is a game-changer for getting clean, smooth balls without the mixture sticking to everything.
