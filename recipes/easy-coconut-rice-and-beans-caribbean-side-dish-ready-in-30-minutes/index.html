Easy Coconut Rice and Beans — Caribbean Side Dish Ready in 30 Minutes

Published September 3, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy coconut rice and beans Caribbean side is the kind of dish that makes everything else on the plate taste better — fragrant, creamy, and subtly sweet from the coconut milk with a little thyme and allspice doing the heavy lifting in the background. It's a staple across the Caribbean for good reason, and once you make it this way, plain steamed rice is going to feel like a serious downgrade. One pot, pantry staples, and about 30 minutes is all you need.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup long-grain white rice
  • 1 (13.5 oz) can full-fat coconut milk
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 (15 oz) can kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 green onions, whole (plus more sliced for garnish)
  • 2 sprigs fresh thyme (or 1/2 tsp dried thyme)
  • 1/4 tsp allspice
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp neutral oil or butter
  • Optional: 1/2 scotch bonnet or habanero pepper, left whole for mild heat

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and cook for about 60 seconds, stirring, until fragrant but not browned.
  2. 2Pour in the coconut milk and water, then add the kidney beans, green onions, thyme sprigs, allspice, salt, black pepper, and the whole scotch bonnet if you're using it. Stir everything together and bring to a gentle boil.
  3. 3Add the rice and stir once to combine. Taste the liquid — it should be pleasantly seasoned, like a well-seasoned soup. Adjust salt now because you won't be stirring again.
  4. 4Reduce heat to the lowest setting, cover with a tight-fitting lid, and cook for 18 to 20 minutes. Resist the urge to lift the lid — the steam is doing the work.
  5. 5After 18 minutes, lift the lid and check the rice. It should be cooked through and the liquid absorbed. If there's still liquid at the bottom, cover and cook another 2 to 3 minutes.
  6. 6Remove from heat and let the pot sit covered for 5 minutes. Fish out the thyme sprigs, green onion pieces, and the scotch bonnet (if using). Fluff gently with a fork, taste for salt, and garnish with sliced green onions before serving.
💡 Improv Tip

Full-fat coconut milk is non-negotiable here — the lite stuff gives you watery rice with a faint coconut whisper instead of that rich, creamy result you're going for. Also, leaving the scotch bonnet whole (without piercing it) gives you that subtle Caribbean warmth and flavor without blowing anyone's head off. If someone accidentally bites into it, that's on them, but warn your guests.
