Simple 5-Ingredient Meal Prep Recipes: Honey Garlic Chicken Thighs

Published November 18, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been hunting for simple 5-ingredient meal prep recipes that actually taste like you tried, these honey garlic chicken thighs are your answer — sticky, savory, and ridiculously easy to batch cook on a Sunday. Bone-in thighs go into a simple glaze made from pantry staples, then roast hands-off while you do literally anything else. Make a batch, portion it out, and you've got four days of seriously good lunches or dinners locked in.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
40 mins
Total
50 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 2.5 lbs)
  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 teaspoon garlic powder in a pinch)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste (pantry freebies — don't count these)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 425°F and line a rimmed baking sheet with foil. This is your cleanup insurance — don't skip it.
  2. 2In a small bowl, whisk together the honey, soy sauce, minced garlic, and olive oil until it looks like a glossy, golden sauce. Give it a taste — it should be sweet, salty, and punchy all at once.
  3. 3Pat your chicken thighs completely dry with paper towels. This one step is what gets you that beautiful golden skin instead of steamed, sad chicken. Season both sides lightly with salt and pepper.
  4. 4Place the thighs skin-side up on your prepared baking sheet, spaced out so they're not crowding each other. Pour and brush about two-thirds of your honey garlic sauce over the top of each piece.
  5. 5Roast at 425°F for 35-40 minutes, until the skin is deeply caramelized and an instant-read thermometer reads 165°F at the thickest part. At the 20-minute mark, brush on the remaining sauce — this gives you that double-layered, lacquered glaze.
  6. 6Let the chicken rest on the pan for 5 minutes before you do anything with it. This keeps the juices inside the meat instead of all over your cutting board.
  7. 7For meal prep: let the thighs cool completely, then store in airtight containers in the fridge for up to 4 days. They reheat beautifully in the oven at 350°F for 10 minutes, or microwave with a damp paper towel over the top to keep them juicy. Pair with whatever you've got — rice, roasted veggies, a simple salad, crusty bread — this glaze plays well with everything.
💡 Improv Tip

Bone-in thighs are the move here for two reasons: they're usually the cheapest cut in the whole poultry case (often under $1.50/lb on sale), and they stay juicy through reheating in a way boneless breasts absolutely will not. If you want to stretch this into a full meal-prep spread, throw a sheet pan of broccoli or potatoes in the oven at the same time on a separate rack — same oven, no extra effort, full balanced meals for the week.
