Simple Breakfast Migas with Tortillas

Published June 6, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple breakfast migas with tortillas recipe is the kind of thing that makes you feel like you have your whole life together — even when you're just using up leftovers from the week. Crispy tortilla strips, fluffy scrambled eggs, a little heat, a little cheese — it all comes together in one pan in under 30 minutes. Big flavor, zero stress, and it costs practically nothing.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 6 small corn tortillas, cut or torn into rough strips
  • 6 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons milk or water
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil or butter
  • 1/2 medium white onion, diced
  • 1/2 medium green bell pepper, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced (optional but recommended)
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 cup canned diced tomatoes, drained (or 1 fresh roma tomato, chopped)
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar or Monterey Jack cheese
  • Fresh cilantro for topping
  • Hot sauce, sour cream, or avocado for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the torn tortilla strips in a single layer and let them fry undisturbed for 2 minutes, then toss and cook another 1-2 minutes until they're golden and starting to get crispy at the edges. They don't need to be totally crunchy — a little chew is part of the magic. Transfer them to a plate and set aside.
  2. 2Lower the heat to medium and add the remaining tablespoon of oil to the same pan. Toss in the onion, bell pepper, and jalapeño. Cook for 4-5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the onion is soft and translucent. Add the garlic and cook for another 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. 3Add the drained diced tomatoes, cumin, and chili powder to the pan. Stir everything together and let it cook for about 2 minutes so the flavors meld and any extra liquid cooks off. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. 4Crack your eggs into a bowl, add the milk or water, and whisk them until combined. Pour the egg mixture directly into the skillet over the veggie mix. Let them sit for about 15 seconds before you start gently folding them with a spatula — you want soft, pillowy curds, not dry rubbery scramble. Pull the pan off the heat when the eggs are just barely set.
  5. 5Fold in the crispy tortilla strips gently so they get coated in all that eggy goodness but don't completely disintegrate. Sprinkle the shredded cheese over the top, cover the pan with a lid for 60 seconds to let it melt, then take the lid off and marvel at what you just made.
  6. 6Serve immediately topped with fresh cilantro, a drizzle of hot sauce, a spoonful of sour cream, or sliced avocado if you've got one sitting on the counter. Eat it straight from the pan if you want — no judgment here.
💡 Improv Tip

Day-old or slightly stale tortillas are actually perfect for this — they crisp up faster and hold their texture better once the eggs go in. If your tortillas are super fresh and soft, tear them up and leave them out on the counter for 20 minutes before you start cooking. And listen, corn tortillas are traditional and give you that real migas flavor, but flour tortillas work too if that's what you've got. Cure that refrigerator blindness — whatever's in there, it probably belongs in this pan.
