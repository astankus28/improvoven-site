Bell Pepper and Egg Skillet Breakfast (Budget Recipe, Ready in 20 Minutes)

Published August 21, 2026 · Improv Oven

This bell pepper budget recipe is one of those weekday morning wins that looks way more impressive than the effort it takes — sliced peppers, eggs, and a handful of pantry spices all cooked in one pan. It's got that warm, savory vibe Miami mornings were made for, with a little cumin and smoked paprika doing the heavy lifting. Cheap, fast, filling, and genuinely delicious — your mornings just got an upgrade.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
20 mins
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 bell peppers (any color), thinly sliced into strips
  • 1/2 medium yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Pinch of red pepper flakes (optional, for heat)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro or parsley, roughly chopped (optional garnish)
  • Hot sauce, for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the oil in a medium skillet over medium heat. Once it shimmers, add the sliced onion and bell peppers. Spread them out in the pan and let them cook undisturbed for about 2 minutes so they start to get a little color on the bottom.
  2. 2Stir the peppers and onions and continue cooking for another 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they're soft, sweet, and starting to caramelize at the edges. This is where the flavor is — don't rush it.
  3. 3Sprinkle in the cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes if using. Stir everything together and let the spices toast in the pan for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. 4Use the back of a spoon to create 4 small wells in the pepper mixture, spacing them out evenly. Crack one egg into each well. If your skillet is crowded, you can do 2 eggs at a time.
  5. 5Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover the pan with a lid (or a sheet of foil if you don't have one), and cook for 3 to 4 minutes for runny yolks or 5 to 6 minutes for fully set yolks. Check at 3 minutes — egg doneness moves fast.
  6. 6Slide the whole situation onto plates, scatter cilantro or parsley over the top, and hit it with a splash of hot sauce. Serve with toast, warm tortillas, or just as-is.
💡 Improv Tip

Mixed-color bell peppers make this dish look like a million bucks for basically no extra cost — grab a bag of the tricolor ones at Costco or Aldi when they're on sale and keep them in the freezer. Frozen sliced peppers work perfectly here too; just add them straight to the pan and let any extra moisture cook off before you add the spices.
