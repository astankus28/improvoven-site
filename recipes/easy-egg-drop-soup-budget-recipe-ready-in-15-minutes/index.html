Easy Egg Drop Soup Budget Recipe (Ready in 15 Minutes)

Published October 4, 2026 · Improv Oven

This egg drop budget recipe is proof that a bowl of something warm and deeply satisfying doesn't have to cost you more than pocket change. We're giving the classic Chinese-American diner staple a little Miami magic — a splash of lime, a pinch of cumin, and some fresh cilantro that make it feel right at home in a South Florida kitchen. It comes together in one pot, in under 15 minutes, and costs less than a dollar a serving.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
10 mins
Total
15 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 cups chicken broth (or vegetable broth)
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 3 tablespoons cold water
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon white pepper (or black pepper)
  • Juice of 1/2 lime
  • 2 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
  • Salt to taste
  • Crusty bread or crackers for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Pour the chicken broth into a medium saucepan and set it over medium-high heat. Add the soy sauce, cumin, turmeric, garlic powder, and white pepper. Stir to combine and bring the broth to a gentle boil.
  2. 2While the broth is heating, whisk together the cornstarch and cold water in a small bowl until completely smooth with no lumps. This is your slurry — it's what gives egg drop soup that silky, slightly thickened texture.
  3. 3Once the broth is boiling, reduce the heat to medium-low and slowly pour in the cornstarch slurry while stirring continuously. Let it simmer for about 1 minute until the broth thickens slightly and turns glossy.
  4. 4Crack the 4 eggs into a small bowl or measuring cup and beat them well with a fork. Now here's the magic move: use a spoon or fork to stir the broth in a slow, steady circular motion to create a gentle whirlpool. While the broth is moving, slowly drizzle the beaten eggs into the pot in a thin, steady stream. The motion of the broth will pull the eggs into those beautiful ribbons. Stop stirring immediately after you've poured in all the egg — let them set for about 30 seconds.
  5. 5Squeeze in the lime juice, then taste and adjust salt if needed. Ladle into bowls and finish each one with sliced green onions and fresh cilantro.
  6. 6Serve immediately on its own or with a side of crusty bread, crackers, or even a scoop of white rice if you want to stretch it into a heartier lunch.
💡 Improv Tip

The egg ribbon technique is everything here — don't rush it and don't stir after you pour. If you want extra silky ribbons, strain your beaten eggs through a fine mesh strainer before drizzling. And if your broth is homemade or low-sodium, bump up the soy sauce a little bit and trust your taste buds over any measurement I give you.
