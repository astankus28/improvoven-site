Simple Pasta Salad Recipe for Labor Day (Classic, Crowd-Pleasing, and Make-Ahead Easy)

Published August 29, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple pasta salad recipe is exactly what your Labor Day cookout needs — it comes together fast, travels well, and tastes even better after it sits in the fridge for a few hours. Think tangy Italian dressing, crisp veggies, savory salami, and rotini that soaks up every bit of flavor. It's the kind of dish that disappears before the burgers are even off the grill.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
10 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
8

Ingredients

  • 1 lb rotini pasta (or any short pasta you have)
  • 1/3 cup Italian dressing (store-bought is totally fine)
  • 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes (optional but good)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 4 oz salami or pepperoni, sliced into quarters
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved (or 1 large tomato, chopped)
  • 1 cup cucumber, diced (about half a large cucumber)
  • 1/2 cup black olives, sliced
  • 1/2 cup roasted red peppers from a jar, chopped
  • 1/4 cup red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup shredded or cubed mozzarella (or cheddar if that's what you've got)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped (optional, but brightens it up)

Instructions

  1. 1Cook the rotini in a big pot of well-salted boiling water according to the package directions until just al dente — you want a little bite left, not mushy. It's going to keep softening as it absorbs the dressing. Drain it, then rinse under cold water until completely cool. Shake off the excess water and let it sit in the colander for a minute or two.
  2. 2While the pasta cools, whisk together the Italian dressing, red wine vinegar, dried oregano, garlic powder, crushed red pepper flakes, salt, and a generous amount of black pepper in a small bowl or measuring cup. Taste it — it should be punchy and a little acidic. That's what makes this thing sing.
  3. 3Add the cooled pasta to a large mixing bowl. Pour about two-thirds of the dressing over it and toss well to coat every noodle. Don't dump it all in yet — you'll add more later.
  4. 4Add the salami, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, black olives, roasted red peppers, red onion, and mozzarella to the bowl. Toss everything together gently until well combined.
  5. 5Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes — an hour is better, two hours is best. Right before serving, give it another toss, pour in the remaining dressing (the pasta will have absorbed a lot of it), and taste for seasoning. Add salt, pepper, or a splash more vinegar if it needs a lift. Top with fresh parsley if you've got it.
💡 Improv Tip

The number one pasta salad mistake is dressing it all at once and calling it done. Pasta is greedy — it soaks up dressing as it sits, and by the time you serve it the whole thing tastes dry and flat. Always hold back a third of your dressing and add it right before serving. Saved so many potluck moments this way.
