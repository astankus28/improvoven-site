Easy Loaded Baked Potato Recipe (That Hits Every Time)

Published June 12, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy loaded baked potato recipe is the move when you want something hearty, satisfying, and built entirely from stuff you probably already have at home. We're talking crispy skin, fluffy insides, and a mountain of toppings that make this work as a full meal — not just a side dish. Very Miami of us to turn a humble potato into something you'd brag about.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
55 mins
Total
65 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 large russet potatoes
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 4 strips bacon, cooked and crumbled (or 1/2 cup canned black beans for a meatless swap)
  • 3 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Hot sauce, to taste (we love Cholula around here)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 425°F. While it heats up, scrub your potatoes really well under cold water and pat them dry — dry skin is the secret to that satisfying crisp.
  2. 2Pierce each potato all over with a fork, about 8 to 10 times per potato. This lets the steam escape so you don't end up with a potato explosion situation in your oven.
  3. 3Rub each potato all over with olive oil, then season generously with kosher salt and black pepper. Don't be shy — the skin is part of the experience.
  4. 4Place the potatoes directly on the oven rack with a baking sheet on the rack below to catch any drips. Bake for 50 to 60 minutes until the skin is deeply golden and a fork slides into the center with zero resistance.
  5. 5While the potatoes bake, cook your bacon in a skillet over medium heat until crispy, then drain on a paper towel and crumble it up. If you're going the black bean route, just warm them in a small pan with a pinch of cumin and a little salt — trust me on this.
  6. 6Pull the potatoes from the oven and let them rest for 5 minutes. Then cut a deep cross into the top of each one and push the ends toward the center to open them up like a little edible bowl.
  7. 7Drop half a tablespoon of butter right into the center of each hot potato and let it melt into all the crevices. Season the inside with a little garlic powder.
  8. 8Now load them up: pile on the shredded cheddar first so it melts from the heat, then add your bacon or black beans, a generous dollop of sour cream, a shower of green onions, and as many hits of hot sauce as your heart desires.
  9. 9Serve immediately while everything is still melty and glorious. This is not a wait-around kind of dish.
💡 Improv Tip

The real cheat code here is using russet potatoes — their high starch content gives you that ultra-fluffy interior that soaks up butter like a dream. And if you skip the microwave and go full oven the whole time, you will taste the difference. Patience on this one pays off, I promise.
