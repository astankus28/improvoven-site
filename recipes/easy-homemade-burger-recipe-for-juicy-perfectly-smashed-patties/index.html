Easy Homemade Burger Recipe for Juicy, Perfectly Smashed Patties

Published November 24, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy homemade burger recipe delivers genuinely juicy patties every single time — no fancy equipment, no grill required, just a hot skillet and a few pantry tricks. The secret is a simple seasoning blend pressed right into the meat and a smash technique that creates those crispy, lacey edges you dream about. These come together in under 30 minutes and cost a fraction of what you'd spend at a restaurant.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil (vegetable or canola)
  • 4 slices American or cheddar cheese
  • 4 burger buns (brioche or potato buns work great)
  • 2 tbsp butter, softened
  • Toppings of your choice: lettuce, tomato, pickles, onion, ketchup, mustard, mayo

Instructions

  1. 1Take your ground beef out of the fridge about 10 minutes before cooking — slightly less cold meat makes a juicier burger. Divide it into 4 equal loose balls, about 4 oz each. Do not compact or overwork the meat at this stage; loose and shaggy is what you want.
  2. 2Mix together the garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper in a small bowl. Drizzle the Worcestershire sauce over your beef balls, then sprinkle the seasoning blend on top. Gently toss just enough to distribute — think 10 seconds, not kneading bread.
  3. 3Heat a cast iron skillet or heavy stainless pan over medium-high heat until it's genuinely hot — a drop of water should dance and evaporate immediately. Add the oil and swirl to coat.
  4. 4Place two beef balls in the skillet and immediately press them flat with a heavy spatula or the bottom of a small saucepan. Press down hard — you want patties about 1/3 inch thick. This is the smash technique and it's what creates the crispy, flavorful crust.
  5. 5Season the top side lightly with a pinch of salt. Cook undisturbed for 2 to 3 minutes until the edges look brown and lacy and the top surface has turned from pink to mostly gray. Do not press again after the initial smash.
  6. 6Flip each patty once. Immediately lay a slice of cheese on top of each patty. Cook for another 1 to 2 minutes for medium doneness. Remove to a plate and tent loosely with foil. Repeat with remaining two patties.
  7. 7While the patties rest, butter the cut sides of your burger buns and toast them in the same skillet over medium heat for about 1 minute until golden. This step sounds small but it makes the whole burger.
  8. 8Build your burgers: toasted bun bottom, any sauces you like, the juicy patty with melted cheese, then your toppings. Put that toasted top bun on and press down gently. Serve immediately.
💡 Improv Tip

The single biggest mistake people make with homemade burgers is overworking the meat — the more you handle it, the tighter the protein bonds get and the drier your burger ends up. Mix as little as possible, smash once and leave it alone, and you'll be rewarded with that juicy, almost-falling-apart texture every time. If your beef is leaner than 80/20, add a tiny drizzle of olive oil into the mix to compensate.
