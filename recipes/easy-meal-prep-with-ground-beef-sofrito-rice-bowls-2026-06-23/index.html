Easy Meal Prep with Ground Beef: Sofrito Rice Bowls

Published June 23, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been looking for an easy meal prep with ground beef that actually tastes like somebody's abuela cooked it, you just found your new Sunday ritual. We're talking seasoned ground beef simmered with sofrito, tomatoes, and a little sazón magic, piled over fluffy rice and ready to carry you through the whole week. Five ingredients you already have, one pan, zero stress.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef (80/20 is your friend here)
  • 1 cup long-grain white rice
  • 2 cups water or chicken broth
  • 3 tablespoons sofrito (jarred is perfectly fine, no judgment)
  • 1 packet sazón con culantro y achiote (or 1 tsp each garlic powder, cumin, and a pinch of turmeric)
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, drained
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Optional toppings: sliced avocado, hot sauce, fresh cilantro, lime wedges

Instructions

  1. 1Start your rice first so everything finishes around the same time. Combine 1 cup rice with 2 cups water or broth in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, drop the heat to low, cover, and let it do its thing for 18 minutes. Do not lift that lid — trust the process.
  2. 2While the rice is cooking, heat 1 teaspoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and break it up with a wooden spoon. Cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it's mostly browned. Don't drain all the fat — a little bit in there is flavor, not the enemy.
  3. 3Push the beef to one side of the pan and drop the sofrito right onto the empty side. Let it sizzle for about 60 seconds until it smells incredible and starts to darken slightly. Then stir it all together with the beef.
  4. 4Add the sazón packet, salt, pepper, and dried oregano. Stir everything to coat the beef evenly in all that color and spice.
  5. 5Pour in the drained diced tomatoes and stir to combine. Lower the heat to medium and let everything simmer together for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce tightens up and coats the beef beautifully. Taste and adjust salt if needed.
  6. 6By now your rice should be done. Fluff it with a fork. Divide the rice into 4 meal prep containers and spoon the beef mixture generously over the top. If you're eating right now, hit it with avocado, a squeeze of lime, and some fresh cilantro. If you're prepping for the week, let it cool completely before sealing the containers and refrigerating for up to 4 days.
💡 Improv Tip

The real secret to this tasting like it cooked all day is letting that sofrito actually fry for a full minute before you stir it into the meat — that step blooms all the aromatics and makes the whole dish smell like a real kitchen. Skip it and it tastes fine. Do it and it tastes like you know what you're doing.
