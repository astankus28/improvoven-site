Fast Homemade Pizza Recipe with Whatever You've Got

Published June 4, 2026 · Improv Oven

This fast homemade pizza recipe is the one I make when dinner needs to happen in under 30 minutes and nobody has time for fancy dough. We're talking a crispy, golden crust loaded with good stuff — all from things you probably already have sitting around. No special equipment, no culinary degree, just a hot oven and a little confidence.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 packet (2¼ tsp) instant yeast
  • ¾ cup warm water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, plus more for drizzling
  • ½ cup canned tomato sauce or crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1½ cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Your toppings of choice — pepperoni, sliced onion, bell pepper, whatever needs to be used up

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C). If you have a baking sheet, put it in the oven now while it heats up — that hot surface is the secret to a crispy bottom crust.
  2. 2In a large bowl, mix the warm water, sugar, and instant yeast together. Let it sit for about 2 minutes. You don't need it to foam like crazy with instant yeast — just give it a moment to wake up.
  3. 3Add the flour, salt, and olive oil to the yeast mixture. Stir it together until a shaggy dough forms, then turn it out onto a lightly floured surface and knead for about 3 minutes until smooth. Instant yeast means no long rise time — this dough is ready to roll immediately.
  4. 4On a piece of parchment paper, stretch or roll your dough out into roughly a 12-inch circle or rectangle. Don't stress about making it perfect — rustic edges are part of the charm.
  5. 5Mix your tomato sauce with the garlic powder and oregano, then spread it over the dough leaving about half an inch around the edges for the crust.
  6. 6Pile on your mozzarella and then whatever toppings you're working with. Drizzle a little olive oil over the top.
  7. 7Carefully slide the pizza (parchment paper and all) onto your preheated baking sheet. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden and the crust edges have some color on them.
  8. 8Let it cool for 2 minutes before slicing — I know it's hard to wait, but the cheese needs a second to set so it doesn't slide off everywhere.
💡 Improv Tip

The number one move for a crispier crust without a pizza stone is preheating your baking sheet in the oven before the pizza ever touches it. That immediate blast of bottom heat changes everything. Also, if your canned tomatoes are a little watery, spoon the sauce onto the dough and let it sit for 60 seconds before adding cheese — keeps the crust from getting soggy in the middle.
