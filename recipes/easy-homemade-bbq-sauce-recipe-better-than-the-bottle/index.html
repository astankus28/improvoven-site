Easy Homemade BBQ Sauce Recipe (Better Than the Bottle)

Published July 29, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy homemade BBQ sauce recipe comes together in about 20 minutes with pantry staples you almost certainly already have — ketchup, brown sugar, vinegar, and a handful of spices. Slather it on chicken thighs, brush it over ribs, or use it as a dipping sauce for literally anything that needs a little smoky-sweet moment. Once you make your own, the bottled stuff starts feeling like a lazy shortcut you don't need.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 4 pieces)
  • 1 cup ketchup
  • 3 tablespoons brown sugar (light or dark)
  • 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tablespoon yellow mustard
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional, for heat)
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil (for the chicken)

Instructions

  1. 1Make the BBQ sauce first: In a small saucepan over medium-low heat, combine the ketchup, brown sugar, apple cider vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, mustard, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and cayenne if you're using it. Stir everything together until it looks uniform.
  2. 2Let the sauce simmer gently for 15 to 20 minutes, stirring occasionally. You'll see it darken slightly, thicken up, and smell absolutely incredible. Taste it after 10 minutes — adjust salt, sugar, or vinegar to your preference. Pull it off the heat and set aside.
  3. 3While the sauce simmers, pat your chicken thighs completely dry with paper towels — this is the secret to good browning. Season both sides generously with salt and pepper.
  4. 4Heat a tablespoon of oil in a large oven-safe skillet (cast iron is perfect here) over medium-high heat. Once the oil shimmers, add the chicken thighs skin-side down. Don't touch them for 6 to 7 minutes. You want a deep golden crust. Flip and cook the other side for another 4 minutes.
  5. 5Preheat your broiler to high. Brush a generous layer of your homemade BBQ sauce over the tops of the chicken thighs. Slide the skillet under the broiler for 3 to 4 minutes, watching closely, until the sauce is caramelized, bubbly, and has those gorgeous dark edges. Pull it out, brush on one more layer of sauce, and let it rest for 5 minutes before serving.
  6. 6Serve with whatever you've got — cornbread, coleslaw, roasted potatoes, or just eat them standing over the stove because you can't wait. Reserve any remaining sauce on the side for dipping.
💡 Improv Tip

Don't skip the dry-pat on the chicken — moisture is the enemy of a good sear, and a good sear is the whole difference between sad pale chicken and something that looks like it came off a real backyard grill. Also, the BBQ sauce recipe makes more than you need for this meal, so jar the rest and keep it in the fridge for up to two weeks. Future you will be very grateful.
