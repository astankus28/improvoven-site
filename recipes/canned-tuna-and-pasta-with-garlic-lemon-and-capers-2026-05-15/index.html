Canned Tuna and Pasta with Garlic, Lemon, and Capers

Published May 15, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're staring into your pantry wondering what to make with canned tuna and pasta, this is your answer — and it's better than you'd expect from a weeknight rescue meal. We're talking silky olive oil, bright lemon, briny capers, and a little kick of red pepper flakes bringing everything together in under 30 minutes. This one's a regular at my house because it tastes like something from a little Italian-Cuban spot on Calle Ocho, but it costs almost nothing to make.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti or linguine
  • 2 cans (5 oz each) tuna in olive oil, drained
  • 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 3 tablespoons capers, drained
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (more if you like heat)
  • 1/3 cup good olive oil
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1/2 cup reserved pasta water
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, roughly chopped
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Optional: a handful of olives or a pinch of smoked paprika

Instructions

  1. 1Bring a big pot of water to a boil and salt it generously — it should taste like the ocean. Cook your pasta according to the package directions until just al dente. Before you drain it, scoop out about half a cup of that starchy pasta water and set it aside. That liquid is liquid gold for your sauce.
  2. 2While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add the sliced garlic and let it slowly sizzle and turn light golden — about 3 to 4 minutes. You want it fragrant and toasty, not brown and bitter, so keep your eye on it.
  3. 3Add the red pepper flakes and capers to the pan and stir them around for about 30 seconds so they bloom in that garlicky oil. You'll hear them pop a little — that's flavor happening right in front of you.
  4. 4Add the drained tuna to the skillet and break it up gently with a wooden spoon. You want some bigger chunks to stick around for texture. Let it warm through for about 2 minutes, tossing it lightly in that garlic oil.
  5. 5Add your drained pasta straight into the skillet along with a splash of pasta water — start with about a quarter cup. Toss everything together over medium heat for a minute or two until the sauce coats every strand. Add more pasta water little by little if it looks too dry.
  6. 6Pull the pan off the heat and hit it with the lemon zest, lemon juice, and fresh parsley. Give it one more good toss, taste for salt and pepper, and adjust. Serve immediately with an extra drizzle of olive oil over the top if you're feeling generous — and you should be.
💡 Improv Tip

Tuna packed in olive oil is the move here — it adds way more flavor and richness than the water-packed kind. But if water-packed is what you've got, just drain it well and add an extra splash of your own olive oil to the pan. No stress, still delicious.
