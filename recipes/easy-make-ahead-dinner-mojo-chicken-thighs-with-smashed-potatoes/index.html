Easy Make-Ahead Dinner: Mojo Chicken Thighs with Smashed Potatoes

Published November 11, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been hunting for easy make-ahead dinner recipes that actually taste better the next day, this one's going to become a regular in your rotation. Bone-in chicken thighs marinated in a punchy citrus mojo, roasted until golden, and served alongside crispy smashed potatoes — it's the kind of meal that does the heavy lifting on Sunday so your whole week feels a little easier. Miami soul, weeknight hustle.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
45 mins
Total
1 hr (plus marinating time)
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 2 lbs)
  • 1/3 cup orange juice (fresh or from a carton — both work)
  • 3 tablespoons lime juice (about 2 limes)
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced or grated
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1.5 lbs baby potatoes or small Yukon Golds
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Fresh parsley or cilantro for serving (optional but encouraged)

Instructions

  1. 1Make the mojo marinade: In a bowl or zip-lock bag, whisk together the orange juice, lime juice, minced garlic, oregano, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, pepper, and 2 tablespoons of olive oil. Add the chicken thighs and turn to coat. Marinate for at least 30 minutes at room temp, or up to 24 hours in the fridge — the longer the better for a make-ahead situation.
  2. 2Boil the potatoes: Place baby potatoes in a pot, cover with cold salted water, and bring to a boil. Cook for 15–18 minutes until fork-tender but not falling apart. Drain and let them steam-dry for a couple of minutes.
  3. 3Preheat your oven to 425°F. Line a large baking sheet with foil or parchment for easy cleanup.
  4. 4Smash the potatoes: Arrange the drained potatoes on one half of the baking sheet. Use the bottom of a glass or a fork to press each one down until it cracks open and flattens to about 1/2 inch. Drizzle with the remaining tablespoon of olive oil, sprinkle with garlic powder and a pinch of salt.
  5. 5Roast everything together: Pull the chicken from the marinade and place skin-side up on the other half of the baking sheet. Discard the used marinade. Slide the pan into the oven and roast for 38–42 minutes, until the chicken skin is deep golden and the thighs reach 165°F internally. The potatoes should be crispy on the edges and golden on the bottom.
  6. 6If you're eating now, let the chicken rest for 5 minutes before serving. Top with fresh parsley or cilantro and a squeeze of lime. If you're making this ahead, let everything cool completely before storing chicken and potatoes separately in airtight containers in the fridge for up to 4 days.
  7. 7To reheat: Pop the potatoes in a 400°F oven or air fryer for 8–10 minutes to bring back the crisp. Reheat chicken in the same oven for 10–12 minutes, or microwave covered with a damp paper towel if you're in a hurry.
💡 Improv Tip

The mojo marinade is where this whole dish lives — don't skip it and don't rush it. If you're meal prepping on a Sunday, mix the marinade Saturday night, drop the chicken in before bed, and you'll wake up to thighs that are practically already cooked in flavor. Also, if your potatoes are on the larger side, give them an extra 5 minutes in the boil so they smash without crumbling. You want them to split open dramatically, not turn to mush.
