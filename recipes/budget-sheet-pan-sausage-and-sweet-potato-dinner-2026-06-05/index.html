Budget Sheet Pan Sausage and Sweet Potato Dinner

Published June 5, 2026 · Improv Oven

This budget sheet pan sausage and sweet potato dinner is the kind of meal that makes your whole kitchen smell incredible with almost zero effort. Everything roasts together on one pan — caramelized edges, smoky sausage, tender sweet potato — and you're done in about 30 minutes. It's the cure for that 'I don't know what to cook' feeling on a Tuesday night.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb smoked sausage or kielbasa, sliced into coins
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 medium red onion, cut into wedges
  • 1 bell pepper (any color), sliced
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 425°F and line a large sheet pan with foil or parchment paper — this is a budget meal, not a scrubbing-the-pan meal.
  2. 2Cube your sweet potatoes into roughly 1-inch pieces so they cook evenly. Toss them onto the sheet pan first since they need a head start.
  3. 3Drizzle the sweet potatoes with 2 tablespoons of olive oil, sprinkle with half the spices, and toss to coat. Spread them out in a single layer and roast for 10 minutes.
  4. 4While the sweet potatoes get going, slice your sausage into coins, cut the onion into wedges, and slice the bell pepper. Toss everything together in a bowl with the remaining tablespoon of olive oil and the rest of the spices.
  5. 5Pull the pan out after 10 minutes and add the sausage, onion, and bell pepper right on top of and around the sweet potatoes. Spread everything out so nothing is piled up — give it room to roast, not steam.
  6. 6Return the pan to the oven and roast for another 15 minutes, until the sweet potatoes are fork-tender and starting to caramelize at the edges and the sausage has those beautiful golden-brown spots.
  7. 7Taste and adjust salt if needed. Scatter fresh cilantro or parsley on top if you've got it, and serve straight from the pan. Less dishes, more life.
💡 Improv Tip

The number one sheet pan mistake is crowding — if everything is piled on top of each other, it steams instead of roasts and you miss those crispy caramelized edges that make this dish. If your pan looks too packed, split it across two pans. Also, any smoked sausage works here — chorizo, andouille, even chicken sausage — so buy whatever's on sale and roll with it.
