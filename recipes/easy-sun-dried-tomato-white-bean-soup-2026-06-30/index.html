Easy Sun Dried Tomato White Bean Soup

Published June 30, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy sun dried tomato white bean soup is the kind of thing you throw together on a Tuesday night when you think there's nothing in the house — and it ends up tasting like you actually tried. Sun dried tomatoes do all the heavy lifting here, bringing that deep, jammy, almost smoky flavor that makes people ask what your secret is. It's pantry magic, baby.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 oz each) white beans, drained and rinsed (cannellini or Great Northern)
  • 1/3 cup sun dried tomatoes in oil, roughly chopped (plus 2 tbsp of the oil from the jar)
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 cups vegetable broth (or chicken broth)
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional but recommended)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 cups fresh spinach or kale, roughly chopped
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • Crusty bread or crackers for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the oil from your sun dried tomato jar in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. That oil is liquid gold — it's already infused with tomato flavor and you'd be throwing money away not using it.
  2. 2Add the diced onion and cook for about 4-5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and translucent. Don't rush this step — a good base makes everything taste better.
  3. 3Add the minced garlic, red pepper flakes, and smoked paprika. Stir and cook for about 60 seconds until fragrant. Your kitchen should start smelling amazing right about now.
  4. 4Stir in the tomato paste and cook it for another minute, letting it caramelize slightly against the bottom of the pot. This deepens the flavor in a way that makes people think you cooked all day.
  5. 5Add the chopped sun dried tomatoes, drained white beans, vegetable broth, water, and dried oregano. Stir everything together and bring to a gentle boil.
  6. 6Once boiling, reduce heat to medium-low and let the soup simmer uncovered for 10-12 minutes. Use the back of a spoon or a potato masher to crush about a quarter of the beans against the side of the pot — this thickens the soup naturally without any cream or flour.
  7. 7Stir in the spinach or kale and cook for 2 more minutes until just wilted. Finish with a squeeze of lemon juice, then taste and adjust salt and pepper as needed.
  8. 8Ladle into bowls and serve with crusty bread. A drizzle of good olive oil on top takes it over the edge if you have it.
💡 Improv Tip

If your sun dried tomatoes are the dry-packed kind (not in oil), just rehydrate them in warm water for 10 minutes before chopping. And listen — don't skip smashing those beans. That's the move that turns this from a brothy soup into something thick, cozy, and satisfying without adding a single extra ingredient.
