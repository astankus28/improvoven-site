Homemade Tres Leches Cake Recipe (The Real Way)

Published July 4, 2026 · Improv Oven

This homemade tres leches cake recipe is the one your abuela's neighbor always brought to every quinceañera and birthday — and nobody ever left without asking for the recipe. It's spongy, soaked, impossibly creamy, and way easier to pull off than you think. If you've got a can of evaporated milk, a can of sweetened condensed milk, and some heavy cream sitting in your pantry, you're already halfway there.

Prep
25 mins
Cook
30 mins
Total
3 hrs (includes soaking time)
Serves
12

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 5 large eggs, separated
  • 1 cup granulated sugar, divided
  • 1/3 cup whole milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream (for the soak)
  • 1 1/2 cups heavy whipping cream (for the topping)
  • 3 tablespoons powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (for the topping)
  • Ground cinnamon for dusting

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 350°F and grease a 9x13 inch baking dish. This is your canvas — don't skip greasing it or your beautiful cake will stick and your heart will break.
  2. 2In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.
  3. 3In a large bowl, beat the egg yolks with 3/4 cup of the sugar until the mixture turns pale yellow and thick — about 3 to 4 minutes with a hand mixer. Mix in the 1/3 cup whole milk and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract.
  4. 4Gently fold the flour mixture into the egg yolk mixture until just combined. Don't overwork it — you want a tender cake, not a workout.
  5. 5In a clean bowl (seriously, make sure it's clean and dry or your whites won't whip up), beat the egg whites until soft peaks form. Gradually add the remaining 1/4 cup sugar and keep beating until you get stiff, glossy peaks.
  6. 6Fold the egg whites into the batter in three additions, being gentle and patient. You're building air into this cake — that's what makes it soak up all that good milk without turning into mush.
  7. 7Pour the batter into your prepared baking dish and spread it out evenly. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until the top is golden and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  8. 8Let the cake cool in the pan for about 15 minutes. Then, using a fork or skewer, poke holes all over the entire surface of the cake — go crazy, get every inch. These holes are how the tres leches soaks in deep.
  9. 9In a large bowl or pitcher, whisk together the evaporated milk, sweetened condensed milk, and 1 cup heavy cream until combined. This is your tres leches mixture — taste it and try not to drink the whole thing.
  10. 10Slowly pour the milk mixture all over the warm cake, starting from the edges and working your way in. Let it soak for a few minutes, then pour any remaining liquid on top. Cover the pan with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, but overnight is the move if you can wait.
  11. 11When you're ready to serve, whip the 1 1/2 cups heavy cream with the powdered sugar and 1 teaspoon vanilla until you get soft, billowy peaks. Spread it over the top of the chilled cake like you're frosting a cloud.
  12. 12Dust generously with ground cinnamon, cut into squares, and serve cold. Watch it disappear in about four minutes flat.
💡 Improv Tip

The overnight soak is not optional in spirit — the longer that cake sits in the refrigerator soaking up those three milks, the more outrageously good it gets. Day-one tres leches is great. Day-two tres leches is transcendent. If you're making it for a party, do yourself a favor and make it the night before. Also, if you only have half-and-half instead of heavy cream for the soak, it still works — the cake will be a little lighter but just as delicious. Cure that refrigerator blindness!
