Quick Instant Pot Pinto Beans Recipe (No Soaking Required)

Published May 31, 2026 · Improv Oven

This quick Instant Pot pinto beans recipe is the kind of thing that'll make you feel like you had your life together all along — no soaking, no babysitting a pot for hours, just deeply flavorful beans ready in about 30 minutes. We're throwing in sofrito vibes, a little cumin, and some aromatics that'll make your whole kitchen smell incredible. Perfect over rice, stuffed in a burrito, or honestly just eaten straight from the pot with a warm tortilla.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried pinto beans, rinsed and picked over (no soaking needed)
  • 1 small white onion, roughly chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, roughly chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 4 cups water or low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
  • Optional: 1 smoked ham hock or 2 strips of bacon for extra depth
  • Optional garnish: fresh cilantro, a squeeze of lime, sliced jalapeño

Instructions

  1. 1Set your Instant Pot to the Sauté mode and let it heat up for about a minute. Add the oil, then toss in the chopped onion and green bell pepper. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they start to soften and smell amazing.
  2. 2Add the smashed garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, and oregano directly into the pot. Stir everything together and let it cook for about 60 seconds — you want those spices to bloom in the oil and get fragrant. This is where a lot of the flavor is built, so don't skip it.
  3. 3Add your rinsed pinto beans, bay leaf, salt, black pepper, and the water or broth. If you're using a ham hock or bacon, nestle it in now. Give everything a good stir so nothing is stuck to the bottom — this helps avoid the dreaded burn notice.
  4. 4Lock the lid into place and make sure the steam valve is set to Sealing. Press the Manual or Pressure Cook button and set it to High Pressure for 35 minutes. The pot will take about 10 minutes to come up to pressure before the countdown starts.
  5. 5Once the cook time is up, let the pressure release naturally for at least 15 minutes before carefully switching the valve to Venting to release any remaining steam. Open the lid away from your face.
  6. 6Fish out the bay leaf and the ham hock if you used one. Give the beans a taste and adjust salt as needed. If you want thicker, creamier beans, use the back of a spoon or a potato masher to smash a handful of beans right in the pot and stir — that starchy magic will thicken everything up beautifully.
  7. 7Serve over white rice, with warm tortillas, or as a hearty side dish. Finish with a squeeze of lime and fresh cilantro if you have them — they take it from great to absolutely unreal.
💡 Improv Tip

If your beans are on the older side — like they've been sitting in your pantry for over a year — bump the pressure cook time up to 40 minutes. Older dried beans are drier and take longer to fully soften. And listen, broth over water every single time if you have it — even a cheap store-brand broth adds a whole extra layer of flavor that water just can't touch.
