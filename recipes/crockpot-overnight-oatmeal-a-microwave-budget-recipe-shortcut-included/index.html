Crockpot Overnight Oatmeal (A Microwave Budget Recipe Shortcut Included)

Published August 23, 2026 · Improv Oven

This crockpot microwave budget recipe covers both bases — set it in your slow cooker before bed for a creamy, steel-cut oat breakfast, or use the microwave shortcut if you forgot to plan ahead. Either way, you're waking up to something warm, filling, and way better than a sad granola bar. It costs almost nothing per serving and keeps the whole family going until lunch.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
7 hrs (crockpot) or 8 mins (microwave)
Total
7 hrs 5 mins or 13 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup steel-cut oats (or rolled oats for the microwave version)
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 cup milk (any kind — dairy, oat, whatever's open)
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar or maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 apple, peeled and diced (or 1/2 cup raisins or frozen berries)
  • 2 tablespoons butter or coconut oil
  • Optional toppings: peanut butter, honey, banana slices, chopped nuts, more cinnamon

Instructions

  1. 1CROCKPOT METHOD: Lightly grease the inside of your crockpot with butter or a quick spray of oil — this is non-negotiable, oats will cement themselves to the sides if you skip it.
  2. 2Add the steel-cut oats, water, milk, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, salt, vanilla extract, and diced apple (or raisins or frozen berries) to the crockpot. Give everything a stir to combine.
  3. 3Drop in the butter, put the lid on, and set your crockpot to LOW. Cook for 7 to 8 hours overnight. By morning, your kitchen will smell incredible and breakfast will be completely done.
  4. 4Give the oats a good stir when you wake up — they'll look thick and almost porridge-like, which is exactly right. If they're thicker than you like, splash in a little warm milk and stir until you hit your preferred texture.
  5. 5Spoon into bowls and top however you like. Peanut butter swirled in is the move. Leftovers keep in the fridge for 4 days and reheat beautifully in the microwave with a splash of water or milk.
  6. 6MICROWAVE SHORTCUT METHOD: Use rolled oats (not steel-cut — they won't cook fast enough). Combine 1/2 cup rolled oats, 1 cup water or milk, a pinch of salt, and a pinch of cinnamon in a large microwave-safe bowl. Use a big bowl — oats bubble up and will make a mess in a small one.
  7. 7Microwave on HIGH for 2 minutes. Stir. Microwave for another 1 to 2 minutes, watching it. Stir again. Add your sweetener, vanilla, and any fruit you're using, then microwave for a final 30 seconds to 1 minute until the oats are creamy and cooked through.
  8. 8Let it sit for 1 minute — the oats will thicken up as they rest. Top and serve immediately. This version is faster but creamier with overnight time, so when you can plan ahead, the crockpot wins every time.
💡 Improv Tip

The crockpot version is best on LOW heat — HIGH will scorch the bottom and give you a sad crusty layer. If your slow cooker runs hot, check it at the 6-hour mark. For the microwave version, oat-to-liquid ratio matters more than anything: too little liquid and you get wallpaper paste, too much and it's soup. Stick to 1 part oats to 2 parts liquid and you're golden.
