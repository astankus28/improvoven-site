Simple Overnight Meal Prep Oats Variations (5 Ways to Never Be Bored)

Published June 20, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been sleeping on simple overnight meal prep oats variations, mi amor, your mornings are about to change completely. These five riffs on classic overnight oats use whatever you've got sitting in your pantry — canned coconut milk, a sad banana, some leftover guava paste — and turn them into something you'll actually look forward to. No cooking, no stress, just shake it up the night before and wake up like you have your life together.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
10 mins plus overnight
Serves
1 jar per variation

Ingredients

  • BASE FOR ALL VARIATIONS:
  • 1/2 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup milk of choice (dairy, oat, or canned coconut milk)
  • 1/4 cup plain yogurt (or more milk if you don't have yogurt)
  • 1 tablespoon chia seeds
  • 1 teaspoon honey or maple syrup
  • Pinch of salt
  • ---
  • VARIATION 1 — GUAVA & CREAM CHEESE (Miami Classic):
  • 2 tablespoons guava paste or guava jam
  • 2 tablespoons cream cheese softened
  • ---
  • VARIATION 2 — MANGO COCONUT (Tropical Escape):
  • 1/2 cup frozen or fresh mango chunks
  • 1/4 cup canned coconut milk (use as part of your liquid)
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of cinnamon
  • ---
  • VARIATION 3 — PLATANO & PEANUT BUTTER (Abuela Approved):
  • 1 ripe banana mashed
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter or any nut butter
  • Pinch of cinnamon
  • Drizzle of honey on top
  • ---
  • VARIATION 4 — CAFE CON LECHE (Coffee Lover):
  • 2 tablespoons strong brewed espresso or cold brew concentrate
  • 1 tablespoon sweetened condensed milk
  • Pinch of cinnamon
  • ---
  • VARIATION 5 — PANTRY HERO (Use What You Got):
  • 2 tablespoons any jam or preserves you have open
  • 1 tablespoon any nut butter or sunflower butter
  • Optional handful of granola or crushed crackers for crunch

Instructions

  1. 1Grab a mason jar, a container with a lid, or honestly any bowl you can cover with plastic wrap — no special equipment required here.
  2. 2Add your rolled oats, milk, yogurt, chia seeds, honey, and that pinch of salt directly into the jar. Stir everything together really well so the chia seeds don't just clump at the bottom.
  3. 3Now pick your variation. For Guava and Cream Cheese: dollop the cream cheese and guava paste right on top without stirring — you want those swirls when you open it tomorrow morning. For Mango Coconut: swap a quarter cup of your regular milk for coconut milk, stir in the vanilla and cinnamon, then pile the mango chunks on top. For Platano and Peanut Butter: mash your ripe banana right into the base before you seal the jar, then swirl in the peanut butter on top. For Cafe Con Leche: stir the espresso and condensed milk directly into your base — this one tastes like the best cafecito of your life, cold. For Pantry Hero: swirl in whatever jam you've got, then drop a spoonful of nut butter on top. Done.
  4. 4Seal your jar or cover your bowl tightly and put it in the fridge. Walk away. Go to sleep. Watch a show. You are done cooking for the night.
  5. 5In the morning, pull it out and give it a good stir. The oats will have soaked up all that liquid and gotten creamy and thick overnight — that's exactly what you want. If it feels too thick for your taste, splash in a little extra milk and stir again.
  6. 6Add any toppings you like — a few extra mango chunks, a sprinkle of toasted coconut flakes, some granola for crunch, a drizzle more honey — or eat it straight from the jar standing over your sink like a real one. No judgment here.
  7. 7These jars keep in the fridge for up to 4 days, so prep multiple variations on Sunday and you have a different breakfast every single morning of the week.
💡 Improv Tip

The biggest mistake people make with overnight oats is using instant oats — don't do it. Instant oats turn into mush and lose all their texture overnight. You want old-fashioned rolled oats, which hold up and get that perfect creamy-but-still-has-some-chew consistency. They're usually the cheapest oats on the shelf anyway, so it's a win all around.
