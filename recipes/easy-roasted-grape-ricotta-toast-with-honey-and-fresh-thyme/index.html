Easy Roasted Grape Ricotta Toast with Honey and Fresh Thyme

Published July 21, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy roasted grape ricotta toast is the kind of thing that looks way too fancy for how little effort it actually takes — sweet jammy grapes, creamy ricotta, a drizzle of honey, and fresh thyme all piled onto thick toasted bread. It works as a lazy weekend breakfast, a light lunch, or even a starter when friends come over and you want to look like you have your life together.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups red or black seedless grapes
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 4 thick slices of sourdough or country bread
  • 1 cup whole-milk ricotta
  • 1 tablespoon honey, plus more for drizzling
  • 1/2 teaspoon lemon zest
  • 4–5 fresh thyme sprigs (or a pinch of dried thyme in a pinch)
  • Flaky sea salt, for finishing
  • Optional: a few walnut halves, roughly chopped

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 400°F. Toss the grapes on a small rimmed baking sheet with 1 tablespoon of the olive oil, the balsamic vinegar, salt, and pepper. Spread them in a single layer and roast for 18–22 minutes, until they've burst, shriveled at the edges, and left behind a gorgeous jammy syrup in the pan. Don't rush this — those caramelized pan juices are liquid gold.
  2. 2While the grapes roast, mix the ricotta in a small bowl with 1 tablespoon of honey and the lemon zest. Stir until smooth and taste it — it should be lightly sweet with a bright little zing. Set aside.
  3. 3Toast your bread slices. You can do this in a toaster, under the broiler for 2–3 minutes per side, or in a dry skillet over medium heat with a little butter if you want to get fancy. You want real crunch here so the toast holds up under all the toppings.
  4. 4Brush each toasted slice lightly with the remaining tablespoon of olive oil while still warm — this keeps the bread from getting soggy and adds a nice savory backbone.
  5. 5Spread a generous layer of the honeyed ricotta over each toast. Don't be shy; this is the creamy base everything else lands on.
  6. 6Spoon the roasted grapes over the ricotta, making sure to drag some of that syrupy pan juice over the top. It will pool into the ricotta in the best possible way.
  7. 7Finish each toast with a drizzle of honey, a few fresh thyme leaves stripped from the sprigs, a pinch of flaky sea salt, and the optional chopped walnuts if you have them. Serve immediately while the grapes are still warm.
💡 Improv Tip

Whole-milk ricotta is non-negotiable here — part-skim turns watery and sad under warm fruit. If your grocery store ricotta is on the wet side, drain it in a fine mesh strainer for 10 minutes before mixing. And if your grapes are on the tart side, lean into it: a slightly tart grape against sweet honey ricotta is actually better than a uniformly sweet version.
