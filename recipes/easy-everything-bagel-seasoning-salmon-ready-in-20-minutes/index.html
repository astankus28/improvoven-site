Easy Everything Bagel Seasoning Salmon (Ready in 20 Minutes)

Published November 3, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy everything bagel seasoning salmon is the weeknight dinner you didn't know you were missing — all that garlicky, seedy, oniony magic pressed right into a salmon fillet and roasted until the crust is crispy and the inside is buttery and flaky. It takes about five minutes of actual effort, uses a spice blend you probably already have rattling around in your pantry, and tastes way more impressive than it has any right to. If you've got salmon and a jar of everything bagel seasoning, dinner is basically already done.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
20 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each, skin-on or skinless)
  • 3 tablespoons everything bagel seasoning
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • Lemon wedges, for serving
  • Fresh dill or chives for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Pull your salmon out of the fridge about 10 minutes before cooking — letting it come closer to room temperature helps it cook more evenly and stay juicy. Preheat your oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with foil or parchment. Lightly brush it with a little olive oil so nothing sticks.
  2. 2In a small bowl, whisk together the Dijon mustard, honey, lemon juice, and olive oil until smooth. This little mixture is your secret weapon — it acts as the glue that makes the everything bagel seasoning stick to the fish AND adds a subtle tangy-sweet flavor underneath the crust.
  3. 3Pat the salmon fillets dry with a paper towel. Dry fish = crispy crust. Wet fish = steamed fish. Take the extra 10 seconds — it matters. Place the fillets on your prepared baking sheet.
  4. 4Brush the mustard-honey mixture generously over the top and sides of each fillet. Then press the everything bagel seasoning firmly onto the coated surface of each piece — don't be shy, you want a good even crust. Use about 2 teaspoons of seasoning per fillet.
  5. 5Slide the baking sheet into the oven and roast for 12 to 15 minutes depending on the thickness of your fillets. A good rule of thumb: 4 minutes per half-inch of thickness. The salmon is done when it flakes easily with a fork and the seasoning crust is golden and fragrant. If you want extra color on top, hit it under the broiler for just 1 to 2 minutes at the end.
  6. 6Let the fillets rest for a minute before serving — this keeps all the juices inside where they belong. Squeeze fresh lemon over everything, scatter on some dill or chives if you've got them, and serve immediately. This goes great over a simple green salad, alongside roasted asparagus, on top of cream cheese schmeared rice cakes, or just straight off the pan while standing at the counter. No judgment.
💡 Improv Tip

Everything bagel seasoning is already pretty salty — it's got sesame seeds, poppy seeds, dried garlic, dried onion, and sea salt all in one jar. Don't add extra salt to the fish before coating it or you'll overdo it. Taste your seasoning blend first if you're unsure, and if it's on the saltier side, use slightly less per fillet. Also, if you're working with frozen salmon fillets (totally fine, very budget-friendly), make sure they're fully thawed and thoroughly patted dry before you start — excess moisture from freezing is the enemy of a good crust.
