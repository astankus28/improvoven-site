Simple Garlic Bread Recipe (The Only One You Need)

Published July 7, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple garlic bread recipe is the kind of thing you throw together in ten minutes and suddenly everyone at the table thinks you're a culinary genius — no special skills required. We're talking crispy edges, buttery centers, and enough garlic to keep the vampires away for a week. It's the perfect side dish, the perfect snack, the perfect 'I don't know what else to make' move.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
12 mins
Total
17 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf Italian or French bread, sliced in half lengthwise
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
  • 4 cloves fresh garlic, minced (or 1 teaspoon garlic powder in a pinch)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, finely chopped (or 1 teaspoon dried parsley)
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Optional: 1/4 cup shredded mozzarella or parmesan for cheesy garlic bread

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 375°F. While it heats up, pull your butter out of the fridge if you haven't already — soft butter is non-negotiable here, otherwise you'll tear up the bread trying to spread it.
  2. 2In a small bowl, mix together the softened butter, olive oil, minced garlic, parsley, salt, and black pepper until everything is well combined. Give it a little taste — you want it garlicky, buttery, and just slightly salty. Adjust as needed.
  3. 3Lay your bread halves cut-side up on a baking sheet lined with foil (trust me, the foil makes cleanup so much easier). Spread the garlic butter mixture generously over both halves — don't be shy, get it all the way to the edges.
  4. 4If you're going cheesy, sprinkle your shredded mozzarella or parmesan over the top now. If you're keeping it classic, skip straight to the oven.
  5. 5Bake at 375°F for 10 minutes, then switch your oven to broil and let it go for another 1 to 2 minutes until the top is golden and the edges are just starting to crisp up. Watch it closely during the broil — it goes from perfect to burnt faster than you can check your phone.
  6. 6Pull it out, let it cool for just a minute so you don't burn the roof of your mouth (we've all learned that lesson), then slice into pieces and serve immediately while it's still hot and crispy.
💡 Improv Tip

If your butter is still cold and hard, don't microwave it into a melted puddle — instead, grate it on a cheese grater directly onto the bread and let the oven do the work of melting it in. It spreads way more evenly that way and you still get that perfect buttery finish. Game changer.
