Red Lentil and Jasmine Rice Kitchari (Budget, Cozy, and Done in 30 Minutes)

Published July 27, 2026 · Improv Oven

This red lentils and jasmine rice budget recipe is one of those magical combinations where two humble pantry staples become something genuinely craveable — creamy, spiced, and deeply satisfying. Kitchari is a South Asian comfort dish that's been feeding people well for centuries, and once you try it, you'll understand why. It's the kind of meal that makes you feel taken care of, and it costs less than two dollars a bowl.

Prep
8 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
33 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed well
  • 3/4 cup jasmine rice, rinsed well
  • 2 tablespoons butter or neutral oil
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated (or 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger)
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional, adjust to taste)
  • 4 cups water or low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice (about half a lemon)
  • Fresh cilantro, for serving (optional)
  • Plain yogurt or a drizzle of olive oil, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Rinse your red lentils and jasmine rice separately under cold water until the water runs mostly clear. Red lentils break down into a creamy texture as they cook — that's exactly what you want here, not something to work against.
  2. 2Heat the butter or oil in a medium saucepan or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook, stirring occasionally, for 5 to 6 minutes until softened and lightly golden at the edges.
  3. 3Add the garlic and ginger and cook for another minute until fragrant. Your kitchen should smell incredible right about now.
  4. 4Add the cumin, turmeric, coriander, and cayenne (if using) directly to the pan. Stir everything together and let the spices toast in the fat for about 30 seconds — this step wakes them up and makes the whole dish taste deeper.
  5. 5Add the rinsed lentils and rice to the pot and stir to coat them in the spiced onion mixture.
  6. 6Pour in the 4 cups of water or broth and add the salt. Stir once, bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low. Cover and cook for 20 minutes, stirring once or twice to prevent sticking on the bottom.
  7. 7After 20 minutes, the lentils will have dissolved into the rice, creating a thick, porridge-like consistency. If it looks too thick for your taste, stir in a splash of hot water until it loosens to where you like it. If it seems too loose, let it sit covered off the heat for 5 minutes.
  8. 8Stir in the lemon juice, taste, and adjust salt as needed. Serve in bowls topped with fresh cilantro, a dollop of plain yogurt, or just a drizzle of good olive oil. All three are great — none are required.
💡 Improv Tip

Don't skip rinsing both the lentils and the rice — the extra starch on the surface can make the finished dish gluey instead of creamy. And if your kitchari thickens up too much by the time you go back for seconds (it will), just stir in a little warm water and it comes right back to life.
