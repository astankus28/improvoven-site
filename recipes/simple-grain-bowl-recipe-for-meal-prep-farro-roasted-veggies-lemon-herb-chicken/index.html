Simple Grain Bowl Recipe for Meal Prep (Farro, Roasted Veggies & Lemon-Herb Chicken)

Published November 5, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple grain bowl recipe is exactly what your meal prep Sunday has been missing — hearty farro, golden roasted vegetables, juicy lemon-herb chicken, and a quick yogurt-tahini drizzle that ties it all together. Make four bowls at once and you've got lunches or dinners sorted for most of the week. It's the kind of meal that actually gets better on day two when everything marinates together in the fridge.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
35 mins
Total
50 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dry farro (or pearl barley if that's what you've got)
  • 2 ½ cups water or chicken broth
  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 medium zucchini, cut into half-moons
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • ½ red onion, sliced into wedges
  • For the yogurt-tahini drizzle: ⅓ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated
  • 2-3 tbsp water (to thin)
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional toppings: fresh parsley, cucumber slices, crumbled feta, red pepper flakes

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 425°F and line a large sheet pan with parchment or foil. The higher heat is what gets you those beautiful caramelized edges on the vegetables — don't go lower.
  2. 2Cook the farro: Rinse it under cold water, then combine with 2 ½ cups water or broth in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, cover, and cook for 25-30 minutes until tender but still chewy. Drain any excess liquid and fluff with a fork. Season with a little salt and a drizzle of olive oil.
  3. 3While the farro cooks, marinate the chicken: In a bowl, combine 1 ½ tbsp olive oil, lemon zest, half the lemon juice, oregano, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Add the chicken thighs and toss to coat. Let them sit while you prep the vegetables — even 10 minutes makes a difference.
  4. 4Prep the vegetables: Toss the zucchini, bell pepper, cherry tomatoes, chickpeas, and red onion on your sheet pan with the remaining 1 ½ tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread everything out in a single layer — crowding is the enemy of roasting.
  5. 5Nestle the marinated chicken thighs right on top of or alongside the vegetables on the same sheet pan. Roast everything at 425°F for 25-30 minutes, flipping the vegetables once halfway through. The chicken is done when it hits 165°F internally and has a little color on top.
  6. 6While the oven does its thing, make the yogurt-tahini drizzle: Whisk together the Greek yogurt, tahini, lemon juice, grated garlic, and a pinch of salt. Add water one tablespoon at a time until it's a pourable, creamy consistency. Taste it — it should be bright, nutty, and a little tangy.
  7. 7Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes, then slice or chop it into bite-sized pieces.
  8. 8Assemble the bowls: Divide the farro evenly among four meal prep containers. Top each with a portion of the roasted vegetables and chickpeas, then the sliced chicken. Drizzle the yogurt-tahini sauce over the top — or store it in a small separate container on the side if you prefer the sauce fresh.
  9. 9Add any optional toppings you like — fresh parsley, a few cucumber slices for crunch, crumbled feta for saltiness, or a pinch of red pepper flakes for heat. Seal the containers and refrigerate for up to 4 days.
💡 Improv Tip

Store the yogurt-tahini drizzle in a separate small container for the first couple days — it keeps the farro from getting soggy and means you get a fresh, bright sauce every time you open a bowl. If you can only find pearled farro (which cooks faster), check it at 20 minutes. And honestly, leftover roasted vegetables from last night? Throw them in. That's the whole point.
