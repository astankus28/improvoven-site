Simple Grain Bowl Recipe for Meal Prep (Ready in 30 Minutes)

Published May 18, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple grain bowl recipe is exactly what your meal prep Sundays have been missing — hearty, colorful, and packed with flavor without breaking the bank. We're building it Miami-style with cumin-spiced black beans, fluffy rice, roasted veggies, and a tangy garlic-lime drizzle that ties everything together. Make a big batch on Sunday and you've got lunches or dinners handled for the whole week.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain white rice (uncooked)
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (15 oz) corn, drained (or 1 cup frozen corn)
  • 1 medium zucchini, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 small red onion, diced
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil (for the drizzle)
  • 1 teaspoon honey or agave
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Fresh cilantro for topping (optional but highly recommended)
  • 1 avocado, sliced (optional, add fresh when serving)

Instructions

  1. 1Cook your rice according to package directions. While that's going, preheat your oven to 425°F and line a large baking sheet with foil or parchment paper.
  2. 2Toss the diced zucchini, red bell pepper, and red onion with 2 tablespoons of olive oil, the cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Spread everything out in a single layer on your baking sheet — don't crowd the veggies or they'll steam instead of roast.
  3. 3Roast the vegetables for 18 to 20 minutes, flipping once halfway through, until they're golden and slightly caramelized at the edges. That's where all the flavor lives.
  4. 4While the veggies roast, warm the black beans in a small saucepan over medium-low heat with a pinch of cumin, salt, and a splash of water. Stir occasionally and let them simmer for about 5 minutes until heated through and fragrant.
  5. 5Make the garlic-lime drizzle by whisking together the minced garlic, lime juice, 2 tablespoons of olive oil, honey, red pepper flakes, and a pinch of salt in a small bowl. Taste it — it should be bright, a little tangy, and just slightly sweet.
  6. 6To assemble the bowls, add a scoop of rice to the base, then layer on the roasted vegetables, black beans, and corn. Drizzle generously with the garlic-lime sauce.
  7. 7For meal prep, divide everything into four airtight containers and store in the fridge for up to 4 days. Keep the avocado separate and slice it fresh when you're ready to eat. Reheat in the microwave for 90 seconds and add your toppings.
💡 Improv Tip

The garlic-lime drizzle is your secret weapon here — make a double batch and keep it in a jar in the fridge all week. It works on everything: eggs, roasted chicken, even just dipping bread. That one little jar will cure a lot of refrigerator blindness on its own.
