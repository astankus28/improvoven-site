Easy Chipotle Copycat Sofritas Bowl (Better Than Takeout, Real Talk)

Published May 31, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy chipotle copycat sofritas bowl is what happens when you stop paying $15 for a burrito bowl and start making magic at home — we're talking spicy, smoky braised tofu over rice with all your favorite toppings. The secret is in the chipotle peppers in adobo sauce, which do basically all the heavy lifting for you. Make a big batch on Sunday and you'll be eating like royalty all week for the price of one Chipotle order.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 block (14 oz) extra-firm tofu, drained and pressed
  • 2-3 chipotle peppers in adobo sauce, plus 2 tablespoons of the adobo sauce
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 white onion, finely diced
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 1/2 cup vegetable broth or water
  • 2 cups cooked white or brown rice
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup fresh or jarred salsa
  • 1 cup shredded romaine lettuce
  • 1 avocado, sliced or roughly mashed
  • Sour cream or plain Greek yogurt, for topping (optional)
  • Fresh lime juice, for finishing
  • Fresh cilantro, for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Start by pressing your tofu if you haven't already — wrap it in a clean kitchen towel, set something heavy on top like a cast iron or a big can, and let it sit for at least 10 minutes. The drier the tofu, the better it browns and absorbs all that smoky goodness.
  2. 2While the tofu presses, make your sofritas sauce. Add the chipotle peppers, adobo sauce, tomato paste, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, salt, and vegetable broth to a blender or use an immersion blender in a tall cup. Blend until smooth and set aside. This sauce is the whole vibe — taste it and adjust heat now if you need to.
  3. 3Once pressed, crumble the tofu into a bowl using your hands. You want rustic, uneven pieces — some small and almost ground, some bigger. This texture is what makes it feel meaty and satisfying.
  4. 4Heat your oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced onion and cook for 3-4 minutes until softened and just starting to turn golden. Add the minced garlic and cook another 30 seconds until fragrant.
  5. 5Add the crumbled tofu to the skillet and spread it out. Let it cook undisturbed for 2-3 minutes so it gets a little golden on the bottom — resist the urge to stir constantly. Then give it a good toss and let it sit again for another 2 minutes. That color is flavor.
  6. 6Pour your blended sofritas sauce over the tofu and stir everything together. Reduce heat to medium and let it all simmer together for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened and coated every piece of tofu beautifully. Taste and add more salt or a squeeze of lime if it needs it.
  7. 7While the sofritas finish cooking, warm your black beans in a small saucepan with a pinch of salt and cumin, or just microwave them — no judgment here.
  8. 8Build your bowls: start with a big scoop of rice, then add a generous portion of sofritas, your warm black beans, salsa, shredded lettuce, and avocado. Finish with a big squeeze of fresh lime juice, a dollop of sour cream if you're using it, and fresh cilantro. Eat immediately and feel very proud of yourself.
💡 Improv Tip

Don't skip pressing the tofu — soggy tofu won't brown, and browning is where all that meaty texture comes from. If you're in a rush, cut the tofu into slabs and press them in the skillet itself over medium heat with a little oil, flipping once, before you crumble it in. Also, that can of chipotles in adobo? Freeze the leftovers in a zip-lock bag flat — it lasts forever and you'll reach for it constantly once you see how much flavor it adds to everything.
