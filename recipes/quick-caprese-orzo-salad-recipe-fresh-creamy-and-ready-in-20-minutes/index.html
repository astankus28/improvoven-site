Quick Caprese Orzo Salad Recipe (Fresh, Creamy, and Ready in 20 Minutes)

Published September 29, 2026 · Improv Oven

This quick caprese orzo salad recipe is everything you love about a classic caprese — juicy tomatoes, fresh mozzarella, fragrant basil — tossed with tender orzo and a bright, garlicky drizzle that makes it impossible to stop eating. It works as a light lunch, a side dish for dinner, or honestly just a big bowl you eat standing over the stove because you can't wait. Budget-friendly, no-fuss, and packed with flavor.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
12 mins
Total
22 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dry orzo pasta
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 8 oz fresh mozzarella, torn into bite-sized pieces (or 1 cup mozzarella pearls)
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves, roughly torn
  • 3 tablespoons good olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more for pasta water
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 2 tablespoons sun-dried tomatoes, chopped (optional but highly recommended)

Instructions

  1. 1Bring a medium pot of generously salted water to a boil. Cook the orzo according to package directions until just al dente, usually 8 to 9 minutes. You want it with a tiny bit of bite — it's going into a salad, not a soup. Drain it, then rinse briefly under cool water to stop the cooking and prevent clumping. Give it a little shake and let it sit in the colander while you put everything else together.
  2. 2While the orzo cooks, whisk together the olive oil, balsamic vinegar, minced garlic, red pepper flakes, honey, salt, and pepper in a large mixing bowl. Taste it — it should be tangy, a little sweet, and have that warm garlic kick. Adjust with a pinch more salt or a tiny extra drizzle of honey if needed.
  3. 3Add the drained orzo straight into the bowl with the dressing and toss well while it's still slightly warm. Warm pasta absorbs dressing like a dream, which means every bite is flavorful all the way through rather than just coated on the outside.
  4. 4Fold in the halved cherry tomatoes and the chopped sun-dried tomatoes if you're using them. The sun-dried tomatoes add a concentrated, jammy depth that takes this from simple to seriously good without adding cost.
  5. 5Add the torn fresh mozzarella pieces and gently fold them in — you want big creamy chunks in every bite, not broken-up shreds. Finish by scattering the torn basil leaves over the top and giving everything one more gentle toss.
  6. 6Taste and adjust seasoning one final time. Serve immediately at room temperature for the best texture, or refrigerate for up to 2 days. If it's been in the fridge, let it sit out for 10 minutes before eating and add a fresh drizzle of olive oil to wake it back up.
💡 Improv Tip

Here's the move that makes all the difference: toss your orzo with the dressing while it's still warm. Cold pasta is closed off — warm pasta drinks everything in. This is the secret to a salad that tastes deeply seasoned instead of like pasta someone poured dressing on top of. Same trick works for any pasta salad you make from here on out.
