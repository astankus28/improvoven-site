Easy Potato Salad Recipe for BBQ Season (Creamy, Crowd-Pleasing & Done in 30)

Published May 24, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you need an easy potato salad recipe for your next BBQ, this is the one you're making from now on — creamy, tangy, with a little Cuban-inspired kick that'll have everyone asking what's in it. It comes together with pantry staples you already have, costs almost nothing to make, and holds up beautifully on a picnic table in the summer heat. This is the dish that earns you a permanent spot on the potluck invite list.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs russet or Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons yellow mustard
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika (plus more for garnish)
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 3 stalks celery, finely diced
  • 1/2 small red onion, finely diced
  • 2 tablespoons dill pickle relish (or chopped dill pickles)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped

Instructions

  1. 1Add your potato chunks to a large pot and cover with cold salted water — starting cold helps them cook evenly without turning mushy. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then gently lower in the eggs right into the same pot. Cook everything together for 12-13 minutes until the potatoes are fork-tender but still holding their shape.
  2. 2While that's going, mix together the mayonnaise, yellow mustard, apple cider vinegar, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, and cumin in a large bowl. Give it a taste — it should be creamy, tangy, and a little smoky. Season with salt and pepper. Set it aside.
  3. 3Drain the potatoes and transfer them to a sheet pan or flat surface to cool for 5-10 minutes. Peel and chop the boiled eggs into rough chunks. You want everything slightly warm when it meets the dressing — that's how the potatoes absorb all that flavor.
  4. 4Add the warm potatoes and eggs to the dressing bowl along with the diced celery, red onion, and pickle relish. Fold everything together gently — you're not mashing, you're mixing. You want creamy pockets and chunky bites coexisting peacefully.
  5. 5Taste and adjust — more vinegar if you want more tang, more mayo if you want it creamier, more salt if it needs to wake up. Transfer to a serving dish, dust the top with smoked paprika, and scatter the fresh parsley or cilantro over everything. Refrigerate for at least 20 minutes before serving if you can wait — it only gets better.
💡 Improv Tip

Cold potato salad that tastes flat is usually just under-seasoned — potatoes drink up salt fast, so taste after it chills and don't be shy about adding a pinch more salt and a little extra splash of vinegar right before you serve it. That little hit of acid at the end wakes the whole thing up.
