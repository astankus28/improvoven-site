Easy Fish Cakes with Canned Tuna (Crispy, Cheap, and Seriously Good)

Published June 23, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy fish cake recipe with canned tuna is the kind of thing your abuela would make on a Tuesday when the fridge looked empty but dinner still had to happen. We're talking crispy on the outside, tender on the inside, with a little sofrito magic that takes them from basic to borderline addictive. Budget-friendly, pantry-driven, and on the table in 30 minutes — this one's going into your regular rotation.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (5 oz each) canned tuna in water, drained well
  • 1 cup mashed potatoes (leftover or instant works perfectly)
  • 1/4 cup plain breadcrumbs, plus extra for coating
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup white or yellow onion, finely diced
  • 1/4 cup green bell pepper, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped (or flat-leaf parsley)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional, for a little kick)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil or neutral oil, for pan-frying
  • Lime wedges and hot sauce, for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Drain your tuna really well — press it with a fork against the side of the can or squeeze it in a clean kitchen towel. Wet tuna is the enemy of a good fish cake that holds together, so don't skip this step.
  2. 2In a medium mixing bowl, combine the drained tuna, mashed potatoes, breadcrumbs, beaten egg, and mayonnaise. Mix until everything comes together into a rough paste.
  3. 3Add the diced onion, bell pepper, garlic, cilantro, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes if you're using them. Mix well until the vegetables and spices are evenly distributed throughout.
  4. 4Taste the mixture before it hits the heat — adjust salt, pepper, or spices now so you're not guessing later. This is your moment.
  5. 5Using your hands, divide the mixture into 8 equal portions and shape each one into a round patty about 3/4 inch thick. Press them firmly so they hold their shape. Place a handful of breadcrumbs on a plate and lightly coat each patty on both sides.
  6. 6Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. You want the oil hot but not smoking — a breadcrumb dropped in should sizzle right away.
  7. 7Add the fish cakes in a single layer, working in batches if needed. Do not crowd the pan. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes on the first side without moving them — let that crust build. Flip carefully and cook another 3 to 4 minutes until deeply golden and cooked through.
  8. 8Transfer to a plate lined with paper towels to drain for a minute. Serve hot with lime wedges and your favorite hot sauce. A simple cabbage slaw or sliced avocado on the side makes this a full meal.
💡 Improv Tip

Leftover mashed potatoes are the secret weapon here — they have a drier, starchier texture than fresh-made, which means your fish cakes will hold together better and get crispier in the pan. If you're starting from scratch, boil two medium potatoes, mash them plain with no butter or milk, and let them cool completely before mixing. Cold potato, drained tuna, hot pan — that's the trilogy.
