Easy Potato Salad Recipe for BBQ Season (Creamy, Tangy, Never Boring)

Published May 25, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy potato salad recipe is the one you bring to every BBQ and somehow always go home with an empty bowl — it's creamy, a little tangy, and has just enough personality to make people ask for the recipe. We keep it simple with pantry staples but sneak in a few Latin-inspired touches that take it from basic to 'okay who made this.' No fancy ingredients, no stress — just good food that feeds a crowd without emptying your wallet.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs russet or Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 3 large eggs, hard boiled
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons yellow mustard
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika, plus more for garnish
  • 1/2 cup red onion, finely diced
  • 2 stalks celery, finely chopped
  • 1/4 cup dill pickle relish or finely chopped dill pickles
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley or cilantro for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Add your cubed potatoes to a large pot and cover with cold salted water. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat and cook for 12 to 15 minutes, until fork-tender but not falling apart. You want them soft enough to absorb the dressing but still holding their shape. Drain and spread them out on a sheet pan or large plate so they can steam dry and cool down for about 10 minutes — this step matters, trust me.
  2. 2While the potatoes cool, peel and roughly chop your hard boiled eggs. Set them aside.
  3. 3In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, yellow mustard, apple cider vinegar, garlic powder, cumin, and smoked paprika until smooth and creamy. Taste it — it should be tangy and a little smoky with a good backbone of flavor.
  4. 4Add the cooled potatoes to the bowl and fold gently with the dressing. You're not mashing here, just coating everything nicely. Then add the chopped eggs, red onion, celery, and pickle relish and fold everything together until well combined.
  5. 5Season generously with salt and black pepper. Give it one final taste and adjust — more vinegar if you want extra tang, more mayo if you want it creamier.
  6. 6Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving. This rest time is not optional — it lets the potatoes fully absorb that dressing and brings the whole thing together. Right before serving, dust the top with a little smoked paprika and scatter some fresh parsley or cilantro if you have it. Show up to that BBQ like you know what you're doing.
💡 Improv Tip

The secret weapon here is letting your potatoes cool on a flat surface instead of leaving them in the pot — potatoes trap steam and keep cooking if you leave them piled up, and that's how you end up with mushy potato salad. Also, make this the night before if you can. Cold potato salad that had time to sit overnight is on a completely different level than one made the same day. Your future self will thank you.
