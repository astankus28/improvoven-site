Easy Greek Salad Bowls with Chickpeas

Published May 27, 2026 · Improv Oven

These easy Greek salad bowls with chickpeas are the kind of meal that makes you feel like you actually have your life together — fresh, filling, and on the table in under 20 minutes. We're talking crispy cucumbers, briny olives, creamy feta, and protein-packed chickpeas all sitting on a bed of whatever grain you've got hiding in your pantry. No cooking required, no stress, just pure refrigerator-cleaning magic.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
15 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 large English cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup kalamata olives, pitted and halved
  • 4 oz feta cheese, crumbled
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 cups cooked grain for the base (rice, farro, quinoa, or couscous — whatever you've got)
  • 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley or fresh mint for garnish (optional but worth it)

Instructions

  1. 1Start with your grain base. If you're using leftover rice, couscous, farro, or quinoa from the fridge, even better — this whole bowl comes together in minutes when you've already got that ready. No grain cooked? Couscous is your fastest friend, it's ready in 5 minutes flat. Just pour boiling water over it, cover, and wait.
  2. 2Make the simple Greek dressing right in the bottom of a large bowl: whisk together the olive oil, red wine vinegar, lemon juice, dried oregano, garlic powder, a big pinch of salt, and several cracks of black pepper. Taste it — it should be bright, a little tangy, and smell like a taverna in the best way possible.
  3. 3Add the drained and rinsed chickpeas directly into the dressing and toss to coat. Letting them sit in that dressing for even 5 minutes makes a huge difference — they absorb all that flavor and go from bland to absolutely delicious.
  4. 4Add the diced cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, green bell pepper, and kalamata olives to the bowl with the chickpeas. Toss everything together gently so it all gets coated in that dressing.
  5. 5Divide your cooked grain evenly among four bowls. Spoon the Greek chickpea mixture generously over the top, making sure everyone gets a good amount of that flavorful dressing pooling at the bottom.
  6. 6Finish each bowl with a good crumble of feta cheese over the top. Don't mix it in — let it sit on top so it stays creamy and beautiful. Scatter some fresh parsley or mint if you've got it, and serve immediately or refrigerate for up to 3 days as meal prep.
💡 Improv Tip

The move that separates a good Greek bowl from a great one is letting those chickpeas marinate in the dressing for at least 5 minutes before you build the bowls — or even overnight if you're meal prepping. Chickpeas are like little flavor sponges, mija. They need time to soak up all that oregano and lemon goodness. Also, if your red onion is too sharp for your taste, soak the slices in cold water for 10 minutes and they'll mellow right out.
