What to Make with Canned Sardines: Garlicky Sardine Pasta with Tomatoes and Olives

Published June 14, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're staring into your pantry wondering what to make with canned sardines, this is your answer — a bold, briny, deeply satisfying pasta that comes together in under 30 minutes. It's the kind of dish your Abuela's Italian neighbor would've made on a Friday night when the budget was tight and the flavor had to be loud. Canned sardines are an underrated pantry hero, and this recipe is about to make you a believer.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti or linguine
  • 2 cans (3.75 oz each) canned sardines in olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, drained
  • 1/3 cup pitted Kalamata or green olives, roughly chopped
  • 2 tablespoons capers, drained
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 cup reserved pasta water
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish

Instructions

  1. 1Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook your pasta according to the package directions until just al dente. Before you drain it, scoop out about 1/4 cup of that starchy pasta water and set it aside — that's liquid gold right there.
  2. 2While the pasta cooks, open your sardine cans and drain the oil into a large skillet over medium heat. Don't throw that oil away — it's packed with flavor and it's doing the cooking for you.
  3. 3Add the 2 tablespoons of olive oil to the sardine oil in the skillet. Once it's warm and shimmering, add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes. Let them sizzle gently for about 90 seconds until the garlic is golden and fragrant. Watch it closely — burnt garlic will wreck the whole vibe.
  4. 4Add the drained diced tomatoes to the skillet and stir to combine. Let that cook down for about 5 minutes over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes deepen in color and the sauce tightens up a little.
  5. 5Add the sardines directly into the pan. Using a wooden spoon or spatula, gently break them apart into chunky pieces — you want some texture, not baby food. Stir in the olives and capers and let everything mingle together for 2 minutes.
  6. 6Add the drained pasta straight into the skillet. Toss everything together, adding the reserved pasta water a little at a time to loosen the sauce and help it cling to every strand. Squeeze the lemon juice over the top and toss once more.
  7. 7Taste it. Adjust salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes to your liking. Plate it up, hit it with a generous shower of fresh parsley, and serve immediately.
💡 Improv Tip

Don't skip the pasta water — that starchy, salty liquid is what turns a dry, clunky mess into a silky sauce that actually coats the pasta. And if your sardines come packed in tomato sauce instead of oil, use them! Just reduce the canned tomatoes by half so the dish doesn't get too wet. The ocean is flexible, and so is this recipe.
