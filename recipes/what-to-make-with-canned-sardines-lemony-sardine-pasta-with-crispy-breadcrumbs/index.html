What to Make with Canned Sardines: Lemony Sardine Pasta with Crispy Breadcrumbs

Published October 10, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're staring at a tin of fish and wondering what to make with canned sardines, this lemony pasta is your answer — and it's going to surprise you. Inspired by the Sicilian classic pasta con le sarde, this version keeps things simple with pantry staples but delivers bold, savory flavor that tastes way more intentional than the effort involved. It's ready in under 30 minutes, costs about $2 a serving, and converts sardine skeptics on the first bite.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
18 mins
Total
28 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti or linguine
  • 2 cans (4 oz each) sardines in olive oil, undrained
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (more to taste)
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 2 tablespoons capers, drained (optional but highly recommended)
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley (or 1 tablespoon dried)
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1/2 cup reserved pasta water

Instructions

  1. 1Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a boil. Cook your pasta according to package directions until just al dente. Before draining, scoop out about 1/2 cup of the starchy pasta water and set it aside — this is your secret sauce thickener.
  2. 2While the pasta cooks, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a small skillet over medium heat. Add the panko breadcrumbs and a pinch of salt. Toast them, stirring often, for 3 to 4 minutes until golden and crispy. Remove from heat and set aside — these are your flavor-packed finishing crunch.
  3. 3In a large skillet or Dutch oven, heat the remaining 2 tablespoons of olive oil over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring, until the garlic is fragrant and just starting to turn golden — don't let it brown.
  4. 4Add the sardines directly into the skillet, oil from the tin included. Use a wooden spoon or fork to break them up into chunky pieces as they warm through, about 2 minutes. Don't mash them to paste — you want some texture. Stir in the capers if using.
  5. 5Add the drained pasta directly to the skillet along with a splash of the reserved pasta water. Toss everything together over medium heat for 1 to 2 minutes, adding more pasta water a little at a time until the sauce is glossy and lightly coats the noodles.
  6. 6Remove from heat. Add the lemon zest, lemon juice, and parsley. Toss once more. Taste and adjust with salt, black pepper, and more lemon if you like brightness.
  7. 7Plate the pasta and top each serving with a generous handful of the toasted panko breadcrumbs. Serve immediately — the crunch is the whole experience.
💡 Improv Tip

Breadcrumbs are non-negotiable here — they play the role that Parmesan usually plays in other pasta dishes (the Sicilian tradition actually avoids cheese with fish, and once you taste the crunch you'll understand why). Toast them in the same pan you just used for the garlic and you've got built-in flavor with zero extra dishes.
