Easy American Potato Salad Recipe (The Classic You Actually Crave)

Published June 28, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy American potato salad recipe is the one you bring to every cookout and people think you spent way more time on it than you did — that's the magic. It's creamy, tangy, a little mustardy, and made with stuff you probably already have sitting in your fridge right now. Classic comfort food that hits every single time.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs russet or Yukon gold potatoes, peeled and cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 1 teaspoon salt (for boiling water)
  • 3 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon yellow mustard
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon paprika (plus more for garnish)
  • 3 stalks celery, diced small
  • 1/2 medium white or yellow onion, diced small
  • 2 tablespoons sweet pickle relish (or 3 diced dill pickles if that's your thing)

Instructions

  1. 1Add your potato chunks to a large pot and cover with cold water by about an inch. Add a generous teaspoon of salt, bring it to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce to a steady simmer. Cook until the potatoes are fork-tender but not falling apart, about 12 to 15 minutes. You want them cooked through, not mushy — they'll keep cooking a little even after you drain them.
  2. 2While the potatoes are boiling, drop your eggs into a small saucepan, cover with cold water, and bring to a boil. Once boiling, cover the pot, turn off the heat, and let them sit for 11 minutes. Then transfer the eggs to a bowl of ice water to stop the cooking. This gives you perfectly set yolks every time.
  3. 3Drain the potatoes and spread them out on a sheet pan or large plate. Let them cool for about 10 to 15 minutes at room temperature. Do not skip this — hot potatoes dumped into mayo turns into a greasy mess and nobody wants that.
  4. 4While everything cools, peel and chop your hard-boiled eggs. In a large bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, yellow mustard, apple cider vinegar, sugar, salt, black pepper, and paprika until smooth and creamy. Taste it — this is your dressing, so make sure you love it before everything goes in.
  5. 5Add the cooled potatoes, chopped eggs, diced celery, onion, and relish to the bowl with the dressing. Fold everything together gently using a large spoon or spatula. You want the potatoes coated and creamy, but you're not mashing them — leave some texture in there.
  6. 6Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving. This is not optional — the salad needs that time for the flavors to come together and the dressing to soak into the potatoes. Right before serving, dust the top with a little extra paprika for that classic look.
💡 Improv Tip

If your potato salad ever tastes a little flat after it's been in the fridge, add a small splash of apple cider vinegar and a pinch of salt before serving — cold dulls flavors, so it almost always needs a little refresh before it hits the table.
