Quick Vegetable Stir Fry Recipe (Use Whatever's in Your Fridge)

Published June 15, 2026 · Improv Oven

This quick vegetable stir fry recipe is exactly what you need when the fridge looks empty but actually isn't — you just can't see it yet. We're pulling together whatever vegetables are hanging around, hitting them with a punchy sauce, and getting dinner on the table in under 20 minutes. Miami style: fast, bold, and nothing goes to waste.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
10 mins
Total
20 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil or any neutral oil you have
  • 1 medium onion, sliced thin
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated (or 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger)
  • 1 bell pepper, any color, sliced into strips
  • 2 cups broccoli florets (fresh or frozen, both work)
  • 1 medium zucchini, sliced into half-moons
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage or bok choy
  • 1 medium carrot, sliced thin on the diagonal
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce (or hoisin sauce, or just more soy sauce)
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil (optional but worth it)
  • 1 teaspoon sugar or honey
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch dissolved in 2 tablespoons cold water
  • Pinch of red pepper flakes
  • Cooked rice or noodles, for serving
  • Sesame seeds or sliced green onion for topping, optional

Instructions

  1. 1Mix your sauce first so you are not scrambling later. In a small bowl, stir together the soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, sugar, and red pepper flakes. Give the cornstarch slurry a stir too and set both bowls right next to the stove. This is your mise en place moment and it makes everything go smooth.
  2. 2Get your pan screaming hot before anything goes in. A wok is ideal but a large skillet or even a wide pot works just fine. Heat the vegetable oil over high heat until it just starts to shimmer — you want real heat here, that is what gives stir fry that slightly smoky, restaurant flavor at home.
  3. 3Add the onion and carrot first since they take the longest. Stir constantly and let them cook for about 2 minutes until the onion starts to soften and get a little color on the edges.
  4. 4Add the garlic and ginger and stir for about 30 seconds. Your kitchen is going to smell incredible right now. Do not walk away because garlic burns fast on high heat.
  5. 5Toss in the broccoli, bell pepper, zucchini, and cabbage all at once. Keep everything moving with a wooden spoon or tongs. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring frequently. You want the vegetables tender but still with a little bite — nobody wants mushy stir fry.
  6. 6Pour the sauce over the vegetables and toss everything to coat. Let it cook for 1 minute so the sauce heats through and starts to caramelize slightly on the vegetables.
  7. 7Give the cornstarch slurry one more stir and drizzle it into the pan while you keep stirring. Within about 30 seconds the sauce will thicken up and cling to every piece of vegetable. That glossy coat is exactly what you are going for.
  8. 8Pull the pan off the heat and taste. Adjust with a little more soy sauce if it needs salt or a pinch more sugar if it feels too sharp. Serve immediately over rice or noodles and top with sesame seeds or green onion if you have them.
💡 Improv Tip

The number one secret to stir fry that actually tastes like something is a dry pan and dry vegetables — if your veggies are wet from washing, pat them down with a paper towel before they go in. Water in a hot pan steams everything instead of searing it and you lose all that gorgeous caramelized flavor. Also do not overcrowd the pan; if you are doubling the recipe, cook it in two batches. I know that sounds like extra work but it takes three extra minutes and the difference is real.
