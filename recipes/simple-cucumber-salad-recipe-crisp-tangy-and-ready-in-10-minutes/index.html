Simple Cucumber Salad Recipe (Crisp, Tangy, and Ready in 10 Minutes)

Published October 2, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple cucumber salad recipe is the kind of thing you throw together in minutes and somehow end up eating straight from the bowl. Thinly sliced cucumbers get tossed in a light vinegar dressing with a little sesame oil, soy sauce, and a pinch of sugar — it's bright, refreshing, and honestly addictive. It works as a side dish, a light lunch, or a palate cleanser next to anything rich and heavy.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
10 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 large English cucumbers (or 4 Persian cucumbers)
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (or honey)
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional, but recommended)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated (optional)
  • 2 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds, toasted
  • Small handful of fresh cilantro (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Slice the cucumbers thin — about 1/8 inch thick. You can use a sharp knife, a mandoline if you have one, or even a vegetable peeler for ribbon-style strips. No mandoline needed; a steady hand works just fine.
  2. 2Place the sliced cucumbers in a colander or bowl, toss them with the kosher salt, and let them sit for 5 to 10 minutes. This draws out excess water so your dressing doesn't get watered down. Then pat them dry with a clean towel or paper towels.
  3. 3While the cucumbers are resting, whisk together the rice vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar, garlic, and ginger (if using) in a large bowl until the sugar dissolves. Taste it — it should be tangy, a little savory, and just barely sweet.
  4. 4Add the dried cucumber slices to the bowl and toss everything together until the cucumbers are well coated. Let the salad sit for 2 to 3 minutes so the flavors can settle in — this is a good time to slice your green onions.
  5. 5Transfer to a serving plate or bowl. Top with sliced green onions, toasted sesame seeds, and fresh cilantro if you're using it. Serve immediately for maximum crunch, or refrigerate for up to an hour if you want it extra cold and slightly more pickled-tasting.
💡 Improv Tip

Salting the cucumbers is the move that separates a soggy cucumber salad from a crisp one — don't skip it. If you only have regular cucumbers (not English or Persian), scoop out the seeds with a spoon before slicing so you don't end up with a watery mess. And if you don't have rice vinegar, plain white vinegar works — just use a little less since it's sharper.
