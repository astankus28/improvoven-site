Waffle Bites Budget Recipe: Cinnamon Sugar Dessert Waffles with Maple Dipping Sauce

Published October 14, 2026 · Improv Oven

These waffle bites are the budget recipe you never knew you needed — golden, crispy little squares dusted in cinnamon sugar and served with a two-ingredient maple dipping sauce that tastes like something from a fancy brunch spot. Made entirely from pantry staples, this dessert comes together in under 30 minutes and costs next to nothing. Think funnel cake meets churro, but way easier and made right in your waffle iron.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk (any kind)
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter or neutral oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar (for coating)
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon (for coating)
  • 1 tablespoon butter (for coating)
  • 3 tablespoons maple syrup (for dipping sauce)
  • 2 tablespoons butter (for dipping sauce)
  • pinch of salt (for dipping sauce)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your waffle iron on medium-high heat. While it heats up, whisk together the flour, 1 tablespoon sugar, baking powder, and salt in a medium bowl.
  2. 2In a separate small bowl or measuring cup, whisk the egg, milk, 2 tablespoons melted butter, and vanilla extract until combined. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir just until a smooth batter forms — a few small lumps are totally fine. Don't overmix or your waffles will be tough.
  3. 3Lightly grease your waffle iron with a little butter or cooking spray. Pour in just enough batter to fill about half the iron (you want thin, quick-cooking waffles here, not thick Belgian-style ones). Close and cook until golden and crispy, about 3 to 4 minutes. Repeat with remaining batter.
  4. 4While the waffles cook, mix the 3 tablespoons sugar and 1 teaspoon cinnamon together in a shallow bowl or plate. Set aside.
  5. 5As each waffle comes off the iron, immediately cut or tear it into bite-sized pieces — roughly 1 to 2 inches each. Toss the hot pieces in the 1 tablespoon melted butter, then roll them in the cinnamon sugar mixture until coated all over. The heat from the fresh waffle makes the sugar stick perfectly.
  6. 6Make the maple dipping sauce: In a small saucepan over low heat, melt 2 tablespoons butter with the maple syrup and a pinch of salt. Stir until combined and slightly glossy, about 1 to 2 minutes. Pour into a small bowl for dipping.
  7. 7Pile the cinnamon sugar waffle bites onto a plate and serve immediately with the warm maple dipping sauce on the side. These are best eaten fresh while they're still crispy.
💡 Improv Tip

The single biggest mistake people make is letting the waffle bites sit before coating them — you want to toss them in the cinnamon sugar while they're steaming hot right off the iron, just like you would with fresh-fried churros. That heat is what makes the coating stick and gives you that slightly caramelized crunch. If you're making a big batch, work in waves so nobody's eating a soggy waffle bite.
