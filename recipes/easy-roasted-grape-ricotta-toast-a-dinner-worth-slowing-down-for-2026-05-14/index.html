Easy Roasted Grape Ricotta Toast (A Dinner Worth Slowing Down For)

Published May 14, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy roasted grape ricotta toast is the kind of dinner that sounds fancy but comes together in under 30 minutes with stuff you probably already have. The grapes get jammy and sweet in the oven, the ricotta stays cool and creamy underneath, and together on a thick slice of crusty bread it hits like a full meal — no stove drama required. It's giving aperitivo night in your own kitchen, Miami style.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups red seedless grapes
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 4 thick slices crusty bread (sourdough or Cuban bread work great)
  • 1 cup whole milk ricotta cheese
  • 1 garlic clove, halved
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh thyme or rosemary (a few sprigs)
  • Optional: a drizzle of hot honey or extra balsamic for finishing

Instructions

  1. 1Heat your oven to 400°F. Toss the grapes on a small sheet pan with 2 tablespoons of olive oil, the balsamic vinegar, honey, a pinch of salt, and a few sprigs of thyme or rosemary. Spread them out so they have room to roast — don't pile them or they'll steam instead of caramelize.
  2. 2Roast the grapes for 18 to 20 minutes, until they're blistered, jammy, and starting to burst and pool into a little glossy sauce at the bottom of the pan. Your kitchen is going to smell incredible right about now.
  3. 3While the grapes are doing their thing, season the ricotta in a bowl with a pinch of salt, black pepper, and the red pepper flakes. Give it a stir and taste it — it should have a little kick and flavor on its own.
  4. 4Toast or grill your bread slices until golden and sturdy. The moment they come out, rub each slice with the cut side of the garlic clove — just one or two passes, enough to perfume the bread without overpowering it.
  5. 5Drizzle the remaining tablespoon of olive oil over your toasts, then spread a generous layer of the seasoned ricotta on each slice. Spoon the roasted grapes on top along with all those jammy juices from the pan — that's liquid gold, don't leave it behind.
  6. 6Finish with a pinch of flaky salt, a crack of black pepper, and a drizzle of hot honey or extra balsamic if you want to go there. Serve immediately while the toast is still crispy and the contrast between warm grapes and cool ricotta is at its best.
💡 Improv Tip

If your ricotta feels a little watery straight out of the container, set it in a fine mesh strainer over a bowl for 10 minutes before seasoning it — this thickens it up so it stays on the toast instead of sliding off. And honestly, any grapes work here, but red or black varieties go sweeter and more jammy than green, so grab those if you can.
