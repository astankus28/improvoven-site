Easy One-Pot Tortellini Soup Recipe You'll Make Every Week

Published July 1, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy tortellini soup recipe comes together in one pot in about 30 minutes — which means less dishes and more time actually enjoying your meal, and we love that. It's the kind of cozy, filling soup that tastes like you spent all day on it, but really you just opened a few pantry staples and let the pot do the work. Big flavors, tiny effort, and your whole family is going to ask for it again.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional but recommended)
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 cup water
  • 9 oz refrigerated or frozen cheese tortellini
  • 2 cups fresh or frozen spinach
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Parmesan cheese for topping
  • Fresh basil or parsley for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Once it's shimmering, add the diced onion and cook for about 4 minutes until it's soft and translucent. Don't rush this part — those soft onions are your flavor foundation.
  2. 2Add the minced garlic, Italian seasoning, and red pepper flakes. Stir everything together and let it cook for about 1 minute until the garlic is fragrant. Your kitchen should smell incredible right about now.
  3. 3Pour in the diced tomatoes with all their juices, the broth, and the water. Stir it all together and bring it up to a gentle boil over medium-high heat.
  4. 4Once it's boiling, add the tortellini right into the pot. Cook according to the package directions — usually about 3 to 5 minutes for refrigerated tortellini or a couple minutes longer for frozen. Stir occasionally so they don't stick together.
  5. 5Drop in the spinach and stir until it wilts down, which takes about 1 to 2 minutes. Taste the broth and season with salt and black pepper until it's just right for you.
  6. 6Ladle into bowls, hit it with a generous handful of Parmesan, and some fresh basil if you've got it. Serve hot and watch it disappear.
💡 Improv Tip

If your broth is looking a little thin after everything cooks, let it simmer uncovered for an extra 3 to 5 minutes — it'll concentrate the flavors and thicken up naturally. Also, tortellini will keep absorbing liquid as the soup sits, so if you're saving leftovers, add a splash of broth when you reheat it to bring it back to life.
