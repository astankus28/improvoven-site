Simple Tostadas de Tinga Recipe (Quick Weeknight Win)

Published May 11, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple tostadas de tinga recipe comes together fast — we're talking 30 minutes from fridge to table, no culinary degree required. The smoky chipotle chicken piled high on a crunchy tostada is the kind of meal that makes people think you spent way more time in the kitchen than you did. Classic Miami move: maximum flavor, minimum effort.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked shredded chicken (rotisserie works perfectly here)
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes, drained
  • 2-3 chipotle peppers in adobo sauce, chopped (plus 1 tbsp adobo sauce from the can)
  • 1/2 medium white onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil (vegetable or canola)
  • 8 tostada shells (store-bought is totally fine)
  • 1 can (15 oz) refried beans, warmed
  • 1 cup shredded iceberg or romaine lettuce
  • 1/2 cup Mexican crema or sour cream
  • 1/2 cup crumbled cotija cheese (or feta if that's what you've got)
  • 1 avocado, sliced, or a few spoonfuls of guacamole
  • Lime wedges for serving
  • Hot sauce, optional but encouraged

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the sliced onion and cook, stirring occasionally, for about 4-5 minutes until it softens and starts to get a little golden around the edges. Add the garlic and cook one more minute until fragrant — your kitchen should be smelling amazing right now.
  2. 2Add the drained diced tomatoes, chopped chipotle peppers, and that spoonful of adobo sauce into the pan. Stir everything together and let it cook for about 3-4 minutes, mashing the tomatoes down a little with your spoon so it becomes a rough, chunky sauce.
  3. 3Season the sauce with oregano, cumin, and salt, then stir in your shredded chicken. Toss everything together until the chicken is fully coated and soaked into that smoky tomato sauce. Let it simmer on medium-low for 5-6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the mixture tightens up and isn't too saucy. Taste it — adjust salt or add more adobo if you want more heat.
  4. 4While the tinga simmers, warm your refried beans in a small saucepan or microwave until they're spreadable and hot. Lay out your tostada shells on a flat surface so you're ready to build.
  5. 5Time to stack. Spread a generous layer of warm refried beans on each tostada shell — this is the glue that holds everything together, don't skip it. Pile on a good scoop of the chicken tinga, then top with shredded lettuce, a drizzle of crema, crumbled cotija, and a few slices of avocado.
  6. 6Finish with a squeeze of fresh lime over everything and a few dashes of hot sauce if you like it with some kick. Serve immediately while the tostadas are still crunchy — these don't wait around.
💡 Improv Tip

Leftover tinga is the real prize here. It reheats beautifully and works stuffed into tacos, spooned over rice, or scrambled into eggs the next morning. Make a double batch of the chicken while you're at it — future you will be very grateful.
