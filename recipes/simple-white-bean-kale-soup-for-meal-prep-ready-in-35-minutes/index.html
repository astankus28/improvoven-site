Simple White Bean Kale Soup for Meal Prep (Ready in 35 Minutes)

Published November 19, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple white bean kale soup is exactly the kind of meal prep that saves your week — hearty, nourishing, and so good it actually gets better by day three. It's built almost entirely from pantry staples and a bunch of kale, which means you can make it any time the fridge looks sad. One big pot Sunday night, lunches and dinners handled.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 medium carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon dried rosemary, crumbled
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 2 (15 oz) cans white beans (cannellini or great northern), drained and rinsed
  • 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 5 cups low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 large bunch curly or lacinato kale, stems removed and leaves roughly chopped (about 4 cups packed)
  • 1 parmesan rind (optional but highly recommended)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Grated parmesan and crusty bread for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Warm the olive oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery with a generous pinch of salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, for about 6–7 minutes until the vegetables have softened and the onion is translucent.
  2. 2Add the garlic, thyme, rosemary, and red pepper flakes. Stir and cook for another 60 seconds until fragrant — your kitchen should smell amazing at this point.
  3. 3Pour in the diced tomatoes (with their juices) and stir everything together. Let it cook for 2 minutes to deepen the flavor.
  4. 4Add the drained white beans and the broth. If you have a parmesan rind in your freezer, drop it in now — it melts quietly into the broth and gives the soup a subtle, savory richness that's hard to explain and impossible to skip once you've tried it.
  5. 5Bring the soup to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat and let it simmer uncovered for 10 minutes. Use the back of a wooden spoon to lightly mash some of the beans against the side of the pot — this thickens the broth naturally without any flour or cream.
  6. 6Add the chopped kale in batches, stirring it in as it wilts down. Simmer for another 5 minutes until the kale is tender but still bright green. Fish out and discard the parmesan rind if you used one.
  7. 7Taste and adjust salt and pepper. Ladle into bowls with a shower of grated parmesan and a thick slice of crusty bread on the side. For meal prep, let the soup cool completely before transferring to airtight containers — it keeps in the fridge for up to 5 days and freezes beautifully for up to 3 months.
💡 Improv Tip

Kale is one of the rare greens that actually holds up in soup over several days — it stays tender without turning to mush, which makes this genuinely one of the best meal prep soups you can make. If you want even more body, blend about 1 cup of the soup in a blender (or use an immersion blender briefly) before adding the kale. It makes the broth silky and thick without changing the flavor at all.
