Boxed Mac and Cheese Breakfast Skillet (Budget Recipe With Eggs and Crispy Edges)

Published August 25, 2026 · Improv Oven

This boxed mac cheese budget recipe is about to completely rewire how you think about breakfast — creamy, cheesy noodles hit a hot skillet until the bottom crisps up like a potato cake, then you nest a couple of eggs right on top and let them cook in the residual heat. It sounds unhinged, it tastes like diner comfort food, and it comes together for under two dollars a serving. Your Tuesday morning just got a serious upgrade.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 box (7.25 oz) boxed macaroni and cheese
  • 2 tablespoons butter (divided)
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 tablespoons shredded cheddar (optional, for extra melt)
  • 1 green onion, thinly sliced (optional garnish)
  • Hot sauce for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Cook the macaroni according to box directions in salted boiling water until just tender, about 7–8 minutes. Drain well and return the pot to low heat.
  2. 2Add 1 tablespoon of the butter, the milk, and the cheese powder packet. Stir until you have a smooth, creamy mac and cheese. Pull it off the heat and stir in the garlic powder, smoked paprika, and a pinch of black pepper. Taste it — season if needed.
  3. 3Heat a 10-inch nonstick or cast iron skillet over medium heat. Add the remaining tablespoon of butter and swirl to coat. Once the butter is foamy and just starting to turn golden, pour in all the mac and cheese and spread it into an even layer that covers the whole pan.
  4. 4Let the mac and cheese sit untouched for 4–5 minutes. You're building a crispy bottom crust — resist the urge to stir. You'll know it's ready when the edges start to look set and golden.
  5. 5Use a spoon to make 4 small wells in the mac and cheese (evenly spaced). Crack one egg into each well. Sprinkle the optional shredded cheddar around the eggs if using.
  6. 6Cover the skillet with a lid or a sheet of foil. Cook on medium-low heat for 3–5 minutes, until the egg whites are fully set but the yolks are still jammy and runny. If you prefer fully cooked yolks, give it another 1–2 minutes.
  7. 7Slide the whole skillet onto the table. Scatter green onions over the top if you've got them, hit everything with a few dashes of hot sauce, and serve straight from the pan with a big spoon. The crispy bottom will release in chunks — that's the best part.
💡 Improv Tip

Day-old mac and cheese works even better here — leftover mac from the fridge is drier, which means it crisps up faster and holds together more like a hash. If your fresh mac feels too loose to form a crust, stir in a tiny splash less milk than the box calls for, or let it cool in the pot for five minutes before it hits the skillet.
