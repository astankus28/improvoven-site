Easy Egg Muffin Recipe (Cheesy, Loaded, Meal-Prep Ready)

Published September 13, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy egg muffin recipe is the answer to every chaotic morning when you need something real but have zero time — just crack some eggs, raid the fridge for mix-ins, and the oven does the rest. These little guys bake up fluffy, cheesy, and satisfying in a standard muffin tin, and they reheat beautifully all week long. Think of them as your personal frittata in portable form — endlessly riffable, totally forgiving, and genuinely delicious.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
22 mins
Total
32 mins
Serves
12 muffins

Ingredients

  • 8 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup milk (any kind works)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 3/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese, divided
  • 1/2 cup diced bell pepper (any color)
  • 1/3 cup diced yellow onion
  • 1/2 cup baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • 4 strips bacon or 1/3 cup diced ham (optional)
  • cooking spray or a little butter for greasing

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 375°F. Grease a standard 12-cup muffin tin really well — every cup, including the rims — with cooking spray or a light coat of butter. Eggs are sneaky and will stick if you give them half a chance.
  2. 2If you're using bacon, cook it in a skillet over medium heat until crispy, then let it cool and crumble it up. If you're using ham, just dice it small and you're ready to go. Skip this step entirely for a vegetarian version — the veggies carry it just fine.
  3. 3In a large bowl or a big liquid measuring cup (the pour spout is your friend here), crack in all 8 eggs. Add the milk, salt, pepper, and garlic powder and whisk everything together until the yolks and whites are fully combined and the mixture looks uniform. No streaks of white.
  4. 4Divide your diced bell pepper, onion, spinach, and bacon or ham evenly among the 12 muffin cups — about a tablespoon or so of filling in each. Sprinkle about half the cheddar over the fillings.
  5. 5Pour the egg mixture slowly and evenly over the fillings in each cup, filling them about three-quarters of the way up. The filling will float a little — that's completely normal and fine.
  6. 6Sprinkle the remaining cheddar on top of each cup. Slide the pan into the oven and bake for 20 to 22 minutes, until the egg muffins are puffed up, set in the center, and just lightly golden on top.
  7. 7Let them cool in the pan for 5 minutes before running a thin knife or small spatula around the edges and popping them out. They'll deflate a little as they cool — totally normal. Serve warm right away, or let them cool completely before storing.
💡 Improv Tip

For meal prep, let the egg muffins cool completely, then store them in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days. Reheat two at a time in the microwave for about 45 seconds — they come back to life fast. You can also freeze them individually wrapped for up to 2 months and just thaw overnight in the fridge. Mix-in swaps are wide open: try sun-dried tomatoes and feta, leftover roasted veggies, or a little hot sauce stirred right into the egg base.
