Simple Brownies Recipe from Scratch (Fudgy, One Bowl, No Mixer)

Published July 30, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple brownies recipe from scratch uses nothing but pantry staples — butter, sugar, cocoa powder, eggs, and flour — and it comes together in one bowl in about 30 minutes. No box mix, no special equipment, no stress. Just dense, fudgy brownies with crackly tops that taste like you actually know what you're doing.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
16 brownies

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup (1 stick / 113g) unsalted butter
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
  • Optional: 1/2 cup chocolate chips or chopped walnuts

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease an 8x8-inch baking pan with butter or cooking spray, then line it with a strip of parchment paper leaving a little overhang on two sides — that's your handle for lifting them out cleanly later.
  2. 2Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over low heat, or in a large microwave-safe bowl in 30-second bursts. You want it fully melted but not bubbling hot. Let it cool for just a minute.
  3. 3Whisk the sugar into the melted butter until combined. It'll look grainy — that's totally fine. Add the eggs one at a time, whisking well after each one, then stir in the vanilla. The mixture should look glossy and smooth at this point.
  4. 4Add the cocoa powder, flour, salt, and baking powder directly to the bowl. Switch to a rubber spatula or wooden spoon and fold everything together until just combined — stop as soon as you don't see dry streaks. Overmixing makes cakey brownies; we want fudgy ones. Fold in chocolate chips or walnuts now if you're using them.
  5. 5Pour the batter into your prepared pan and spread it into an even layer all the way to the corners. Bake for 22 to 26 minutes. Start checking at 22 — a toothpick inserted 1 inch from the edge should come out with moist crumbs (not raw batter, not totally clean). The center will look slightly underdone; that's exactly right.
  6. 6Let the brownies cool in the pan on a wire rack for at least 20 minutes before lifting them out and cutting. I know. It's hard. But warm brownies cut into a mess — give them the time. Slice into 16 squares and try not to eat four of them immediately.
💡 Improv Tip

The secret to that shiny, crackly top (the thing everyone loves about bakery brownies) is vigorously whisking the eggs and sugar together until the mixture is pale and slightly thick before you add anything else. That extra minute of whisking dissolves the sugar just enough to form that gorgeous crust as it bakes. Worth it every single time.
