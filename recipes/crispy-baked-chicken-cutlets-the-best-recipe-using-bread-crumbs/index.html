Crispy Baked Chicken Cutlets: The Best Recipe Using Bread Crumbs

Published November 20, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're looking for recipes using bread crumbs that actually deliver a satisfying crunch without deep frying, this is your new weeknight hero. These Italian-American style baked chicken cutlets come out golden and crispy on the outside, juicy on the inside — and cost next to nothing to make. All you need is a pack of chicken breasts, whatever bread crumbs are sitting in your pantry, and a handful of pantry staples you already have.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
22 mins
Total
37 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 large boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 1.5 lbs total)
  • 1 cup plain or Italian-seasoned bread crumbs
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan (the shelf-stable kind works great here)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons milk
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil or neutral oil (for drizzling)
  • Lemon wedges and fresh parsley for serving (optional but worth it)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with a wire rack if you have one — this lets hot air circulate under the cutlets for maximum crispiness. No rack? Just line the pan with foil and it'll still be delicious.
  2. 2Butterfly your chicken breasts: lay each one flat, press down gently with your palm, and slice horizontally through the middle to get two thin cutlets. You'll end up with 4 pieces total. If they're uneven, give them a few whacks with a rolling pin or the bottom of a heavy pan to even them out to about 1/2 inch thick.
  3. 3Set up your breading station with three shallow dishes: flour in the first, eggs beaten with milk in the second, and bread crumbs mixed with Parmesan, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, paprika, salt, and pepper in the third.
  4. 4Working one cutlet at a time, dredge in flour and shake off any excess — this gives the egg something to grip. Dip into the egg mixture, letting any drips fall off. Then press firmly into the bread crumb mixture, coating both sides well. Don't be shy — press those crumbs in.
  5. 5Place the breaded cutlets on your prepared baking sheet in a single layer. Drizzle or lightly brush the tops with olive oil — this is what gives you that golden, fried-looking crust without the mess of a frying pan.
  6. 6Bake at 425°F for 20 to 22 minutes, flipping once halfway through at the 11-minute mark. The cutlets are done when golden brown on both sides and the internal temperature hits 165°F. If you want extra browning, hit them under the broiler for 1 to 2 minutes at the end — just don't walk away.
  7. 7Let them rest for 3 minutes before cutting in. Serve with lemon wedges for squeezing over the top, and a sprinkle of fresh parsley if you've got it. These go with everything — salad, pasta, roasted veggies, or straight from the pan over the sink with no judgment from us.
💡 Improv Tip

Plain bread crumbs with no seasoning? No problem — just add a little more garlic powder, a pinch of dried Italian herbs, and a good hit of salt and you're right back on track. Panko works here too and gives you an even crunchier crust, so use whatever's in the back of your cupboard.
