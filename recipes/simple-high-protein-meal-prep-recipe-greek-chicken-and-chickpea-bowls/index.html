Simple High Protein Meal Prep Recipe: Greek Chicken and Chickpea Bowls

Published November 23, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're hunting for simple high protein meal prep recipe ideas that don't taste like sad desk lunches, these Greek chicken and chickpea bowls are your answer. We're talking juicy spiced chicken thighs, fiber-packed chickpeas, crisp cucumber, and a lemony yogurt drizzle — all prepped in about 35 minutes and ready to grab all week. It's fridge-to-fork fast, genuinely filling, and the kind of meal that makes you actually look forward to lunch.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 English cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups cooked rice or farro (about 2/3 cup dry)
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice (about 1 lemon)
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated or minced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh or dried dill (or parsley works great too)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. 1Heat your oven to 425°F and line a large sheet pan with foil or parchment — this is your cleanup insurance policy.
  2. 2Pat the chicken thighs dry with a paper towel. This matters more than people think: dry chicken browns, wet chicken steams. Toss them in a bowl with 1 tablespoon olive oil, garlic powder, oregano, smoked paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper until every piece is well coated.
  3. 3Drain and rinse your chickpeas, then pat those dry too. Toss them in the remaining tablespoon of olive oil with a pinch of salt and pepper. Spread the chickpeas on one side of the sheet pan and the chicken thighs on the other side — roasting them together is the whole trick here.
  4. 4Slide the pan into the oven and roast for 22 to 25 minutes, until the chicken hits 165°F internally and the chickpeas are golden and starting to crisp at the edges. If you have a minute, give the chickpeas a quick shake at the halfway point.
  5. 5While everything roasts, whisk together the Greek yogurt, lemon juice, grated garlic, and dill in a small bowl. Taste it — it should be bright, creamy, and a little garlicky. Season with salt and pepper, then set it aside.
  6. 6Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes off the pan, then slice it into strips or just cut each thigh into chunks — whatever makes your meal prep containers happy.
  7. 7Now assemble your four containers: a base of rice or farro, a scoop of roasted chickpeas, a few slices of chicken, and a handful of diced cucumber, cherry tomatoes, and red onion. Store the yogurt sauce separately in small containers or zip bags so nothing gets soggy.
  8. 8These bowls keep beautifully in the fridge for up to 4 days. When you're ready to eat, just drizzle on the yogurt sauce and you're done. No reheating required if you like them at room temperature — which honestly makes them even better for on-the-go.
💡 Improv Tip

The yogurt sauce is secretly the MVP of this whole recipe — make a double batch. It works as a dip, a salad dressing, a spread for pita, and a sauce for pretty much any protein you roast the rest of the week. Fridge life is about 5 days and it only gets better as the garlic mellows out.
