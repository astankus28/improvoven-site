Easy Flan Recipe (Silky, Classic, and Foolproof)

Published August 7, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy flan recipe is the one you'll make over and over — creamy, wobbling perfection with that golden caramel on top that looks like you really know what you're doing. It's a classic Cuban and Latin American dessert that uses just a handful of pantry staples you probably already have. No fancy equipment, no water bath drama — just simple, honest flan that tastes like it came from your abuela's kitchen.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
50 mins
Total
1 hr 5 mins (plus 2 hrs chilling)
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar (for caramel)
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 350°F. Get a 9-inch round baking dish or a standard loaf pan ready — either works great for this.
  2. 2Make the caramel: Pour the sugar and water into a small saucepan over medium heat. Do not stir — just let it melt and bubble. Swirl the pan gently if you need to, but keep your hands off the spoon. After about 7–10 minutes it'll turn a deep amber color, like iced tea. Pull it off the heat immediately and carefully pour it into the bottom of your baking dish. Tilt the dish to coat the bottom evenly, then set it aside to harden. It hardens fast — that's fine.
  3. 3Make the custard: In a blender or a large bowl with a whisk, combine the sweetened condensed milk, evaporated milk, eggs, vanilla, and salt. Blend or whisk until completely smooth, about 30 seconds. Try not to create too many bubbles — go slow and gentle at the end if whisking by hand.
  4. 4Gently pour the custard mixture over the hardened caramel in the baking dish. It's okay if the caramel cracks a little — it'll melt back together in the oven.
  5. 5Set up a water bath: Place your flan dish inside a larger roasting pan or baking dish. Pour hot tap water into the outer pan until it comes about halfway up the sides of the flan dish. This gentle steam is what gives flan its silky texture — don't skip it.
  6. 6Carefully transfer to the oven and bake for 45–55 minutes, until the edges are set but the center still has a gentle jiggle — like Jell-O, not like water. Start checking at 45 minutes.
  7. 7Remove the flan dish from the water bath and let it cool on the counter for 30 minutes. Then cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or overnight. Overnight is honestly better.
  8. 8To unmold, run a thin knife or offset spatula around the edge of the flan. Place a large plate or rimmed platter on top of the dish — bigger than the dish so the caramel sauce has somewhere to go. Flip it confidently in one motion. Lift the dish slowly and let the caramel pour over the top. Slice and serve cold.
💡 Improv Tip

The caramel is the part that scares people, but here's the secret: don't touch it. Once the sugar and water hit the pan, resist every urge to stir. Just watch, swirl gently if it's cooking unevenly, and pull it off the moment it hits that deep golden-amber. Too light and it'll be sweet without depth. Too dark and it turns bitter. You've got about a 60-second window at the right color — so stay right there with it. Once you nail the caramel, the rest of this recipe is genuinely effortless.
