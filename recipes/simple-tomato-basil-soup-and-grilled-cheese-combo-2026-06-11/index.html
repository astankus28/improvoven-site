Simple Tomato Basil Soup and Grilled Cheese Combo

Published June 11, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple tomato basil soup and grilled cheese combo is the kind of meal that makes you wonder why you ever ordered delivery — it comes together in 30 minutes from stuff you probably already have sitting around. We're talking a rich, velvety soup with real depth and a buttery, golden grilled cheese that's basically made for dunking. Classic comfort food, Miami pantry style.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, roughly chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 2 cans (28 oz each) whole peeled tomatoes
  • 1 cup vegetable broth or water
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves, plus more for garnish
  • 2 tablespoons butter (for soup, optional but worth it)
  • 8 slices sandwich bread
  • 8 slices American or cheddar cheese
  • 3 tablespoons softened butter (for grilled cheese)

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it softens and starts to turn a little golden on the edges.
  2. 2Add the smashed garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant — you'll smell it, trust the process.
  3. 3Pour in both cans of tomatoes with all their juices, the broth, sugar, oregano, and smoked paprika. Stir everything together, bring it up to a gentle simmer, and let it cook uncovered for 15 minutes so the flavors get friendly with each other.
  4. 4While the soup simmers, make your grilled cheese. Spread softened butter on one side of each bread slice. Layer 2 slices of cheese between two pieces of bread, butter-side out.
  5. 5Heat a skillet or griddle over medium-low heat. Cook each sandwich for about 3 to 4 minutes per side, pressing gently with a spatula, until both sides are deep golden brown and the cheese is fully melted. Work in batches if needed and keep finished sandwiches warm in a low oven.
  6. 6Back to the soup — take it off the heat and toss in the fresh basil. Use an immersion blender to blend it smooth right in the pot. No immersion blender? Carefully transfer in batches to a regular blender, leave the lid slightly vented, and blend until silky. Return to the pot.
  7. 7Stir in the 2 tablespoons of butter if using — it makes the soup glossy and extra rich. Taste and adjust salt, pepper, and sugar as needed.
  8. 8Ladle the soup into bowls, garnish with a fresh basil leaf or a little drizzle of olive oil, and serve immediately alongside your grilled cheese for dunking. That's the whole move.
💡 Improv Tip

If your canned tomatoes taste a little sharp or acidic, that pinch of sugar is your best friend — add it a little at a time until the bitterness backs off. And don't sleep on the smoked paprika in this soup, it adds this subtle warmth that people can't quite put their finger on but everyone notices. Leftovers? The soup gets even better the next day.
