Homemade Chicken Vegetable Soup That Actually Tastes Like Someone's Abuela Made It

Published May 30, 2026 · Improv Oven

This homemade chicken vegetable soup recipe is the kind of thing that makes your whole apartment smell incredible and costs you maybe six dollars to pull off. We're talking real, soul-warming soup built from whatever vegetables are hanging out in your fridge looking sad — carrots, celery, zucchini, potatoes, doesn't matter. It's the cure for a long week, a cold, or just that feeling like you need something real on the table tonight.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
35 mins
Total
45 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs bone-in chicken thighs or drumsticks (cheapest cut, most flavor)
  • 3 medium carrots, peeled and sliced into coins
  • 3 stalks celery, sliced
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 medium potatoes, cubed (Yukon gold or russet, whatever you have)
  • 1 cup green beans, trimmed and cut into pieces (fresh or frozen both work)
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 6 cups chicken broth or water with 2 bouillon cubes
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon sazon or adobo seasoning (trust me on this one)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh cilantro for serving (optional but highly recommended)

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Season your chicken pieces generously with salt, pepper, and the adobo or sazon. Sear the chicken for about 3 minutes per side until you get some golden color on it — we're building flavor right here at the bottom of that pot, don't skip this.
  2. 2Remove the chicken and set it aside on a plate. In that same pot with all those good browned bits, add the diced onion and celery. Cook for about 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the onion starts to turn translucent. Add the garlic, cumin, and oregano and cook for one more minute until it smells amazing.
  3. 3Pour in the chicken broth and use your spoon to scrape up any browned bits stuck to the bottom of the pot — that's pure flavor, don't waste it. Add the can of diced tomatoes, then nestle the seared chicken back in. Bring everything to a boil, then reduce to a steady simmer.
  4. 4After about 15 minutes of simmering, add the carrots, potatoes, and green beans. Let the soup cook for another 15-20 minutes until the potatoes are fork-tender and the chicken is cooked all the way through with no pink remaining.
  5. 5Carefully pull the chicken pieces out of the pot and place them on a cutting board. Use two forks to shred the meat right off the bone — it should fall apart easily at this point. Discard the bones and add all that shredded chicken back into the pot.
  6. 6Give the soup a taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper. Let it simmer for 5 more minutes so everything gets friendly with each other. Ladle into bowls and top with fresh cilantro if you have it. Serve with crusty bread, white rice, or just as-is — this soup carries itself.
💡 Improv Tip

Bone-in chicken is the secret weapon here — it makes the broth naturally richer and more flavorful than boneless chicken ever could. If you only have boneless chicken breasts or thighs, no stress, it'll still be good, but add an extra bouillon cube to make up for it. And listen, this soup is basically designed for refrigerator cleanout — zucchini, corn, spinach, chayote, bell pepper — if it's about to go bad in your fridge, it probably belongs in this pot.
