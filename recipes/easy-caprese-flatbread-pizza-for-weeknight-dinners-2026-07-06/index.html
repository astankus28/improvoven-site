Easy Caprese Flatbread Pizza for Weeknight Dinners

Published July 6, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy caprese flatbread pizza recipe is your new weeknight hero — on the table in under 30 minutes with ingredients you probably already have hanging around. We're talking crispy flatbread loaded with melty mozzarella, juicy tomatoes, and fresh basil drizzled with a little olive oil. Simple, gorgeous, and honestly way better than it has any right to be for how little effort you put in.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
12 mins
Total
22 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 large flatbreads or naan breads
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced or grated
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional but do it)
  • 8 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced into rounds
  • 2 medium ripe tomatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved (if you have them)
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic glaze, for drizzling

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with foil or parchment. This is your secret to a crispy bottom — don't skip preheating the pan if you want that crunch.
  2. 2Mix 2 tablespoons of the olive oil with the minced garlic, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes in a small bowl. This little garlicky oil is doing all the heavy lifting flavor-wise, so taste it and make sure it's seasoned right.
  3. 3Lay your flatbreads on the baking sheet and brush them generously with that garlic oil all the way to the edges. Don't be shy — every corner deserves love.
  4. 4Layer the sliced mozzarella across the flatbreads first, then shingle the tomato slices right on top. If you've got cherry tomatoes too, scatter those around. The more tomato the better, honestly.
  5. 5Drizzle the remaining tablespoon of olive oil over the top and slide the pan into the oven. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbling and the edges of the flatbread are golden and crisp.
  6. 6Pull it out and immediately scatter fresh basil leaves all over — the heat will make them fragrant and slightly wilted in the best way. Finish with a generous drizzle of balsamic glaze, slice it up, and get it to the table before someone eats it straight off the pan.
💡 Improv Tip

The number one move for a crispy flatbread pizza is to not overload it with wet toppings before it bakes — pat your tomato slices with a paper towel to pull out some of that extra moisture. Takes five seconds and makes a real difference between soggy and spectacular.
