Easy Homemade Vanilla Pudding Recipe (Creamy, Stovetop, No Box Required)

Published September 9, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy homemade vanilla pudding recipe is the kind of thing that makes you wonder why you ever bought the box mix — it comes together in about 15 minutes with ingredients you almost certainly already have. Silky, rich, and deeply vanilla-flavored, it's pure comfort in a bowl. In Miami, we love it layered with crushed Maria cookies and a little dulce de leche drizzle, but honestly it's perfect on its own too.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
20 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk (or 2% — whole is creamier)
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 3 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1/4 teaspoon fine salt
  • 2 large egg yolks
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
  • Optional: whipped cream, crushed Maria cookies, or a drizzle of dulce de leche for serving

Instructions

  1. 1In a medium saucepan off the heat, whisk together the sugar, cornstarch, and salt until evenly combined. This dry-whisk step prevents cornstarch lumps later — don't skip it.
  2. 2Add the egg yolks and about 1/4 cup of the milk to the dry mixture and whisk into a smooth paste. This is called tempering your base and it keeps the eggs from scrambling when heat hits.
  3. 3Pour in the remaining milk gradually, whisking as you go, until everything is smooth and fully combined.
  4. 4Set the saucepan over medium heat and cook, stirring constantly with a heatproof spatula or whisk, making sure to scrape the bottom and corners of the pan. Don't walk away — this is a 10-minute commitment.
  5. 5After about 8 to 10 minutes, the mixture will thicken noticeably and begin to bubble. Once you see a couple of big, slow bubbles pop at the surface, cook for one more full minute while still stirring, then remove from heat. Those bubbles mean the cornstarch is fully activated.
  6. 6Add the butter and vanilla extract and stir until the butter melts completely and the pudding is glossy and smooth. Taste it — yes, right now. It's amazing.
  7. 7Pour the pudding into 4 individual serving cups or one large bowl. If you don't want a skin to form on top, press a piece of plastic wrap directly against the surface before refrigerating.
  8. 8Chill for at least 1 hour until set and cold, or eat it warm straight from the pot — no judgment here. Serve with whipped cream, crushed Maria cookies, or a little dulce de leche if you want to give it that Miami magic.
💡 Improv Tip

The number one mistake people make with stovetop pudding is pulling it off the heat too early because it looks thick enough. You need to see actual bubbles and cook one full minute past that point — otherwise the cornstarch won't fully set and you'll end up with pudding soup. Stay at medium heat, keep stirring, and trust the process. Also: real vanilla extract matters here. This recipe is simple enough that every ingredient is front and center.
