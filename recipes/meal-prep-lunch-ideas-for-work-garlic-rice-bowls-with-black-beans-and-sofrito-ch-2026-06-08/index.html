Meal Prep Lunch Ideas for Work: Garlic Rice Bowls with Black Beans and Sofrito Chicken

Published June 8, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been stuck searching for meal prep lunch ideas for work, this garlic rice bowl is about to become your Monday through Friday lifesaver. We're talking juicy sofrito-marinated chicken thighs, fluffy rice, and black beans — all prepped in one Sunday session so your whole week is handled. It's the kind of lunch that makes your coworkers peek over and ask what smells so good.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 3 tablespoons sofrito (jarred is totally fine)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon sazón seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 cups long grain white rice
  • 4 cups chicken broth (or water with bouillon)
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil (for beans)
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin (for beans)
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional toppings: lime wedges, sliced avocado, hot sauce, fresh cilantro

Instructions

  1. 1In a bowl or zip-lock bag, combine the chicken thighs with sofrito, olive oil, garlic powder, cumin, sazón, salt, and pepper. Mix it all together so every piece is coated. Let it marinate for at least 10 minutes while you get everything else going — or do this the night before if you really want to level up the flavor.
  2. 2Cook your rice: add the 2 cups of rice and 4 cups of chicken broth to a medium saucepan. Bring it to a boil, then drop the heat to low, cover, and let it simmer for 18 minutes. Take it off the heat and let it rest covered for 5 minutes. Fluff it with a fork — that steam time is the secret to non-mushy rice.
  3. 3While the rice is doing its thing, heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the marinated chicken thighs and cook for 5 to 6 minutes per side until they're golden brown and cooked through (internal temp should hit 165°F). Pull them off the heat and let them rest for 5 minutes before slicing — don't skip the rest or all those juices run right out.
  4. 4In a small saucepan, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and let it get fragrant, about 1 minute — just don't let it burn. Add the black beans, cumin, and a pinch of salt. Stir everything together and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the beans are warmed through and slightly saucy. Taste and adjust salt if needed.
  5. 5Now assemble your meal prep containers. Divide the rice evenly among 4 containers, then add a portion of sliced chicken and a scoop of the garlic black beans to each one. Let everything cool completely before putting the lids on — sealing in steam leads to soggy sad rice, and nobody wants that. Store in the fridge for up to 4 days.
  6. 6When lunchtime hits, reheat in the microwave for about 2 minutes, add a squeeze of lime, some hot sauce if you're about that life, and whatever toppings you packed. You just made yourself the best lunch in the office — quietly, on a Sunday, with one pan and less than $15.
💡 Improv Tip

Buy chicken thighs over chicken breasts every single time for meal prep — they stay juicy when you reheat them and they're usually a dollar or two cheaper per pound. Breasts dry out in the microwave and nobody is checking for that at noon on a Wednesday.
