Easy Cookout Side Dishes: Smoky Black Bean and Corn Salad

Published July 3, 2026 · Improv Oven

When you need easy cookout side dishes that actually impress, this smoky black bean and corn salad is your secret weapon — it comes together in 15 minutes and gets better as it sits. We're talking pantry staples doing big things: canned black beans, frozen or canned corn, a little lime, and that cumin you forgot you had. Miami cookout approved, no grill required.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
15 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (15 oz) whole kernel corn, drained (or 1.5 cups thawed frozen corn)
  • 1/2 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lime juice (about 2 limes)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, roughly chopped (optional but highly recommended)
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced (optional, for heat)

Instructions

  1. 1Drain and rinse your black beans really well under cold water — this removes that starchy liquid and keeps the salad from getting murky. Drain your corn the same way and set both aside in a large mixing bowl.
  2. 2Dice your red onion as fine as you can get it. If raw onion feels aggressive to you, soak the diced pieces in cold water for 5 minutes, then drain — it takes the sharp edge off while keeping that crunch. Add to the bowl along with the diced red bell pepper.
  3. 3In a small bowl or jar, whisk together the olive oil, lime juice, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper until combined. Taste it — it should be bright, a little smoky, and punchy. That's your dressing doing its job.
  4. 4Pour the dressing over the bean and corn mixture and toss everything together until well coated. Fold in the fresh cilantro and jalapeño if using.
  5. 5Here's the move that separates good from great — let this salad sit for at least 20 minutes before serving. The beans soak up that dressing and everything gets more cohesive and delicious. You can make it up to 24 hours ahead and keep it covered in the fridge. Give it a stir and a fresh squeeze of lime before serving.
💡 Improv Tip

This salad is basically a blank canvas — and that's the whole point. Got a ripe avocado? Cube it and fold it in right before serving. A lonely tomato on the counter? Chop it up and throw it in. No red bell pepper? Green works, or skip it entirely. The cumin and smoked paprika are what give this its depth, so don't skip those — but everything else is fair game for the improv treatment.
