Simple Sunday Meal Prep for Beginners: Sofrito Rice Bowls with Black Beans and Roasted Veggies

Published July 6, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been looking for simple Sunday meal prep recipes for beginners, this is your starting point — no fancy equipment, no stress, just one pot of sofrito rice, a can of black beans, and whatever vegetables are hiding in your fridge. We're talking real food that actually tastes good all week, not sad sad containers you dread opening on Wednesday. This is how we do it in Miami — flavor first, budget always.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
35 mins
Total
50 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 4 cups water or chicken broth (broth if you have it — trust me)
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 1 small white or yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon sazon seasoning (or a pinch of turmeric plus paprika if you don't have it)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 cups broccoli florets (or any vegetable you have — zucchini, carrots, sweet potato, all work)
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Fresh cilantro for topping (optional but highly recommended)
  • Hot sauce for serving (optional but spiritually required)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 400°F. Toss your broccoli florets (or whatever veggie you grabbed) with 1 tablespoon of oil, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Spread them out on a baking sheet in a single layer — don't pile them up or they'll steam instead of roast. Pop them in the oven for 20 to 25 minutes until the edges get a little golden and crispy.
  2. 2While the veggies roast, heat the remaining tablespoon of oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the diced onion and green bell pepper and cook for about 4 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they soften and start smelling amazing. Add the minced garlic and cook for one more minute — just until fragrant, don't let it burn.
  3. 3Add the rice to the pot and stir it around with the vegetables for about 1 minute. This little toast step adds so much flavor and it takes zero extra effort. Pour in your water or broth, add the cumin, sazon, and a good pinch of salt. Stir everything together, bring it to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover the pot tightly, and let it cook for 18 minutes undisturbed. No peeking — let the steam do its thing.
  4. 4While the rice is cooking, pour your drained black beans into a small saucepan over low heat. Season them with a pinch of cumin, salt, pepper, and a tiny splash of the lime juice. Stir and warm them through for about 5 minutes. You're not doing anything wild here — just waking them up with a little seasoning so they don't taste like they came straight from a can.
  5. 5Once the rice timer goes off, remove it from heat and let it sit covered for 5 more minutes. Then fluff it with a fork and squeeze the rest of the lime juice over the top. Your veggies should be done around the same time — pull them out and let everything cool for 10 to 15 minutes before packing.
  6. 6Now build your bowls! Divide the sofrito rice into 4 meal prep containers. Add a scoop of black beans and a portion of roasted veggies to each one. Top with fresh cilantro if you have it. Store in the fridge for up to 4 days. When you reheat, add a tiny splash of water before microwaving so the rice steams back to life instead of drying out.
💡 Improv Tip

The real secret to not getting bored with meal prep is changing your toppings each day — one day add a fried egg on top, another day slice up some avocado, the next day drizzle on some hot sauce or a spoonful of sour cream. Same base, totally different vibe. That's the whole Improv Oven philosophy right there — you cooked once, you eat four different ways.
