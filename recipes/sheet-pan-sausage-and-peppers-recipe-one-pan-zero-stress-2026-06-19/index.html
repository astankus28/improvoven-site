Sheet Pan Sausage and Peppers Recipe (One Pan, Zero Stress)

Published June 19, 2026 · Improv Oven

This sheet pan sausage and peppers recipe is the kind of dinner that makes you look like you had your whole life together — even on a Tuesday when you definitely did not. Everything roasts together on one pan, the edges get all caramelized and beautiful, and cleanup is basically nothing. It's the meal your refrigerator has been begging you to make.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
30 mins
Total
40 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs smoked sausage or Italian sausage, sliced into 1-inch rounds
  • 3 bell peppers (any color mix), sliced into strips
  • 1 large yellow onion, sliced into half-moons
  • 4 cloves garlic, roughly chopped
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Red pepper flakes to taste (optional but recommended)
  • Fresh parsley or cilantro for garnish

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 425°F and line a large sheet pan with foil or parchment paper. High heat is the secret here — that's what gives you the caramelized, slightly charred edges that make this dish so good.
  2. 2Slice your sausage into rounds, your peppers into strips, and your onion into half-moons. Try to keep everything roughly the same thickness so it cooks evenly. No need to be precise — this is Improv Oven, not culinary school.
  3. 3Toss the sausage, peppers, onion, and garlic directly on the sheet pan. Drizzle the olive oil over everything, then sprinkle on the smoked paprika, oregano, cumin, onion powder, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes if you're using them.
  4. 4Use your hands or a big spoon to toss everything together right on the pan until well coated. Spread it out into a single layer as much as possible — crowding the pan will steam everything instead of roasting it, and we want those crispy caramelized edges.
  5. 5Roast in the oven for 25 to 30 minutes, flipping everything once at the halfway point around 15 minutes. You're looking for the peppers to be tender with slightly charred tips and the sausage to have some good color on the cut sides.
  6. 6Pull the pan out, hit it with a handful of fresh parsley or cilantro, and serve straight from the pan over rice, inside hoagie rolls, with crusty bread, or honestly just as-is. Dinner is done and the whole neighborhood probably smells incredible.
💡 Improv Tip

The sausage you use changes everything here — smoked sausage gives you that deep savory flavor with zero extra work, but Italian sausage brings a little more richness. At most Latin grocery stores you can find a seasoned chorizo or longaniza that will take this whole pan to another level for the same price or less. Swap in whatever you've got and don't stress it.
