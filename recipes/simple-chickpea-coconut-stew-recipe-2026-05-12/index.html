Simple Chickpea Coconut Stew Recipe

Published May 12, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple chickpea coconut stew recipe is the kind of thing you throw together on a Tuesday when life is moving fast and your wallet is feeling thin — and somehow it tastes like you actually tried. One pot, pantry staples, and about 30 minutes stand between you and something creamy, fragrant, and genuinely satisfying. We're talking Caribbean-inspired comfort food that'll have everyone asking what restaurant you ordered from.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated (or 1 teaspoon ground ginger)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (adjust to your heat preference)
  • 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup low-sodium vegetable broth or water
  • 2 cups fresh baby spinach (or frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry)
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Fresh cilantro for serving
  • Cooked white rice or crusty bread for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the oil in a large deep skillet or pot over medium heat. Once it shimmers, add the diced onion and cook for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and just starting to turn golden at the edges.
  2. 2Add the garlic and ginger to the pan and stir constantly for about 60 seconds — you want them fragrant but not burned. Your kitchen should smell amazing right about now.
  3. 3Add the cumin, coriander, smoked paprika, turmeric, cayenne, salt, and black pepper directly to the onion mixture. Stir everything together and let those spices toast for about 30 seconds. This step wakes them up and deepens the whole flavor of the stew.
  4. 4Pour in the diced tomatoes with their juices and stir to combine, scraping up any bits stuck to the bottom of the pan. Let this cook for 2 minutes so the tomatoes start to break down a little.
  5. 5Add the drained chickpeas, coconut milk, and vegetable broth. Stir everything together and bring the stew to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat to medium-low and let it simmer uncovered for 12 to 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the stew thickens and the flavors come together beautifully.
  6. 6Stir in the spinach and cook for another 1 to 2 minutes until it wilts into the stew. Squeeze in the fresh lime juice and give everything a final taste — adjust salt or cayenne if needed.
  7. 7Serve hot over white rice or with crusty bread for scooping. Top with fresh cilantro and an extra squeeze of lime if you're feeling fancy. Leftovers taste even better the next day.
💡 Improv Tip

If your coconut milk has separated in the can, don't panic — that's totally normal. Just scrape it all in and stir. The thick cream on top is actually where all the richness lives, so you want every drop of it in this stew. And if you want more body, mash about a quarter of the chickpeas with the back of your spoon right in the pot before adding the spinach — it thickens things up without any extra ingredients.
