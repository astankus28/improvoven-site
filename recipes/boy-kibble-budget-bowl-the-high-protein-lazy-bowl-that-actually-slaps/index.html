Boy Kibble Budget Bowl (The High-Protein Lazy Bowl That Actually Slaps)

Published July 9, 2026 · Improv Oven

The boy kibble budget bowl is exactly what it sounds like — a no-fuss, high-protein, throw-it-together bowl that keeps you full for hours without draining your wallet. Think seasoned ground beef or turkey, canned chickpeas, roasted whatever-vegetable-you-have, and a punchy garlic-soy drizzle over rice or whatever grain you've got lurking in the pantry. It's not fancy, it doesn't pretend to be, and that's exactly why it works.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef or ground turkey (80/20 ground beef works great)
  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups cooked white or brown rice (day-old rice is perfect)
  • 2 cups broccoli florets, fresh or frozen
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or neutral oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional but recommended)
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil (optional, adds a nice depth)
  • Salt to taste
  • Sliced green onions and a fried egg, for topping (optional but encouraged)

Instructions

  1. 1Get your broccoli going first. If using fresh, toss the florets in a little oil, season with salt and pepper, and roast at 425°F for 15 minutes until the edges are a little crispy and charred. If using frozen, just microwave according to package directions or toss straight into a dry hot skillet for a few minutes to get some color on them.
  2. 2While the broccoli does its thing, heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add your olive oil and let it get hot, then add the ground meat. Break it up with a wooden spoon and let it cook undisturbed for about 2 minutes to get some good browning happening before you start stirring.
  3. 3Season the meat with smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper. Keep cooking and stirring until the meat is browned all the way through, about 5-6 minutes total. Drain off any excess fat if needed, but leave a little — that's flavor.
  4. 4Push the meat to one side of the skillet and add the minced garlic to the cleared space. Let it sizzle for about 30 seconds until fragrant, then stir everything together.
  5. 5Add the drained chickpeas to the skillet and stir to combine with the meat. Pour in the soy sauce and Worcestershire sauce. Stir everything together and let it cook for 2-3 minutes so the chickpeas absorb the sauce and everything gets a little caramelized and glossy. Taste and adjust salt — soy sauce is salty, so go easy until you've tasted it.
  6. 6If you're using sesame oil, drizzle it in now and stir. Kill the heat.
  7. 7Build your bowls: start with a scoop of rice, pile on the meat and chickpea mixture, add your broccoli alongside it. If you're doing a fried egg on top, now's the time — fry it in a small pan with a little oil until the whites are set and the yolk is still runny. Lay it right on top of the bowl.
  8. 8Finish with red pepper flakes, sliced green onions, and an extra tiny drizzle of soy sauce if you want. Eat immediately and feel genuinely good about your life choices.
💡 Improv Tip

The chickpeas are the secret weapon here — don't skip them. They soak up the soy-garlic sauce and bulk up the bowl with fiber and protein for practically nothing. If you want to stretch this even further, swap half the ground meat for a second can of chickpeas. You'll honestly barely notice and the cost per serving drops to almost nothing.
