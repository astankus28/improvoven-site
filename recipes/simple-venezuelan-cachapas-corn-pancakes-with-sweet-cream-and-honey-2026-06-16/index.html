Simple Venezuelan Cachapas Corn Pancakes with Sweet Cream and Honey

Published June 16, 2026 · Improv Oven

Simple Venezuelan cachapas corn pancakes are already a little magical on their own, but turn them into dessert and you've got something your whole family will be asking for every weekend. These thick, golden, slightly sweet corn cakes come together with pantry staples you probably already have sitting around — and trust me, once you taste that crispy edge with a drizzle of honey, you're gonna forget all about regular pancakes. This is that Miami kitchen energy where humble ingredients become something really special.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups frozen or canned corn kernels, drained well
  • 1/2 cup masarepa (pre-cooked white cornmeal, like P.A.N. brand)
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons whole milk or any milk you have
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided (for cooking)
  • 1/2 cup cream cheese or queso blanco, softened (for serving)
  • 2 tablespoons powdered sugar (for the sweet cream)
  • 3 tablespoons honey or dulce de leche (for drizzling)
  • Optional: a pinch of cinnamon for topping

Instructions

  1. 1Add your drained corn kernels to a blender or food processor and pulse about 5 to 6 times — you want a chunky, thick paste, not a smooth puree. Some whole kernels left behind is totally fine and actually gives you that authentic cachapa texture.
  2. 2Transfer the corn paste to a mixing bowl and add the masarepa, sugar, salt, milk, and egg. Stir everything together until it forms a thick batter that holds its shape. If it feels too loose, add a tiny bit more masarepa, one teaspoon at a time. Let it rest for 5 minutes so the masarepa can absorb the moisture.
  3. 3While the batter rests, make your sweet cream topping: mix the softened cream cheese with the powdered sugar in a small bowl until smooth and fluffy. Set aside at room temperature so it stays soft and spreadable.
  4. 4Heat a large non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat and add half a tablespoon of butter. Once it melts and starts to bubble, scoop about 1/3 cup of batter per cachapa into the pan. Gently flatten each one with the back of a spoon into a round about 4 to 5 inches wide.
  5. 5Cook for 4 to 5 minutes on the first side without touching them — you'll know they're ready to flip when the edges look set and the bottom is deep golden brown. Flip carefully and cook another 3 to 4 minutes on the second side. Add more butter between batches as needed.
  6. 6To serve your dessert cachapas, spread a generous spoonful of the sweet cream cheese mixture over each warm cachapa, then drizzle with honey or dulce de leche. Finish with a tiny pinch of cinnamon if you're feeling fancy. Serve immediately while they're still warm — that's when they're at their absolute best.
💡 Improv Tip

The biggest mistake people make with cachapas is rushing the flip — if you try to turn them too early they'll fall apart on you. Be patient, let that bottom get truly golden and set before you even think about flipping. Also, canned corn works great here but make sure you drain it really well and even pat it dry with a paper towel — extra water in the batter is what makes them fall apart or cook unevenly.
