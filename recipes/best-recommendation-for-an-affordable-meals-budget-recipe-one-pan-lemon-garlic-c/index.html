Best Recommendation for an Affordable Meals Budget Recipe: One-Pan Lemon Garlic Chicken Thighs with White Beans

Published September 28, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're looking for a recommendation for affordable meals on a budget, this one-pan lemon garlic chicken thighs recipe is the answer — juicy, golden chicken nestled into creamy white beans with just a handful of pantry staples. It tastes like something you'd order at a cozy Italian trattoria, but it comes together in about 35 minutes and costs next to nothing. This is the kind of weeknight dinner that makes you feel like you actually have it together.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 2 lbs)
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 6 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1 cup chicken broth (or water with a bouillon cube)
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) white beans, drained and rinsed
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest
  • 1 tablespoon fresh parsley or a pinch of dried Italian seasoning
  • Optional: 2 cups baby spinach or chopped kale, stirred in at the end

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your chicken thighs completely dry with paper towels — this is the secret to that gorgeous golden crust. Season them on both sides with salt, pepper, and smoked paprika.
  2. 2Heat the olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet or heavy pan over medium-high heat. Once it shimmers, lay the chicken thighs skin-side down. Don't touch them for 6 to 7 minutes — just let them do their thing until the skin is deeply golden and pulls away from the pan easily. Flip and cook the other side for 3 minutes, then transfer the chicken to a plate.
  3. 3Pour off all but about 1 tablespoon of the fat in the pan. Reduce heat to medium and add the smashed garlic and red pepper flakes. Stir for about 60 seconds until fragrant — you want the garlic golden, not brown.
  4. 4Pour in the chicken broth and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Those bits are flavor — get every one of them.
  5. 5Add the drained white beans, lemon juice, and lemon zest. Stir to combine, then nestle the chicken thighs back in skin-side up, resting on top of the beans so the skin stays crispy.
  6. 6Reduce heat to medium-low, cover loosely, and let everything simmer together for 12 to 15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through (internal temp of 165°F) and the beans have soaked up all that lemony, garlicky goodness.
  7. 7If you're adding greens, stir them into the beans now and let them wilt for 2 minutes. Taste the beans and adjust salt and lemon as needed. Finish with fresh parsley or a pinch of dried Italian seasoning and serve straight from the pan.
💡 Improv Tip

Bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs are almost always the cheapest cut at the store AND they're the most forgiving — they stay juicy even if you cook them a few minutes too long. If you only have boneless thighs, reduce the final simmer time to about 8 minutes. And that rendered chicken fat you poured off? Save it in a little jar in the fridge — it's liquid gold for roasting vegetables.
