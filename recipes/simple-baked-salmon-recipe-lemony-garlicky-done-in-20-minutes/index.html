Simple Baked Salmon Recipe (Lemony, Garlicky, Done in 20 Minutes)

Published August 1, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple baked salmon recipe is the kind of weeknight dinner that makes you feel like you have your life together — even when you absolutely don't. A handful of pantry staples, one sheet pan, and about 20 minutes is all it takes to get flaky, flavorful salmon on the table. It's genuinely that easy, and genuinely that good.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each, skin-on or skinless)
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lemon, half juiced and half sliced into thin rounds
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional, but do it)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, roughly chopped (for serving)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with foil or parchment paper. This is non-negotiable for easy cleanup — don't skip it.
  2. 2In a small bowl, mix together the olive oil, minced garlic, lemon juice, oregano, smoked paprika, onion powder, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes. Give it a stir and take a sniff — it should smell amazing already.
  3. 3Pat the salmon fillets dry with a paper towel. This helps the glaze stick and gives you a better surface on the fish. Place them on your prepared baking sheet with a little space between each one.
  4. 4Spoon the garlic-olive oil mixture generously over each fillet, spreading it across the top and sides. Lay a slice or two of lemon directly on top of each piece — they'll get slightly caramelized in the oven and look beautiful.
  5. 5Bake at 400°F for 12 to 15 minutes, depending on the thickness of your fillets. The salmon is done when it flakes easily with a fork and the thickest part has turned from translucent to opaque. If you have a thermometer, you're looking for 125–130°F for medium or 145°F for fully cooked.
  6. 6Pull the salmon out of the oven and let it rest for 2 minutes — it keeps cooking just a little even off the heat. Scatter fresh parsley over the top and serve immediately with whatever you've got going: roasted veggies, a simple salad, rice, crusty bread, you name it.
💡 Improv Tip

Frozen salmon works great here — just thaw it overnight in the fridge or do a quick cold-water thaw in a sealed bag for 30 minutes. Once it's thawed and patted dry, you'd never know the difference. Also, don't overbake it. The number one salmon mistake is leaving it in too long. Check it at 12 minutes and go from there — slightly translucent in the very center is perfectly fine and keeps it moist.
