Easy Potato Salad Recipe for BBQ Season (Creamy, Tangy, Perfect)

Published May 22, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy potato salad recipe is the one you bring to every BBQ and people ask you to make again before the party's even over — that's how good it is. We're talking creamy, a little tangy, with a kick of mustard and some fresh herbs that make it feel homemade in the best way. Classic enough for the crowd, but with just enough flavor to make people wonder what your secret is.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs Yukon Gold or russet potatoes, cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 1 tablespoon salt (for boiling water)
  • 3/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons yellow mustard
  • 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika (plus more for garnish)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 3 hard-boiled eggs, roughly chopped
  • 3 stalks celery, diced small
  • 1/2 small red onion, diced small
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped
  • Optional: 2 tablespoons diced dill pickles or pickle juice for extra tang

Instructions

  1. 1Place your potato chunks in a large pot and cover with cold water by about an inch. Add a generous tablespoon of salt — this is your only chance to season the potatoes from the inside out, so don't skip it. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce to a steady simmer and cook for 12 to 15 minutes until the potatoes are fork-tender but not falling apart. You want them holding their shape, not turning into mashed potatoes.
  2. 2While the potatoes cook, make your dressing. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, yellow mustard, apple cider vinegar, sugar, garlic powder, smoked paprika, a good pinch of salt, and several cracks of black pepper. Taste it — it should be creamy, tangy, and just a little bold. That's exactly what you want because the potatoes will mellow it out.
  3. 3Drain the cooked potatoes and spread them out on a sheet pan or cutting board for about 5 minutes. You want them to stop steaming and release some of that surface moisture before dressing them. Hot, wet potatoes will turn your salad soupy and nobody's coming back for seconds of that.
  4. 4Add the warm potatoes to the bowl with your dressing. Toss gently to coat — warm potatoes absorb dressing better than cold ones, and that's the move right there. Then fold in the chopped hard-boiled eggs, diced celery, red onion, and parsley or cilantro. If you're using pickles or pickle juice, add them now.
  5. 5Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving — 2 to 3 hours is even better. This is non-negotiable. The salad needs that time to chill and come together. Right before serving, taste for seasoning, add a little more salt or a splash of vinegar if needed, and finish with a dusting of smoked paprika on top for that BBQ side dish energy.
💡 Improv Tip

The single biggest upgrade you can make? Season the dressing a little more aggressively than feels comfortable before the potatoes go in. Once everything chills in the fridge, flavors flatten out — what tastes bold at room temperature becomes just right when cold. Also, if your potato salad ever comes out dry the next day, stir in a spoonful of mayo and a tiny splash of vinegar and it'll come right back to life.
