Budget Five Ingredient Pasta That Actually Slaps

Published June 29, 2026 · Improv Oven

This budget five ingredient pasta recipe is the kind of thing your tía would throw together on a Tuesday and somehow make it taste like she cooked all day. We're talking pantry staples, one pot, and a flavor payoff that makes zero sense for how little effort you put in. Miami kitchens run on this energy — stretch what you've got and make it delicious.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spaghetti or any long pasta you have
  • 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 cup pasta water, reserved before draining

Instructions

  1. 1Bring a big pot of heavily salted water to a boil — it should taste like the ocean, seriously. Drop in your pasta and cook it one minute shy of the package directions. Before you drain it, scoop out at least half a cup of that starchy pasta water and set it aside. That liquid is liquid gold and we are not throwing it away.
  2. 2While the pasta cooks, pour your olive oil into a wide skillet over medium-low heat. Add the sliced garlic and let it slowly turn golden — we're talking a gentle sizzle, not a scorch. This takes about 4 to 5 minutes. Stay close and keep the heat low. The moment it smells nutty and looks pale golden, toss in the red pepper flakes and let them bloom in the oil for about 30 seconds.
  3. 3Add the drained pasta directly into the skillet and toss it hard to coat every strand in that garlicky oil. Now splash in your reserved pasta water a little at a time, tossing as you go. The starch in that water will create a silky, glossy sauce that clings to the pasta like it was always meant to be there. Use as much or as little as you need to get it saucy but not soupy.
  4. 4Taste it. Adjust the salt. Plate it up and eat it immediately — this one doesn't wait for anybody. If you have parsley, a squeeze of lemon, or some parmesan hanging around, go ahead and use them, but the recipe is complete and beautiful without them.
💡 Improv Tip

The real secret here is not rushing the garlic. I know you're hungry, I know it smells amazing, but burned garlic will make the whole dish bitter and there's no coming back from that. Keep your flame on medium-low, trust the process, and you'll get that sweet, mellow, almost caramelized flavor that makes aglio e olio one of the most iconic pasta dishes in the world — for five ingredients and under six bucks.
