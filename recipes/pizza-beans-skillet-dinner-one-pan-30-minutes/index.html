Pizza Beans Skillet Dinner (One Pan, 30 Minutes)

Published July 10, 2026 · Improv Oven

This pizza beans skillet dinner is exactly what it sounds like — all the flavors of your favorite pizza, built around a pot of hearty white beans, and done in one pan in about half an hour. It's saucy, cheesy, deeply savory, and the kind of thing you'll make on a Tuesday when the fridge looks bleak and delivery feels too expensive. Think of it as lazy lasagna's even lazier cousin, and honestly? It might be better.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
22 mins
Total
32 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 oz each) cannellini or white kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 3 oz pepperoni, sliced (about 30 slices) — or use Italian sausage, chopped olives, or skip for vegetarian
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1/2 teaspoon fennel seeds (optional but magic)
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 cup shredded low-moisture mozzarella
  • 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan
  • Fresh basil or parsley for serving (optional)
  • Crusty bread or garlic toast for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and just starting to turn golden at the edges.
  2. 2Add the garlic, red pepper flakes, and fennel seeds (if using). Stir and cook for about 60 seconds until fragrant — you'll smell when it's ready.
  3. 3Push the onion and garlic to the side and add the pepperoni slices in a single layer. Let them sizzle for 1–2 minutes so they crisp up slightly and release some of their oil into the pan. This is where a lot of the pizza flavor comes from.
  4. 4Add the crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, and garlic powder. Stir everything together and let it bubble for 2–3 minutes so the sauce picks up all those fond bits from the bottom of the pan.
  5. 5Add the drained beans and stir to coat them fully in the sauce. Season with salt and pepper. Reduce heat to medium-low and let everything simmer together for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened and the beans have absorbed all that pizza flavor.
  6. 6If your skillet is oven-safe, preheat your broiler to high. If not, just leave it on the stovetop for the next step. Scatter the shredded mozzarella evenly over the top of the skillet, then sprinkle the Parmesan over that.
  7. 7For the broiler method: slide the skillet under the broiler for 2–3 minutes, watching closely, until the cheese is melted, bubbling, and starting to get golden brown spots. For the stovetop method: cover the skillet with a lid for 3–4 minutes on low heat until the cheese is fully melted.
  8. 8Pull it out, let it sit for a minute, then top with fresh basil or parsley if you have it. Serve straight from the skillet with crusty bread for scooping. Nobody is walking away hungry.
💡 Improv Tip

Don't skip the fennel seeds — they're the secret ingredient that makes this taste like real Italian sausage pizza even when you're using pepperoni. No fennel seeds? Add a pinch of fennel-heavy Italian seasoning blend instead. And if your beans feel too thick after simmering, splash in a quarter cup of water or chicken broth to loosen things up before you add the cheese.
