Easy Grilled Chicken Recipe for BBQ Memorial Day Cookouts

Published May 19, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you need an easy grilled chicken recipe for your BBQ Memorial Day spread, this is the one to bookmark — juicy, charred, and packed with that smoky-citrus flavor that screams summer in Miami. We're keeping it simple with a pantry-friendly marinade that does all the heavy lifting while you prep the rest of the cookout. No fancy ingredients, no stress — just the kind of chicken that disappears off the platter in ten minutes flat.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (or drumsticks — whatever's on sale)
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  • 3 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon oregano
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh cilantro for garnish (optional but highly encouraged)

Instructions

  1. 1Make your marinade: In a bowl or zip-lock bag, mix together the olive oil, soy sauce, apple cider vinegar, brown sugar, minced garlic, smoked paprika, cumin, onion powder, black pepper, oregano, and lime juice. Give it a good stir until the sugar dissolves and everything comes together into a dark, fragrant marinade.
  2. 2Add your chicken to the marinade and toss to coat every piece well. If you have time, let it marinate in the fridge for at least 30 minutes — or up to 24 hours if you're planning ahead. Even 15 minutes will give you great flavor, so don't stress if you're short on time.
  3. 3When you're ready to grill, preheat your grill to medium-high heat (around 400°F). Clean and oil the grates so your chicken doesn't stick.
  4. 4Pull the chicken out of the marinade and let any excess drip off. Place the pieces skin-side down on the grill. Cook for about 8 to 10 minutes on the first side without moving them — you want those beautiful grill marks and a good sear.
  5. 5Flip the chicken and cook another 8 to 10 minutes on the second side. Bone-in thighs and drumsticks are done when they reach an internal temperature of 165°F. If you don't have a thermometer, cut into the thickest part — the juices should run clear with no pink.
  6. 6In the last 2 minutes of cooking, brush a little extra marinade (or your favorite BBQ sauce) over the chicken and let it caramelize on the grill. Watch it closely — the brown sugar can burn fast.
  7. 7Pull the chicken off the grill and let it rest for 5 minutes before serving. This keeps all those juices locked in. Top with fresh cilantro, squeeze a little extra lime over the top, and bring it to the table.
💡 Improv Tip

Listen — the secret weapon here is the soy sauce. It sounds random for BBQ chicken, but it adds this deep savory saltiness that makes people think you marinated for days. Don't skip it, and don't swap it for anything else. Also, bone-in thighs are almost always cheaper than breasts and way more forgiving on the grill — they stay juicy even if you leave them on a couple minutes too long, which is exactly what you want when you're also managing potato salad, drinks, and your tía asking questions.
