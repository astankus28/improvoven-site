Quick Hearts of Palm Ceviche (Vegan & Totally Refreshing)

Published June 27, 2026 · Improv Oven

This quick hearts of palm ceviche vegan style is about to become your new go-to when you need something fresh, impressive, and ready in minutes — no fish, no fuss. Hearts of palm soak up that citrus-garlic marinade like they were born for it, and honestly? Your guests won't even ask where the seafood is. This one's straight Miami backyard energy in a bowl.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
15 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (14 oz each) hearts of palm, drained and sliced into rounds or half-moons
  • 1/2 cup fresh lime juice (about 4-5 limes — please, fresh only)
  • 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice (about 2 lemons)
  • 1 medium red onion, finely diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 medium cucumber, diced small
  • 1 jalapeño or serrano pepper, seeded and minced (leave seeds in if you want heat)
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced or grated
  • 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil (optional but adds a little richness)
  • 1 avocado, diced (add right before serving)
  • Tostones, tortilla chips, or crackers for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Drain your hearts of palm well and slice them into rounds or half-moons about a quarter inch thick. If you want more of a shredded texture that mimics fish flakes, use two forks to gently pull them apart after slicing. Both ways are delicious — pick your vibe.
  2. 2Add the sliced hearts of palm to a large mixing bowl. Pour in all the lime juice and lemon juice. Give it a stir and let it sit for 5 minutes while you prep everything else. That citrus is doing the 'cooking' just like in traditional ceviche.
  3. 3While the hearts of palm are marinating, dice your red onion and soak it in cold water for 3 minutes to mellow out that raw bite — then drain it. This is a small move that makes a big difference.
  4. 4Add the drained red onion, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, jalapeño, garlic, cilantro, salt, and black pepper to the bowl. Drizzle in the olive oil if using. Toss everything together gently so you don't break up the hearts of palm too much.
  5. 5Taste it. Adjust salt and citrus to your liking — if it needs more punch, squeeze in another half lime. Let it sit for at least 5 more minutes so the flavors can get to know each other.
  6. 6Right before serving, fold in your diced avocado. Scoop it into cups, bowls, or onto a big platter. Serve immediately with tostones, tortilla chips, or whatever you're working with.
💡 Improv Tip

The marinade is everything here — don't skip the full 10 minutes of citrus time even though you're in a hurry. And if your limes are looking sad and dry at the store, grab lemons instead and go heavy. Also, this ceviche is even better the next day once it's been sitting overnight in the fridge, so make a double batch and thank yourself tomorrow.
