Easy Family Meal Prep for Picky Eaters: Build-Your-Own Taco Bowls

Published June 28, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been searching for an easy family meal prep for picky eaters, this build-your-own taco bowl situation is your new best friend — everybody picks what they want, nobody's crying at the table. We're talking seasoned ground beef, fluffy rice, and a lineup of simple toppings that even the most stubborn little food critics will actually eat. Make it Sunday, eat it all week, done.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ground beef (or ground turkey)
  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 4 cups water or chicken broth
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (15 oz) corn, drained
  • 1 packet taco seasoning (or 1 tsp each cumin, garlic powder, chili powder, salt)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup shredded Mexican cheese blend
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup salsa or pico de gallo
  • 1 avocado, sliced (optional but worth it)
  • Flour or corn tortillas, for serving on the side

Instructions

  1. 1Start your rice first because it takes the longest. Add 2 cups of rice and 4 cups of water or chicken broth to a medium pot. Bring it to a boil, then drop the heat to low, cover, and let it cook for 18 minutes. Broth over water every time — free flavor, no extra work.
  2. 2While the rice does its thing, heat 1 tbsp of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add your ground beef and break it up with a spoon as it browns. Cook until there's no more pink, about 7 to 8 minutes, then drain off the excess fat.
  3. 3Season your meat. Add the taco seasoning plus a splash of water (about 3 tablespoons) directly into the skillet with the beef. Stir it all together and let it cook another 2 minutes until the seasoning coats everything and smells incredible. Turn the heat off.
  4. 4Warm your black beans in a small saucepan over low heat with a pinch of cumin and salt, just 3 to 4 minutes. Same thing with the corn if you want it warm — totally optional, cold corn from the can works just fine.
  5. 5Now set up your taco bowl station. Lay out the rice, seasoned beef, black beans, corn, cheese, sour cream, salsa, and avocado in separate bowls or containers. Let everyone build their own bowl with whatever they want. Picky eaters get control, you get peace — everybody wins.
  6. 6For meal prep, divide the rice and beef into airtight containers. Store toppings separately so nothing gets soggy. Everything keeps in the fridge for up to 4 days. Reheat the rice and beef with a tiny splash of water in the microwave for 90 seconds and you're back in business.
💡 Improv Tip

Here's the move for seriously picky kids — keep the toppings completely separate even at dinner, never mixed in. When a kid can see exactly what's touching what, they feel safe. And honestly? A bowl of plain rice and beef with just cheese is still a solid, nutritious meal. You're not failing as a parent, you're being strategic. The salsa and avocado are for you, enjoy them without guilt.
