Simple 5 Ingredient Meal Prep Recipe: Sofrito Rice and Beans

Published June 28, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been hunting for simple 5 ingredient meal prep recipes that actually taste like someone's abuela made them, this is your sign to stop scrolling. We're talking fluffy rice cooked right in seasoned black beans with a hit of sofrito — one pot, four days of lunches, zero stress. This is the kind of meal that cures refrigerator blindness for real.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup long grain white rice
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 3 tablespoons sofrito (jarred is totally fine)
  • 2 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a medium saucepan or deep skillet over medium heat. Once it shimmers, add the sofrito and let it cook for about 2 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it deepens in color and smells incredible — that sizzle is flavor building right in front of you.
  2. 2Add the drained black beans to the pot and stir them into the sofrito until everything is well coated. Let the beans cook with the sofrito for another 2 minutes so they can soak up all that goodness.
  3. 3Pour in the dry rice and stir to coat every grain in the sofrito-bean mixture. Toast the rice for about 1 minute — this step makes the texture so much better, don't skip it.
  4. 4Add the broth and stir everything together. Bring it to a boil over medium-high heat, then drop the heat to low, cover tightly, and let it cook undisturbed for 18 minutes.
  5. 5After 18 minutes, take it off the heat and let it steam with the lid still on for 5 more minutes. Then fluff with a fork and divide into 4 meal prep containers. Done. That's your week sorted.
💡 Improv Tip

Jarred sofrito is your best friend here and there is absolutely no shame in it — Goya, Knorr, whatever's on the shelf. But if you want to level it up, throw a spoonful of tomato paste in with the sofrito when you're frying it in step one. It adds a little sweetness and depth that makes people think you were cooking all day.
