Budget Cuban Sandwich Soup Recipe (All the Flavor, None of the Fuss)

Published June 24, 2026 · Improv Oven

This budget Cuban sandwich soup recipe takes everything you love about a classic Cubano — the ham, the pickles, the mustard, that melty Swiss — and turns it into a cozy, slurpable soup that costs almost nothing to make. It's the kind of meal that hits different on a weeknight when you're tired but still want something that feels like love. Miami approved, refrigerator raid required.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or butter
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 2 tablespoons yellow mustard
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 4 cups chicken broth (or water with a bouillon cube)
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 medium russet potatoes, peeled and diced small
  • 1/2 pound deli ham or leftover ham, chopped
  • 1/2 cup dill pickle slices, roughly chopped, plus extra for topping
  • 2 tablespoons dill pickle brine (from the jar — don't sleep on this)
  • 1 cup shredded Swiss cheese, divided
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Crusty Cuban bread or sandwich rolls, for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook for about 4 minutes until it softens and turns a little translucent. Add the garlic and cook another 60 seconds until fragrant — your kitchen should already be smelling good right now.
  2. 2Stir in the cumin, oregano, smoked paprika, yellow mustard, and Dijon mustard. Let everything cook together for about 1 minute so those spices bloom and the mustard gets a little toasty. This step is where the soup gets its soul.
  3. 3Pour in the chicken broth and water, then add the diced potatoes. Bring the whole thing to a boil, then reduce the heat to a steady simmer. Cook uncovered for about 12 to 15 minutes until the potatoes are completely fork-tender.
  4. 4Once the potatoes are soft, use the back of a wooden spoon or a potato masher to lightly mash some of them right in the pot — maybe a quarter of them. This thickens the broth naturally and gives it that hearty, stew-like body without any flour or cornstarch.
  5. 5Add the chopped ham and the dill pickles. Stir everything together and let it simmer for another 3 to 4 minutes so the ham warms through and the pickle flavor gets into the broth. Pour in the pickle brine and stir — taste it. That's the move right there.
  6. 6Turn the heat down to low and stir in half the shredded Swiss cheese until it melts into the soup. Season with salt and black pepper to taste, keeping in mind the ham and broth are already salty so go slow.
  7. 7Ladle into bowls and top each one with the remaining Swiss cheese and a few extra pickle slices. Serve with thick slices of Cuban bread or a toasted roll on the side for dunking. Eat immediately and feel very good about your life choices.
💡 Improv Tip

The pickle brine is non-negotiable — it's the secret weapon that makes this taste unmistakably Cuban sandwich and not just ham soup. If your broth tastes flat halfway through cooking, add another splash of brine before you reach for the salt shaker. Also, leftover roast pork or pulled pork mixed in with the ham? Absolutely elite move.
