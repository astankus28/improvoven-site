Shakshuka: The Healthy Egg Recipe for Dinner You'll Make Every Week

Published June 8, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're looking for a healthy egg recipe for dinner that feels fancy but costs almost nothing, shakshuka is your answer — eggs poached right in a spiced tomato sauce, all in one pan. It's big on flavor, packed with protein and veggies, and honestly it feels like something you'd order at a trendy Wynwood brunch spot. The best part? Your pantry probably already has everything you need.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, any color, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili flakes (or to taste)
  • 1/2 teaspoon oregano
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley for topping
  • Crusty bread or warm tortillas for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large skillet or wide sauté pan over medium heat. Toss in your diced onion and bell pepper and cook for about 5-6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they soften and start to smell amazing.
  2. 2Add the minced garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, chili flakes, and oregano. Stir everything together and let those spices toast for about 60 seconds — you'll smell when it's ready, trust me.
  3. 3Pour in the crushed tomatoes and stir to combine. Season with salt and black pepper. Let the sauce simmer on medium-low heat for about 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it thickens up a little and the flavors come together.
  4. 4Taste your sauce and adjust seasoning — this is your moment. More cumin? Go for it. Want it spicier? Add more chili flakes.
  5. 5Use a spoon to create 6 little wells in the sauce. Crack one egg into each well. Cover the pan with a lid and let the eggs cook for 5-7 minutes. You want the whites fully set but the yolks still a little runny — that's the sweet spot. If you prefer fully cooked yolks, leave them covered for 8-9 minutes.
  6. 6Pull the pan off the heat, scatter fresh cilantro or parsley all over the top, and bring that beauty straight to the table. Serve with crusty bread or warm tortillas for scooping up every last drop of that sauce.
💡 Improv Tip

Don't skip the lid — that's what gently steams the eggs and gets you those perfectly set whites without ruining the yolks. And if you've got a half a zucchini or some leftover spinach sitting in the fridge giving you sad eyes, throw it into the sauce when you add the tomatoes. Shakshuka is very forgiving and very into whatever you've got going on.
