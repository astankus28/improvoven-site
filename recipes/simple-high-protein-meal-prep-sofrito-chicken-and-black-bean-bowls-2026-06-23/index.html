Simple High Protein Meal Prep: Sofrito Chicken and Black Bean Bowls

Published June 23, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been searching for simple high protein meal prep recipe ideas that actually taste like something, not just sad chicken and rice, this one's for you. We're talking juicy sofrito-marinated chicken thighs, creamy black beans, and fluffy rice all prepped in under 45 minutes for the whole week. This is how we eat well in Miami without spending a fortune.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
40 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons sofrito (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon sazon seasoning
  • 2 cups long grain white rice
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 teaspoon salt for rice
  • 1 tablespoon butter or olive oil for rice
  • Fresh cilantro for garnish (optional)
  • Lime wedges for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your chicken thighs dry with paper towels — this is the move for getting a good sear. In a large bowl, combine the sofrito, olive oil, garlic powder, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, salt, and pepper. Toss the chicken in that marinade and let it sit for at least 10 minutes while you get everything else going. If you can marinate overnight in the fridge, even better.
  2. 2Start your rice. Add 2 cups of rice, 4 cups of water, salt, and butter or olive oil to a medium saucepan. Bring it to a boil, then drop the heat to the lowest setting, cover tight, and let it cook for 18 minutes. Do not lift that lid. When the time is up, pull it off the heat and let it steam covered for 5 more minutes, then fluff with a fork.
  3. 3While the rice cooks, heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add a drizzle of olive oil and place the chicken thighs in a single layer. Sear for about 5 to 6 minutes per side until you get that gorgeous golden-brown crust and the internal temperature hits 165 degrees F. Pull them off the heat and let them rest for 5 minutes before slicing or chopping.
  4. 4In a small saucepan over medium heat, add your drained black beans, chicken broth, and sazon seasoning. Stir everything together and let it simmer for about 8 to 10 minutes until the broth reduces slightly and the beans get thick and saucy. Taste and adjust salt if needed.
  5. 5Now build your meal prep bowls. Grab 4 airtight containers and divide the rice evenly among them. Spoon the saucy black beans over the rice, then top with your sliced chicken. Add cilantro and a lime wedge if you're feeling fancy. Seal them up and store in the fridge for up to 4 days. When you're ready to eat, microwave for about 2 minutes with a splash of water to keep things moist.
💡 Improv Tip

Chicken thighs are your best friend for meal prep — they stay juicy even after a few days in the fridge, unlike chicken breast which turns into cardboard by Tuesday. Each bowl comes out to roughly 45 to 50 grams of protein, so you're covered. If your store-bought sofrito has been sitting in the back of your fridge looking lonely, this is exactly the recipe it was waiting for.
