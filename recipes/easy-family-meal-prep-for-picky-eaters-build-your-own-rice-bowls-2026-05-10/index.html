Easy Family Meal Prep for Picky Eaters: Build-Your-Own Rice Bowls

Published May 10, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been searching for easy family meal prep for picky eaters, this build-your-own rice bowl situation is about to become your weeknight best friend. You cook everything once, lay it all out, and let everybody build their own plate — no more short-order cooking, no more complaints. It's the kind of meal that works whether your kid only eats plain rice or your partner wants the whole loaded situation with sazón chicken and pickled onions on top.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
40 mins
Serves
4-6

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 4 cups water or chicken broth
  • 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (15 oz) corn, drained (or 1.5 cups frozen corn)
  • 1 packet sazón seasoning (or 1 tsp cumin + 1 tsp garlic powder + 1/2 tsp paprika)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese (cheddar or Mexican blend)
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce or baby spinach
  • 1 avocado, sliced (optional but highly recommended)
  • Hot sauce for the table (everyone seasons their own)

Instructions

  1. 1Start your rice first because it takes the longest. Combine 2 cups of rice with 4 cups of water or chicken broth in a medium pot. Add a pinch of salt and a tiny drizzle of oil. Bring to a boil, then drop the heat to low, cover, and let it cook undisturbed for 18 minutes. When it's done, fluff it with a fork and keep it covered until you're ready to serve.
  2. 2While the rice is doing its thing, season your chicken thighs. Pat them dry with a paper towel — this is the secret to getting a nice sear. Mix together the sazón, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin, salt, and pepper in a small bowl, then rub that all over both sides of the chicken.
  3. 3Heat your oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Once the oil is shimmering, add the chicken thighs in a single layer — don't crowd them. Cook for about 6-7 minutes per side until they're golden brown and cooked through (internal temp 165°F). Pull them off the heat and let them rest for 5 minutes before slicing or chopping into bite-sized pieces.
  4. 4While the chicken rests, warm up your black beans in a small saucepan over medium-low heat with a pinch of cumin and salt. Give them about 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally. For the corn, you can either warm it in the same way or just serve it room temp straight from the can — nobody's going to fight you on that.
  5. 5Now set up your bowl station like a little taco bar situation. Put everything in separate bowls or sections of a big sheet pan: the rice, sliced chicken, beans, corn, cheese, sour cream, lettuce or spinach, avocado slices, lime wedges, and hot sauce on the side. Let everyone build their own bowl. The picky eater gets plain rice and cheese, the adventurous one gets everything, and you get peace at the dinner table.
💡 Improv Tip

Here's the real meal prep move — cook a double batch of everything on Sunday and store each component in its own container in the fridge. The chicken, rice, and beans all keep beautifully for up to 4 days. Keeping them separate is key because it means everybody (picky kids included) can mix and match all week without committing to one combination. It also reheats way better than trying to store a fully assembled bowl.
