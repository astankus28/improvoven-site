Cheap Chicken Dinner Ideas Under $10: Crispy Skillet Chicken Thighs with Garlic Pan Sauce

Published July 23, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're hunting for cheap chicken dinner ideas under $10, this skillet chicken thigh recipe is exactly what you need — golden, crispy skin, tender meat, and a buttery garlic pan sauce that tastes wildly more expensive than it is. Bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs are the unsung heroes of budget cooking, almost always under $2 a pound and nearly impossible to overcook. This whole meal comes together in one pan in about 30 minutes, and the cleanup is as easy as the cooking.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 2 lbs)
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil (vegetable or canola)
  • 5 cloves garlic, smashed and peeled
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth (or water with a pinch of bouillon)
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon fresh parsley, roughly chopped (optional but worth it)
  • Lemon wedges for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your chicken thighs completely dry with paper towels — this is the single most important step for getting that crackling, golden skin. Don't skip it. Mix together the salt, pepper, garlic powder, and smoked paprika in a small bowl, then season the chicken generously on both sides.
  2. 2Heat the oil in a large cast-iron skillet or heavy oven-safe pan over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Lay the chicken thighs skin-side DOWN and resist every urge to move them. Let them cook undisturbed for 10 to 12 minutes. You'll know they're ready to flip when the skin releases easily from the pan and is a deep golden brown.
  3. 3Flip the chicken and nestle the smashed garlic cloves around it. Continue cooking skin-side up for another 10 to 12 minutes over medium heat, until the thighs are cooked through to an internal temperature of 165°F. The garlic should be soft and golden by now — that's flavor gold.
  4. 4Transfer the chicken to a plate and tent loosely with foil to rest. Don't wipe out the pan — all those brown bits are money. Pour in the chicken broth and scrape the bottom of the pan with a wooden spoon to lift everything up. Let it bubble and reduce for about 2 minutes.
  5. 5Remove the pan from heat and stir in the butter and Dijon mustard until the butter melts and the sauce comes together into something glossy and seriously good. Taste it — adjust salt if needed.
  6. 6Spoon the garlic pan sauce over the rested chicken thighs, scatter the parsley on top, and serve with lemon wedges if you have them. A side of crusty bread to mop up the sauce, or simple roasted potatoes, and you've got a full dinner that feels like a restaurant night in.
💡 Improv Tip

The secret to truly crispy chicken skin is a dry surface and patience — don't rush the first sear by cranking the heat too high or you'll scorch the skin before it renders. Medium-high to start, then back it down to medium after the flip. Also, bone-in thighs vary in size, so if yours are on the larger side, give them a couple extra minutes and always check with an instant-read thermometer rather than guessing. If you don't have Dijon, a splash of Worcestershire or even a tiny squeeze of lemon into the pan sauce works beautifully.
