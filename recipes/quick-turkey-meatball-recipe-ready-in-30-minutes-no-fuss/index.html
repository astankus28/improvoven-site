Quick Turkey Meatball Recipe (Ready in 30 Minutes, No Fuss)

Published November 22, 2026 · Improv Oven

This quick turkey meatball recipe is exactly what a busy weeknight looks like when it goes right — lean ground turkey, a handful of pantry staples, and about half an hour between you and dinner. Toss them into marinara over pasta, stuff them into a hoagie roll, or just eat them straight off the sheet pan because nobody's judging. They're juicy, well-seasoned, and genuinely easy to pull off even if you've never made a meatball in your life.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground turkey (93% lean works great)
  • 1/3 cup breadcrumbs (plain or Italian-seasoned)
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoons milk
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan (the green can works fine here)
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil (if pan-searing) or cooking spray (if baking)
  • 2 cups marinara sauce (jarred is totally fine)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 400°F if baking, or set a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat if you want a pan-sear finish. Either method works — baking is more hands-off, searing gives you a little more crust.
  2. 2In a large bowl, combine the ground turkey, breadcrumbs, egg, milk, garlic, Parmesan, Italian seasoning, onion powder, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes if using. Mix everything together with your hands or a fork just until combined — overmixing makes tough meatballs, so stop as soon as it looks uniform.
  3. 3Scoop and roll the mixture into balls about 1.5 inches in diameter — roughly the size of a golf ball. You should get around 16 to 18 meatballs. Wet your hands lightly between rolls to keep the mixture from sticking.
  4. 4To bake: Place meatballs on a lightly greased baking sheet or a rack set over a sheet pan. Bake at 400°F for 18 to 20 minutes, until cooked through and lightly golden on the outside. To pan-sear: Add olive oil to your skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the meatballs in batches, turning every 2 to 3 minutes to brown on multiple sides, about 8 to 10 minutes total. Then reduce heat, pour in the marinara, cover, and simmer 5 minutes to finish cooking through.
  5. 5While the meatballs cook, warm your marinara in a separate saucepan if baking. Once the meatballs come out of the oven, nestle them right into the sauce and let everything hang out together for 3 to 5 minutes. This is not optional — it's what makes them taste like they simmered all day.
  6. 6Serve over spaghetti or rigatoni, piled into a toasted hoagie roll with extra sauce and melted mozzarella, or alongside roasted vegetables for a lighter plate. Leftovers reheat beautifully with a splash of water or broth to loosen the sauce.
💡 Improv Tip

Ground turkey is leaner than beef, which means it wants to dry out on you if you let it. The milk-and-breadcrumb combo (basically a quick panade) is your insurance policy — it traps moisture inside the meatball so the inside stays tender even if the outside gets some color. Don't skip it, and don't skip the Parmesan either — it adds salt, fat, and a little savory depth that turkey on its own just doesn't have.
