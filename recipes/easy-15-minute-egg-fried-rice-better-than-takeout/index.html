Easy 15-Minute Egg Fried Rice (Better Than Takeout)

Published October 16, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy 15-minute egg fried rice is exactly what your leftover rice has been waiting for — hot, savory, a little smoky, and ready before you'd even finish placing a delivery order. We're keeping it simple but giving it that Miami kitchen energy with a splash of soy, a hit of sesame, and a little sofrito-style seasoning that makes it feel like home. Budget lunch doesn't get more satisfying than this.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
10 mins
Total
15 mins
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked white rice (day-old or cold, straight from the fridge)
  • 3 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil or any neutral oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 small white onion, diced
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas and carrots (no need to thaw)
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin (the Miami twist — trust it)
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional: a few dashes of hot sauce or sriracha to serve

Instructions

  1. 1Get your pan ripping hot. Set a large skillet or wok over high heat for a full minute before adding anything — this is the move that gives you fried rice instead of steamed rice. Add the vegetable oil and let it shimmer.
  2. 2Add the diced onion and cook for 90 seconds, stirring frequently, until it starts to soften and pick up a little color. Add the minced garlic and stir constantly for another 30 seconds until fragrant. Don't walk away — garlic burns fast on high heat.
  3. 3Add the frozen peas and carrots directly into the pan. Stir and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until they're warmed through and any water has cooked off.
  4. 4Push everything to one side of the pan. Crack the 3 eggs into the empty side, scramble them quickly with your spatula, and let them cook about 70% of the way through before breaking them up and folding them into the vegetables. You want soft, jammy egg bits — not fully dry.
  5. 5Add the cold rice, breaking up any clumps as it hits the pan. Spread it out and let it sit for 30 seconds without touching it — that's how you get the slightly toasted, not-mushy texture. Then stir and fold everything together.
  6. 6Drizzle the soy sauce and sesame oil evenly over the rice. Add the cumin and black pepper. Stir everything together vigorously for 1 to 2 minutes until the rice is evenly coated and any liquid has absorbed.
  7. 7Taste and adjust — add a pinch of salt if needed, more soy sauce if you want more savory depth. Kill the heat, top with sliced green onions, and serve immediately with hot sauce on the side if you like.
💡 Improv Tip

Cold rice is non-negotiable here — freshly cooked rice is too moist and will turn your fried rice into a clumpy mess. Spread leftover rice on a plate and refrigerate it uncovered for even an hour if you're in a pinch. No leftovers? Cook a batch, spread it thin, and let it cool while you prep everything else.
