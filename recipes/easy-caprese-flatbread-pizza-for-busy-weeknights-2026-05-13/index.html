Easy Caprese Flatbread Pizza for Busy Weeknights

Published May 13, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy caprese flatbread pizza recipe is exactly what weeknight cooking should look like — fast, fresh, and so good you'll forget you made it in under 30 minutes. We're talking crispy flatbread loaded with juicy tomatoes, melty mozzarella, and fresh basil drizzled with olive oil. It's the kind of meal that makes your kitchen smell amazing and your wallet stay happy.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
12 mins
Total
22 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 store-bought flatbreads or naan breads
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced or 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 2 medium ripe tomatoes, thinly sliced
  • 8 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced (or shredded low-moisture mozzarella)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional but recommended)
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 2 tablespoons balsamic glaze for drizzling

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 425°F and line a large baking sheet with foil or parchment paper. If you have a pizza stone, now's a great time to use it — slide it in while the oven heats up.
  2. 2Mix your olive oil and minced garlic together in a small bowl. Brush that garlicky oil generously over each flatbread all the way to the edges. This is your flavor base, so don't be shy with it.
  3. 3Lay your tomato slices out on a paper towel and pat them gently dry. This is the move — wet tomatoes will make your flatbread soggy, and we are NOT doing soggy tonight.
  4. 4Arrange the mozzarella slices evenly across each flatbread, then layer the tomatoes on top. Season everything with salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes if you're using them.
  5. 5Slide the flatbreads onto your baking sheet and bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbly and the edges of the flatbread are golden and crisp. If you want a little char on top, hit the broiler for the last 60 to 90 seconds — just stay close and watch it.
  6. 6Pull them out of the oven and immediately scatter fresh basil leaves all over the top. Finish with a generous drizzle of balsamic glaze. Slice them up and serve right away while everything is hot and the cheese is still stretchy.
💡 Improv Tip

Listen, fresh mozzarella is dreamy but it does release more water than low-moisture. If that's all you have, just slice it thin, pat it dry like we did the tomatoes, and don't skip the high oven temp — the heat is what keeps things crispy. No balsamic glaze? Mix a splash of balsamic vinegar with a tiny pinch of sugar and reduce it in a small pan for two minutes. Boom, done.
