Black Bean and Rice Bowls: Affordable Healthy Dinner Recipes Done Right

Published June 12, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're hunting for affordable healthy dinner recipes that actually taste like something, this Cuban-inspired black bean and rice bowl is your new weeknight best friend. We're talking creamy seasoned black beans over fluffy white rice, topped with bright pico de gallo and a squeeze of lime — all for under two bucks a serving. This is the kind of meal your abuela would respect and your wallet will love.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 1/2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 3 cups water (for rice)
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
  • 2 limes, cut into wedges
  • 1 avocado, sliced (optional but highly encouraged)

Instructions

  1. 1Start your rice first so everything finishes around the same time. Combine 1 1/2 cups rice with 3 cups water and a big pinch of salt in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, then drop the heat to low, cover tight, and let it do its thing for about 18 minutes. No peeking — trust the process.
  2. 2While the rice is cooking, heat your olive oil in a large skillet or saucepan over medium heat. Toss in the diced onion and green bell pepper with a pinch of salt. Cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they soften and get a little golden around the edges.
  3. 3Add the minced garlic, cumin, oregano, and smoked paprika to the pan. Stir everything together and let those spices bloom for about 60 seconds — you'll smell exactly when they're ready. It should smell amazing in your kitchen right now.
  4. 4Pour in both cans of drained black beans along with the broth. Stir to combine, then bring to a gentle simmer. Let the beans cook uncovered for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the liquid reduces and the beans get thick and saucy. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. 5While the beans finish up, make your quick pico by combining the diced tomatoes, half the cilantro, a pinch of salt, and a squeeze of lime juice in a small bowl. Give it a stir and let it sit while you plate everything.
  6. 6Fluff your rice with a fork. Spoon a generous scoop into each bowl, ladle the black beans right over the top, and finish with the fresh pico, remaining cilantro, avocado slices if you've got them, and a big squeeze of lime. Eat immediately and feel very good about yourself.
💡 Improv Tip

The secret weapon here is letting those beans simmer down until they're almost stew-like and thick — watery beans over rice is sad, and we don't do sad food at Improv Oven. If your beans look too soucy, just give them a few more minutes uncovered on medium heat. Also, a tiny splash of apple cider vinegar stirred in right at the end brightens the whole pot like you wouldn't believe.
