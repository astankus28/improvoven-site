Easy Baked Chicken Thighs Recipe (Crispy Skin, Juicy Every Time)

Published July 9, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy baked chicken thighs recipe is the one you'll come back to every single week — minimal ingredients, barely any prep, and results that taste like you actually tried. Bone-in, skin-on thighs go into a hot oven with a simple spice rub and come out with crackling golden skin and meat so juicy it practically falls off the bone. No marinating, no fuss, just real dinner on the table.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
40 mins
Total
50 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 2 lbs)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon brown sugar (optional, helps the skin caramelize)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a rimmed baking sheet with foil and set a wire rack on top if you have one — this lets the heat circulate under the thighs for crispier skin. No rack? No problem, the foil pan works fine too.
  2. 2Pat the chicken thighs completely dry with paper towels. This is the single most important step for crispy skin — any moisture on the surface will steam instead of sear, and you'll end up with a sad, soft skin situation.
  3. 3In a small bowl, mix together the garlic powder, smoked paprika, onion powder, dried thyme, black pepper, salt, and brown sugar if using. Drizzle the olive oil over the chicken thighs, then coat them all over with the spice rub — top, bottom, and under the skin if you can get your fingers in there.
  4. 4Arrange the thighs skin-side up on your prepared baking sheet. Make sure they're not crowded — give each piece a little breathing room so the heat can do its thing.
  5. 5Bake at 425°F for 35 to 40 minutes, until the skin is deep golden brown and a meat thermometer reads 165°F at the thickest part (avoiding the bone). If you want extra-crispy skin, flip the broiler on high for the last 2 to 3 minutes and watch them closely.
  6. 6Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes before serving. This keeps all those juices locked inside instead of running all over your cutting board. Serve with whatever you've got — roasted veggies, a simple salad, crusty bread, mashed potatoes, rice — chicken thighs play well with everything.
💡 Improv Tip

Don't skip drying the chicken — it sounds like a small thing but it's the difference between skin that crackles and skin that chews. If you have an extra five minutes, leave the seasoned thighs uncovered in the fridge for 30 minutes or even overnight. That little air-dry step pulls out even more surface moisture and kicks the crispiness up another level.
