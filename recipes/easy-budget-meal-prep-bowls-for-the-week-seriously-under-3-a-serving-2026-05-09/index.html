Easy Budget Meal Prep Bowls for the Week (Seriously, Under $3 a Serving)

Published May 9, 2026 · Improv Oven

These easy meal prep bowls for the week on a budget are your Monday morning gift to your future self — colorful, filling, and so much better than whatever sad desk lunch you were about to buy. Think cilantro-lime rice, seasoned black beans, roasted veggies, and a creamy avocado crema all stacked up in four grab-and-go containers. Miami taught me that good food doesn't need a big price tag, just a little love and the right spices.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup long-grain white rice (dry)
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 lime, juiced and zested
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped (or 1 tsp dried if that's what you've got)
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 large zucchini, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 cup frozen or canned corn, drained
  • 2 tbsp olive oil (or any neutral oil)
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 2 tbsp sour cream or plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • Pinch of salt
  • Hot sauce for serving (optional but highly encouraged)

Instructions

  1. 1Cook your rice: Combine the dry rice and water in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, then drop the heat to low, cover, and let it do its thing for 18 minutes. When it's done, fluff it with a fork and stir in the lime juice, lime zest, cilantro, and a good pinch of salt. Set aside.
  2. 2While the rice cooks, preheat your oven to 425°F. Toss the diced zucchini, bell pepper, and corn with the olive oil, a pinch of salt, pepper, and the smoked paprika. Spread everything out on a baking sheet in a single layer — don't crowd them or they'll steam instead of roast. Roast for 20-22 minutes until you get some nice golden edges.
  3. 3Season the beans: Pour the drained black beans into a small saucepan over medium heat. Add the cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Stir and heat through for about 5 minutes until warmed and fragrant. If they look a little dry, splash in a tablespoon of water. You want them saucy, not dusty.
  4. 4Make the avocado crema: Scoop the avocado into a bowl and mash it well with a fork. Mix in the sour cream (or Greek yogurt), lime juice, and a pinch of salt until smooth and creamy. Taste it — if it needs more lime or salt, you know what to do.
  5. 5Build your bowls: Grab four meal prep containers. Divide the cilantro-lime rice evenly across all four as your base. Add a generous scoop of seasoned black beans to one side, then pile the roasted veggies on the other. Add a big dollop of avocado crema on top of each bowl.
  6. 6Seal them up and refrigerate. They'll keep great for up to 4 days. When you're ready to eat, reheat the rice, beans, and veggies in the microwave for about 2 minutes, then add a fresh spoonful of crema on top. If you made the crema ahead, press plastic wrap directly onto the surface so it doesn't turn brown.
💡 Improv Tip

The avocado crema is the move that makes these bowls feel restaurant-level, but if your avocado isn't quite ripe yet, don't stress — just do a simple squeeze of lime over the bowl and call it a drizzle. Also, this recipe is basically a blank canvas: swap the zucchini for whatever sad vegetable is looking at you from the crisper drawer. Broccoli, sweet potato, even frozen peas work beautifully here. That's the whole point — cure that refrigerator blindness.
