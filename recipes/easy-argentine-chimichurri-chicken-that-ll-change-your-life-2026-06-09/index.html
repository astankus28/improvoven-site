Easy Argentine Chimichurri Chicken That'll Change Your Life

Published June 9, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy Argentine chimichurri chicken is the kind of meal that makes you feel like you actually have your life together — and it comes together in about 30 minutes with stuff you probably already have. Chimichurri is basically Argentina's gift to the world: a bright, herby, garlicky sauce that turns simple grilled chicken into something that tastes like a whole event. Make a big batch of that sauce because trust me, you're going to want to put it on everything.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless skinless chicken thighs or breasts
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 cup fresh flat-leaf parsley, tightly packed
  • 4 cloves garlic, peeled
  • 2 tablespoons fresh oregano or 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your chicken dry with a paper towel — this is non-negotiable if you want that good golden sear. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons olive oil and season all over with the salt, pepper, garlic powder, and smoked paprika. Let it sit for at least 5 minutes while you make the chimichurri.
  2. 2For the chimichurri, add the parsley, garlic cloves, oregano, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper to a food processor or blender. Pulse a few times to roughly chop everything together. Then drizzle in the olive oil and red wine vinegar and pulse a few more times until you get a chunky, loose sauce — don't over-blend it, you want texture, not a smoothie. Taste it and adjust salt or vinegar to your liking. Set it aside.
  3. 3Heat a large skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat until it's properly hot — about 2 minutes. Place the chicken in the pan and don't touch it. Let it sear undisturbed for 5 to 6 minutes on the first side until it releases easily and has a deep golden crust.
  4. 4Flip the chicken and cook another 5 to 7 minutes depending on thickness, until the internal temperature hits 165°F. If you're using a skillet, you can add a splash of water and cover it for the last 2 minutes to help it cook through without drying out.
  5. 5Pull the chicken off the heat and let it rest for 3 to 5 minutes — seriously, don't skip the rest or all those juices are going straight to the cutting board instead of your mouth. Slice or serve whole, then spoon a generous amount of chimichurri right over the top. Add more on the side because there's never enough.
💡 Improv Tip

No fresh parsley? No problem — cilantro works beautifully here and gives it more of a Caribbean edge, which honestly feels right at home in Miami. You can also make the chimichurri up to 3 days ahead and keep it in a jar in the fridge. The flavor actually gets better overnight as everything gets cozy together.
