Simple Grilled Burgers Recipe for Memorial Day

Published May 22, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple grilled burgers recipe is exactly what your Memorial Day cookout needs — juicy, flavorful, and ready without any fuss. We're keeping it real here: good beef, a few smart seasonings, and a hot grill is all you need to make everybody at the party stop talking and start eating. Think backyard vibes with a little Miami magic built right in.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs 80/20 ground beef
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices American or cheddar cheese
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil for grill grates
  • Lettuce, tomato, onion, and pickles for topping
  • Ketchup, mustard, or mayo for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Pull your ground beef out of the fridge about 10 minutes before cooking — cold meat straight to a hot grill is a recipe for uneven cooking. While it rests, preheat your grill to medium-high heat, around 400°F.
  2. 2In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and black pepper. Mix gently with your hands until just combined — don't overwork it or your burgers will come out tough and dense.
  3. 3Divide the meat into 4 equal portions, roughly 6 oz each. Shape them into patties about 3/4 inch thick. Press your thumb into the center of each patty to make a small indent — this keeps them from puffing up into little meatballs on the grill.
  4. 4Brush your grill grates with a little neutral oil so nothing sticks. Place the patties on the grill and don't touch them for 3 to 4 minutes — let them build that crust. Flip once, and cook another 3 to 4 minutes for medium doneness.
  5. 5In the last minute of cooking, lay a slice of cheese on each patty and close the grill lid so it melts beautifully. Toast your buns cut-side down on the grill for about 30 seconds while you're at it.
  6. 6Pull the burgers off the grill and let them rest for 2 minutes — yes, even burgers need a rest. Load them up with your toppings and sauces and serve immediately while everything is hot and the cheese is still oozing.
💡 Improv Tip

Here's the move nobody tells you: the 80/20 ground beef is non-negotiable for a Memorial Day cookout. That fat is your flavor and your juiciness — if you go leaner trying to be healthy, you're going to end up with a dry hockey puck and sad guests. Splurge on the good fat ratio and save money somewhere else, like skipping the fancy buns.
