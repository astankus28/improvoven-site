Easy Grilled Corn on the Cob Recipe with Garlic Butter

Published May 20, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy grilled corn on the cob recipe is the one you'll come back to all summer long — smoky, buttery, and ready in under 30 minutes. We keep it simple but punch it up Miami-style with garlic, lime, and a little heat. No fancy equipment, no stress — just fire, corn, and flavor.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 ears of fresh corn, husks removed
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional but do it)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)
  • Cotija cheese or Parmesan for topping (optional but highly recommended)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your grill to medium-high heat, around 400°F. If you're using a gas grill, give it about 10 minutes to get there. Charcoal grill? Even better — that extra smokiness is going to do something beautiful to the corn.
  2. 2While the grill heats up, mix your softened butter with the minced garlic, smoked paprika, cayenne, salt, and black pepper in a small bowl. Stir it all together until it looks like the most dangerous compound butter you've ever made. Set it aside.
  3. 3Place your shucked corn directly on the grill grates. No foil, no soaking — we want char. Real char. Grill for about 15 to 20 minutes total, turning every 4 to 5 minutes so you get color on all sides. You're looking for those deep golden-brown spots that smell like everything good in life.
  4. 4Pull the corn off the grill and immediately brush each ear generously with your garlic butter mixture. The heat from the corn will melt it right in — get into all those little kernel crevices.
  5. 5Finish with a hard squeeze of fresh lime juice over each ear, then top with chopped cilantro and a crumble of Cotija or Parmesan if you've got it. Serve hot and watch it disappear in about 90 seconds flat.
💡 Improv Tip

If your corn is a little older and not as sweet, don't worry — the grill is going to save you. That caramelization from direct heat concentrates the natural sugars and hides a multitude of sins. Also, that garlic butter works on literally everything in your fridge, so make a double batch and thank yourself later.
