The Best Cuban Sandwich Pressed Recipe You'll Make at Home

Published May 29, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been searching for the best Cuban sandwich pressed recipe, you already know — there's nothing quite like that crispy, golden bread giving way to layers of roasted pork, ham, Swiss, and pickles all melted together into something magical. Growing up in Miami, this wasn't restaurant food, it was Tuesday food. Let me show you how to make it at home with simple ingredients that won't break the bank.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf Cuban bread (or a soft Italian or hoagie roll if that's what you've got)
  • 1/2 lb deli ham, sliced thin
  • 1/2 lb roasted pork (leftover pernil works beautifully here)
  • 4 slices Swiss cheese
  • 1/4 cup dill pickle slices
  • 3 tablespoons yellow mustard
  • 2 tablespoons butter, softened

Instructions

  1. 1Slice your bread lengthwise without cutting all the way through — you want it to open like a book, not fall apart. If you're using a full loaf, cut it into four equal sections first.
  2. 2Spread yellow mustard generously on both inner sides of the bread. Don't be shy here — the mustard is doing real work in a Cuban sandwich, it's not just a condiment.
  3. 3Layer the ingredients in this order on the bottom half: Swiss cheese first (it acts as a barrier so the bread doesn't get soggy), then ham, then roasted pork, then pickles. Close the sandwich.
  4. 4Spread softened butter on the outside of both the top and bottom of the sandwich. This is your ticket to that gorgeous golden crust.
  5. 5Heat a large skillet or cast iron pan over medium heat. Place the sandwich in the pan and press it down firmly with a heavy pan, a foil-wrapped brick, or whatever flat heavy thing you have. A cast iron skillet on top works perfectly.
  6. 6Cook for 3 to 4 minutes on the first side, keeping that weight on top. The bread should be deeply golden and crispy — not just warm, actually golden. Flip carefully, press again, and cook another 3 to 4 minutes until the second side matches and the cheese is fully melted.
  7. 7Let the sandwich rest for just one minute before cutting. Slice on the diagonal — always on the diagonal, this is non-negotiable — and serve immediately while everything is still hot and melty.
💡 Improv Tip

The number one mistake people make is not pressing hard enough or not letting the pan get hot enough before the sandwich goes in. You want that sizzle when it hits the pan. No panini press? No problem. Two cast iron skillets stacked on top of each other is actually how a lot of Miami ventanitas do it, and it works just as well. Also — leftover pernil or even a rotisserie chicken can stand in for the roasted pork if that's what you're working with. Improvise, that's what we do.
