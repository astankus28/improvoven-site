Easy Protein Meal Prep Ideas: Sofrito Chicken and Rice Bowls

Published June 25, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been scrolling for easy protein meal prep ideas that actually taste like something, stop right here — these sofrito chicken and rice bowls are your answer. We're talking juicy, seasoned chicken thighs over fluffy rice with black beans, all packed into containers for the week ahead. Big flavor, low effort, and your wallet stays happy.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 2 tablespoons sofrito (jarred is totally fine)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon sazon seasoning
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 cups long grain white rice
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder (for beans)
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin (for beans)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil (for beans)
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Fresh cilantro for topping (optional but do it)

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your chicken thighs dry with paper towels — this is the move for getting a good sear. Season them on both sides with the garlic powder, cumin, smoked paprika, sazon, salt, and pepper. Then rub the sofrito all over them and let them sit for at least 5 minutes while your pan heats up.
  2. 2Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Once it's shimmering, add the chicken thighs and cook undisturbed for 6-7 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through (internal temp of 165°F). Don't rush this — that crust is where the flavor lives.
  3. 3While the chicken cooks, bring 4 cups of chicken broth to a boil in a medium saucepan. Add 2 cups of rice, stir once, reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 18 minutes. Pull it off the heat and let it steam covered for 5 more minutes, then fluff with a fork.
  4. 4In a small saucepan over medium heat, warm 1 tablespoon of olive oil and add the drained black beans with garlic powder, cumin, salt, and a splash of water. Stir and cook for 4-5 minutes until heated through and fragrant. Finish with a squeeze of lime juice.
  5. 5Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes, then slice or chop it into big chunks. Now build your bowls — rice on the bottom, chicken on top, black beans on the side. Squeeze extra lime over everything and hit it with fresh cilantro if you've got it.
  6. 6Divide everything evenly into 4 airtight meal prep containers. These will keep in the fridge for up to 4 days. When reheating, add a tiny splash of water before microwaving to keep the rice fluffy and the chicken from drying out.
💡 Improv Tip

Chicken thighs are the secret weapon of budget meal prep — they're cheaper than breasts, harder to overcook, and they actually taste better after sitting in the fridge for a day because the sofrito and spices keep soaking in. If your store has them bone-in, grab those and just add about 10 more minutes of cook time for even more flavor.
