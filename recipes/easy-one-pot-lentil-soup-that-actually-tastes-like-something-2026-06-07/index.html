Easy One Pot Lentil Soup That Actually Tastes Like Something

Published June 7, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy one pot lentil soup recipe is the kind of thing your abuela would make on a Tuesday when the fridge was looking sad — humble ingredients, big flavor, zero stress. We're talking smoky, savory, and deeply satisfying with a Latin-Caribbean spin that'll have everyone asking what you put in there. One pot, one hour or less, and you're feeding four people for under ten bucks.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
30 mins
Total
40 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes (optional but recommended)
  • 1 cup green or brown lentils, rinsed
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 4 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 teaspoons sazón seasoning (or 1/2 teaspoon turmeric as a swap)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Fresh cilantro for topping (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Once it shimmers, add your diced onion and green bell pepper. Cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until everything softens and the onion turns translucent.
  2. 2Add the minced garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, and red pepper flakes. Stir everything together and let it cook for about 60 seconds — you want those spices to bloom in the oil and get fragrant. This step is where the magic starts, don't skip it.
  3. 3Add the rinsed lentils, diced tomatoes (with their juices), broth, water, and sazón. Stir to combine, then crank the heat to bring it all to a boil.
  4. 4Once boiling, reduce the heat to medium-low and let the soup simmer uncovered for 25 to 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils are completely tender and have started to break down slightly into the broth. The soup will thicken up beautifully on its own.
  5. 5Taste and season generously with salt and black pepper. Squeeze in the lime juice and give it one final stir — that brightness at the end makes everything pop. Ladle into bowls and top with fresh cilantro if you've got it.
💡 Improv Tip

If your lentils are sitting in the pantry and you can't remember when you bought them, they'll still work — just expect them to take a few extra minutes to soften. And listen, if the soup gets too thick as it sits (lentils love to absorb liquid), just splash in some broth or water when you reheat it and it'll come right back to life.
