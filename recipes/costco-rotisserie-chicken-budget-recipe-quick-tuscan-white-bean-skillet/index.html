Costco Rotisserie Chicken Budget Recipe: Quick Tuscan White Bean Skillet

Published July 19, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've ever grabbed a Costco rotisserie chicken and stared at half of it wondering what to do next, this is your answer — a Costco chicken budget recipe that turns leftover shredded meat into a hearty, restaurant-worthy Tuscan skillet dinner in under 30 minutes. Cannellini beans, garlic, a splash of broth, and a handful of spinach do the heavy lifting, and the whole thing comes together in one pan. It's the kind of meal that feels like you planned it, even when you absolutely did not.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
18 mins
Total
28 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups shredded rotisserie chicken (about half a Costco bird)
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 3 cups fresh baby spinach (or 1 cup frozen, thawed and squeezed dry)
  • 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes in oil, roughly chopped (or 1/2 cup canned diced tomatoes, drained)
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan, plus more for serving
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried rosemary
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Crusty bread or toasted sourdough for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes and cook, stirring often, for about 90 seconds — you want it fragrant and just barely golden, not brown. If it starts going dark fast, pull the pan off the heat for a second.
  2. 2Add the sun-dried tomatoes (or canned diced tomatoes), dried rosemary, and thyme. Stir everything together and let it cook for another 2 minutes so the flavors bloom in that oil.
  3. 3Pour in the chicken broth and add the cannellini beans. Stir to combine, then bring to a gentle simmer. Use the back of a spoon to lightly smash about a quarter of the beans against the side of the pan — this thickens the whole dish into something silky and almost creamy without any cream at all.
  4. 4Add the shredded rotisserie chicken and stir it in. Let everything simmer together for 5 minutes so the chicken soaks up all those savory juices and gets happy.
  5. 5Fold in the spinach a handful at a time, letting each addition wilt before adding more. This only takes a minute or two. Taste and season generously with salt and black pepper.
  6. 6Remove from heat and stir in the Parmesan. The cheese will melt right in and make the broth even silkier. Serve immediately in shallow bowls with extra Parmesan scattered on top and plenty of crusty bread on the side for scooping up every last bit of that broth.
💡 Improv Tip

Costco rotisserie chickens are genuinely one of the best bargains in the store — you can easily stretch one bird into two full meals for a family of four. Pull all the meat off when you get home, divide it into two containers, and refrigerate. One half for this skillet tonight, one half for lunches or another dinner later in the week. And don't throw away the carcass — toss it in a pot with water, a halved onion, and a few peppercorns and you've got free chicken stock in an hour.
