Simple Caesar Pasta Salad for Meal Prep

Published June 13, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple Caesar pasta salad meal prep is the move when you want lunches that actually taste good on day three — not sad, not soggy, just right. We're talking rotini coated in creamy Caesar dressing, crunchy croutons on the side, and enough protein to keep you going. It's the kind of thing your Miami abuela would approve of if she was into Italian-American classics.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 12 oz rotini or penne pasta
  • 1/2 cup Caesar dressing (store-bought is totally fine)
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 large head romaine lettuce, chopped (kept separate for storage)
  • 1/2 cup shaved or grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 cup store-bought croutons (kept separate for storage)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. 1Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to package directions until al dente — you want a little bite left because it'll keep softening in the fridge. Drain and rinse under cold water to stop the cooking, then toss with a tiny drizzle of olive oil so it doesn't stick together.
  2. 2While the pasta cooks, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the drained chickpeas along with the garlic powder, smoked paprika, and salt. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chickpeas are golden and a little crispy on the outside. Set aside to cool.
  3. 3In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the Caesar dressing, Greek yogurt, lemon juice, and a few cracks of black pepper. The Greek yogurt stretches the dressing, adds creaminess, and cuts the cost — don't skip it.
  4. 4Add the cooled pasta to the bowl with the dressing and toss everything together until every piece of pasta is coated. Fold in the Parmesan cheese and crispy chickpeas.
  5. 5For meal prep storage, divide the dressed pasta into four airtight containers. Keep the chopped romaine in a separate container or zip-lock bag, and store the croutons in a small bag on the side. When you're ready to eat, just toss everything together. The dressed pasta stays good in the fridge for up to 4 days.
💡 Improv Tip

The biggest meal prep mistake people make with pasta salad is dressing it all at once — including the lettuce. Keep that romaine separate until the moment you eat it and your day-four lunch will taste almost as fresh as day one. Also, if the pasta looks a little dry when you pull it from the fridge, just add a small splash of lemon juice or a tiny spoon of extra dressing and toss it back to life.
