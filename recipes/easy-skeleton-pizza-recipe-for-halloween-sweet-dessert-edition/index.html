Easy Skeleton Pizza Recipe for Halloween (Sweet Dessert Edition)

Published October 23, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy skeleton pizza recipe for Halloween is the dessert your spooky spread has been missing — a crispy sugar cookie crust topped with vanilla cream cheese frosting and white chocolate bones arranged into a grinning skeleton face. It's genuinely simple, endlessly fun to decorate, and way more impressive-looking than the effort it takes. Kids go absolutely wild for it, and adults will be sneaking slices too.

Prep
20 mins
Cook
14 mins
Total
34 mins
Serves
8

Ingredients

  • 1 tube (16.5 oz) refrigerated sugar cookie dough (or homemade if you have it)
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp milk or heavy cream
  • 1 cup white chocolate chips or white melting wafers
  • 2 tbsp black decorating gel or black food coloring mixed with a little frosting
  • Mini chocolate chips or small black candy pearls (for eyes and nose)
  • Optional: edible black sanding sugar for the background

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 350°F. Press the sugar cookie dough evenly onto a large round pizza pan or a parchment-lined baking sheet, shaping it into a circle about 12 inches wide and roughly 1/4 inch thick. Don't worry about perfection — rustic edges are fine.
  2. 2Bake for 12–14 minutes until the edges are just golden and the center looks set. It will firm up as it cools, so don't overbake. Let it cool completely on the pan — at least 20 minutes. This step is non-negotiable or your frosting will melt.
  3. 3While the crust cools, make the cream cheese frosting. Beat the softened cream cheese with a hand mixer or a fork until smooth, then add the powdered sugar, vanilla, and milk. Mix until fluffy and spreadable. It should be thick but not stiff — add a tiny splash more milk if needed.
  4. 4Spread the frosting over the cooled cookie crust, leaving about a half-inch border around the edge (like a real pizza). You're building the skeleton's 'face' on this white canvas.
  5. 5Melt the white chocolate chips in a microwave-safe bowl in 30-second bursts, stirring between each, until fully smooth. Let it cool for 2–3 minutes so it's pipeable but not runny. Transfer to a zip-lock bag and snip a tiny corner off.
  6. 6Pipe a large oval or circle near the top center of the pizza for the skull — it should take up roughly the top third of the pizza. Then pipe rib bones in curved pairs radiating downward from the skull, like a classic cartoon skeleton chest. Add arm bones and leg bones around the edges if you have space. You can also pipe white chocolate directly into lines and let them set on parchment before placing them on the pizza for cleaner shapes.
  7. 7Use mini chocolate chips pressed into the white chocolate skull for eyes, and a small candy or a dot of black gel for the nose. Use the black decorating gel to draw on the classic skeleton smile — a wide grin with rectangular teeth blocks across the bottom of the skull.
  8. 8Let everything set for 10 minutes at room temperature (or pop it in the fridge for 5 minutes to speed it up). Slice like a pizza and serve. Watch everyone lose their minds.
💡 Improv Tip

The easiest way to get clean skeleton bones without a piping bag is to melt your white chocolate, pour it into a zip-lock bag, chill it flat on a parchment-lined tray for about 3 minutes until just firm enough to hold a shape, then pipe directly. If your white chocolate is too runny, let it cool another minute before piping — rushing this is the number one reason bones go wobbly.
