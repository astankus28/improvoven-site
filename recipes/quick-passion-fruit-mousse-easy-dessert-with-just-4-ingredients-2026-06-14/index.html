Quick Passion Fruit Mousse (Easy Dessert with Just 4 Ingredients)

Published June 14, 2026 · Improv Oven

This quick passion fruit mousse is the easy dessert you make when you want to impress someone without spending your whole evening in the kitchen — we're talking 10 minutes of actual work. Passion fruit has that bright, tropical tartness that makes people think you did something complicated, but babe, you really didn't. It's the kind of recipe that lives in every Miami abuela's back pocket, and now it lives in yours.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
2 hours 10 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup passion fruit pulp (fresh, frozen, or from a jar — all work)
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream, cold
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional: extra passion fruit pulp for topping

Instructions

  1. 1Pour your cold heavy cream into a large bowl and whip it with a hand mixer or stand mixer on medium-high until you get stiff peaks — about 3 to 4 minutes. Don't walk away from it, it moves fast once it gets going. Set it aside in the fridge while you do the next step.
  2. 2In a separate bowl, stir together the sweetened condensed milk, passion fruit pulp, lime juice, and that pinch of salt. Taste it — it should hit you with that punchy tropical flavor up front and finish a little sweet. If your passion fruit is super tart, you can add a tiny drizzle more of condensed milk.
  3. 3Now fold the passion fruit mixture into your whipped cream. Use a rubber spatula and go slow — you worked to get that air in there, don't knock it all out. Fold from the bottom up in big lazy circles until it's fully combined and you've got this gorgeous pale yellow cloud situation happening.
  4. 4Spoon the mousse into individual cups, glasses, or ramekins. Cover them loosely with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 2 hours so everything sets up properly. Overnight is even better if you can wait that long.
  5. 5When you're ready to serve, spoon a little extra passion fruit pulp right on top. Those little seeds are beautiful and add a nice fresh hit against the creamy mousse. Serve cold straight from the fridge.
💡 Improv Tip

Frozen passion fruit pulp is your best friend here — you can find it in the frozen section at almost any Latin grocery store or Walmart for around two dollars, and it tastes just as good as fresh. If all you can find is passion fruit juice, reduce it in a small saucepan over medium heat until it thickens slightly before using, otherwise your mousse won't set up as firm.
