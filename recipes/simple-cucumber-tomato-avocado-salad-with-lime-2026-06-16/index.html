Simple Cucumber Tomato Avocado Salad with Lime

Published June 16, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple cucumber tomato avocado salad with lime is the kind of thing you throw together in ten minutes and somehow everyone thinks you planned it all along. Fresh, bright, creamy — it hits every note without turning on the stove. Very Miami, very easy, very much what your lunch should be today.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
10 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 medium cucumbers, diced
  • 2 medium ripe tomatoes, diced
  • 2 ripe avocados, diced
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Optional: 1 jalapeño, finely diced

Instructions

  1. 1Dice your cucumbers and tomatoes into similar-sized chunks — about half an inch. You want every bite to have a little of everything, so keeping the sizes close matters more than being perfect.
  2. 2Halve your avocados, remove the pits, and dice them right in the skin before scooping out with a spoon. This keeps them chunky and your cutting board clean.
  3. 3Thinly slice the red onion. If raw onion is a little sharp for your taste, soak the slices in cold water for five minutes and pat them dry — takes the edge off without losing the flavor.
  4. 4Add the cucumber, tomato, avocado, red onion, and cilantro to a large bowl. If you're using jalapeño, toss it in now.
  5. 5Squeeze both limes over everything, drizzle with olive oil, and season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Gently fold everything together — you want the avocado to stay in pieces, not turn into guacamole.
  6. 6Taste and adjust. Maybe it needs another squeeze of lime, maybe a pinch more salt. Trust your palate — you've got this. Serve immediately for the best texture.
💡 Improv Tip

The lime juice does double duty here — it seasons the salad AND keeps your avocado from turning that sad brown color. Don't skip it and don't make this too far ahead. If you need to prep in advance, mix everything except the avocado, then fold it in right before serving.
