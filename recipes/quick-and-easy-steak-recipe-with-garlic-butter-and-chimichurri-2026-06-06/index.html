Quick and Easy Steak Recipe with Garlic Butter and Chimichurri

Published June 6, 2026 · Improv Oven

This quick and easy steak recipe is your answer when you want something that feels fancy but comes together in under 30 minutes with stuff you probably already have. We're talking a perfectly seared skillet steak finished with garlic butter, served alongside a bright chimichurri that brings that Miami energy straight to your plate. No grill, no fuss — just big flavor on a budget.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
10 mins
Total
20 mins
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 sirloin steaks (about 6-8 oz each, roughly 1 inch thick)
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary or thyme (optional but worth it)
  • 1/2 cup fresh parsley, roughly chopped
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil (for chimichurri)
  • 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced (for chimichurri)
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. 1Pull your steaks out of the fridge at least 10 minutes before cooking — this is non-negotiable, mijo. Cold steak hitting a hot pan means uneven cooking, and we're not doing that today. Pat them completely dry with paper towels. Dry steak = better crust. That's just science.
  2. 2Mix together the kosher salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and cumin in a small bowl. Season both sides of each steak generously with the blend. Press it in gently so it sticks. You want that seasoning on there like it means something.
  3. 3While the steaks are sitting, make your quick chimichurri. In a small bowl, combine the parsley, cilantro, minced garlic, red wine vinegar, olive oil, red pepper flakes, and a pinch of salt. Stir it all together. Taste it — it should be herby, a little sharp, a little spicy. Adjust the vinegar or salt until it sings. Set it aside.
  4. 4Heat a cast iron skillet or heavy pan over high heat for about 2 minutes until it is genuinely screaming hot. Add the 2 tablespoons of olive oil and swirl to coat. You should see a little smoke — that is exactly what you want.
  5. 5Lay the steaks down away from you so you don't splash hot oil on yourself. Do not move them. Let them sear undisturbed for 3 minutes for medium-rare, or 4 minutes if you like more cook on it.
  6. 6Flip the steaks once. Now add the butter, smashed garlic cloves, and your rosemary or thyme sprigs to the pan. As the butter melts and starts to foam, tilt the pan slightly and use a spoon to continuously baste the steaks with that garlicky butter. Do this for another 3 minutes. Your kitchen is going to smell incredible right now.
  7. 7Pull the steaks off the heat and let them rest on a cutting board for 5 full minutes. I know it is hard to wait but this is where the juices redistribute and the steak goes from good to great. Do not skip this.
  8. 8Slice against the grain or serve whole, spoon that chimichurri generously over the top, and finish with a tiny pinch of flaky salt if you have it. Serve immediately and accept the compliments graciously.
💡 Improv Tip

Sirloin is your best friend for budget steak dinners — it has great beefy flavor, cooks fast, and won't break the bank the way ribeye will. If you can find it on sale, grab a few and freeze them. The chimichurri here also works amazingly as a marinade if you plan ahead, just let the steak sit in it for a couple hours in the fridge and the flavor goes even deeper.
