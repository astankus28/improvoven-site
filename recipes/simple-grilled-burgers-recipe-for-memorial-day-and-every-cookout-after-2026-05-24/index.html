Simple Grilled Burgers Recipe for Memorial Day (and Every Cookout After)

Published May 24, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you need a simple grilled burgers recipe for Memorial Day that actually delivers — juicy, smoky, and loaded with flavor — this is the one to bookmark. We're keeping it classic but dialing up the seasoning the way we do it in Miami, with a little Worcestershire, garlic, and a touch of sofrito magic. No fancy equipment, no expensive cuts — just a hot grill, good technique, and ingredients you probably already have.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ground beef (80/20 blend)
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp sofrito (store-bought is totally fine)
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 slices American or cheddar cheese
  • Lettuce, tomato, onion, and your favorite condiments for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Pull your ground beef out of the fridge about 10 minutes before you start — cold meat straight to the grill is a one-way ticket to uneven cooking. While it takes the chill off, preheat your grill to medium-high heat, around 400°F.
  2. 2In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, Worcestershire sauce, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, sofrito, salt, and black pepper. Mix everything together gently with your hands — and this is important — do not overwork the meat. Mix just until everything is incorporated, then stop. Overworking makes tough burgers, and nobody wants that.
  3. 3Divide the mixture into 4 equal portions and form them into patties about 3/4 inch thick. Here's the move that separates good burgers from great ones: press a small indent with your thumb right in the center of each patty. This keeps them from puffing up into a meatball shape on the grill.
  4. 4Lightly oil your grill grates — a paper towel dipped in vegetable oil held with tongs works great. Place the patties on the hot grill and do not touch them for at least 4 minutes. Let the grill do its thing and build that crust.
  5. 5Flip the burgers once, then cook another 3 to 4 minutes for medium doneness. In the last minute of cooking, lay a slice of cheese on each patty and close the grill lid so it melts perfectly.
  6. 6While the burgers finish, toast your buns cut-side down directly on the grill for about 30 seconds — this step takes two seconds and makes a huge difference. Remove everything from the grill, build your burgers with your favorite toppings, and serve immediately.
💡 Improv Tip

The sofrito is the secret weapon here — it adds this background depth that people will taste but not be able to place, and they will absolutely ask you what you did different. If you don't have sofrito, a tablespoon of finely minced green bell pepper and cilantro blended together gets you close. Either way, do not skip the thumb indent in the patty — it sounds like a small thing but it genuinely changes the shape and cook on the burger.
