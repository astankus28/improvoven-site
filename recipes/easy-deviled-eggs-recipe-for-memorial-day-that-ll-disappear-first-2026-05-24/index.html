Easy Deviled Eggs Recipe for Memorial Day That'll Disappear First

Published May 24, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you need an easy deviled eggs recipe for Memorial Day, this is the one to bookmark — creamy, tangy, with a little smoky kick that makes people think you did something fancy. These come together with stuff you already have in the fridge, and they travel well to the cookout without falling apart on you. Trust me, make a double batch because they never last.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
12 mins
Total
27 mins
Serves
12 deviled egg halves

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon yellow mustard
  • 1 teaspoon white vinegar or apple cider vinegar
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon onion powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Paprika for topping (smoked paprika if you have it)
  • Optional: a few dashes of hot sauce like Tabasco or Cholula
  • Optional: fresh chives or parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. 1Place your eggs in a single layer in a saucepan and cover with cold water by about an inch. Bring to a full boil over medium-high heat, then immediately cut the heat, cover the pot, and let the eggs sit for exactly 11 minutes. This is the move for perfectly cooked yolks with no gray ring.
  2. 2While the eggs are resting, fill a big bowl with ice water. When the 11 minutes are up, transfer the eggs straight into the ice bath and let them chill for at least 5 minutes. This stops the cooking and makes peeling so much easier — don't skip it.
  3. 3Peel your eggs under a thin stream of running water if the shells are being stubborn. Slice each egg in half lengthwise and pop the yolks out into a bowl. Set the whites on a plate or tray.
  4. 4Mash the yolks with a fork until they're crumbly, then add the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, and hot sauce if you're using it. Mix everything together until it's smooth and creamy. Taste it — this is your moment to adjust. A little more salt? Another dash of hot sauce? You're the chef.
  5. 5Spoon or pipe the filling back into the egg white halves. A zip-lock bag with the corner snipped off works perfectly if you don't have a piping bag — no fancy tools needed.
  6. 6Dust each egg with paprika and add a little sprinkle of chives or parsley if you've got them. Smoked paprika gives it that extra something that makes people ask what your secret is. Refrigerate until you're ready to go and serve cold.
💡 Improv Tip

Make these the night before and store them covered in the fridge — they actually taste better the next day once the flavors settle in together. For the cookout, keep them in a container nested over a tray of ice so they stay cold and safe in the Miami heat. And if your yolks come out a little dry, just add a tiny splash more mayo and vinegar until it loosens up to that creamy, silky texture you want.
