Easy Lemon Butter Cod Recipe (Ready in 20 Minutes)

Published May 11, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy lemon butter cod recipe is the kind of weeknight miracle that makes you feel like you actually have your life together — flaky, golden fish in a bright, garlicky butter sauce with barely any effort. Cod is one of the most forgiving fish out there, so even if you're nervous about cooking seafood, this one's got your back. Mira, once you taste that butter melting into the lemon juice over that perfectly seared fillet, you're going to wonder why you ever ordered takeout.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 cod fillets (about 6 oz each, fresh or thawed from frozen)
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 large lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional but recommended)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
  • Lemon slices for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Pull your cod fillets out of the fridge about 10 minutes before cooking so they come closer to room temperature — this helps them cook evenly and get that beautiful golden crust instead of steaming in the pan. Pat them completely dry with paper towels. This step is non-negotiable, mija. Dry fish = golden fish.
  2. 2Mix together the paprika, onion powder, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes in a small bowl. Sprinkle the seasoning blend evenly over both sides of each fillet, pressing gently so it sticks.
  3. 3Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add 1 tablespoon of butter and the olive oil. Using both fat together raises the smoke point so your butter doesn't burn before the fish gets its color.
  4. 4Once the butter is melted and the pan is hot — you should see it shimmer — carefully lay the cod fillets in the pan. Do not crowd them. Cook undisturbed for 3 to 4 minutes until the bottom is golden and the fish releases easily from the pan. Flip gently and cook another 2 to 3 minutes on the other side. The fish is done when it flakes easily with a fork and is opaque all the way through. Transfer to a plate.
  5. 5Turn the heat down to medium-low. Add the remaining 2 tablespoons of butter to the same pan. Once it melts, add the minced garlic and cook for about 60 seconds, stirring constantly, until fragrant. Don't walk away here — garlic goes from golden to burnt real fast.
  6. 6Add the lemon juice and lemon zest to the garlic butter. Stir everything together and let it bubble for about 30 seconds. You're going to smell this and immediately feel like a chef.
  7. 7Spoon that gorgeous lemon butter sauce generously over each fillet. Finish with fresh chopped parsley and a few lemon slices on the side. Serve immediately over white rice, with tostones, or alongside a simple green salad.
💡 Improv Tip

Frozen cod works beautifully here and it's almost always cheaper than fresh — just make sure it's fully thawed in the fridge overnight and pressed super dry before it hits the pan. If your fillets are thin on one end and thick on the other (which cod usually is), fold the thin end under itself so the whole piece cooks at the same rate. Little tricks like that are what separate a good cook from a great one.
