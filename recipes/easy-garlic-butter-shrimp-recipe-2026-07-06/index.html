Easy Garlic Butter Shrimp Recipe

Published July 6, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy garlic butter shrimp recipe is the kind of thing you throw together on a Tuesday when you need dinner to feel fancy but your wallet says otherwise. We're talking juicy shrimp swimming in a garlicky, buttery sauce with a little kick — ready in under 20 minutes and made almost entirely from stuff you probably already have. In Miami we don't wait for a special occasion to eat good, mija.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
10 mins
Total
20 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb medium or large shrimp, peeled and deveined (fresh or thawed from frozen)
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley or cilantro, roughly chopped
  • Optional: crusty bread, white rice, or pasta for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your shrimp completely dry with paper towels — this is the step most people skip and then wonder why their shrimp steam instead of sear. Dry shrimp = golden shrimp. Season them with the smoked paprika, cumin, salt, and black pepper and toss to coat evenly.
  2. 2Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add 1 tablespoon of butter and the olive oil. When the butter starts to foam and shimmer, you're ready. Add the shrimp in a single layer — don't crowd them or they'll just steam each other. Cook for about 1 to 2 minutes per side until they curl into a C shape and turn pink. Pull them out and set aside on a plate.
  3. 3Turn the heat down to medium and add the remaining 3 tablespoons of butter to the same pan. Once it melts, add the minced garlic and red pepper flakes. Stir constantly and let it cook for about 60 to 90 seconds — you want it fragrant and just barely golden, not brown. The moment it smells incredible is the moment you move on.
  4. 4Add the shrimp back into the pan and toss everything together in that garlic butter sauce. Squeeze the lime juice right over the top, give it one final toss, and taste for salt. Finish with the chopped parsley or cilantro and pull it off the heat immediately.
  5. 5Serve straight from the pan over white rice, with crusty bread to soak up that sauce, or tossed into pasta. Either way, someone at the table is going to ask you for this recipe.
💡 Improv Tip

Frozen shrimp is 100% the move here — it's cheaper, it's already cleaned half the time, and it thaws in about 15 minutes under cold running water. Don't let anyone make you feel some kind of way about using frozen shrimp. The secret to garlic butter shrimp isn't fresh shrimp, it's dry shrimp and hot pan. That's it.
