Simple Meal Prep Salad Recipes: The Only Base You Need All Week

Published June 12, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been scrolling for simple meal prep salad recipes that actually hold up in the fridge without turning into sad, soggy mush — this is your answer. We're building a hearty black bean and grain salad with a zippy lime-cumin dressing that gets better every single day it sits. Think Cuban pantry meets Miami lunch break, and your future self is going to be so grateful.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dry brown rice or farro (or 2 cups leftover cooked grain)
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (15 oz) corn, drained (or 1 cup frozen corn, thawed)
  • 1 large red bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lime juice (about 2 limes)
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Optional: 1 avocado, diced (add fresh each day)

Instructions

  1. 1Cook your grain according to package directions. Brown rice usually takes about 20 minutes, farro about 25. Once it's done, spread it out on a baking sheet or large plate and let it cool completely — this is the move that keeps your salad from getting mushy. Do not skip this step, I'm serious.
  2. 2While the grain cools, whisk together your dressing in a small bowl or jar: olive oil, lime juice, cumin, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Give it a taste — it should be bright, a little smoky, and make you want to drink it. Adjust lime or salt as needed.
  3. 3In a large mixing bowl, combine the cooled grain, black beans, corn, red bell pepper, red onion, cherry tomatoes, and cilantro. Pour the dressing over everything and toss it well so every single thing gets coated.
  4. 4Divide the salad evenly into 4 airtight containers. This is your meal prep done. Seal them up and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. The flavors are going to keep developing and honestly, day 3 hits different.
  5. 5When you're ready to eat, pull a container out, let it sit for a few minutes to take the chill off, and if you're adding avocado, dice it fresh right before eating. You can serve this as-is, over a bed of greens, stuffed into a wrap, or topped with a fried egg for breakfast — it works for literally any meal.
💡 Improv Tip

The secret to a meal prep salad that doesn't turn tragic by Wednesday? Cool your grains all the way before dressing them. Hot grains steam everything around them and you end up with a warm, wilted situation. Give it 15-20 minutes on the counter or throw it in the fridge for a quick cool-down. Also, if you have a little leftover mango or pineapple hanging out in your fridge — dice it in. That sweet-savory thing is very Miami and it slaps.
