Simple Freezer Meal: Sofrito Black Bean and Rice Bake

Published July 3, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been looking for simple freezer meal recipes that actually taste like someone's abuela made them, this sofrito black bean and rice bake is about to change your whole week. It comes together in under 30 minutes, freezes beautifully, and reheats like a dream — no sad, mushy leftovers here. This is the kind of meal that makes you feel like you have your life together even on the craziest Tuesday.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, with juices
  • 1 cup frozen corn kernels
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1/2 cup sofrito (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella or Colby-Jack cheese
  • Fresh cilantro for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 375°F. Grab a large oven-safe baking dish — a 9x13 works perfectly here.
  2. 2Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the sofrito and let it cook for about 2 to 3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it smells incredible and deepens in color. That right there is your flavor base — don't skip this step.
  3. 3Add the cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper directly into the sofrito and stir everything together for about 30 seconds. You're basically blooming those spices in the oil and sofrito, and it makes a huge difference.
  4. 4Pour the broth, diced tomatoes with their juices, black beans, frozen corn, and the sofrito-spice mixture into your baking dish. Give it a good stir to combine everything evenly.
  5. 5Add the dry rice and stir again so the rice gets coated and distributed throughout the liquid. Make sure the rice is mostly submerged — press it down gently with a spoon if needed.
  6. 6Cover the dish tightly with aluminum foil and bake for 45 minutes. The key word here is tightly — you want to trap all that steam so the rice cooks through properly.
  7. 7Remove the foil, sprinkle the shredded cheese evenly over the top, and pop it back in the oven uncovered for another 10 minutes until the cheese is melted and just starting to get those golden bubbly spots.
  8. 8Let it rest for 5 minutes before serving. This helps everything set up so it scoops out cleanly instead of falling apart. Top with fresh cilantro if you've got it.
  9. 9To freeze: Let the bake cool completely, then portion it into individual airtight freezer containers or divide the whole dish into two foil pans before baking — freeze one, bake one. Label with the date. It keeps well for up to 3 months. To reheat, thaw overnight in the fridge and warm in the oven at 350°F covered with foil for about 20 minutes, or microwave individual portions for 2 to 3 minutes.
💡 Improv Tip

The real secret to this freezing like a champ is using long-grain white rice — it holds its texture way better than short-grain after freezing and reheating. Brown rice needs more liquid and time, so if you swap it in, add an extra half cup of broth and tack on about 15 more minutes of covered bake time. And listen — if your grocery store sofrito looks a little sad, grab a jar of Goya. It's like a dollar fifty and it does the heavy lifting for you.
