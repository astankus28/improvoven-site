Simple Labor Day Dessert Recipe: Easy No-Bake Strawberry Icebox Cake

Published September 5, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you need a simple Labor Day dessert recipe that's easy enough to make the night before and impressive enough to actually wow your crowd, this no-bake strawberry icebox cake is your answer. It comes together with just a handful of pantry and fridge staples — graham crackers, whipped cream, and fresh strawberries — and does all its magic in the refrigerator while you go enjoy the holiday. No oven, no stress, no sad store-bought sheet cake.

Prep
20 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
4 hrs 20 mins (includes chilling)
Serves
8

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs fresh strawberries, hulled and sliced
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 cups heavy whipping cream, cold
  • 3 tablespoons powdered sugar
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 14–16 full graham cracker sheets
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest (optional but really good)

Instructions

  1. 1Toss the sliced strawberries with the granulated sugar and lemon zest if using. Let them sit in a bowl for 10–15 minutes — this pulls out their juice and makes them extra saucy and sweet. Set a small handful aside for the top.
  2. 2In a large bowl, beat the cold heavy cream with the powdered sugar using a hand mixer or stand mixer until stiff peaks form. In a separate bowl, beat the softened cream cheese with the vanilla until smooth and fluffy. Gently fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese in two or three additions until fully combined and airy. Don't deflate it — you want it light.
  3. 3Spread a thin layer of the cream mixture on the bottom of a 9x13 baking dish — just enough to anchor the first layer of crackers.
  4. 4Lay graham crackers in a single layer over the cream, breaking pieces as needed to cover the whole bottom of the dish.
  5. 5Spread about one-third of the cream mixture over the crackers, then layer on about one-third of the macerated strawberries with some of their juices. Those juices are what soften the crackers into cake-like layers.
  6. 6Repeat with another layer of graham crackers, another third of the cream, and another third of the strawberries. Then do one final layer: crackers, the remaining cream spread all the way to the edges.
  7. 7Top with the reserved fresh strawberry slices arranged however you like — a simple fan, a grid, or just scattered rustic-style. It will look great either way.
  8. 8Cover tightly with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or overnight. The longer it chills, the more the crackers soften into those dreamy, cake-like layers. Overnight is genuinely better.
  9. 9Slice into squares with a sharp knife, wipe the blade between cuts for clean edges, and serve cold straight from the dish.
💡 Improv Tip

The overnight chill is not optional if you want those soft, cake-like layers — 4 hours is the minimum but morning-of prep for a Labor Day cookout is genuinely the move here. Also, don't skip macerating the strawberries: that syrupy juice is what soaks into the crackers and transforms this from 'a stack of crackers' into something that tastes like it took real effort.
