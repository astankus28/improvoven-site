Simple Cucumber Dill Salad Recipe (Quick, Crisp & Totally Refreshing)

Published October 31, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple cucumber dill salad recipe comes together in about 10 minutes flat — no cooking, no fuss, just pure, crisp refreshment. It's the kind of side dish that makes everything feel a little more put-together, whether you're throwing it next to grilled fish, tucking it into a pita, or honestly just eating it straight from the bowl. With a little Miami sunshine in the mix — a splash of lime alongside the classic vinegar — this one's got a brightness that'll have people asking for the recipe.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
10 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 large English cucumbers (or 4 Persian cucumbers), thinly sliced
  • 1/2 small red onion, very thinly sliced
  • 3 tablespoons fresh dill, roughly chopped (or 1 tablespoon dried dill in a pinch)
  • 3 tablespoons white wine vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon granulated sugar (or honey)
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Optional: 2 tablespoons sour cream or plain Greek yogurt for a creamy version

Instructions

  1. 1Slice your cucumbers as thin as you can — a mandoline is your best friend here, but a sharp knife and a little patience works just fine. Thin slices mean more surface area to soak up that dressing, and the texture stays beautifully delicate instead of chunky.
  2. 2Slice the red onion paper-thin and toss it into a small bowl of cold water. Let it sit for 5 minutes while you prep everything else — this pulls out the harsh bite and leaves you with a mellow, almost sweet onion flavor that plays nicely with the dill.
  3. 3In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the white wine vinegar, lime juice, olive oil, sugar, salt, pepper, and garlic powder until the sugar and salt dissolve. Give it a taste — it should be tangy, a little bright, and just barely sweet.
  4. 4Drain the red onion and pat it dry. Add the sliced cucumbers, red onion, and fresh dill to the bowl with the dressing. Toss everything together gently so every cucumber slice gets coated.
  5. 5If you're going the creamy route, stir in the sour cream or Greek yogurt now and toss again until everything is evenly coated. This turns it into something closer to a tzatziki-style salad — incredible next to grilled chicken or lamb.
  6. 6Let the salad sit for at least 5 minutes before serving so the cucumbers can absorb the dressing and everything gets happy together. Taste one more time and adjust salt or lime if needed. Serve chilled or at room temperature — both are great.
💡 Improv Tip

The biggest secret to a great cucumber salad is salting your cucumbers first if you have an extra 15 minutes — toss the slices with a pinch of salt, let them sit in a colander, then pat them dry before dressing. It pulls out excess water so your dressing doesn't get diluted and watery by the time it hits the table. If you're in a rush though, skip it — this salad is genuinely great either way.
