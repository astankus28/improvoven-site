Quick Pork Chop Recipe (Easy Pan Sauce, Ready in 25 Minutes)

Published October 8, 2026 · Improv Oven

This quick pork chop recipe is exactly what easy weeknight dinners should look like — one skillet, pantry staples, and a glossy pan sauce that makes it taste like you actually tried. Bone-in chops get a hard sear, then you scrape up all those gorgeous brown bits with a little broth, mustard, and garlic to make a sauce that does the heavy lifting. No fancy techniques, no long marinade — just real, satisfying food on the table fast.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in pork chops (about 3/4 inch thick)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • Fresh parsley, chopped (optional, for serving)

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your pork chops completely dry with paper towels — this is the single biggest thing you can do for a good sear. Moisture is the enemy of a golden crust. Mix the garlic powder, smoked paprika, onion powder, salt, and pepper in a small bowl, then rub it all over both sides of each chop.
  2. 2Heat a large cast iron or stainless steel skillet over medium-high heat. Add the olive oil and let it shimmer — about 1 minute. You want the pan genuinely hot before the pork goes in.
  3. 3Add the pork chops in a single layer. Don't move them. Let them sear undisturbed for 4 to 5 minutes until a deep golden-brown crust forms on the bottom. Flip once and cook another 3 to 4 minutes. For 3/4-inch bone-in chops, you're looking for an internal temperature of 145°F. Pull them out and rest them on a plate tented loosely with foil.
  4. 4Turn the heat down to medium. Add the minced garlic to the same skillet and stir for about 30 seconds until fragrant — just long enough to soften it without burning. Pour in the chicken broth and scrape up every bit of the browned bits stuck to the bottom. Those bits are flavor gold.
  5. 5Stir in the Dijon mustard and Worcestershire sauce. Let the sauce simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until it reduces by about a third and thickens slightly. Remove from heat and swirl in the butter until it melts into the sauce and gives it a glossy finish.
  6. 6Return the pork chops to the pan and spoon the sauce over them. Let them sit in the sauce for about a minute to soak up that flavor, then plate them up. Spoon any extra sauce over the top, scatter some parsley if you've got it, and serve immediately.
💡 Improv Tip

Thickness matters more than you think here — thin chops (under 1/2 inch) will overcook before you get any real crust. If that's what you've got, drop the sear time to 2 to 3 minutes per side and watch the temp closely. Bone-in chops are almost always cheaper per pound than boneless too, so they're the Improv Oven move all the way.
