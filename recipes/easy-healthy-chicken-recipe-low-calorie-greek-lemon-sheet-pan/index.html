Easy Healthy Chicken Recipe: Low-Calorie Greek Lemon Sheet Pan

Published September 8, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're looking for an easy healthy chicken recipe that's actually low calorie and doesn't taste like sad diet food, this Greek lemon sheet pan is your answer. Thin-cut chicken breasts roast alongside cherry tomatoes, zucchini, and olives in a punchy lemon-herb marinade — the whole thing comes together on one pan in about 30 minutes. It's bright, satisfying, and genuinely something you'll want to make again on a Tuesday night.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
22 mins
Total
32 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts, sliced thin (about ½ inch)
  • 1 medium zucchini, sliced into half-moons
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • ⅓ cup kalamata olives, pitted and halved
  • ½ red onion, sliced thin
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Juice of 1 large lemon (about 3 tablespoons)
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • ½ teaspoon dried thyme
  • ½ teaspoon paprika
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons crumbled feta cheese (optional but worth it)
  • Fresh parsley or fresh dill for garnish

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 425°F and line a large sheet pan with parchment paper or foil. A hot oven is the secret to getting good color on the chicken instead of steaming it.
  2. 2In a large bowl, whisk together the olive oil, minced garlic, lemon juice, oregano, thyme, paprika, salt, and pepper. Add the sliced chicken and toss to coat every piece well. Let it sit for 5 minutes while you prep the vegetables — that's enough time for the flavors to start soaking in.
  3. 3Add the zucchini, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and olives to the same bowl and toss everything together so the vegetables pick up the leftover marinade. You want all of it seasoned, not just the chicken.
  4. 4Spread the chicken pieces out in a single layer on one half of the sheet pan. Arrange the vegetables on the other half, making sure nothing is piled on top of itself. Crowding kills caramelization — give everything room to breathe.
  5. 5Roast at 425°F for 18 to 22 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through (internal temp 165°F) and the edges have a little color. The tomatoes should be blistered and jammy, and the zucchini should be tender with some golden edges.
  6. 6Pull the pan out, scatter feta over the top if using, and finish with a shower of fresh parsley or dill and a final squeeze of lemon if you have one left. Serve straight from the pan — no extra dishes needed.
💡 Improv Tip

Slicing the chicken thin is the biggest move here. Thick chicken breasts on a sheet pan take forever and dry out before the vegetables are done. Thin cuts mean everything finishes at the same time, and you get better browning too. If your chicken breasts are thick, just butterfly them or pound them between two pieces of plastic wrap before slicing.
