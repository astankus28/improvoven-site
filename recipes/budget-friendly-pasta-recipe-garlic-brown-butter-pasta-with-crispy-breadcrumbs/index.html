Budget Friendly Pasta Recipe: Garlic Brown Butter Pasta with Crispy Breadcrumbs

Published November 6, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're looking for budget friendly pasta recipes that actually taste like you tried, this one's going to become a weeknight staple. It's built on pantry staples — pasta, butter, garlic, and stale bread — but the combination is straight-up Italian comfort food magic. The toasted breadcrumbs on top do something almost unfair to a simple bowl of pasta.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti or linguine
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
  • 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional but recommended)
  • 1 cup coarse breadcrumbs (from stale bread or store-bought panko)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more for pasta water
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/3 cup freshly grated Parmesan (or Pecorino), plus more for serving
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped (optional)
  • Zest of 1 lemon (optional but great)

Instructions

  1. 1Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to package directions until just al dente. Before you drain it, scoop out about 1 cup of that starchy pasta water and set it aside — this is your secret weapon for the sauce.
  2. 2While the pasta cooks, heat a large skillet over medium heat and add the olive oil plus 1 tablespoon of the butter. Once it shimmers, add the breadcrumbs and a pinch of salt. Toast them, stirring frequently, for 3 to 4 minutes until they're deeply golden and smell nutty. Transfer to a bowl and set aside — don't leave them in the pan or they'll keep cooking.
  3. 3In that same skillet, melt the remaining 3 tablespoons of butter over medium-low heat. Add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes. Let the garlic slowly turn golden and fragrant, about 2 to 3 minutes. Watch it closely — you want golden, not burned. The moment it smells nutty and looks lightly toasted, you're there.
  4. 4Add the drained pasta directly to the skillet with the garlic brown butter. Toss it all together over low heat. Add a splash of the reserved pasta water (start with 1/4 cup) and keep tossing until a loose, glossy sauce coats every strand. Add more pasta water as needed — it comes together fast.
  5. 5Remove the pan from heat and stir in the Parmesan, lemon zest if using, and black pepper. Taste and adjust salt. Divide into bowls, pile on the crispy breadcrumbs, scatter some fresh parsley over the top, and hit it with a little extra Parmesan. Serve immediately while those breadcrumbs are still crunchy.
💡 Improv Tip

Stale bread is genuinely better here than fresh — it toasts drier and crunchier. Tear or blitz a few slices of whatever bread is going stale on your counter. No stale bread? Panko from a box works great and costs almost nothing. Either way, don't skip the breadcrumbs — they turn this from 'plain buttered pasta' into something that feels intentional and restaurant-worthy.
