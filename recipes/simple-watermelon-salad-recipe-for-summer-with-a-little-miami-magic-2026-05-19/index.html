Simple Watermelon Salad Recipe for Summer (With a Little Miami Magic)

Published May 19, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple watermelon salad recipe is the one you're going to make every weekend this summer — it's fresh, it takes 10 minutes, and it disappears fast. We're throwing in some cotija cheese, mint, and a little chili-lime kick because that's just how we do it down here. No cooking, no fuss, just vibes and flavor.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
10 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 6 cups seedless watermelon, cut into 1-inch cubes (about half a small watermelon)
  • 1/2 small red onion, very thinly sliced
  • 1/3 cup crumbled cotija cheese (feta works great too)
  • 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves, torn
  • 1 jalapeño, thinly sliced (optional but highly recommended)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice (about 2 limes)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder or Tajín
  • Pinch of salt
  • Pinch of black pepper

Instructions

  1. 1Slice your watermelon into roughly 1-inch cubes and spread them out on a large plate or wide shallow bowl — you want everything to lay kind of flat so every bite gets dressed evenly.
  2. 2Soak your sliced red onion in cold water for 5 minutes while you prep everything else. This takes the sharp bite off and makes the onion way more pleasant in a fresh salad. Drain and pat dry.
  3. 3Scatter the red onion, cotija cheese, torn mint, and jalapeño slices over the watermelon.
  4. 4In a small bowl or just directly over the salad, drizzle the lime juice and olive oil. Sprinkle the chili powder or Tajín, salt, and pepper over the top.
  5. 5Give it one gentle toss — or honestly just serve it as-is and let people mix it themselves at the table. Eat immediately while it's cold and the watermelon is still firm.
💡 Improv Tip

The secret to this salad not getting watery and sad is eating it right after you dress it — watermelon releases juice fast once it hits acid and salt. If you're bringing it to a cookout, keep everything prepped separately in baggies and assemble it on-site like a pro. Also, taste your watermelon first. If it's super sweet, add a little extra lime. If it's on the bland side, a tiny extra pinch of salt will wake it right up.
