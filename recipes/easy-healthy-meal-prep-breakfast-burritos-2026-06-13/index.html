Easy Healthy Meal Prep Breakfast Burritos

Published June 13, 2026 · Improv Oven

These easy healthy meal prep breakfast burritos are the cure for your Monday morning chaos — make a batch on Sunday and you're set for the whole week. We're talking fluffy eggs, black beans, roasted peppers, and a little sofrito magic all wrapped up tight and ready to grab from the freezer. Budget-friendly, packed with protein, and honestly way better than anything you'd find at a drive-through.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 6 large flour tortillas (burrito size)
  • 8 large eggs
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup frozen diced peppers and onions blend
  • 1/2 cup frozen corn
  • 1/2 cup shredded Mexican cheese blend
  • 2 tablespoons jarred sofrito
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Hot sauce for serving (optional but strongly encouraged)

Instructions

  1. 1Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the frozen pepper and onion blend and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until softened and starting to get a little color. Add the frozen corn and cook another 2 minutes.
  2. 2Push the veggies to one side of the pan and add the remaining tablespoon of olive oil to the empty side. Add the sofrito and let it sizzle for about 30 seconds — you'll smell it and you'll know it's right. Stir everything together.
  3. 3Add the drained black beans to the pan along with the cumin, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine and cook for 2 minutes so the beans soak up all that flavor. Transfer the veggie and bean mixture to a bowl and set aside.
  4. 4Crack the eggs into a bowl, season with a pinch of salt and pepper, and whisk well. Wipe out the skillet, set it back over medium-low heat, and add a tiny drizzle of olive oil. Pour in the eggs and scramble them low and slow — pull them off the heat just before they look fully done because they'll keep cooking. You want them creamy, not rubbery.
  5. 5Warm your tortillas one at a time in a dry pan or wrap them in a damp paper towel and microwave for 20 seconds — this keeps them from cracking when you roll.
  6. 6To assemble, lay a tortilla flat and add a scoop of the bean mixture slightly below the center, then top with a scoop of scrambled eggs and a sprinkle of shredded cheese. Don't overfill or the burrito will fight you when you try to roll it.
  7. 7Fold the sides in first, then roll the bottom up and over the filling, tucking it tight as you go. Place seam-side down on a baking sheet. Repeat with all six burritos.
  8. 8To meal prep for the week: let the assembled burritos cool completely, then wrap each one tightly in aluminum foil. Store in the fridge for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 2 months. To reheat from the fridge, microwave wrapped in a damp paper towel for 90 seconds to 2 minutes. From frozen, thaw overnight in the fridge or microwave on defrost for 2 minutes then full power for 1 minute.
  9. 9Serve with hot sauce, a spoonful of salsa, or a dollop of Greek yogurt instead of sour cream if you want to keep it on the lighter side.
💡 Improv Tip

The freezer is your best friend here — double the batch and you've got two weeks of breakfasts handled. The real secret though is pulling those eggs off the heat while they still look just a little underdone. By the time they cool and get reheated, they'll be perfectly creamy instead of that sad rubbery texture that makes people give up on meal prep forever.
