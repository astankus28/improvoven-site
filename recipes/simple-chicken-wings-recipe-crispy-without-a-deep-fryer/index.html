Simple Chicken Wings Recipe — Crispy Without a Deep Fryer

Published July 17, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple chicken wings recipe delivers genuinely crispy skin using one genius pantry trick — baking powder — and your oven does all the heavy lifting. No hot oil splattering everywhere, no fancy equipment, just wings that shatter when you bite into them. We're keeping the seasoning classic and bold so you can sauce them any direction you want.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
50 mins
Total
1 hr
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken wings, split into flats and drums (tips removed or saved for stock)
  • 1 tbsp baking powder (aluminum-free — this is the crispy secret)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper (optional, for heat)
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil (vegetable or avocado oil)

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your wings completely dry with paper towels — and we mean really dry. Moisture is the enemy of crispiness, so press firmly on every surface. This single step makes a huge difference.
  2. 2Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large rimmed baking sheet with foil, then set a wire rack on top. If you don't have a rack, just use the foil-lined pan — the wings will still get crispy, just flip them halfway.
  3. 3In a large bowl, toss the dried wings with the oil until lightly coated. Add the baking powder, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, salt, black pepper, and cayenne. Toss everything together until every wing is evenly coated. The baking powder will look like a light dusting — that's exactly right.
  4. 4Arrange the wings in a single layer on the rack, skin-side up. Make sure they're not touching each other — crowding steams them instead of roasting them, and we want crispy, not sad and soggy.
  5. 5Bake at 425°F for 25 minutes, then flip the wings and crank the oven up to 450°F. Bake for another 20-25 minutes until the skin is deeply golden, blistered in spots, and audibly crispy when you tap it.
  6. 6Pull them out and let them rest for 5 minutes before tossing in your sauce of choice — buffalo, honey garlic, lemon pepper butter, whatever you're feeling. Or eat them exactly as is. They're that good.
💡 Improv Tip

The baking powder trick works because it raises the pH of the skin, helping it brown faster and bubble up into those craggy, crunchy textures you see at good wing spots. One non-negotiable though: make sure you're using BAKING POWDER, not baking soda — baking soda will make your wings taste metallic and weird. Been there. Learn from my mistakes.
