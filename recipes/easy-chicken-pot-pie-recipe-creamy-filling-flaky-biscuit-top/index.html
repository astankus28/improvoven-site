Easy Chicken Pot Pie Recipe (Creamy Filling, Flaky Biscuit Top)

Published July 29, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy chicken pot pie recipe skips the fussy double crust and uses store-bought biscuits on top — all the cozy, creamy comfort with about half the effort. It comes together in one skillet and bakes in under 30 minutes, which means it's genuinely doable on a weeknight. Whether you're using leftover rotisserie chicken or a couple of raw thighs you need to use up, this one's going to become a regular.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
30 mins
Total
45 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 2 medium carrots, peeled and diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 3/4 cup whole milk (or half-and-half)
  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded or chopped (rotisserie works great)
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves (or 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme)
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 can (16 oz) refrigerated biscuit dough (8 biscuits)
  • 1 egg, beaten (for egg wash)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 375°F. Get an oven-safe 10 or 12-inch skillet ready — cast iron is perfect here, but any oven-safe skillet works.
  2. 2Melt the butter in your skillet over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery and cook for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables have softened and the onion is translucent. Add the garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
  3. 3Sprinkle the flour over the vegetables and stir everything together so the flour coats the veggies. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring constantly — this cooks out the raw flour taste and is the key to a silky filling, not a pasty one.
  4. 4Pour in the chicken broth while stirring, scraping up any bits from the bottom of the pan. Add the milk and stir to combine. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer over medium heat, stirring frequently, until the sauce thickens — about 3 to 4 minutes.
  5. 5Stir in the shredded chicken, frozen peas, thyme, and onion powder. Season generously with salt and black pepper. Taste it — the filling should be well-seasoned because the biscuits on top are fairly neutral. Remove from heat.
  6. 6Arrange the biscuits directly on top of the filling in a single layer. They won't cover every inch perfectly, and that's totally fine — the gaps let steam escape and give you those golden, crispy edges. Brush the tops of the biscuits with the beaten egg.
  7. 7Transfer the skillet to the oven and bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until the biscuits are deep golden brown and cooked through. Let the pot pie rest for 5 minutes before serving — the filling will thicken slightly as it sits.
💡 Improv Tip

Rotisserie chicken is the move here — one bird gives you plenty of meat and you can use the carcass for broth later. But if you're starting from raw, just simmer two boneless chicken thighs in salted water for about 15 minutes, shred them, and you're good to go. Also: don't skip the egg wash. It's the difference between pale biscuits and the gorgeous golden top you actually want.
