Homemade Coleslaw Recipe (Easy, Creamy, Ready in 10 Minutes)

Published September 11, 2026 · Improv Oven

This homemade coleslaw recipe is so easy you'll never buy the bagged kit again — just a simple creamy dressing, crunchy cabbage, and a few pantry staples that come together in minutes. It's the kind of side dish that makes everything around it taste better, whether that's a backyard burger, a pulled pork sandwich, or just something you needed to use up in the fridge. Cool, tangy, a little sweet — this is the coleslaw that becomes your default.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
10 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 4 cups green cabbage, thinly shredded (about half a small head)
  • 1 cup purple cabbage, thinly shredded
  • 1 large carrot, peeled and grated
  • 3 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 teaspoon celery seed
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. 1Shred your cabbage as thin as you can — a sharp knife, a mandoline, or even the shredding disc on a food processor all work great. Aim for long, delicate ribbons rather than chunky pieces. Add both cabbages and the grated carrot to a large mixing bowl.
  2. 2In a separate small bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, apple cider vinegar, sugar, Dijon mustard, celery seed, salt, and black pepper until completely smooth. Taste it — it should be creamy, tangy, and just barely sweet. Adjust from there.
  3. 3Pour the dressing over the cabbage and carrot mixture. Add the sliced green onions. Toss everything together really well, making sure every shred gets coated. Don't be shy — get in there with tongs or clean hands.
  4. 4Give it a final taste and adjust salt, vinegar, or sugar to your liking. For the best results, cover and refrigerate for at least 20 to 30 minutes before serving. The cabbage will soften slightly and the flavors will mellow and come together beautifully. Serve cold.
💡 Improv Tip

Salting the cabbage first is your secret weapon — toss the shredded cabbage with a pinch of salt, let it sit in a colander for 15 minutes, then squeeze out the excess water before mixing. It sounds fussy but it takes two minutes and it means your coleslaw won't turn into a puddle at the bottom of the bowl an hour later. Game-changer for make-ahead situations.
