Poach These Pork Tenderloin Slices: A Budget Recipe Worth Bookmarking

Published November 17, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've never thought to poach these pork tenderloin slices, this budget recipe is about to change your weeknight game completely. Gently simmered in a ginger-soy broth until silky and tender, then served over rice with a punchy scallion drizzle — it's the kind of dish that tastes like you spent way more than you did. Pork tenderloin is one of the most underrated budget proteins out there, and this method makes it practically foolproof.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs pork tenderloin
  • 4 cups water
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar (or apple cider vinegar)
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 1-inch piece fresh ginger, sliced (or 1/2 tsp ground ginger)
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 3 scallions, divided — 2 whole for poaching, 1 thinly sliced for garnish
  • 2 cups white or jasmine rice, cooked for serving
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds (optional garnish)

Instructions

  1. 1In a medium saucepan or deep skillet, combine the water, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sugar, smashed garlic, sliced ginger, black pepper, red pepper flakes if using, and the 2 whole scallions. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce to a gentle simmer — you want small bubbles, not a rolling boil.
  2. 2Pat the pork tenderloin dry and lower it carefully into the poaching liquid. The liquid should come at least halfway up the pork; add a splash more water if needed. Cover with a lid and poach on low-medium heat for 18 to 22 minutes, flipping once halfway through, until the thickest part reaches an internal temperature of 145°F. Don't rush it — a gentle simmer keeps the pork juicy and tender.
  3. 3While the pork poaches, make your scallion oil: heat the vegetable oil in a small skillet or saucepan until it just starts to shimmer — almost smoking. Place your thinly sliced scallions in a small heatproof bowl, pour the hot oil directly over them, and stir. It'll sizzle dramatically. Add a pinch of salt. That's your finishing sauce.
  4. 4Remove the pork from the poaching liquid and let it rest on a cutting board for 5 minutes. Don't skip the rest — it keeps the juices inside. Meanwhile, ladle about half a cup of the poaching broth and set it aside; it's packed with flavor and great for spooning over your rice.
  5. 5Slice the pork tenderloin into thin medallions, about half an inch thick. Serve over bowls of cooked rice, drizzle with the scallion oil, spoon over a little of the reserved broth, and finish with sesame seeds if you have them. Dinner is done.
💡 Improv Tip

The poaching liquid is basically a light, savory broth by the time you're done — don't dump it. Freeze what's left and use it as a base for noodle soup, a quick ramen broth, or cooking your next pot of rice in. Free flavor is free flavor.
