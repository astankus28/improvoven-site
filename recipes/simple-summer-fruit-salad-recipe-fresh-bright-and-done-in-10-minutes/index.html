Simple Summer Fruit Salad Recipe (Fresh, Bright, and Done in 10 Minutes)

Published September 2, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple summer fruit salad recipe is the kind of thing you make when the fruit bowl is overflowing and you want something that feels a little more special than just eating a peach over the sink. It comes together in about 10 minutes, uses whatever fruit looks good right now, and gets a little lift from a honey-lime dressing and fresh mint that makes everyone think you tried harder than you did. Bring it to a cookout, serve it at brunch, or eat it straight from the bowl for breakfast — it works for basically any occasion.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
10 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2 cups strawberries, hulled and halved
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • 2 peaches or nectarines, pitted and sliced
  • 1 cup seedless watermelon, cubed
  • 1 cup green or red grapes, halved
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice (about 1 lime)
  • 1/2 teaspoon lime zest
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 tablespoons fresh mint leaves, torn or thinly sliced

Instructions

  1. 1Wash and prep all your fruit first — hull and halve the strawberries, slice the peaches, cube the watermelon, and halve the grapes if using. Add everything to a large mixing bowl.
  2. 2In a small bowl, whisk together the honey, lime juice, lime zest, vanilla extract, and pinch of salt until the honey is fully dissolved and the dressing looks smooth and glossy.
  3. 3Pour the honey-lime dressing over the fruit and gently toss everything together with a large spoon or spatula. You want each piece coated without bruising the softer fruit — go slow and easy.
  4. 4Scatter the torn fresh mint over the top and give it one more gentle toss. Taste a piece of fruit — if it needs more brightness, add a little extra squeeze of lime. If it needs more sweetness, drizzle in another small bit of honey.
  5. 5Serve immediately for the freshest texture, or cover and refrigerate for up to 2 hours before serving. Give it a gentle stir right before plating, since the juices will pool at the bottom — that dressing is delicious spooned back over the top.
💡 Improv Tip

The real secret to a fruit salad that tastes like more than just chopped fruit is that pinch of salt — it sounds wrong on a sweet dish but it wakes everything up and makes the flavors pop. Same goes for the vanilla extract: it's half a teaspoon but it gives the whole dressing a warmth that makes people ask what's in it. And if you're making this a few hours ahead, hold the mint until right before serving so it stays bright green instead of going dark and sad.
