Budget Meal Prep Ideas for the Week: One-Pan Cuban-Style Black Beans and Rice

Published June 16, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been searching for budget meal prep ideas for the week, this one-pan Cuban-style black beans and rice is about to become your Sunday staple. We're talking sofrito, cumin, a can of black beans, and long-grain rice coming together into something that tastes like it came from your abuela's kitchen — not your panic-shopping trip. Make a big batch on Sunday and you've got lunch or dinner handled for the next four days, no stress.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
30 mins
Total
40 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon sazon seasoning (or a pinch of turmeric for color)
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Optional toppings: sliced avocado, hot sauce, fresh cilantro, lime wedges, fried egg

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large deep skillet or wide pot over medium heat. Add the diced onion and green bell pepper and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and the onion starts to turn translucent.
  2. 2Add the minced garlic and cook for another 60 seconds until fragrant — don't walk away here, garlic burns fast and it will absolutely ruin your vibe.
  3. 3Stir in the tomato paste, cumin, oregano, smoked paprika, and sazon. Cook the spices in the oil for about 1 minute, stirring constantly. This step is called blooming your spices and it makes a huge difference in depth of flavor.
  4. 4Add the drained black beans to the pot and stir everything together so the beans get coated in all that sofrito goodness. Let them cook for 2 minutes.
  5. 5Pour in the dry white rice and stir to coat the grains in the mixture. This toasts the rice slightly and gives it more flavor.
  6. 6Pour in the chicken or vegetable broth and water. Stir once to combine, taste the liquid and adjust salt if needed, then bring everything to a boil over medium-high heat.
  7. 7Once boiling, reduce the heat to low, cover tightly with a lid, and cook for 18 to 20 minutes without lifting the lid. Resist the urge — lifting the lid lets the steam escape and you'll end up with crunchy rice.
  8. 8After 18 minutes, check the rice. If it's tender and the liquid is absorbed, you're done. If there's still a little liquid, cover and cook 2 more minutes. Remove from heat and let it steam with the lid on for 5 more minutes.
  9. 9Fluff with a fork, taste for seasoning, and serve it up however you like — with avocado, a fried egg on top, hot sauce, or just straight out of the pot with a spoon. No judgment.
  10. 10For meal prep, divide into airtight containers once cooled and refrigerate for up to 5 days. It actually tastes better on day two after everything melds together.
💡 Improv Tip

The real secret to this lasting all week without getting boring is keeping your toppings separate. Store the base plain and then change up what you put on top each day — avocado and lime on Monday, a fried egg on Tuesday, some rotisserie chicken and hot sauce on Wednesday. Same pot of rice and beans, four completely different meals. That's how you cure refrigerator blindness, baby.
