Simple Beef Empanadas Recipe (Crispy, Flavorful, No Fuss)

Published July 7, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple beef empanadas recipe is the one my abuela would approve of — bold filling, golden crust, and zero complicated steps. We're talking real Miami-style empanadas made with pantry staples you probably already have sitting around. Once you make these, store-bought is never gonna hit the same.

Prep
25 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
50 mins
Serves
12 empanadas

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef (80/20 works great)
  • 1 small white onion, finely diced
  • 1/2 green bell pepper, finely diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce (canned is perfect)
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp oregano
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 cup green olives, sliced (optional but highly encouraged)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 1 package store-bought empanada discs (Goya or similar, 12 count — found in the frozen Latin food section)
  • 1 egg, beaten (for egg wash)

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the diced onion and green bell pepper and cook for about 4-5 minutes until softened and just starting to turn golden.
  2. 2Add the minced garlic and cook for another 30 seconds — just until fragrant. Don't let it burn or it'll turn bitter on you.
  3. 3Add the ground beef, breaking it apart with a wooden spoon. Cook until fully browned, about 6-7 minutes. Drain off any excess fat if there's a lot pooling in the pan.
  4. 4Stir in the tomato sauce, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, salt, and black pepper. Let everything simmer together on medium-low for about 5 minutes so the flavors get cozy. Taste it — this is your moment to adjust seasoning. Toss in those sliced olives now if you're using them.
  5. 5Take the filling off the heat and let it cool for at least 10 minutes. Warm filling will make your dough soggy and hard to seal — patience here pays off.
  6. 6Preheat your oven to 400°F (or heat about 2 inches of oil in a deep pan to 350°F if you want to fry them). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper if baking.
  7. 7Lay out a thawed empanada disc on a clean surface. Spoon about 2 tablespoons of filling into the center — don't overfill or they'll bust open on you.
  8. 8Fold the disc in half over the filling to form a half-moon shape. Press the edges together firmly with your fingers, then crimp the edges by folding and pressing in a rope pattern, or just press down with a fork to seal. Either way works.
  9. 9Place the empanadas on the prepared baking sheet. Brush the tops with the beaten egg — this gives you that gorgeous golden color.
  10. 10Bake for 20-25 minutes until deep golden brown, or fry in batches for about 3-4 minutes per side until crispy and golden. Let them cool for a few minutes before eating — the filling holds heat like a champ.
💡 Improv Tip

The real secret to a flavorful beef filling is letting it simmer in that tomato sauce long enough to reduce — you don't want it watery or your discs will tear. If your filling looks a little wet, just cook it an extra 3-4 minutes uncovered. Also, store-bought Goya empanada discs are a total game-changer for keeping this recipe actually simple — no shame in it, that's exactly what they're made for.
