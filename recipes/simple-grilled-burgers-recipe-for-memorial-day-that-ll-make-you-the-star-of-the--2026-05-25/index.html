Simple Grilled Burgers Recipe for Memorial Day (That'll Make You the Star of the Cookout)

Published May 25, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple grilled burgers recipe for Memorial Day is the only one you need bookmarked before the long weekend hits — juicy, seasoned right, and ready in under 30 minutes. No fancy steakhouse blend, no secret sauce you have to order online, just real ingredients from your fridge and pantry doing what they do best. This is the kind of burger your neighbors are going to ask about, mijo.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs 80/20 ground beef
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 4 slices American or cheddar cheese
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 2 tablespoons butter or mayo (for toasting the buns)
  • Toppings of your choice: lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, ketchup, mustard

Instructions

  1. 1Pull the ground beef out of the fridge about 10 minutes before you start — letting it come closer to room temperature helps it cook evenly. While you wait, preheat your grill or grill pan to medium-high heat. You want it hot enough that a drop of water sizzles immediately on contact.
  2. 2In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and black pepper. Mix with your hands just until everything is combined — do not overwork the meat or your burgers will come out tough and sad.
  3. 3Divide the mixture into 4 equal portions and form them into patties about 3/4 inch thick. Here's the move: press your thumb into the center of each patty to make a small indent. That little dimple keeps the burger from puffing up into a meatball on the grill.
  4. 4Place the patties on the hot grill and don't touch them for at least 3 to 4 minutes. Let the grill do its thing. Flip once, cook another 3 to 4 minutes for medium doneness. In the last minute of cooking, lay a slice of cheese on each patty and close the grill lid so it melts perfectly.
  5. 5While the burgers rest, butter your buns and place them face-down on the grill for about 60 seconds until golden and toasty. Don't skip this — a toasted bun is the difference between a good burger and a great one.
  6. 6Build your burgers however you like and serve immediately. Set up a little topping station so everyone can customize their own — that's the move for a cookout crowd.
💡 Improv Tip

The biggest mistake people make is pressing down on the burger with a spatula while it's cooking — I know it's tempting, but stop. All you're doing is squeezing out the juices that make the burger delicious. Leave it alone, let the grill work, and you'll have the juiciest burger at the whole Memorial Day cookout.
