Black Bean and Rice Stuffed Peppers (Easy Make Ahead Dinner Recipe)

Published June 29, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're looking for easy make ahead dinner recipes that actually taste better the next day, these stuffed peppers are about to become your new weekly ritual. We're talking tender bell peppers loaded with seasoned black beans, rice, and sofrito — pure Miami comfort food that costs almost nothing to pull together. Make a big batch on Sunday and you've got weeknight dinners handled, no stress, no excuses.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
40 mins
Total
55 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 large bell peppers, any color, tops cut off and seeds removed
  • 1 cup long-grain white rice, uncooked
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, drained
  • 1/2 cup frozen corn
  • 1/2 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella or cheddar cheese
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. 1Cook your rice according to package directions and set aside. While that's going, preheat your oven to 375°F and lightly grease a baking dish large enough to hold all four peppers standing upright.
  2. 2Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook for about 4 minutes until soft and translucent. Add the garlic and cook another minute until fragrant — your kitchen should smell amazing right about now.
  3. 3Add the cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes to the skillet and stir everything together for about 30 seconds to toast those spices a little. This step makes a big difference, don't skip it.
  4. 4Add the drained black beans, diced tomatoes, and frozen corn to the skillet. Stir to combine and let it cook together for about 3-4 minutes. Remove from heat and fold in the cooked rice until everything is well mixed. Taste and adjust salt if needed.
  5. 5Spoon the filling generously into each bell pepper, packing it in and mounding it slightly at the top. Place the stuffed peppers in your prepared baking dish and cover tightly with aluminum foil.
  6. 6Bake covered for 30 minutes. Remove the foil, sprinkle shredded cheese over each pepper, and bake uncovered for another 8-10 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly and the pepper skins are tender.
  7. 7Let them cool for 5 minutes before serving. Garnish with fresh cilantro or parsley and serve as-is or alongside a simple green salad. To make ahead, stop after filling the peppers, cover the baking dish tightly with foil, and refrigerate for up to 3 days. Bake straight from the fridge — just add 10 extra minutes to the covered baking time.
💡 Improv Tip

The filling is your canvas, mami — swap the black beans for leftover picadillo, cooked ground turkey, or whatever protein you've got hanging around. The sofrito base keeps everything tasting intentional no matter what you throw in. These also freeze beautifully for up to 2 months; just thaw overnight in the fridge before baking.
