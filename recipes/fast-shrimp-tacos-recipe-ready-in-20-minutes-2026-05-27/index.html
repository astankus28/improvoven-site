Fast Shrimp Tacos Recipe (Ready in 20 Minutes!)

Published May 27, 2026 · Improv Oven

This fast shrimp tacos recipe is what happens when you've got a bag of frozen shrimp and zero patience — dinner is on the table before you even finish your first cafecito. We're keeping it simple, bold, and loaded with flavor the way we do it in Miami: a little lime, a little heat, and a creamy slaw that ties everything together.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
10 mins
Total
20 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb medium shrimp, peeled and deveined (fresh or frozen and thawed)
  • 8 small corn or flour tortillas
  • 2 cups pre-shredded coleslaw mix
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon hot sauce (Cholula or Tabasco work great)
  • 2 limes, divided
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil or vegetable oil
  • Fresh cilantro, for topping (optional but highly encouraged)
  • 1 avocado, sliced (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Make your creamy slaw first so the flavors have a few minutes to come together. In a medium bowl, combine the coleslaw mix, mayonnaise, hot sauce, juice of 1 lime, and a pinch of salt. Toss it well, taste it, and set it aside. That's your secret weapon right there.
  2. 2Pat your shrimp completely dry with paper towels — this is the move that gets you that beautiful sear instead of a sad steam. In a bowl, toss the shrimp with garlic powder, cumin, smoked paprika, chili powder, salt, and black pepper until every piece is coated.
  3. 3Heat your oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Add the shrimp in a single layer — don't crowd them or they'll steam instead of sear. Cook for about 2 minutes per side until pink, curled, and slightly golden at the edges. Squeeze half of your remaining lime over the shrimp right in the pan and toss once. Pull them off the heat immediately so they don't overcook.
  4. 4While the shrimp cook, warm your tortillas. You can do them one at a time directly over a gas burner flame for those beautiful char spots, or wrap them in a damp paper towel and microwave for 45 seconds. Either way works — just get them warm and pliable.
  5. 5Build your tacos: lay down a spoonful of the creamy slaw, top with 3 or 4 shrimp, add your avocado slices, a few fresh cilantro leaves, and a squeeze of that last lime wedge. Eat immediately while everything is hot and the slaw is still cold and crunchy. That contrast is the whole point.
💡 Improv Tip

Frozen shrimp is genuinely your best friend here — it's almost always cheaper than fresh and honestly just as good when you thaw it right. Put the bag in a bowl of cold water for 15 minutes and they're ready to go. Also, don't skip drying the shrimp before seasoning. It's the one step people skip and then wonder why their shrimp taste steamed and rubbery. Dry shrimp = golden shrimp. That's the rule.
