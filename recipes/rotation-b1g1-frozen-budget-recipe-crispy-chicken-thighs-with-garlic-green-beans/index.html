Rotation B1G1 Frozen Budget Recipe: Crispy Chicken Thighs with Garlic Green Beans

Published August 10, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been stacking your freezer with rotation B1G1 deals, this budget recipe was made for exactly that haul — frozen chicken thighs and a bag of frozen green beans turn into a weeknight dinner that tastes way more intentional than it has any right to. We're talking crispy-skinned chicken, garlicky sautéed green beans, and a simple pan sauce that ties it all together in under 40 minutes. This is the kind of meal that makes you feel smart for shopping the sales.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
30 mins
Total
40 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (frozen, thawed overnight or under cold water)
  • 12 oz frozen green beans (no need to thaw)
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth (or water with a pinch of bouillon)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried Italian seasoning or dried thyme
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Lemon wedges for serving (optional but great)

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your thawed chicken thighs very dry with paper towels — this is the single most important step for getting crispy skin. Season generously on both sides with garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper.
  2. 2Heat a large oven-safe skillet (cast iron is ideal) over medium-high heat. Add the oil and let it get hot — you want it shimmering. Place the chicken thighs skin-side down and don't touch them for 7 to 8 minutes. You're building a crust here. Resist the urge to peek too early.
  3. 3Flip the chicken and cook for another 3 minutes on the other side. Remove the chicken from the pan and set aside on a plate. If you don't have an oven-safe skillet, you can finish the chicken in a 400°F oven on a baking sheet for 15 minutes — just make sure internal temp hits 165°F.
  4. 4Pour off most of the fat from the pan, leaving about a tablespoon behind. Reduce heat to medium. Add the butter and minced garlic and cook for 60 seconds, stirring constantly, until fragrant and just golden.
  5. 5Add the frozen green beans directly to the pan — no thawing needed. Toss them in the garlicky butter. Pour in the chicken broth, scraping up any brown bits from the bottom of the pan (that's flavor, don't waste it). Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  6. 6Nestle the chicken thighs back into the pan on top of the green beans, skin-side up. Cover and cook on medium-low for 10 to 12 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the green beans are tender but still have a little bite.
  7. 7Remove the lid for the last 2 minutes to let the skin re-crisp slightly. Serve straight from the pan with lemon wedges if you have them — a squeeze of lemon over everything at the end is quietly the move.
💡 Improv Tip

The B1G1 freezer life is real, and chicken thighs are almost always the better deal over breasts — they're harder to overcook, more flavorful, and they forgive you when you get distracted. If you grabbed a big rotation sale haul, thaw only what you need in the fridge the night before and keep the rest frozen flat in zip bags so they stack easily and thaw faster next time.
