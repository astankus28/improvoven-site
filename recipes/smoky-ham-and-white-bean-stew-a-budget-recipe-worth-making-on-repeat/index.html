Smoky Ham and White Bean Stew (A Budget Recipe Worth Making on Repeat)

Published August 9, 2026 · Improv Oven

This ham smoked budget recipe is proof that a humble bag of beans and a hunk of budget smoked ham can become something genuinely crave-worthy on a weeknight. It's a thick, hearty stew — loaded with tender white beans, smoky ham, sofrito aromatics, and a little kick — that tastes like it simmered all day but comes together in about 40 minutes. Think of it as the kind of bowl that makes you forget it only cost a few bucks to feed the whole family.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
35 mins
Total
45 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb smoked ham steak or smoked cubed ham, diced into bite-sized chunks
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) white beans (cannellini or Great Northern), drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 stalks celery, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes (optional but recommended)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley for garnish
  • Crusty bread or white rice for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the diced smoked ham and let it sit undisturbed for about 2 minutes until it picks up a little color and the bottom of the pot starts smelling incredible. Stir and cook another minute, then remove the ham and set it aside — you're building flavor here, not cooking it through.
  2. 2In the same pot with all those gorgeous drippings, add the onion, bell pepper, and celery. Cook over medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until everything softens and the onion turns translucent. Don't rush this step — it's your flavor base.
  3. 3Add the minced garlic, smoked paprika, cumin, oregano, and red pepper flakes. Stir everything together and cook for about 60 seconds until fragrant. Your kitchen should smell amazing right now.
  4. 4Pour in the diced tomatoes and stir, scraping up any bits stuck to the bottom of the pot — that's all flavor. Add the chicken broth and stir to combine.
  5. 5Return the ham to the pot. Add both cans of drained white beans. Stir everything together, bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat to medium-low. Let it simmer uncovered for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the broth thickens slightly and the beans start to break down a little around the edges.
  6. 6Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper. Keep in mind the smoked ham is already salty, so go easy on the salt until you taste it first. If you want the stew thicker, use the back of your spoon to mash a handful of beans against the side of the pot and stir them in.
  7. 7Ladle into bowls, hit it with fresh cilantro or parsley, and serve with crusty bread for dunking or a scoop of white rice to soak up the broth. Either way, nobody at the table is going home hungry.
💡 Improv Tip

Smoked ham steaks are usually sold near the breakfast meats at most grocery stores for around $3 to $5 and they are an absolute secret weapon for budget cooking — they're already cooked, already seasoned, and already smoky, so they do a ton of flavor lifting without any effort on your part. If you find them on sale or marked down, grab two and freeze one. Future you will be very grateful.
