Quick Smash Burger Recipe (Cast Iron, 15 Minutes, Crispy-Edge Perfect)

Published September 5, 2026 · Improv Oven

This quick smash burger recipe made in a cast iron skillet gives you that diner-style crust you just can't fake any other way — no grill, no fancy equipment, just a ripping hot pan and a little confidence. The magic is all in the smash: press those balls of beef flat against the iron and let the Maillard reaction do its thing, creating those lacy, caramelized edges that make smash burgers so insanely good. Two thin patties, melty cheese, a soft bun — this is weeknight dinner or weekend lunch that beats any fast food drive-through.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
10 mins
Total
20 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs 80/20 ground beef
  • 4 soft hamburger buns (potato buns if you can find them)
  • 8 slices American cheese (or any melty cheese you have)
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil (vegetable, canola, or avocado)
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter (for toasting buns)
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp pickle relish or 4-5 diced pickle chips
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 cup shredded iceberg lettuce
  • 1 medium tomato, sliced thin
  • 1/2 white or yellow onion, sliced thin (raw or caramelized — your call)

Instructions

  1. 1Mix together the mayonnaise, ketchup, yellow mustard, pickle relish, and smoked paprika in a small bowl. Stir well, taste, and adjust — this is your smash sauce. Set it in the fridge while you cook. (It also keeps for a week, so make extra.)
  2. 2Divide your ground beef into 8 equal balls, roughly 3 oz each. Don't overwork the meat — loose and cold is exactly what you want here. Season each ball generously with salt, pepper, and garlic powder right before they hit the pan.
  3. 3Place your cast iron skillet over high heat for at least 2 full minutes. You want it screaming hot — a drop of water should evaporate instantly. Add the oil and swirl it to coat.
  4. 4Working in batches of 2-3 balls at a time (don't crowd the pan), place a beef ball in the skillet and immediately smash it flat with a heavy spatula, a small pot bottom, or a burger press. Press hard and hold for 10 seconds. You want each patty about 1/4 inch thick — thin is the goal here.
  5. 5Let the patties cook undisturbed for 2 to 2.5 minutes. Resist every urge to move them. You'll see the edges turn brown and lacy — that's exactly what you're after. Flip once, immediately lay a slice of cheese on each patty, and cook for another 60-90 seconds until the cheese is melted and the second side has color.
  6. 6While your last batch of patties cooks, wipe out any burnt bits from the skillet, drop the heat to medium, and add the butter. Toast your buns cut-side down for about 60 seconds until golden. Watch them — they go fast.
  7. 7Stack your burger: smash sauce on the bottom bun, lettuce, tomato, onion, then the double-stacked cheesy patties, and more sauce on the top bun if you want it. Serve immediately — smash burgers wait for no one.
💡 Improv Tip

The number one smash burger mistake is a pan that isn't hot enough. If your beef is steaming and sizzling gently instead of aggressively searing, pull the patties off and let the pan reheat for another minute. That crust only forms when there's real, violent contact between cold meat and a very hot surface. Also — 80/20 beef is non-negotiable here. Leaner beef will give you a dry, sad patty. The fat is the flavor.
