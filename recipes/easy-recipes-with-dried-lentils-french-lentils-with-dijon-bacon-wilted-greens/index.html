Easy Recipes with Dried Lentils: French Lentils with Dijon, Bacon & Wilted Greens

Published October 30, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're hunting for easy recipes with dried lentils, this French-inspired skillet is about to become your new weeknight staple — it comes together in under 40 minutes and tastes like something you'd pay $18 for at a bistro. Earthy green lentils, smoky bacon, a hit of Dijon mustard, and wilted greens all come together in one pan for a meal that's deeply satisfying without being heavy. It's the kind of dish that makes you feel like a real cook, and honestly? It basically makes itself.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
30 mins
Total
40 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dried green or French (Puy-style) lentils, rinsed
  • 2 ½ cups water or low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
  • 4 strips bacon, chopped into small pieces
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 stalks celery, thinly sliced
  • 1 medium carrot, diced small
  • 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves (or ½ tsp dried thyme)
  • 2 big handfuls fresh spinach or chopped kale
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley, roughly chopped, for serving
  • Crusty bread or a fried egg on top, optional but highly recommended

Instructions

  1. 1Cook the lentils first: Add the rinsed lentils and broth (or water) to a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to a gentle simmer. Cook uncovered for 20–25 minutes, until the lentils are tender but still holding their shape — you don't want mush. Drain any excess liquid and set aside.
  2. 2While the lentils cook, set a large skillet over medium heat. Add the chopped bacon and cook, stirring occasionally, until it's rendered and crispy, about 5–6 minutes. Remove the bacon with a slotted spoon and set it on a paper towel-lined plate, but leave that gorgeous bacon fat in the pan.
  3. 3Into the same skillet with the bacon fat, add the onion, celery, and carrot. Cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until softened and starting to turn golden at the edges — about 6–7 minutes. Add the garlic and thyme and cook for another 60 seconds until fragrant.
  4. 4Add the cooked, drained lentils directly into the skillet with the vegetables. Stir everything together so the lentils get coated in all those flavors. Then stir in the Dijon mustard and red wine vinegar. Taste it — this is the moment. Add salt and plenty of black pepper until it sings.
  5. 5Fold in the spinach or kale and stir gently for about 1–2 minutes, just until the greens wilt down into the lentils. Remove from heat.
  6. 6Crumble the crispy bacon back over the top and finish with a handful of fresh parsley. Serve warm, straight from the skillet. A fried egg on top or a thick slice of crusty bread alongside makes this an absolute meal.
💡 Improv Tip

Green or French (Puy-style) lentils are the move here — they hold their shape after cooking and won't turn to baby food on you. Save red or yellow lentils for soups and dals. If you skip the bacon to make it vegetarian, add a tablespoon of olive oil and a tiny splash of soy sauce or smoked paprika to get some of that depth back.
