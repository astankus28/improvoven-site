Easy 20 Minute Beef and Broccoli (Better Than Takeout)

Published September 3, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy 20 minute beef and broccoli is the weeknight dinner that's going to make you stop calling for delivery — glossy, savory sauce, tender beef, and crisp-tender broccoli, all done faster than the app can confirm your order. We're keeping it simple with pantry staples and sneaking in a little Miami magic with a splash of soy and a hint of heat. No fancy wok required, no mystery ingredients — just your skillet and about 20 minutes of your life.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
20 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb flank steak or sirloin, thinly sliced against the grain
  • 4 cups broccoli florets (fresh or frozen)
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch, divided
  • 2 teaspoons sesame oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated (or 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger)
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 cup beef broth or water
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil, divided
  • Cooked white rice, for serving
  • Sesame seeds and sliced green onions, for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Slice your beef as thin as you can — partially freezing it for 15 minutes beforehand makes this so much easier. Toss the slices in a bowl with 1 tablespoon of soy sauce and 1/2 tablespoon of cornstarch. Let it sit while you prep everything else. This little move tenderizes the meat and helps it sear beautifully instead of steaming.
  2. 2In a small bowl, whisk together the remaining 2 tablespoons soy sauce, oyster sauce, brown sugar, the remaining 1/2 tablespoon cornstarch, sesame oil, and beef broth. Stir until the sugar and cornstarch dissolve. Set your sauce aside — this is your flavor MVP.
  3. 3Heat a large skillet or wide pan over high heat until it's genuinely hot. Add 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil and let it shimmer. Add the broccoli florets in a single layer and let them sit for 1 minute without touching, then toss and cook another 1 to 2 minutes until they're bright green with a few charred edges. Season with a pinch of salt, pull them out of the pan, and set aside.
  4. 4Add the remaining tablespoon of oil to the same hot pan. Add the beef in a single layer — work in batches if your pan isn't huge. Let it sear undisturbed for about 1 minute, then flip. You want golden edges, not gray, steamed meat. This is the move everyone skips and then wonders why their beef and broccoli tastes flat.
  5. 5Once all the beef is seared, add the garlic, ginger, and red pepper flakes directly into the pan. Stir everything together and cook for about 30 seconds until fragrant — your kitchen should smell incredible right about now.
  6. 6Pour the sauce over the beef and stir to coat. Let it bubble for about 1 minute until it thickens and turns glossy. Add the broccoli back in and toss everything together so it's all coated in that gorgeous sauce. Taste and adjust — more soy if you want more salt, a pinch more sugar if you want it a little sweeter.
  7. 7Serve immediately over steamed rice and top with sesame seeds and green onions if you've got them. This is peak 'I actually cooked tonight' energy with minimal effort.
💡 Improv Tip

The secret weapon here is a really hot pan — if your beef is releasing liquid and steaming instead of searing, your pan wasn't hot enough. Don't crowd the meat. If you only have a medium skillet, cook the beef in two batches. A little patience on that sear makes the difference between 'meh' and 'wait, YOU made this?'
