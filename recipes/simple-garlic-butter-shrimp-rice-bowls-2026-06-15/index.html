Simple Garlic Butter Shrimp Rice Bowls

Published June 15, 2026 · Improv Oven

Simple garlic butter shrimp rice bowls are one of those meals that looks like you tried way harder than you actually did — and we love that for us. We're talking juicy shrimp swimming in a glossy, garlicky butter sauce piled right on top of fluffy rice, ready in about 30 minutes with ingredients you probably already have. This one's a weeknight staple at Improv Oven for good reason.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb medium shrimp, peeled and deveined (fresh or frozen and thawed)
  • 3 cups cooked white rice (about 1.5 cups dry)
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional but encouraged)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • Lime wedges for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Start your rice first if it isn't already cooked — just follow the package instructions. Day-old rice from the fridge actually works great here and saves you time.
  2. 2Pat your shrimp completely dry with paper towels. This is not a step to skip — dry shrimp get that beautiful golden sear, wet shrimp just steam and get sad. Season them with smoked paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper, then toss to coat.
  3. 3Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add your olive oil and 1 tablespoon of the butter. Once the butter is melted and the pan is hot, add the shrimp in a single layer. Don't crowd them — cook in batches if you need to.
  4. 4Cook the shrimp about 1 to 2 minutes per side until they're pink, curled, and just opaque. Pull them out of the pan and set them aside on a plate. They'll finish cooking slightly from the residual heat, which is exactly what we want.
  5. 5Turn the heat down to medium. Add the remaining 3 tablespoons of butter to the same pan. Once melted, add the minced garlic and red pepper flakes. Stir constantly and cook for about 60 to 90 seconds until the garlic is fragrant and just barely golden — watch it close because garlic goes from perfect to burnt fast.
  6. 6Squeeze in the lime juice and stir everything together. That little hit of acid is going to brighten the whole sauce and balance all that rich butter. Taste it and adjust salt if needed.
  7. 7Add the shrimp back into the pan and toss them gently in the garlic butter sauce for about 30 seconds just to warm them through and coat every single one.
  8. 8To build your bowls, scoop a generous portion of rice into each bowl, spoon the garlic butter shrimp right on top, and drizzle any extra sauce from the pan over everything — do not waste that sauce. Finish with fresh parsley or cilantro, sliced green onions, and a lime wedge on the side. Eat immediately.
💡 Improv Tip

Frozen shrimp are your best friend here — they're usually cheaper than fresh, just as good, and you can keep a bag in the freezer for whenever the craving hits. Run them under cold water for about 10 minutes to thaw, then pat dry like your shrimp's life depends on it.
