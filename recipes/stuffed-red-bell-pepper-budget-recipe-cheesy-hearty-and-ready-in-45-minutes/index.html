Stuffed Red Bell Pepper Budget Recipe (Cheesy, Hearty, and Ready in 45 Minutes)

Published August 20, 2026 · Improv Oven

This stuffed red bell pepper budget recipe is one of those dinners that looks way fancier than what you spent — we're talking under $2 a serving with ground beef, rice, and melted cheese packed into sweet, tender peppers. It's the kind of meal that hits the table and makes everyone think you actually tried. Miami spin incoming: a little cumin, smoked paprika, and a splash of Worcestershire to make that filling sing.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
40 mins
Total
55 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 large red bell peppers
  • 1 lb ground beef (80/20 or whatever's on sale)
  • 1 cup long-grain white rice, uncooked
  • 1 cup chicken or beef broth
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, drained (reserve the liquid)
  • 1/2 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella or Mexican blend cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or vegetable oil
  • Fresh parsley or cilantro for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 375°F. Cook the rice according to package directions but use broth instead of water — this one small swap adds a ton of flavor for zero extra cost. Set the cooked rice aside.
  2. 2While the rice cooks, slice the tops off your bell peppers and pull out the seeds and ribs. Stand them upright in a baking dish. If they wobble, slice just a thin sliver off the bottom to level them out — don't cut through.
  3. 3Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced onion and cook for 3-4 minutes until softened. Add the garlic and cook another 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. 4Add the ground beef to the skillet. Break it up with a wooden spoon and cook until browned all the way through, about 6-7 minutes. Drain any excess fat if needed.
  5. 5Stir in the drained diced tomatoes, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and black pepper. Let it cook together for 2-3 minutes so everything gets friendly.
  6. 6Remove the skillet from heat and fold in the cooked rice. Taste the filling and adjust seasoning — this is your moment. Pour the reserved tomato liquid into the bottom of the baking dish (about 1/4 cup) to create a little steam while the peppers bake.
  7. 7Spoon the filling generously into each pepper, pressing it down lightly so you can really pack it in. Top each pepper with a good handful of shredded cheese.
  8. 8Cover the baking dish loosely with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Then remove the foil and bake another 10-12 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden and the peppers are tender when you poke them with a fork.
  9. 9Let the peppers rest for 5 minutes before serving — they'll be lava-hot inside. Garnish with fresh parsley or cilantro if you've got it, and dig in.
💡 Improv Tip

If your peppers are on the smaller side, you'll have leftover filling — don't stress it. Scoop it into a bowl and serve it alongside like a little bonus side dish, or scramble it into eggs the next morning. Nothing goes to waste at Improv Oven.
