Easy Homemade Fish Stew Recipe (Ready in 30 Minutes)

Published June 7, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy homemade fish stew recipe is exactly what you need when the fridge looks empty but you've got a piece of fish, a can of tomatoes, and about 30 minutes — because that's honestly all it takes. We're building deep, rich flavor with simple pantry staples and a little sofrito magic, the way abuelas have been doing it forever. No fancy technique, no expensive ingredients — just a bowl of something that tastes like you actually know what you're doing.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs white fish fillets (tilapia, cod, or whatever's on sale), cut into 2-inch chunks
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (14 oz) chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional but recommended)
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Fresh cilantro for serving (optional)
  • Crusty bread or white rice for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your fish chunks dry with a paper towel and season them generously with salt, black pepper, and a pinch of the smoked paprika. Drying the fish is key — it helps it hold together in the stew instead of falling apart into mush. Set it aside while you build your base.
  2. 2Heat the oil in a large pot or deep skillet over medium heat. Add your onion and bell pepper and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they soften and start to smell amazing. This is your sofrito foundation — don't rush it.
  3. 3Add the garlic, cumin, remaining smoked paprika, oregano, and red pepper flakes. Stir everything together and cook for about 60 seconds until the spices are fragrant. You'll know it's ready when your kitchen smells like a restaurant.
  4. 4Pour in the diced tomatoes with their juices and the broth. Add the bay leaf, stir to combine, and bring everything to a gentle simmer over medium-high heat. Let it cook for about 5 minutes so the flavors start to come together.
  5. 5Gently nestle the seasoned fish chunks into the simmering broth. Don't stir aggressively — just let them settle in. Cover the pot and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, or until the fish is opaque and flakes easily with a fork. Thinner fillets like tilapia will be done closer to 8 minutes.
  6. 6Remove the bay leaf. Squeeze the lime juice over the stew and taste for seasoning — add more salt, pepper, or lime until it sings. Ladle into bowls, top with fresh cilantro if you have it, and serve with crusty bread to soak up all that broth or over a scoop of white rice.
💡 Improv Tip

The best fish for this stew is whatever white fish is cheapest at your store that day — tilapia, swai, cod, pollock, even frozen fish thawed overnight in the fridge all work beautifully here. If you want to stretch it further and feed more people, throw in a diced potato or two with the broth in step 4 and let them cook until just tender before adding the fish. Budget meal unlocked.
