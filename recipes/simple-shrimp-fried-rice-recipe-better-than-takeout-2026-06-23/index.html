Simple Shrimp Fried Rice Recipe (Better Than Takeout)

Published June 23, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple shrimp fried rice recipe is exactly what you need when it's a weeknight, you've got leftover rice in the fridge, and you're not trying to spend an hour in the kitchen. We're talking big flavor, minimal dishes, and a dinner that tastes like you actually tried — because you did, just not that hard.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb medium shrimp, peeled and deveined (fresh or thawed from frozen)
  • 3 cups cooked white rice, day-old works best
  • 3 large eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 green onions, sliced (whites and greens separated)
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil, divided
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes (optional but recommended)
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your shrimp completely dry with paper towels — this is non-negotiable. Wet shrimp will steam instead of sear and you'll lose all that good color and flavor. Season them lightly with salt and black pepper.
  2. 2Heat a large skillet or wok over high heat until it's genuinely hot — like, properly hot. Add 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil and cook the shrimp in a single layer for about 1 to 2 minutes per side until they're pink and just cooked through. Pull them out and set them aside on a plate.
  3. 3In a small bowl, mix together the soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil. Set that aside — having your sauce ready before things get moving makes everything easier.
  4. 4Add the remaining tablespoon of oil to the same pan over high heat. Toss in the garlic and the white parts of your green onions. Stir constantly for about 30 seconds until fragrant — don't walk away here, garlic burns fast.
  5. 5Add the frozen peas and carrots straight from the bag. Stir-fry for 2 minutes until they're thawed and starting to get a little color.
  6. 6Push everything to one side of the pan and pour the beaten eggs into the empty space. Let them sit for about 15 seconds, then scramble them gently until just set but still a little soft. Break them up and mix into the vegetables.
  7. 7Add the cold rice to the pan, breaking up any clumps with your spatula. Press the rice into the pan and let it sit undisturbed for 1 minute so it can get a little toasty on the bottom — that's where the magic lives. Then stir and repeat once more.
  8. 8Pour the sauce mixture over the rice and toss everything together until evenly coated. Add the cooked shrimp back in along with the red pepper flakes if you're using them. Toss to combine and heat through for another minute.
  9. 9Taste and adjust with a little more soy sauce or salt if needed. Finish with the green parts of your scallions on top and serve hot straight from the pan.
💡 Improv Tip

Day-old rice is the real secret here — fresh rice has too much moisture and turns mushy in the pan. If you only have fresh rice, spread it on a sheet pan and stick it in the fridge uncovered for at least an hour. And listen, frozen shrimp is completely fine for this. Thaw it in a bowl of cold water for 15 minutes, pat it dry, and nobody's going to know the difference.
