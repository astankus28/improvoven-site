Simple Sheet Pan Fajitas Recipe

Published May 16, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple sheet pan fajitas recipe is the weeknight move nobody told you about — everything hits one pan, goes into a hot oven, and comes out sizzling like you actually tried. We're talking juicy chicken, blistered peppers, sweet onions, all that good stuff with zero babysitting. It's the kind of meal that cures your 'I don't know what to cook' paralysis real fast.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
22 mins
Total
32 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken breast, sliced into thin strips
  • 3 bell peppers (any color mix), sliced into strips
  • 1 large yellow onion, sliced into half-moons
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon oregano
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 8 small flour or corn tortillas
  • Optional toppings: sour cream, salsa, shredded cheese, fresh cilantro, sliced avocado

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 425°F and line a large sheet pan with foil or parchment — trust me, you'll thank yourself at dish time.
  2. 2In a small bowl, mix together the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, smoked paprika, onion powder, salt, black pepper, and oregano. That's your fajita seasoning right there — no packet needed.
  3. 3Toss your sliced chicken, peppers, and onion directly on the sheet pan. Drizzle the olive oil over everything, squeeze on that lime juice, then sprinkle the seasoning mix all over. Use your hands or tongs to toss it all together until everything's evenly coated.
  4. 4Spread everything out in a single layer — don't pile it up or it'll steam instead of getting those nice roasted edges. If things look crowded, use two pans.
  5. 5Roast at 425°F for 20 to 22 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the peppers and onions have some gorgeous char on the edges. No flipping required.
  6. 6While the pan is in the oven, warm your tortillas directly on a gas burner for about 30 seconds per side, or wrap them in a damp paper towel and microwave for 45 seconds.
  7. 7Pull the pan out, give everything a good squeeze of extra lime if you have it, and set the whole pan right on the table — family style. Load up your tortillas and go to town.
💡 Improv Tip

Don't skip the high heat — 425°F is the secret. Lower temps just steam your veggies and you lose all that caramelized flavor that makes fajitas taste like fajitas. And if you want to stretch this into more servings, throw in a sliced zucchini or some mushrooms right on the same pan. They absorb all that seasoned oil and honestly nobody misses the extra chicken.
