Easy No Bake Cheesecake Recipe (Creamy, Simple, No Oven Needed)

Published June 4, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy no bake cheesecake recipe is the one you're going to keep coming back to — no oven, no water bath, no drama. It comes together with just a handful of pantry staples and sets up beautifully in the fridge while you go live your life. Think of it like the dessert equivalent of 'I just threw this together' — except everyone at the table is asking for the recipe.

Prep
20 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
4 hours 20 mins
Serves
8

Ingredients

  • 1 and 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs (about 10 full crackers)
  • 5 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar (for crust)
  • 16 oz cream cheese, softened to room temperature
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream, cold
  • Fresh strawberries or canned cherry pie filling for topping (optional but highly encouraged)

Instructions

  1. 1Make the crust: In a medium bowl, mix together the graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, and granulated sugar until it looks like wet sand and holds together when you press it. Dump it into a 9-inch springform pan or a regular pie dish and press it firmly and evenly across the bottom and slightly up the sides. Use the bottom of a flat glass to really pack it in. Stick it in the freezer for 10 minutes while you make the filling.
  2. 2Whip the cream: In a large cold bowl, beat the heavy whipping cream with a hand mixer on high speed until stiff peaks form — meaning when you lift the beaters, the cream stands up on its own without flopping over. This takes about 3 to 4 minutes. Set it aside.
  3. 3Make the filling: In a separate large bowl, beat the softened cream cheese until it's completely smooth with no lumps — this is the most important step, so don't rush it. Add the powdered sugar, vanilla extract, and lemon juice and beat again until everything is creamy and well combined.
  4. 4Fold it together: Using a rubber spatula, gently fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese mixture in two or three additions. You want to keep as much airiness as possible, so fold slowly with big gentle strokes — don't stir like you're making rice.
  5. 5Assemble: Pull your crust out of the freezer and pour the filling right in. Smooth the top with a spatula or the back of a spoon. Cover loosely with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or overnight if you can wait that long. Overnight is honestly better.
  6. 6Serve: Once set, top with fresh sliced strawberries, a spoonful of cherry pie filling, or even just a drizzle of honey. Slice and serve cold. Watch it disappear.
💡 Improv Tip

The number one thing that will make or break this cheesecake is the temperature of your cream cheese — if it's even a little cold, you'll get lumps in the filling and no amount of mixing will fix it. Pull it out of the fridge at least an hour before you start, or if you forgot (we've all been there), cut it into small cubes and let it sit for 20 minutes. Smooth cream cheese equals silky cheesecake, and that's the whole game.
