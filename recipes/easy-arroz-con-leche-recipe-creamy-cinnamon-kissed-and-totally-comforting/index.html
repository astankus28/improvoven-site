Easy Arroz con Leche Recipe (Creamy, Cinnamon-Kissed, and Totally Comforting)

Published September 30, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy arroz con leche recipe is the kind of dessert that makes your whole kitchen smell like a warm hug — milky, lightly spiced, and so simple you'll wonder why you ever skipped it. It's a classic Latin American rice pudding that comes together with a handful of pantry staples and about 35 minutes of your time. Think of it as the dessert your abuela kept on the stove, always ready, always perfect.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
35 mins
Total
40 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 1 cup long-grain white rice
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 strip of lemon peel (about 2 inches, yellow part only)
  • 3 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup evaporated milk (one small 12 oz can works great)
  • 1/2 cup sweetened condensed milk (adjust to taste)
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon, plus more for topping
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. 1Rinse the rice under cold water until the water runs mostly clear — this removes extra starch and keeps your arroz con leche from getting too gluey. Drain it well and set aside.
  2. 2In a medium saucepan over medium heat, combine the rinsed rice, water, cinnamon stick, lemon peel strip, and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover loosely, and let the rice cook until the water is mostly absorbed, about 12 to 15 minutes. Don't walk too far — peek occasionally and give it a stir.
  3. 3Once the water is absorbed, pour in the whole milk and evaporated milk. Stir everything together, raise the heat to medium, and bring it to a gentle simmer. You'll see the mixture start to thicken and get creamy — that's exactly what you want.
  4. 4Let the rice cook uncovered in the milk, stirring frequently (every couple of minutes) so nothing sticks to the bottom, for about 15 to 20 minutes. The rice should be very tender and the milk should coat the back of a spoon. It'll look a little loose — that's fine, it thickens more as it cools.
  5. 5Remove from heat and fish out the cinnamon stick and lemon peel — they've done their job. Stir in the sweetened condensed milk, vanilla extract, and ground cinnamon. Taste it here and add a splash more condensed milk if you want it sweeter.
  6. 6Spoon into bowls, cups, or a big serving dish. Dust generously with ground cinnamon on top. Serve warm right now, or let it cool and refrigerate — it's honestly great either way. Cold arroz con leche with extra cinnamon on top? That's Miami fridge food at its finest.
💡 Improv Tip

The lemon peel is the secret weapon here — don't skip it. It adds a subtle brightness that lifts the whole dish and keeps it from tasting flat. Just be careful to use only the yellow outer layer; the white pith underneath is bitter and will fight you. A vegetable peeler makes this super easy.
