Easy Mexican Beef Picadillo Tacos Recipe

Published May 15, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy picadillo tacos recipe with Mexican beef is the kind of weeknight win that makes you feel like you actually have your life together. Picadillo is that beautiful sofrito-forward ground beef situation — simmered down with tomatoes, olives, and warm spices — that we grew up eating over rice but honestly hits even harder stuffed into a warm tortilla. It's cheap, it's fast, and it tastes like somebody's abuela was involved.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef (80/20 is your friend here)
  • 1 small white onion, finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 small green bell pepper, finely diced
  • 1 roma tomato, diced (or half a can of diced tomatoes, drained)
  • 1/3 cup green olives, sliced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 8 small corn or flour tortillas
  • Optional toppings: fresh cilantro, diced white onion, lime wedges, hot sauce, crumbled cotija cheese

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Once it shimmers, toss in your diced onion and green bell pepper. Cook for about 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and just starting to get some color on the edges.
  2. 2Add the minced garlic and cook for another 30 seconds until fragrant — don't walk away here, garlic burns fast and it will ruin your whole vibe.
  3. 3Add the ground beef, breaking it up with a wooden spoon or spatula as it cooks. Season right now with your cumin, chili powder, oregano, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Cook until the beef is browned all the way through, about 5-6 minutes. Drain excess fat if needed, but leave a little — that's flavor.
  4. 4Stir in the tomato paste and diced tomato, mixing it all through the beef. Let that cook for 2 minutes so the tomato paste loses its raw edge and everything starts looking glossy and gorgeous.
  5. 5Add the sliced green olives and stir to combine. Reduce heat to medium-low and let the whole thing simmer uncovered for about 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the liquid cooks down and the picadillo looks saucy but not soupy. Taste and adjust salt.
  6. 6While the picadillo finishes up, warm your tortillas. Throw them directly on a gas burner flame for 15-20 seconds per side for that charred edge, or wrap them in a damp paper towel and microwave for 45 seconds. Do not skip warming your tortillas — cold tortillas are a crime.
  7. 7Build your tacos: spoon a generous amount of picadillo onto each tortilla, then hit it with whatever toppings you like. A squeeze of fresh lime at the end is non-negotiable.
💡 Improv Tip

The olives are not optional — I know some of you are making a face right now, but that briny, slightly salty pop is exactly what balances out the rich beef and warm spices. It's the whole secret to why picadillo tastes different from regular taco meat. Trust the process, try it once, then thank me later.
