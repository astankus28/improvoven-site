Simple 4th of July Cake with Fresh Berries and Whipped Cream

Published June 21, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple 4th of July cake recipe is the one you make when you want to show up to the cookout looking like you really tried — without spending your whole Saturday in the kitchen. It's a classic red, white, and blue flag cake built on a fluffy vanilla sheet cake, topped with whipped cream and fresh strawberries and blueberries. Miami summers are brutal, so we keep it cold, fresh, and no-fuss.

Prep
20 mins
Cook
30 mins
Total
50 mins plus chilling
Serves
12

Ingredients

  • 1 box vanilla or white cake mix (plus eggs, oil, and water called for on the box)
  • 2 cups heavy whipping cream
  • 3 tablespoons powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 pint fresh strawberries, hulled and sliced
  • 1 pint fresh blueberries
  • 1 pinch salt

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 350°F. Prepare the cake mix according to the box directions and pour the batter into a greased 9x13-inch baking pan. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Let the cake cool completely — this is not a step you can skip, or your whipped cream will slide right off and break your heart.
  2. 2While the cake cools, pour your heavy whipping cream into a large cold bowl. Add the powdered sugar, vanilla extract, and pinch of salt. Using a hand mixer or stand mixer, whip on medium-high speed for about 3 to 4 minutes until stiff peaks form. It should look fluffy and hold its shape. Don't overbeat or you'll start heading toward butter territory.
  3. 3Once the cake is completely cool, spread the whipped cream evenly over the entire top using a spatula or the back of a spoon. Go all the way to the edges — don't be shy with it.
  4. 4Now decorate your flag. In the top left corner of the cake, arrange a rectangle of blueberries — that's your starfield. Then create horizontal stripes across the rest of the cake using rows of sliced strawberries for the red stripes, leaving strips of white whipped cream showing between them for the white stripes. It doesn't have to be perfect. It's a party cake, not a geometry exam.
  5. 5Refrigerate the finished cake for at least 30 minutes before serving so everything sets up nicely. Keep it cold right up until you serve it — especially if you're outside in the July heat. Slice into squares and watch it disappear.
💡 Improv Tip

If strawberries at the store are looking sad and expensive, swap them out for raspberries — same red color, sometimes cheaper, and honestly even more flavor. You can also make the cake the night before and refrigerate it overnight uncovered so it firms up, then add the fruit fresh the morning of the party so nothing gets soggy. That's the move.
