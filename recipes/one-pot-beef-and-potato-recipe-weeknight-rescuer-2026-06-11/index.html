One Pot Beef and Potato Recipe (Weeknight Rescuer)

Published June 11, 2026 · Improv Oven

This one pot beef and potato recipe is the kind of thing your abuela would throw together on a Tuesday without even looking at a recipe — just vibes, a little sofrito energy, and whatever's in the fridge. Tender chunks of beef, buttery potatoes, and a savory, slightly smoky broth that makes the whole apartment smell incredible. One pot, one mission, zero drama.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
35 mins
Total
45 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs beef stew meat or chuck, cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 1.5 lbs Yukon Gold or russet potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 2 cups beef broth (or water with 1 bouillon cube)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley for garnish (optional but do it)

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your beef dry with a paper towel — this is the move that gets you that gorgeous brown crust instead of sad gray meat. Season it all over with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and smoked paprika.
  2. 2Heat olive oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Once it's shimmering, add the beef in a single layer — don't crowd it. Sear for 3-4 minutes per side until deeply browned, then remove and set aside. Work in batches if needed.
  3. 3Lower the heat to medium. In the same pot (don't you dare clean it — those browned bits are flavor gold), add the onion and green bell pepper. Cook for 3-4 minutes until softened, scraping up any stuck bits as you stir.
  4. 4Add the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant. Stir in the tomato paste and let it cook for another minute — you want it to deepen in color slightly. That's where the richness comes from.
  5. 5Add the cumin, oregano, diced tomatoes, and beef broth. Stir everything together, then nestle the seared beef back into the pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer. Cover and cook for 20 minutes.
  6. 6Add the cubed potatoes, stir to submerge them in the broth, and cover again. Cook for another 15-20 minutes until the potatoes are fork-tender and the beef is completely soft. Taste and adjust salt.
  7. 7Let it sit for 5 minutes off the heat — the broth thickens up beautifully as it rests. Serve in deep bowls with crusty bread, white rice, or just straight up with a spoon. Finish with fresh cilantro if you've got it.
💡 Improv Tip

If your broth looks too thin at the end, just mash a couple of the potato chunks against the side of the pot and stir — it thickens the whole thing naturally without any cornstarch tricks. And if you've got a leftover bay leaf rolling around in your spice drawer, throw it in when you add the broth. It's a small thing that makes people ask what your secret is.
