Simple Esquites Cups for Your Next Potluck

Published June 2, 2026 · Improv Oven

These simple esquites cups for potluck are the move when you need something people will actually talk about — creamy, smoky, a little spicy, and served in individual cups so there's zero mess and zero drama. It's Mexican street corn off the cob, dressed up just enough to travel well and feed a crowd without breaking the bank. Trust me, you'll be sending the recipe to five people before the party's even over.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
10

Ingredients

  • 6 cups frozen or canned corn kernels (about 4 cans, drained, or two 12oz bags frozen)
  • 2 tablespoons butter or neutral oil
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup cotija cheese, crumbled (feta works in a pinch)
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (adjust to your heat level)
  • 2 limes, juiced (about 3 tablespoons)
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 10 small disposable cups (9oz work great) and spoons for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Drain your canned corn really well or let your frozen corn thaw and pat it dry with a paper towel. Wet corn won't char — it'll just steam — and you want some of that golden color and smoky flavor in here.
  2. 2Heat a large skillet or cast iron pan over medium-high heat until it's genuinely hot. Add your butter or oil, then spread the corn out in as even a layer as possible. Don't touch it for 2 to 3 minutes — let it sit and get some color on the bottom. Stir once, let it sit again. You're going for golden and slightly charred in spots, not gray and sad. Work in two batches if your pan isn't big enough to spread it out properly. This whole process takes about 8 to 10 minutes.
  3. 3While the corn is charring, whisk together your mayonnaise, sour cream, lime juice, chili powder, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and cayenne in a large mixing bowl. Taste it — it should be creamy, tangy, and have a little kick. Adjust the lime or cayenne to your liking.
  4. 4Once the corn is done and has cooled for just a few minutes, add it into the bowl with the creamy mixture and toss everything together well. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
  5. 5Fold in about three-quarters of the cotija cheese and most of the cilantro, saving some of each for topping. The cheese melts slightly into the warm corn and that's a beautiful thing.
  6. 6To assemble for the potluck, spoon the esquites mixture into your individual cups — about half a cup per serving. Top each one with a pinch of the reserved cotija, a little extra chili powder, and a few cilantro leaves. You can also add a tiny squeeze of fresh lime right on top if you're feeling extra.
  7. 7If you're making these ahead, keep the corn mixture in a container and portion into cups right before serving. They hold up beautifully for up to 2 hours at room temp, or store the mix in the fridge for up to 2 days.
💡 Improv Tip

The real secret to great esquites is getting actual char on that corn — don't let it steam and get mushy. If your pan isn't smoking hot or your corn is too wet, you'll miss the whole point. Frozen corn actually chars beautifully once it's properly dried, so don't sleep on it just because it's not fresh off the cob. And if you want to stretch this even further for a bigger crowd, one extra can of corn and a little more of the creamy dressing is all you need.
