Easy Meal Prep with Beans and Rice (Cuban-Style Bowls That Last All Week)

Published May 8, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been looking for easy meal prep with beans and rice that actually tastes good on day four, this is your recipe. We're making Cuban-inspired black bean and rice bowls loaded with sofrito flavor, a little cumin magic, and enough food to carry you through the whole week without getting bored. This is the kind of meal your abuela would approve of — cheap, filling, and honestly just really good.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 4 cups water or low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, roughly chopped (optional but don't skip it)
  • Lime wedges for serving
  • Hot sauce for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Start your rice first so everything finishes around the same time. Combine the rice and water or broth in a medium saucepan over high heat. Once it boils, drop the heat to low, cover tight, and let it cook undisturbed for 18 minutes. Then kill the heat and let it steam with the lid on for another 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork when you're ready.
  2. 2While the rice does its thing, heat the olive oil in a large skillet or saucepan over medium heat. Add the diced onion and green bell pepper and cook for about 5 to 6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they soften and the onion starts to look a little translucent.
  3. 3Add the minced garlic, cumin, oregano, and smoked paprika. Stir everything together and let it cook for about 60 seconds until the garlic is fragrant and the spices bloom into the oil. Your kitchen should smell incredible right now.
  4. 4Add both cans of drained black beans to the skillet. Stir to coat everything in that sofrito base. Pour in about a quarter cup of water if the beans look dry, then season with salt and black pepper. Let the beans simmer on medium-low for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the flavors come together and the beans get a little creamy.
  5. 5Stir in the apple cider vinegar right at the end — this is the move that brightens everything up and makes it taste like it simmered all day. Taste and adjust salt if needed.
  6. 6To serve now, scoop rice into bowls and spoon the beans right over the top. Hit it with fresh cilantro, a squeeze of lime, and your hot sauce of choice. To meal prep, let both the rice and beans cool completely before portioning into airtight containers. They keep in the fridge for up to 5 days and reheat beautifully with just a splash of water.
💡 Improv Tip

The apple cider vinegar at the end is non-negotiable — it's what separates 'beans from a can' energy from 'she actually cooked' energy. Also, if you want to stretch this even further, fry an egg and lay it right on top of the bowl. Runny yolk into black beans and rice is one of life's underrated pleasures and it costs you almost nothing.
