Simple Sheet Pan Meal Prep Chicken and Vegetables (One Pan, All Week)

Published October 1, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple sheet pan meal prep chicken and vegetables recipe is the kind of thing that makes Sunday cooking actually feel worth it — one pan, one oven, and you've got four days of real meals handled. Juicy bone-in chicken thighs roast alongside hearty vegetables in a lemony herb seasoning that smells incredible and tastes even better. It's the antidote to sad desk lunches and the 6pm 'what are we even eating' spiral.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
40 mins
Total
55 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 2 lbs)
  • 3 medium Yukon Gold or red potatoes, cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 2 medium carrots, sliced into coins
  • 1 medium zucchini, cut into half-moons
  • 1 medium red onion, cut into wedges
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 lemon, half juiced and half sliced into rounds
  • Fresh parsley for finishing (optional but worth it)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 425°F and line a large rimmed sheet pan with foil or parchment — this is non-negotiable for easy cleanup and even browning.
  2. 2In a large bowl, whisk together the olive oil, minced garlic, oregano, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, and the juice of half a lemon. This is your all-purpose workhorse marinade.
  3. 3Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels — this step is the secret to crispy skin. Add them to the bowl and toss to coat, then set aside while you prep the vegetables.
  4. 4Add the potatoes, carrots, zucchini, and red onion to the same bowl and toss everything together until coated in whatever marinade is left. Don't stress about perfect coverage — it'll be great.
  5. 5Spread the vegetables out in a single layer across the sheet pan, then nestle the chicken thighs on top, skin-side up. Lay the lemon rounds around the pan — they'll caramelize and add a gentle sweetness to everything underneath.
  6. 6Roast at 425°F for 35 to 40 minutes, until the chicken skin is deeply golden and crispy and the potatoes are fork-tender. If your oven runs cool, give it an extra 5 minutes. The chicken should reach 165°F internal temperature.
  7. 7Pull the pan out and let it rest for 5 minutes before serving or packing into meal prep containers. Scatter fresh parsley over the top if you have it — the green makes everything look a little more put together.
  8. 8Divide into four containers with a mix of chicken and vegetables in each. Refrigerates well for up to 4 days. Reheat uncovered in a 375°F oven for 10 minutes to bring the skin back to life, or just microwave it — no judgment, lunch is lunch.
💡 Improv Tip

The biggest sheet pan meal prep mistake is overcrowding the pan — if your vegetables are piled on top of each other, they'll steam instead of roast and you'll end up with mush. Use the largest pan you own, and if things look tight, grab a second pan and split it. Also, swap the vegetables freely based on what's in your fridge: broccoli, bell peppers, sweet potatoes, and green beans all work beautifully here with zero changes to the seasoning.
