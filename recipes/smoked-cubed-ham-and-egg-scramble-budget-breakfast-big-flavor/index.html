Smoked Cubed Ham and Egg Scramble (Budget Breakfast, Big Flavor)

Published August 9, 2026 · Improv Oven

This smoked cubed ham budget recipe is proof that a bag of diced ham from the grocery store is basically a weekday morning superpower. It comes together in one skillet in about 15 minutes — crispy ham, fluffy eggs, and just enough cheese to make everyone at the table happy. Perfect for when you need something real before 9am without spending real money.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
20 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups smoked cubed ham (about 8 oz — sold pre-diced near the deli section)
  • 6 large eggs
  • ¼ cup milk (any kind)
  • ½ cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • ½ medium yellow onion, diced
  • 1 small bell pepper, any color, diced
  • 1 tablespoon butter or neutral oil
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
  • Pinch of smoked paprika
  • Salt to taste (go easy — the ham brings plenty)
  • Optional: hot sauce or chopped chives for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Crack your 6 eggs into a bowl or measuring cup, add the milk, black pepper, and garlic powder, and whisk everything together until the yolks are fully broken and the mixture looks uniform. Set it aside — you want this ready to go the moment your pan is hot.
  2. 2Heat a large skillet (10 or 12 inch) over medium-high heat and add the butter or oil. Once it's shimmering and hot, add the diced onion and bell pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 3 to 4 minutes until the onion is softened and just starting to pick up a little color.
  3. 3Add the smoked cubed ham to the skillet and spread it out in a single layer. Let it sit undisturbed for 2 minutes so the bottom gets a little caramelized and crispy — this is where the flavor happens. Stir once, then let it go another minute. You want some golden-brown edges on those ham pieces.
  4. 4Reduce the heat to medium-low. Give your egg mixture one more quick whisk and pour it into the skillet over the ham and vegetables. Let the eggs sit untouched for about 30 seconds so the bottom just starts to set.
  5. 5Using a spatula, gently fold and push the eggs from the edges toward the center, turning them in large, lazy curds. Keep the heat low and resist the urge to stir constantly — you want big, soft, pillowy folds, not tiny rubbery bits. Pull the pan off the heat when the eggs are just barely set; they'll finish cooking from the residual heat.
  6. 6Scatter the shredded cheddar over the top and let it melt for 30 seconds to a minute right in the pan. Taste and add salt only if needed. Serve immediately topped with hot sauce or chives if you've got them.
💡 Improv Tip

The single best move here is letting the ham sit and sear before you pour in the eggs. Smoked ham is already cooked and fully safe — what you're chasing is caramelization, which turns that mild saltiness into something deeper and almost sweet. If you skip the sear and just dump everything in together, it still works, but you'll taste the difference. Two minutes of patience pays off.
