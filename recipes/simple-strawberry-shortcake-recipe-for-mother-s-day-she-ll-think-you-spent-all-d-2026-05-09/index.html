Simple Strawberry Shortcake Recipe for Mother's Day (She'll Think You Spent All Day)

Published May 9, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple strawberry shortcake recipe is the Mother's Day move — it looks like you went all out, but it comes together in about 30 minutes with ingredients you probably already have. Fluffy homemade biscuits, juicy macerated strawberries, and a cloud of whipped cream? Mami deserves nothing less. No fancy equipment, no pastry degree, just real love on a plate.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
18 mins
Total
33 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar (for biscuits)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/3 cup cold butter, cut into small cubes
  • 3/4 cup whole milk or buttermilk
  • 1 lb fresh strawberries, hulled and sliced
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar (for strawberries)
  • 1 teaspoon fresh lime juice
  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream
  • 2 tablespoons powdered sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. 1Start with your strawberries first so they have time to get juicy and delicious. Slice them into a bowl, toss with 3 tablespoons of sugar and that squeeze of lime juice — the lime is the Miami twist that makes people ask 'wait, what IS that?' Stir, cover, and let them sit on the counter while you do everything else.
  2. 2Preheat your oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper or just grease it lightly — whatever you've got.
  3. 3In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, 2 tablespoons sugar, and salt. Add your cold butter cubes and use your fingers to press and rub the butter into the flour until it looks like rough, crumbly sand with some pea-sized pieces still in there. Cold butter is the secret to fluffy biscuits, so work fast and don't overthink it.
  4. 4Pour in your milk all at once and stir just until a shaggy dough comes together. Stop mixing the moment it comes together — overworked dough makes tough biscuits and we are NOT doing that to Mami.
  5. 5Drop the dough onto your prepared baking sheet in 6 rough mounds using a large spoon or ice cream scoop. They don't need to be perfect — rustic is charming. Bake for 15 to 18 minutes until they're golden on top and cooked through. Let them cool for at least 5 minutes before you touch them.
  6. 6While the biscuits bake, make your whipped cream. Pour the heavy cream into a cold bowl, add the powdered sugar and vanilla, and beat with a hand mixer or a whisk until soft, billowy peaks form. Don't walk away — it goes from perfect to butter faster than you think. Soft peaks that hold their shape is the goal.
  7. 7To assemble, slice each biscuit in half. Spoon a generous heap of those macerated strawberries and all their syrupy juices onto the bottom half. Add a big dollop of whipped cream, set the biscuit top on at a little angle so it looks fancy, and finish with a few more strawberries on top. Serve immediately and watch Mami's face light up.
💡 Improv Tip

The lime juice in the strawberries is non-negotiable in my kitchen — it wakes everything up and adds that brightness that makes people think you used some fancy secret ingredient. Also, if your butter isn't cold, pop it in the freezer for 10 minutes before you start. Warm butter makes flat biscuits, and flat biscuits on Mother's Day is a whole situation you don't want.
