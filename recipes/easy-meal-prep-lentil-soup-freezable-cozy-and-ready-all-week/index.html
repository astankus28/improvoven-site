Easy Meal Prep Lentil Soup (Freezable, Cozy, and Ready All Week)

Published October 14, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're looking for easy meal prep soup recipes that are freezable and actually exciting to eat, this red lentil soup is exactly what your lunch rotation needs. It comes together in one pot with pantry staples — lentils, canned tomatoes, onion, garlic, and a handful of warm spices — and it freezes like a dream. Make a big batch on Sunday and you've got five days of lunches handled without touching the stove again.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
35 mins
Total
45 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 medium carrots, diced
  • 2 stalks celery, diced
  • 1 ½ teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon ground turmeric
  • ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional, but recommended)
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 ½ cups red lentils, rinsed
  • 6 cups low-sodium vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped (for serving)

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery and cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables have softened and the onion is translucent — about 6 to 8 minutes. Don't rush this step; a little color on the vegetables builds a lot of flavor.
  2. 2Add the garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, turmeric, and cayenne. Stir everything together and let the spices toast in the oil for about 60 seconds, just until fragrant. This is the moment the whole kitchen starts smelling amazing.
  3. 3Pour in the diced tomatoes with their juices and stir to combine. Let it cook for 2 minutes, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot.
  4. 4Add the rinsed red lentils, broth, salt, and black pepper. Stir well and bring the soup to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce to a gentle simmer. Cook uncovered for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils have completely broken down and the soup is thick and creamy. Red lentils dissolve naturally — no blender needed.
  5. 5Taste and adjust seasoning. Stir in the lemon juice — this is non-negotiable, it brightens the whole thing. If the soup is thicker than you'd like, add a splash of broth or water and stir to loosen it up.
  6. 6Serve with fresh parsley or cilantro on top. For freezing: let the soup cool completely, then portion into freezer-safe containers or zip-top bags. It keeps in the fridge for 5 days and in the freezer for up to 3 months. Reheat on the stovetop or microwave with a splash of water or broth to revive the texture.
💡 Improv Tip

Red lentils are your best friend for freezable meal prep soups because they break down completely on their own — no blending, no extra equipment, just a naturally silky, thick soup every time. If you only have green or brown lentils in the pantry, those work too, but give them an extra 10 to 15 minutes and expect a chunkier, more textured result (which is also delicious, just different). A squeeze of lemon right before serving is the secret move — it keeps the soup from tasting flat, especially after it's been sitting in the fridge.
