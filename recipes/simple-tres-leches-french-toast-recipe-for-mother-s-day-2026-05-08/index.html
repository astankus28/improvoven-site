Simple Tres Leches French Toast Recipe for Mother's Day

Published May 8, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple tres leches French toast recipe is the Mother's Day breakfast move that's going to make everyone think you went to culinary school overnight. It's basically classic French toast soaked in that dreamy three-milk mixture — condensed, evaporated, and heavy cream — so every bite is rich, custardy, and absolutely over the top in the best way. Abuela-approved, budget-friendly, and ready in 30 minutes flat.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 8 slices thick-cut bread (brioche, challah, or whatever sturdy bread you have)
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup evaporated milk
  • 1/2 cup sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 2 tablespoons butter (for the pan)
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream (for whipped topping)
  • 1 tablespoon powdered sugar (for whipped topping)
  • Fresh strawberries or sliced mango for garnish
  • Ground cinnamon for dusting

Instructions

  1. 1Make your tres leches soak: In a wide shallow bowl, whisk together the eggs, evaporated milk, condensed milk, 1/4 cup heavy cream, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt until fully combined and smooth. This is your magic mixture — it smells incredible already.
  2. 2Soak your bread: Lay two or three slices of bread into the mixture at a time and let them sit for about 60 to 90 seconds per side. You want the bread to absorb that custard deeply but not fall apart on you. Thick-cut bread is your best friend here — thin sandwich bread will get too soggy.
  3. 3Cook the French toast: Heat a large skillet or griddle over medium heat and melt about half a tablespoon of butter, swirling to coat. Add your soaked bread slices in batches without crowding the pan. Cook 3 to 4 minutes per side until deeply golden brown and slightly puffed. Repeat with remaining slices, adding more butter as needed between batches.
  4. 4Whip your cream: While the last batch cooks, pour the remaining 1/2 cup heavy cream and powdered sugar into a cold bowl and whisk vigorously by hand or with a hand mixer until soft peaks form. Don't stress about it being perfect — rustic dollops are beautiful.
  5. 5Plate it up like you mean it: Stack two or three slices per person, drop a generous cloud of whipped cream on top, scatter fresh strawberries or mango slices around the plate, and finish with a light dusting of cinnamon. Serve immediately and accept all the compliments with grace.
💡 Improv Tip

If you really want to go full tres leches mode, mix a small drizzle of condensed milk with a splash of evaporated milk and pour a little over the finished stack right before adding the whipped cream — it soaks in like a dream and takes the whole thing to another level. Also, day-old bread works even better than fresh here because it absorbs the custard without turning into mush. That slightly stale loaf sitting on your counter? That's your MVP today.
