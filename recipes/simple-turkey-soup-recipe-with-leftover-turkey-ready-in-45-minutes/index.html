Simple Turkey Soup Recipe with Leftover Turkey (Ready in 45 Minutes)

Published November 4, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple turkey soup recipe with leftover turkey is exactly what your fridge has been waiting for — a rich, cozy bowl that turns holiday stragglers into something you'll actually be excited to eat. We're building deep flavor with a sofrito-style base of onion, garlic, celery, and cumin, then letting everything simmer into a hearty, veggie-packed soup that feels like it took all day. It's one of those recipes that proves leftovers aren't a chore — they're a head start.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
35 mins
Total
45 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2–3 cups cooked leftover turkey, shredded or roughly chopped
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 celery stalks, sliced
  • 2 medium carrots, peeled and sliced into coins
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, with juices
  • 6 cups turkey or chicken broth (homemade from the carcass if you have it)
  • 1 cup frozen corn
  • 1 can (15 oz) white beans or chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • Salt to taste
  • Juice of half a lime
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley for garnish (optional but worth it)

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced onion and celery and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and the onion starts to look a little translucent.
  2. 2Add the minced garlic, cumin, oregano, smoked paprika, and black pepper. Stir everything together and let it cook for about 60 seconds — you want those spices to bloom in the oil and get fragrant. This is where the flavor foundation gets built, so don't rush it.
  3. 3Add the sliced carrots and stir to coat them in the spiced base. Pour in the can of diced tomatoes (with all the juices) and the broth. Stir to combine, then bring everything to a gentle boil.
  4. 4Once boiling, reduce the heat to medium-low and let the soup simmer uncovered for about 15 minutes, until the carrots are just tender when you poke them with a fork.
  5. 5Add the shredded turkey, frozen corn, and drained beans. Stir everything in and let it simmer for another 10 minutes so the turkey heats through and absorbs all those good flavors from the broth.
  6. 6Taste the soup and adjust salt as needed — this depends a lot on how salty your broth is, so always taste before adding. Squeeze in the lime juice, give it one final stir, and ladle into bowls. Top with fresh cilantro or parsley if you've got it.
💡 Improv Tip

If you have a leftover turkey carcass, do yourself a favor and make a quick stock before you start — just simmer the bones in water with a halved onion, a few peppercorns, and a bay leaf for 30–45 minutes, then strain it. That liquid is gold and will make your soup taste like you spent way more time on it than you did. No carcass? Store-bought chicken broth works totally fine — just grab a low-sodium version so you control the salt.
