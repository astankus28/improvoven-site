Easy Pollo Asado Recipe Mexican Style

Published June 16, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy pollo asado recipe Mexican style is the kind of thing that makes your whole kitchen smell like a taqueria, and honestly? That's the whole point. We're talking juicy, citrus-marinated chicken with that gorgeous char on the outside — made with stuff you probably already have sitting around. My Miami version leans into those bright achiote and orange flavors that remind me of every abuela's backyard grill.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
40 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs bone-in chicken thighs and drumsticks
  • 1/4 cup fresh orange juice (about 1 large orange)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice (about 1 lime)
  • 3 tablespoons white vinegar
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon achiote paste (or 1.5 teaspoons ground annatto)
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • Fresh cilantro for serving
  • Warm tortillas for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Make your marinade: In a bowl or zip-lock bag, whisk together the orange juice, lime juice, white vinegar, minced garlic, olive oil, achiote paste, cumin, oregano, smoked paprika, chili powder, salt, and pepper until the achiote is fully dissolved and you've got this beautiful rust-colored marinade going.
  2. 2Score the chicken: Using a sharp knife, make 2-3 shallow cuts into the thickest part of each piece of chicken. This isn't just for looks — it lets that marinade get deep into the meat so every single bite is flavorful.
  3. 3Marinate the chicken: Add your chicken pieces to the marinade and toss everything to coat well. Let it marinate for at least 30 minutes at room temperature, or cover and refrigerate for up to 8 hours. The longer the better, but even 30 minutes will get you there.
  4. 4Heat your cooking surface: Get a cast iron skillet, grill pan, or outdoor grill ripping hot over medium-high heat. You want real heat here — that's what gives you the char that makes pollo asado taste like pollo asado and not just regular grilled chicken.
  5. 5Cook the chicken: Remove the chicken from the marinade and let any excess drip off. Cook skin-side down first for about 6-8 minutes without moving it — let it develop that crust. Flip and cook another 6-8 minutes. For bone-in pieces, finish in a 400°F oven for 10-12 minutes or until the internal temperature hits 165°F.
  6. 6Rest and serve: Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes before serving — this keeps all those good juices locked in. Slice it up and serve with warm tortillas, fresh cilantro, sliced onion, and extra lime wedges on the side. Tacos, rice bowls, or straight off the bone — you do you.
💡 Improv Tip

No achiote paste? Don't let that stop you — just swap in a mix of turmeric and extra smoked paprika to get that earthy color and flavor. The orange juice is non-negotiable though, that's the soul of this marinade, so don't skip it or replace it with bottled stuff if you can help it.
