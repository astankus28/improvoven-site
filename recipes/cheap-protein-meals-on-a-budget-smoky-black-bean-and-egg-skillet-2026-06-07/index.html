Cheap Protein Meals on a Budget: Smoky Black Bean and Egg Skillet

Published June 7, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're hunting for cheap protein meals on a budget, this smoky black bean and egg skillet is about to become your new best friend — we're talking under $2 a serving with zero compromise on flavor. It's the kind of dish that feels like something your Abuela would throw together on a Tuesday night without even thinking about it, built on pantry staples you probably already have sitting around. Big protein, big flavor, minimal effort — that's the Improv Oven way.

Prep
8 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
23 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes, drained
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil or vegetable oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh cilantro for topping (optional but highly encouraged)
  • Hot sauce for serving
  • Warm tortillas or crusty bread for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Heat your oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Once it shimmers, toss in the diced onion and cook for about 4 minutes until it softens and starts going translucent — stir it around, don't let it burn.
  2. 2Add the minced garlic and cook for another 60 seconds until fragrant. You'll know it's ready when your kitchen starts smelling amazing.
  3. 3Sprinkle in the cumin, smoked paprika, chili powder, and oregano. Stir everything together and let those spices toast in the oil for about 30 seconds — this step is what separates a good skillet from a great one.
  4. 4Pour in the drained diced tomatoes and stir to combine. Let that cook down for 2 minutes so the flavors start getting friendly with each other.
  5. 5Add both cans of black beans and stir everything together. Season generously with salt and pepper. Let the mixture simmer on medium-low heat for about 3 minutes until it thickens slightly and looks saucy and gorgeous.
  6. 6Use a spoon to create 6 small wells in the bean mixture — evenly spaced around the skillet. Crack one egg into each well carefully so the yolks stay intact.
  7. 7Cover the skillet with a lid and cook for 5 to 7 minutes depending on how you like your eggs. Five minutes gives you runny yolks perfect for dipping, seven minutes gives you fully set yolks. Check at the 5-minute mark and go from there.
  8. 8Pull it off the heat, hit it with fresh cilantro and as much hot sauce as your heart desires, and bring the whole skillet straight to the table. Serve with warm tortillas or bread to scoop up every last bit.
💡 Improv Tip

The secret weapon here is the smoked paprika — don't skip it or swap it for regular paprika. Smoked paprika is what gives this dish that deep, almost meaty flavor that makes people forget there's no actual meat in the pan. Most grocery stores carry it for about $2 a jar and it lasts forever. Keep a jar in your pantry at all times, you'll thank yourself later.
