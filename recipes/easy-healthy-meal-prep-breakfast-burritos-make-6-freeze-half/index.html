Easy Healthy Meal Prep Breakfast Burritos (Make 6, Freeze Half)

Published November 1, 2026 · Improv Oven

These easy healthy meal prep breakfast burritos are the reason Sunday afternoons are worth protecting — one hour of work, six mornings of done. Packed with scrambled eggs, black beans, roasted sweet potato, and a little sharp cheddar, they're filling without being heavy, and they freeze like a dream. Wrap them up, stash them in the freezer, and you've got a real breakfast waiting for you every single day.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
40 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 6 large flour tortillas (burrito size, 10-inch)
  • 8 large eggs
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 medium sweet potato, peeled and diced small (about 1/2-inch cubes)
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup frozen corn, thawed
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 small yellow onion, diced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro or flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped (optional)
  • Hot sauce or salsa, for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 400°F. Toss the diced sweet potato with 1 tablespoon olive oil, a pinch of salt, and the smoked paprika. Spread on a baking sheet in a single layer and roast for 20-22 minutes, flipping once halfway through, until the edges are golden and the centers are tender. Set aside to cool slightly.
  2. 2While the sweet potato roasts, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add the diced onion and bell pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, for 4-5 minutes until softened and just starting to color at the edges.
  3. 3Add the drained black beans and corn to the skillet. Sprinkle in the chili powder, cumin, and garlic powder. Stir everything together and cook for another 2 minutes until the beans are warmed through and coated in spice. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Transfer the filling to a large bowl.
  4. 4Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk well with a pinch of salt and a few cracks of black pepper. Wipe the skillet clean, return it to medium-low heat, and scramble the eggs gently — pull the eggs slowly with a spatula, removing the pan from heat while they're still just slightly underdone. They'll finish cooking as they cool. This keeps your burritos from getting watery in the freezer.
  5. 5Add the roasted sweet potato and scrambled eggs to the bowl with the bean mixture. Toss gently to combine. Fold in the cilantro or parsley if using. Taste and adjust seasoning.
  6. 6Warm your tortillas one at a time in a dry skillet or for 15 seconds in the microwave — this makes them pliable and way less likely to tear. Lay a tortilla flat, add a heaping 3/4 cup of filling down the center, and top with a generous pinch of shredded cheddar. Fold in the sides, then roll from the bottom up, snugging the filling as you go. Repeat with remaining tortillas.
  7. 7To serve now: set burritos seam-side down in a dry skillet over medium heat for 1-2 minutes per side until golden and lightly crisp. To meal prep: let burritos cool completely, then wrap each tightly in aluminum foil. Refrigerate for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 2 months. To reheat from frozen: unwrap from foil, wrap in a damp paper towel, and microwave for 2.5 to 3 minutes, flipping once halfway through. From the fridge, 1.5 to 2 minutes does it.
💡 Improv Tip

The single biggest meal prep burrito mistake is hot filling — if you roll them while everything is steaming, you'll end up with soggy tortillas by day three. Give the filling 10 minutes to cool down before you start rolling, and your burritos will hold together beautifully all week. Also, slightly undercooking the scrambled eggs before assembly is non-negotiable — they'll firm up perfectly after reheating instead of turning rubbery.
