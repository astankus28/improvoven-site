Easy Cuban Black Bean Soup Recipe (Smoky, Creamy, and Done in 45 Minutes)

Published July 31, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy Cuban black bean soup recipe is the kind of thing that makes people think you spent all day in the kitchen — you didn't. It's deeply savory, a little smoky, and finished with a splash of vinegar and a drizzle of olive oil that makes the whole pot sing. Classic Miami comfort food, totally doable on a weeknight, and genuinely cheap to make.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
35 mins
Total
45 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, for finishing
  • Optional toppings: white rice, sour cream, diced white onion, fresh cilantro, lime wedges

Instructions

  1. 1Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced onion and green bell pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, for about 6 to 8 minutes until softened and the onion is translucent and just starting to turn golden at the edges.
  2. 2Add the minced garlic, cumin, oregano, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper. Stir everything together and cook for about 1 minute until the garlic is fragrant and the spices are bloomed into the oil. This step is quick — don't walk away.
  3. 3Add the drained black beans, broth, and bay leaf. Stir to combine, then bring the pot to a gentle boil. Reduce the heat to medium-low and let everything simmer uncovered for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally. The broth will reduce a little and the beans will start to get creamy around the edges.
  4. 4After 20 minutes, use the back of a wooden spoon or a potato masher to smash about a third of the beans directly in the pot. This is what gives Cuban black bean soup that thick, velvety body without needing a blender. Stir everything together and let it simmer for another 5 minutes.
  5. 5Remove the bay leaf. Stir in the apple cider vinegar and sugar — this is the move that wakes up the whole soup. Taste and adjust salt as needed.
  6. 6Ladle into bowls and finish each one with a drizzle of olive oil. Serve with white rice on the side or right in the bowl, and pile on any toppings you like: diced white onion, sour cream, cilantro, a squeeze of lime. Don't skip the lime. Seriously.
💡 Improv Tip

The apple cider vinegar at the end isn't optional — traditional Cuban black bean soup always gets that little hit of acid to balance the earthy beans and cut through the richness. If you don't have apple cider vinegar, white wine vinegar works great, or even a squeeze of lime juice. And if you want a smokier depth without buying anything new, a tiny pinch of cayenne and an extra shake of smoked paprika does the trick.
