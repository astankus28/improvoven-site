Easy Make-Ahead Lunches for Busy Weekdays: Black Bean & Rice Meal Prep Bowls

Published May 7, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you need easy make-ahead lunches for busy weekdays, these black bean and rice bowls are about to become your new Sunday ritual. We're talking pantry staples — canned beans, rice, whatever veggies you've got — coming together into four filling, flavor-packed lunches that keep beautifully in the fridge all week. Very Miami, very affordable, zero lunchtime stress.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long grain white rice
  • 4 cups water or chicken broth
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, drained
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Fresh cilantro for topping (optional but come on)
  • Hot sauce for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Start your rice first so everything finishes around the same time. Combine 2 cups of rice with 4 cups of water or chicken broth in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, then drop the heat to low, cover, and let it cook for 18 minutes. Don't lift that lid — trust the process.
  2. 2While the rice does its thing, heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the diced onion and bell pepper and cook for about 4-5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they soften up and start to smell amazing.
  3. 3Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute, stirring constantly so it doesn't burn. Burnt garlic will haunt your whole kitchen — we're not doing that today.
  4. 4Add the drained black beans, diced tomatoes, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Stir everything together and let it simmer on medium-low for 8-10 minutes so all those flavors can get acquainted.
  5. 5Squeeze the lime juice over the bean mixture, give it a final stir, and taste for seasoning. Adjust salt, pepper, or lime as needed — you're the chef here.
  6. 6Fluff your rice with a fork. Now grab four airtight meal prep containers and divide the rice evenly between them. Spoon the black bean mixture right on top.
  7. 7Let the bowls cool completely before sealing the lids — this is key to keeping the rice from getting soggy. Once cooled, top with fresh cilantro if you've got it, seal them up, and stack them in the fridge. They'll keep beautifully for up to 5 days.
💡 Improv Tip

The secret weapon here is using chicken broth instead of water for the rice — it adds a whole layer of flavor without any extra work. And don't sleep on the hot sauce when you're reheating; a little Cholula or whatever you keep in the door of your fridge wakes the whole bowl right back up. Reheat in the microwave with a damp paper towel over the top to keep the rice from drying out — about 2 minutes does it.
