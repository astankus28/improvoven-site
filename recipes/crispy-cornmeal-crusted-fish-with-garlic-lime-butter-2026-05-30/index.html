Crispy Cornmeal-Crusted Fish with Garlic Lime Butter

Published May 30, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're looking for recipes using cornmeal, this one's about to become your new weeknight go-to — golden, crunchy fish fillets with a garlicky lime butter that tastes like you ordered it somewhere nice. It all comes together in under 30 minutes with ingredients you probably already have sitting around, and it hits that perfect Miami waterfront-meal energy without leaving your kitchen.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
18 mins
Total
28 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup yellow cornmeal
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional but recommended)
  • 1.5 lbs tilapia, catfish, or any firm white fish fillets
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil or canola oil
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro or parsley, chopped
  • Lime wedges for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your fish fillets completely dry with paper towels — this is not the step to rush. Dry fish is the difference between a crust that sticks and one that slides right off in the pan. Season lightly with salt and pepper on both sides.
  2. 2In a shallow bowl or plate, mix together the cornmeal, garlic powder, smoked paprika, cumin, salt, black pepper, and cayenne. Give it a little taste — it should smell smoky and a little spicy. That's your flavor base.
  3. 3Beat your eggs in a separate shallow bowl. Dip each fillet into the egg first, letting the excess drip off, then press it firmly into the cornmeal mixture coating both sides well. Set the coated fillets aside on a plate while you heat your oil.
  4. 4Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. You want the oil hot before the fish goes in — flick a tiny pinch of cornmeal in there, if it sizzles right away you're ready. Cook the fillets 3 to 4 minutes per side without moving them. Let the crust do its thing. You'll know it's ready to flip when it releases easily from the pan.
  5. 5Transfer the cooked fish to a wire rack or paper-towel-lined plate. Don't stack them or that beautiful crust steams and goes soft on you.
  6. 6In the same pan, lower the heat to medium and add the butter. Once it melts and starts to foam, add the minced garlic and cook for about 60 seconds, stirring constantly, until fragrant and just starting to turn golden. Squeeze in the lime juice — it'll bubble up and sizzle dramatically, which is exactly what you want. Stir it together and take it off the heat.
  7. 7Spoon that garlic lime butter right over your fish, scatter the cilantro on top, and serve immediately with extra lime wedges on the side. Rice and black beans, a simple slaw, or even just a cold beer alongside — you're eating well tonight.
💡 Improv Tip

Cornmeal comes in fine and medium grind — medium is the move here because it gives you that satisfying crunch without feeling like you're biting into gravel. If all you've got is fine grind, mix in a tablespoon of flour to help it bind better. And don't skip drying the fish — seriously, that paper towel step is doing more work than it gets credit for.
