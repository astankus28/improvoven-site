Brown Butter Bread Pudding (A Buttered Bread Budget Recipe That Tastes Like a Hug)

Published October 6, 2026 · Improv Oven

This buttered bread budget recipe is the kind of thing that turns a few humble slices of stale bread and a splash of milk into something genuinely special. Brown butter, eggs, a little sugar, and whatever bread you have kicking around are all you need. It works as a cozy breakfast, a lazy weekend brunch, or an honestly impressive dessert — and it costs almost nothing to make.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
35 mins
Total
45 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 6 cups stale bread, torn into rough chunks (any kind — sandwich bread, a leftover baguette, hamburger buns)
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 ½ cups whole milk (or whatever milk you have)
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar, packed
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ½ teaspoon cinnamon
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • Optional: a small handful of raisins, chocolate chips, or chopped nuts

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 350°F and lightly grease an 8x8 or similar baking dish with a little butter or cooking spray.
  2. 2Melt your butter in a small saucepan over medium heat, then keep going — swirling occasionally — until the butter foams, the foam subsides, and the milk solids at the bottom turn golden brown and smell like toasted hazelnuts. This takes about 3 to 4 minutes. Pull it off the heat immediately and let it cool for a minute.
  3. 3In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, granulated sugar, brown sugar, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt until everything is combined. Slowly drizzle in the browned butter while whisking so it incorporates evenly into the custard.
  4. 4Add your torn bread chunks to the bowl and gently fold everything together. Press the bread down into the custard and let it soak for at least 5 minutes — 10 if your bread is very stale and dry. You want every piece to feel saturated. Fold in any optional add-ins here.
  5. 5Pour the whole mixture into your prepared baking dish and spread it out evenly. Press the top down lightly with the back of a spoon so it's level.
  6. 6Bake uncovered for 32 to 38 minutes, until the top is deep golden, the edges are set and slightly pulling away from the pan, and the center jiggles just slightly when you nudge it. It will firm up as it cools.
  7. 7Let it rest for at least 5 to 10 minutes before slicing. Serve warm, straight from the pan. A drizzle of honey, a dusting of powdered sugar, or a small scoop of vanilla ice cream on top are all excellent decisions.
💡 Improv Tip

Don't skip the brown butter step — it's what takes this from 'used up some old bread' to 'wait, you made this from scratch?' The extra two minutes of standing at the stove swirling a pan is absolutely worth it. And genuinely, the staler your bread, the better this turns out because dry bread soaks up the custard without going mushy.
