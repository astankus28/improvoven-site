Easy No Bake Cheesecake Recipe (Creamy, Dreamy, and Foolproof)

Published September 25, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy no bake cheesecake recipe is the dessert you make when you want to impress without turning on the oven — no water baths, no cracked tops, no stress. It comes together with a handful of pantry staples, sets up beautifully in the fridge, and tastes like something from a bakery case. Whether you top it with strawberries, a drizzle of honey, or nothing at all, this one is a total crowd-pleaser.

Prep
20 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
4 hrs 20 mins (includes chilling time)
Serves
8

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs (about 10 full crackers, crushed)
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar (for the crust)
  • 16 oz (2 blocks) cream cheese, softened to room temperature
  • 1 cup powdered sugar, sifted
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice (about half a lemon)
  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream, cold
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional toppings: fresh strawberries, blueberries, cherry pie filling, or a drizzle of honey

Instructions

  1. 1Make the crust: In a medium bowl, mix together the graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, 2 tablespoons of granulated sugar, and a small pinch of salt. Stir until the mixture looks like wet sand and holds together when you press it between your fingers. Press it firmly and evenly into the bottom (and slightly up the sides) of a 9-inch springform pan or a deep pie dish. Use the flat bottom of a measuring cup to pack it down really well. Pop it in the freezer while you make the filling — at least 10 minutes.
  2. 2Whip the cream: Pour the cold heavy whipping cream into a large bowl (or the bowl of a stand mixer). Beat with a hand mixer on medium-high speed until stiff peaks form, about 3 to 4 minutes. You want it thick and cloud-like — it should hold its shape when you lift the beaters. Set aside.
  3. 3Make the cheesecake filling: In a separate large bowl, beat the softened cream cheese with a hand mixer on medium speed until it's completely smooth and fluffy, about 2 minutes. Scrape down the sides of the bowl. Add the sifted powdered sugar, vanilla extract, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt. Beat again until everything is silky smooth and fully combined — no lumps allowed.
  4. 4Fold in the whipped cream: Using a rubber spatula, gently fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese mixture in two or three additions. Use slow, sweeping folds from the bottom of the bowl upward — don't stir aggressively or you'll deflate the air you just whipped in. Keep folding until the mixture is uniform and beautifully fluffy with no white streaks.
  5. 5Assemble the cheesecake: Spoon the filling over the chilled crust and spread it into an even layer with a spatula or the back of a spoon. Smooth out the top as best you can — it doesn't have to be perfect. Give the pan a gentle tap on the counter to settle any air bubbles.
  6. 6Chill until set: Cover the pan loosely with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, but overnight is even better. The longer it chills, the cleaner it slices and the more defined that creamy texture gets. Don't try to rush this step — it's doing all the work for you.
  7. 7Slice and top: When you're ready to serve, add your toppings of choice. Fresh sliced strawberries are classic and beautiful. A spoonful of cherry pie filling feels retro in the best way. Or just a light dusting of powdered sugar if you want to keep it simple. Slice with a sharp knife, wiping the blade clean between cuts for picture-perfect slices.
💡 Improv Tip

The single most important thing in this recipe: your cream cheese must be fully at room temperature before you beat it. Cold cream cheese = lumpy filling, and no amount of mixing will fix lumps once they're in there. Pull it out of the fridge at least an hour before you start. And if you forget? Set the sealed blocks in a bowl of warm (not hot) water for 15 minutes — works like a charm.
