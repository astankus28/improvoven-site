Easy Cuban Ham Croquetas Recipe

Published May 26, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy Cuban croqueta ham recipe is the one I grew up watching abuelas make with leftover ham and a whole lot of love — crispy on the outside, creamy and savory on the inside, pure magic. You don't need fancy equipment or a culinary degree, just a few pantry staples and about 30 minutes. These little guys disappear fast, so you might want to double the batch.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups finely chopped or ground cooked ham (deli ham or leftover ham both work great)
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup finely diced white onion
  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour, divided
  • 1 cup whole milk (or whatever milk you have)
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cumin
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup plain breadcrumbs
  • Vegetable oil for frying (enough for about 1 inch depth in your pan)

Instructions

  1. 1Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until soft and translucent, stirring occasionally. You want it sweet and tender, not browned.
  2. 2Add 1/4 cup of the flour to the pan and stir constantly for about 1 minute to cook out that raw flour taste. It will look like a thick paste — that's exactly what you want.
  3. 3Slowly pour in the milk while whisking continuously to avoid lumps. Keep whisking over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes until the mixture thickens into a smooth, heavy béchamel.
  4. 4Remove from heat and stir in the chopped ham, garlic powder, cumin, salt, and pepper. Mix well until everything is fully combined. Taste it — adjust seasoning now because this is your only chance before they're fried.
  5. 5Spread the ham mixture onto a plate or shallow dish, cover with plastic wrap pressed directly onto the surface, and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes until firm. If you're in a rush, pop it in the freezer for 15 minutes.
  6. 6Set up your breading station: place the remaining 1/2 cup flour in one shallow bowl, beat the eggs in a second bowl, and spread the breadcrumbs in a third bowl.
  7. 7Scoop about 2 tablespoons of the chilled ham mixture and shape it into a small log or cylinder about 2 inches long. Roll it in flour first, then dip in egg, then roll in breadcrumbs. Set on a plate and repeat with the rest of the mixture.
  8. 8Pour vegetable oil into a heavy skillet to about 1 inch deep and heat over medium-high heat until it reaches around 350°F. No thermometer? Drop a breadcrumb in — if it sizzles immediately, you're ready.
  9. 9Fry the croquetas in batches of 4 to 5, turning gently with tongs, for about 2 to 3 minutes total until deep golden brown all around. Don't crowd the pan or the temperature drops and they get greasy instead of crispy.
  10. 10Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate to drain for a minute, then serve immediately. These are best hot with a little mustard or a squeeze of lime on the side.
💡 Improv Tip

The secret weapon here is grinding or finely chopping the ham in a food processor if you have one — you want it almost paste-like so the filling stays together and gets that classic creamy croqueta texture instead of chunky. And seriously, don't skip the chilling step. Cold filling is everything when it comes to keeping these little logs shaped while you bread them.
