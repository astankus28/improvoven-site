Easy Coconut Lime Popsicles (No Churn, No Fuss)

Published July 4, 2026 · Improv Oven

These easy coconut lime popsicles need no churn, no ice cream maker, and honestly no excuse not to make them today. We're talking creamy coconut milk, bright lime zest, and a little sweetness — all poured into molds and frozen until they're the most refreshing thing you've pulled out of your freezer all summer. This is Miami in a popsicle, and your pantry is already halfway there.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
4 hours 10 mins (includes freeze time)
Serves
8 popsicles

Ingredients

  • 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
  • 3 tablespoons honey or agave syrup
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lime juice (about 2 limes)
  • 1 teaspoon lime zest
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • Optional: 2 tablespoons shredded sweetened coconut for texture

Instructions

  1. 1Pour the full can of coconut milk into a medium mixing bowl — don't drain it, you want all that creamy goodness including any coconut cream that settled on top.
  2. 2Add the honey or agave, fresh lime juice, lime zest, and that pinch of salt. Whisk everything together until smooth and the sweetener is fully dissolved, about one minute.
  3. 3Taste it right here. That's important. Want more lime? Add it. Want it sweeter? A little more honey. This is your popsicle, make it yours.
  4. 4If you're using shredded coconut, stir it in now for little chewy pockets of texture throughout.
  5. 5Carefully pour the mixture into popsicle molds, leaving just a tiny bit of space at the top because liquids expand when they freeze.
  6. 6Insert your popsicle sticks, cover the molds, and freeze for at least 4 hours or overnight — overnight is always better if you can wait.
  7. 7When you're ready to eat, run warm water over the outside of the molds for about 15 to 20 seconds and the popsicles will slide right out. Don't yank them — just be patient for a few seconds.
💡 Improv Tip

No popsicle molds? No problem — pour the mixture into small paper cups, cover each one with a small square of foil, then poke a wooden stick through the foil so it stands straight. Freeze the same way, then just peel the cup off. That's the Improv Oven way — work with what you've got.
