Simple One Pan Meal Prep Dinners: Smoky Chicken and Rice

Published June 10, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're looking for simple one pan meal prep dinners that actually taste like someone cooked with love, this smoky chicken and rice is your answer. Everything goes into one skillet — seasoned chicken thighs, rice, beans, and a punchy sofrito base — and comes out tasting like it took way more effort than it did. Make a big batch Sunday night and you're set for the whole week, no sad desk lunches allowed.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
30 mins
Total
40 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1.5 cups long grain white rice
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes with juices
  • 2.5 cups low sodium chicken broth
  • 1/2 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1.5 teaspoons cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional but recommended)
  • Fresh cilantro for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your chicken thighs dry with a paper towel — this is the move that gets you that golden crust, don't skip it. Mix together the cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, salt, and black pepper in a small bowl. Rub that seasoning all over both sides of the chicken like you mean it.
  2. 2Heat the olive oil in a large deep skillet or wide sauté pan over medium-high heat. Once it's shimmering, lay the chicken thighs in a single layer and let them cook undisturbed for about 4 to 5 minutes per side until you get a nice deep golden brown sear. They don't need to be fully cooked through yet. Remove the chicken to a plate and set aside.
  3. 3In the same pan with all those good drippings, drop the heat to medium and add your diced onion and green bell pepper. Cook for about 3 minutes stirring occasionally until softened, then add the garlic and red pepper flakes and cook one more minute until fragrant. Your kitchen should smell incredible right about now.
  4. 4Pour in the rice and stir it around in the pan for about 1 minute so it gets coated in all that flavor and lightly toasted. Then add the diced tomatoes with all their juices and the chicken broth. Stir everything together and scrape up any bits stuck to the bottom of the pan — that's pure flavor right there.
  5. 5Nestle the seared chicken thighs right on top of the rice mixture. Bring everything to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover tightly with a lid, and let it cook for 18 to 20 minutes without lifting the lid. Trust the process.
  6. 6After 20 minutes, check that the rice has absorbed the liquid and the chicken is cooked through to 165°F internal temperature. Scatter the black beans over the top, replace the lid, and let everything sit off the heat for 5 minutes so the beans warm through and the rice finishes steaming.
  7. 7Fluff the rice gently around the chicken with a fork, taste and adjust salt if needed, and hit it with fresh cilantro if you're using it. Divide into four meal prep containers and refrigerate for up to 4 days. This reheats beautifully with just a splash of water to keep the rice fluffy.
💡 Improv Tip

The secret weapon here is not lifting that lid while the rice cooks — every time you peek you let out steam and end up with uneven, crunchy rice. Set a timer, walk away, maybe do the dishes, and trust that the pan is handling it. Also, chicken thighs are your best friend for meal prep because they stay juicy even after reheating, unlike chicken breast which turns into sad rubber by day three.
