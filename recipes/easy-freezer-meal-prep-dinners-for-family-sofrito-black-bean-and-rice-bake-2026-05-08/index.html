Easy Freezer Meal Prep Dinners for Family: Sofrito Black Bean and Rice Bake

Published May 8, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been searching for easy freezer meal prep dinners for family, this is the one you bookmark and come back to every single week — hearty, deeply flavored, and it reheats like a dream. We're talking a Latin-style black bean and rice bake loaded with sofrito, cumin, and whatever protein you've got hanging around, all portioned and frozen so future-you eats like royalty on a Tuesday. This is pure Improv Oven magic — one pan, real ingredients, zero stress.

Prep
20 mins
Cook
40 mins
Total
60 mins
Serves
8 (makes 2 freezer portions of 4 servings each)

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes with juices
  • 2 cups long-grain white rice, uncooked
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth or vegetable broth
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 lb ground turkey or ground beef (optional but highly recommended)
  • 1/2 cup frozen corn kernels
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Fresh cilantro for topping (optional)
  • Shredded cheese for topping (optional, add after reheating)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 375°F. Grab your biggest oven-safe pot or a deep 9x13 baking dish — you're going to need the real estate for this double batch.
  2. 2Heat the olive oil in a large deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. If you're using ground meat, add it now and cook it all the way through, breaking it up as it goes, about 6 to 8 minutes. Season it with a big pinch of salt and pepper right in the pan. Drain any excess fat and set the meat aside.
  3. 3In that same pan, add the diced onion and green bell pepper. Cook over medium heat until they soften and start smelling incredible, about 4 to 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for one more minute — don't walk away here, garlic burns fast.
  4. 4Stir in the tomato paste and let it cook for 2 minutes, stirring constantly. This step is the flavor foundation — that paste caramelizes a little and goes from sharp to rich. Add your cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, and garlic powder, and stir everything together until the spices are bloomed and fragrant.
  5. 5Add the black beans, diced tomatoes with their juices, frozen corn, browned meat if using, and the lime juice. Stir to combine everything into one beautiful, saucy mixture.
  6. 6Pour in the uncooked rice, chicken broth, and water. Stir well so the rice is evenly distributed and not clumping in one spot. Taste the liquid and adjust salt — the rice is going to absorb all of this, so season it properly now.
  7. 7If your pan is oven-safe, cover tightly with a lid or heavy-duty aluminum foil and transfer to the oven. If not, pour everything into a greased 9x13 baking dish and two 8x8 dishes and cover tightly. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until the rice is fully cooked and has absorbed all the liquid. Remove from oven and let it rest covered for 10 minutes — this is non-negotiable, it finishes the rice perfectly.
  8. 8Divide the bake into two equal portions. One goes on the table tonight topped with cilantro and cheese. The other one: let it cool completely to room temperature — about 30 minutes on the counter — before you do anything else.
  9. 9For freezing: Transfer the cooled second portion into a freezer-safe container or a zip-top gallon freezer bag laid flat. Label it with the date and write 'SOFRITO RICE BAKE — 375°F, 25 min covered' so future-you doesn't have to think. It keeps beautifully for up to 3 months in the freezer.
  10. 10To reheat from frozen: Thaw overnight in the fridge, then reheat covered in a 375°F oven for 20 to 25 minutes until steaming hot throughout. Add a tiny splash of broth or water before covering to bring the moisture back. Top with shredded cheese in the last 5 minutes if you want it melty and golden. From frozen with no thaw, bake covered at 375°F for about 50 to 55 minutes.
💡 Improv Tip

The move that separates okay freezer meals from ones your family actually requests: season aggressively before freezing. Freezing dulls flavors slightly, so go a little bolder on the cumin and salt than you think you need. Also, always freeze WITHOUT the cheese on top — add that fresh when you reheat so it stays gooey instead of turning into a weird rubbery layer. And listen, if you don't have ground turkey, canned tuna stirred in at the end, leftover rotisserie chicken, or even just extra beans works perfectly — this recipe literally exists to use what you've got.
