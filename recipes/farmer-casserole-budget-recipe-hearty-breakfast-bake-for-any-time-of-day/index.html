Farmer Casserole Budget Recipe (Hearty Breakfast Bake for Any Time of Day)

Published September 6, 2026 · Improv Oven

This farmer casserole budget recipe is one of those magical dishes that costs next to nothing, feeds a crowd, and tastes like you put way more effort in than you did. Layers of frozen hash browns, eggs, cheese, and whatever meat you've got rattling around the fridge bake together into something impossibly satisfying. We're giving ours a little Miami twist with smoky chorizo and a hit of pickled jalapeño — because comfort food always benefits from a little personality.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
55 mins
Total
1 hr 10 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 3 cups frozen shredded hash browns, thawed (about half a standard 30 oz bag)
  • 2 tablespoons butter or neutral oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, divided
  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 cup whole milk or evaporated milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/4 teaspoon cumin
  • 6 oz Mexican-style chorizo, casings removed (or diced ham, cooked bacon, or leftover sausage)
  • 1/2 medium onion, diced
  • 1/2 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 tablespoons pickled jalapeño slices, roughly chopped (optional but encouraged)
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded cheese, divided (Colby Jack, cheddar, or whatever block cheese you've got)
  • Sour cream or hot sauce, for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish with butter or cooking spray and set it aside.
  2. 2If your hash browns are still a little icy, spread them out on a clean kitchen towel and press gently to remove excess moisture. Dry hash browns = crispier bottom layer, and that matters.
  3. 3In a skillet over medium heat, cook the chorizo (or your chosen meat) for 4–5 minutes, breaking it up as it goes, until cooked through and starting to brown. If there's a lot of rendered fat in the pan, drain off all but about a tablespoon of it.
  4. 4Add the diced onion and green bell pepper to the same skillet and cook another 3–4 minutes until softened and the onion is translucent. Remove from heat and set aside.
  5. 5Press the thawed hash browns into an even layer on the bottom of your greased baking dish. Drizzle with butter or oil, sprinkle with garlic powder and 1/4 teaspoon of the salt, and use your hands or the back of a spatula to press them down firmly. You want a solid base, not a loose pile.
  6. 6Scatter the chorizo and vegetable mixture evenly over the hash brown layer. Sprinkle the pickled jalapeños over the top if you're using them.
  7. 7Spread 1 cup of the shredded cheese over the meat and veggies, reserving the remaining 1/2 cup for the top.
  8. 8In a medium bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, black pepper, smoked paprika, cumin, and the remaining 1/4 teaspoon salt until fully combined and a little frothy.
  9. 9Pour the egg mixture slowly and evenly over the entire casserole, letting it seep down through the layers. Give the dish a gentle shake to help it settle.
  10. 10Sprinkle the remaining 1/2 cup of cheese over the top.
  11. 11Bake uncovered at 350°F for 50–55 minutes, until the eggs are fully set in the center (a knife inserted in the middle should come out clean) and the top is golden and slightly puffed. If the top is browning too fast, loosely tent with foil after 40 minutes.
  12. 12Let the casserole rest for 10 minutes before slicing — this is important, it firms up and cuts much cleaner. Serve with sour cream, hot sauce, or both.
💡 Improv Tip

Make this the night before and refrigerate it unbaked — just assemble everything, cover with foil, and pop it straight into a cold oven in the morning (add about 10 extra minutes to the bake time since it's starting cold). It's a genuine lifesaver for weekend brunch or when you need a big breakfast-for-dinner with zero morning effort.
