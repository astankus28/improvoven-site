Easy Homemade Potato Salad Recipe (Classic, Creamy, and Always a Hit)

Published October 9, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy homemade potato salad recipe is the one you'll make on repeat all summer long — and honestly, all year. It's creamy without being heavy, tangy without being sharp, and comes together with simple pantry staples you probably already have. No fancy techniques, no mystery ingredients — just the kind of potato salad that disappears first at every cookout.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs Yukon Gold or russet potatoes (about 4-5 medium)
  • 3/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons yellow mustard
  • 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more for boiling
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 3 stalks celery, diced small
  • 1/2 small red onion, diced small
  • 3 hard-boiled eggs, peeled and chopped
  • 2 tablespoons sweet pickle relish (or 3 bread-and-butter pickles, chopped)
  • Paprika and fresh parsley for garnish (optional but worth it)

Instructions

  1. 1Peel your potatoes and cut them into roughly 1-inch chunks — you want them uniform so they cook evenly. No need to be precious about it; rustic chunks are totally fine. Place them in a large pot, cover with cold water by about an inch, and add a generous pinch of salt. Starting in cold water helps the potatoes cook through evenly without the outsides getting mushy before the centers are done.
  2. 2Bring the pot to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce to a steady simmer. Cook for 12 to 15 minutes, or until a fork slides into a potato chunk with almost no resistance. You want them fully tender but not falling apart — they should hold their shape when you stir them. Drain well and spread them out on a sheet pan or plate to let the steam escape for about 5 minutes. This step matters: wet potatoes make watery salad.
  3. 3While the potatoes cool slightly, whisk together the mayonnaise, yellow mustard, apple cider vinegar, sugar, salt, pepper, and garlic powder in a large mixing bowl. Taste the dressing — it should be creamy, a little tangy, and just barely sweet. Adjust anything you like here. This is your salad, after all.
  4. 4Add the still-warm potatoes to the dressing bowl. Toss gently to coat — warm potatoes absorb the dressing better than cold ones, and that's how you get flavor all the way through instead of just on the surface. Add in the diced celery, red onion, chopped eggs, and pickle relish. Fold everything together carefully so the potatoes stay in chunks and don't turn to mash.
  5. 5Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving. This is the patience part, but it's worth it — the flavors come together and the texture settles beautifully as it chills. Right before serving, taste again and adjust salt if needed. Dust with paprika and a little chopped parsley if you want it to look like it came from somewhere fancy. It kind of did.
💡 Improv Tip

Salt your potato water generously — it should taste like mild soup, not the ocean. Under-seasoned potatoes are the number one reason potato salad tastes flat even when the dressing is great. You're seasoning from the inside out.
