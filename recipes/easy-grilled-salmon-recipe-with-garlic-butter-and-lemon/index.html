Easy Grilled Salmon Recipe With Garlic Butter and Lemon

Published August 27, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy grilled salmon recipe is the one you'll come back to every single week — it's fast, foolproof, and uses almost nothing beyond what's already in your kitchen. A quick garlic butter baste, a hit of lemon, and about 10 minutes over heat and you've got restaurant-level fish on a weeknight budget. No marinade, no special equipment, no stress.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
12 mins
Total
22 mins
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each, skin-on if possible)
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lemon (half for juice, half for slices)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Fresh parsley or dried herbs to finish (optional but nice)

Instructions

  1. 1Pull your salmon out of the fridge about 10 minutes before cooking — room temperature fish cooks more evenly and won't seize up the moment it hits heat. Pat the fillets completely dry with a paper towel. This is non-negotiable. Wet fish steams instead of searing, and you want that gorgeous golden crust.
  2. 2Mix the smoked paprika, onion powder, salt, and pepper in a small bowl. Drizzle both sides of each fillet with olive oil, then rub the spice mix all over. Don't be shy — get it on the sides too.
  3. 3Heat a grill pan or outdoor grill over medium-high heat. If you're using a grill pan, add just a thin swipe of oil to the surface. If you're on an outdoor grill, scrape and oil the grates well. You want it hot — a drop of water should evaporate immediately when it hits the surface.
  4. 4Place the salmon skin-side DOWN first. Do not touch it, move it, or press it. Let it do its thing for 4 to 5 minutes. You'll see the color change from deep pink to lighter pink creeping up the sides — when it's about halfway up the fillet, it's ready to flip. Gently lift one edge; if it releases cleanly, flip it. If it sticks, give it another 30 seconds.
  5. 5While the salmon cooks on the first side, melt butter in a small saucepan or right in a microwave-safe bowl. Stir in the minced garlic and a squeeze of lemon juice. That's your baste.
  6. 6Flip the salmon flesh-side down and immediately spoon or brush the garlic butter all over the top. Cook for another 3 to 4 minutes depending on thickness. Salmon is done when it flakes easily with a fork but still has the faintest blush of translucency in the very center — that's the sweet spot before it goes dry.
  7. 7Pull the salmon off the heat and let it rest for 2 minutes. Squeeze the remaining lemon over the top, scatter some parsley if you've got it, and serve with whatever's around — rice, crusty bread, a simple salad, roasted veggies. It'll make anything feel like a real dinner.
💡 Improv Tip

The single biggest mistake people make with salmon is overcooking it because they're nervous. Salmon keeps cooking off the heat, so pull it just before you think it's done. If the thickest part flakes when you press it gently with a fork and it's opaque on the outside but barely translucent in the very center, you're golden. Literally.
