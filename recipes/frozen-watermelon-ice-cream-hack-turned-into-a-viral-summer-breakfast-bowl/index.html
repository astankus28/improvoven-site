Frozen Watermelon Ice Cream Hack (Turned Into a Viral Summer Breakfast Bowl)

Published August 19, 2026 · Improv Oven

The frozen watermelon ice cream hack went viral for good reason — two ingredients, a blender, and five minutes gets you something that genuinely tastes like soft-serve. We're leaning into it for breakfast by topping it with granola, fresh fruit, and a drizzle of honey so it doubles as a legit morning smoothie bowl. No ice cream maker, no dairy, no guilt about eating dessert before noon.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
10 mins (plus freezing time)
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 4 cups seedless watermelon, cubed and frozen solid (about half a small melon)
  • 2 tablespoons full-fat coconut milk or regular heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon honey or agave, plus more for drizzling
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1/2 cup granola (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1/2 cup fresh berries (strawberries, blueberries, or whatever you have)
  • 2 tablespoons nut butter (peanut, almond, or sunflower — optional but highly recommended)
  • Fresh mint leaves for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1The night before (or at least 4 hours ahead): Cut your watermelon into rough 1-inch cubes and spread them in a single layer on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Freeze until completely solid. This is the whole secret to the hack — the watermelon has to be frozen hard, not just cold. Once frozen solid, you can transfer the cubes to a zip-lock bag and keep them in the freezer until you're ready.
  2. 2Pull the frozen watermelon cubes straight from the freezer into your blender or food processor. Do not let them thaw — you want them icy and firm. Add the coconut milk (or cream), honey, vanilla, and pinch of salt.
  3. 3Blend on high, stopping to scrape down the sides every 20 seconds or so. It will look crumbly at first — don't panic, that's normal. Keep blending and scraping until it comes together into a thick, creamy, soft-serve texture. This takes about 60 to 90 seconds total. If your blender is struggling, add just one more tablespoon of coconut milk to loosen it up, but go slowly — you want thick, not soupy.
  4. 4Work fast once it's blended — watermelon ice cream melts quicker than dairy ice cream. Scoop it immediately into two cold bowls (stick your bowls in the freezer for a few minutes while the blender runs if you want to buy yourself a little extra time).
  5. 5Top each bowl with granola, fresh berries, and a thin drizzle of nut butter and honey. Add a few mint leaves if you have them. Eat immediately with a big spoon and feel smug about your breakfast choices.
💡 Improv Tip

The only way this hack fails is if your watermelon isn't frozen all the way through — partially frozen cubes turn into pink liquid fast. Spread them in a single layer when you freeze them and give them a full four hours minimum. Also, buy watermelon when it's on sale and freeze a whole batch of cubes so you have this waiting for you all week. At peak summer, a small seedless watermelon runs two to three dollars and makes enough breakfast bowls for the whole week.
