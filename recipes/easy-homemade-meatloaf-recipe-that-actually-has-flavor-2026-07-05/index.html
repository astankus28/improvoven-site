Easy Homemade Meatloaf Recipe That Actually Has Flavor

Published July 5, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy homemade meatloaf recipe is the one your family is going to ask for on repeat — no dry, sad slices, just juicy, savory goodness with a sticky-sweet glaze on top. We're talking real flavor from pantry staples you already have, because that's how we do it at Improv Oven. Think of it as your classic American comfort food with a little Miami soul.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
1 hour
Total
1 hour 15 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ground beef (80/20 works best)
  • 1/2 cup plain breadcrumbs
  • 1/3 cup whole milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 medium yellow onion, finely grated or minced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 cup ketchup (for the glaze)
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar (for the glaze)
  • 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar (for the glaze)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 350°F. While it heats up, grab a large mixing bowl and combine the breadcrumbs and milk. Let them sit together for about 2 minutes so the breadcrumbs soak up the milk — this is your secret weapon against a dry meatloaf.
  2. 2Add the egg, onion, garlic, Worcestershire sauce, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper to the bowl. Mix everything together until combined.
  3. 3Add your ground beef to the bowl. Using your hands — yes, get in there — gently mix everything together until just combined. Do not overmix or you'll end up with a dense, tough loaf. You want it holding together, not worked to death.
  4. 4Transfer the meat mixture onto a rimmed baking sheet lined with foil or into a 9x5 loaf pan. Shape it into a loaf about 9 inches long and 4 inches wide if using the baking sheet — the free-form method lets the sides caramelize beautifully.
  5. 5In a small bowl, whisk together the ketchup, brown sugar, and apple cider vinegar to make your glaze. Spread half of it over the top of the meatloaf.
  6. 6Bake at 350°F for 45 minutes. Pull it out, spread the remaining glaze on top, then return it to the oven for another 15 minutes, or until the internal temperature reads 160°F on a meat thermometer.
  7. 7Let the meatloaf rest for 10 minutes before slicing. This is non-negotiable — cutting too soon lets all those juices run out onto the pan instead of staying in your food. Slice, serve, and accept the compliments.
💡 Improv Tip

Grating the onion instead of dicing it is a total game changer — it melts right into the meat so you get all that onion flavor without crunchy bits, and it keeps moisture locked in the whole time it bakes. Also, if you have sofrito in your fridge, swap two tablespoons in for the onion and garlic. You're welcome.
