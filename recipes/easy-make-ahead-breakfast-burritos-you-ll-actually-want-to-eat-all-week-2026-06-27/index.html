Easy Make-Ahead Breakfast Burritos You'll Actually Want to Eat All Week

Published June 27, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been searching for make ahead breakfast recipes that are actually easy and don't taste like sad sad sadness by day three, these freezer breakfast burritos are your answer. We're talking scrambled eggs, black beans, cheese, and a smoky sofrito-spiced potato filling all wrapped up tight and ready to grab on your way out the door. Miami mornings move fast — this recipe moves with you.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
8 burritos

Ingredients

  • 8 large flour tortillas (burrito size)
  • 8 large eggs
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 medium russet potatoes, diced small
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar or pepper jack cheese
  • 1/2 green bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 white onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Optional: hot sauce, sour cream, or salsa for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Start with the potatoes because they take the longest. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced potatoes in a single layer, season with salt, pepper, smoked paprika, and garlic powder. Let them sit undisturbed for 3-4 minutes so they get a nice golden crust, then stir and cook another 5-6 minutes until fork-tender and crispy on the outside. Remove from the pan and set aside.
  2. 2In the same skillet, add the remaining tablespoon of oil over medium heat. Toss in the onion and green bell pepper and cook for about 3 minutes until softened. Add the garlic and cumin, stir it all together, and cook one more minute until it smells incredible — that's your sofrito base doing its thing.
  3. 3Add the drained black beans to the skillet with the sofrito veggies. Stir to combine, season with a pinch of salt, and let it cook together for 2 minutes. Then fold the crispy potatoes back in. Give it a taste and adjust seasoning. Turn heat to low while you do the eggs.
  4. 4In a separate pan or after clearing some space, scramble your 8 eggs over medium-low heat with a little salt. Cook them low and slow, pulling them gently with a spatula. You want them just barely set — slightly underdone is actually perfect here because they'll finish cooking a little when you reheat the burrito later.
  5. 5Now let everything cool for about 5-10 minutes before assembling. This is important — hot fillings create steam inside the burrito and make your tortilla soggy by tomorrow. Warm your tortillas for 15-20 seconds in the microwave so they're pliable and won't crack when you roll them.
  6. 6Lay a tortilla flat and layer on some eggs, the bean-potato filling, and a generous pinch of shredded cheese. Don't overfill — about 3/4 cup of filling total per burrito is the sweet spot. Fold in the sides, then roll it up tightly from the bottom. Wrap each burrito snugly in aluminum foil.
  7. 7For the fridge: store wrapped burritos in an airtight bag for up to 5 days. For the freezer: place the foil-wrapped burritos in a zip freezer bag and freeze for up to 3 months. To reheat from fridge, unwrap and microwave for 1.5-2 minutes, flipping halfway. From frozen, microwave for 3-4 minutes flipping halfway, or bake in the foil at 350°F for about 30 minutes for that crispy exterior.
💡 Improv Tip

The number one make-ahead burrito mistake is wrapping them up while the filling is still hot — you'll end up with a steamy, soggy mess by day two. Let everything cool completely, even throw the fillings in the fridge for 10 minutes if you're impatient. Also, don't skip warming the tortilla before rolling — a cold, stiff tortilla will crack on you every single time and there's nothing sadder than a burrito that fell apart before you even got to eat it.
