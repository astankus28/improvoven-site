Simple Tahini Lemon Dressing Salad with Chickpeas and Cucumber

Published August 6, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple tahini lemon dressing salad is the kind of thing that sounds fancy but comes together in about 15 minutes with stuff you probably already have sitting around. Creamy, nutty tahini meets bright citrus, and suddenly a pile of vegetables tastes like something from a real restaurant. We're keeping the Miami spirit alive with a little cumin and fresh herbs — because even a salad deserves some personality.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
15 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 large English cucumber, halved and sliced into half-moons
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups chopped romaine or mixed greens
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley or cilantro, roughly chopped
  • 3 tablespoons tahini
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice (about 1 large lemon)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated or minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/4 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2–4 tablespoons cold water (to thin the dressing)

Instructions

  1. 1Make the tahini lemon dressing first so the flavors have a few minutes to come together. In a small bowl or jar, whisk together the tahini, fresh lemon juice, olive oil, grated garlic, cumin, and smoked paprika. It will seize up and look thick at first — that's normal. Add cold water one tablespoon at a time, whisking until the dressing is smooth, pourable, and about the consistency of a creamy salad dressing. Season generously with salt and pepper, then taste it. It should be bright, nutty, and just a little garlicky. Adjust lemon or salt as needed.
  2. 2While your dressing rests, prep your salad. Add the chopped romaine or mixed greens to a large bowl. Layer on the sliced cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and drained chickpeas. Scatter the fresh parsley or cilantro over the top.
  3. 3Drizzle the tahini lemon dressing over the salad — start with about half and add more to your taste. Toss everything gently so the dressing coats the vegetables and the chickpeas get nice and glossy.
  4. 4Serve immediately as a light lunch or side dish. If you want to bulk it up into a full meal, pile it over warm rice, tucked inside a pita, or alongside a piece of grilled chicken or salmon. The leftovers (dressing stored separately) keep well in the fridge for the next day.
💡 Improv Tip

Tahini brands vary a lot — some are smooth and runny, others are thick and a little bitter. If yours is on the bitter side, add a tiny pinch of sugar or a drizzle of honey to the dressing and it'll mellow right out. And always use cold water to thin it, not warm — it keeps the texture silky instead of gluey.
