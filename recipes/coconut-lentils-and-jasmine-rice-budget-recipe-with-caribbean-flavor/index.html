Coconut Lentils and Jasmine Rice (Budget Recipe with Caribbean Flavor)

Published July 28, 2026 · Improv Oven

This coconut lentils and jasmine rice budget recipe is the kind of meal that makes you feel like you figured something out — creamy, fragrant, and deeply satisfying for under $2 a serving. Green lentils simmer down into a silky, spiced coconut broth that soaks into fluffy jasmine rice in the best way possible. It's got that Miami-Caribbean soul — think cumin, allspice, a little scotch bonnet heat — and it comes together in one pot in about 40 minutes.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
35 mins
Total
45 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup green or brown lentils, rinsed
  • 1 cup jasmine rice, rinsed
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) coconut milk
  • 2 cups water or vegetable broth
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or neutral oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 scotch bonnet or habanero pepper, minced (or 1/4 teaspoon cayenne if that's what you've got)
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Fresh cilantro or green onion for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the oil in a medium saucepan or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and starting to go golden at the edges.
  2. 2Add the garlic and scotch bonnet pepper and cook for another 60 seconds, stirring constantly so the garlic doesn't burn. Your kitchen should smell incredible right about now.
  3. 3Add the cumin, allspice, smoked paprika, turmeric, black pepper, and salt. Stir everything together and let the spices toast in the oil for about 30 seconds — this is what gives the dish that deep, rounded flavor instead of tasting raw and flat.
  4. 4Pour in the rinsed lentils and stir to coat them in the spiced oil. Then add the coconut milk and water (or broth). Stir, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to medium-low. Cover and cook for 20 minutes.
  5. 5After 20 minutes, the lentils should be just about tender and the liquid will have reduced quite a bit. Stir in the rinsed jasmine rice, making sure everything is combined and the rice is submerged in the liquid. If it looks dry, add a splash of water — you want enough liquid to cook the rice without it scorching.
  6. 6Cover the pot tightly, reduce heat to low, and cook for 15 more minutes without lifting the lid. Then take it off the heat and let it sit, still covered, for 5 minutes. This resting step matters — it lets the rice finish steaming and keeps everything fluffy instead of gummy.
  7. 7Lift the lid, squeeze in the lime juice, fluff gently with a fork, and taste for salt. Serve topped with fresh cilantro or sliced green onion, and a little extra lime on the side if you like things bright.
💡 Improv Tip

Green lentils hold their shape better here than red lentils, which tend to dissolve — that contrast of creamy broth and slightly toothsome lentils against fluffy rice is the whole point. That said, if red lentils are what you've got, use them and just expect more of a porridge-style texture, which is honestly cozy in its own right. Either way, rinse both the lentils and the rice before cooking — it makes a real difference in the final texture.
