Simple Chicken Salad Sandwich Meal Prep You'll Actually Look Forward To

Published June 11, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple chicken salad sandwich meal prep is the move when you want something satisfying ready in the fridge all week without spending a ton of money or time. We're talking creamy, tangy, a little crunchy — the kind of thing you'd find at a Cuban deli but made right in your own kitchen. Make it Sunday and you're set through Friday, no sad desk lunches allowed.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 large boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 1.5 lbs)
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon yellow mustard
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 2 celery stalks, finely diced
  • 1/4 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 8 slices sandwich bread of your choice
  • Optional: lettuce leaves and sliced tomato for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Fill a medium pot with enough water to cover your chicken breasts by about an inch. Season the water generously with salt, a smashed garlic clove if you have one, and a bay leaf if you're feeling fancy. Bring it to a boil over medium-high heat.
  2. 2Once boiling, add your chicken breasts, reduce the heat to medium, and let them poach for 15 to 18 minutes until cooked through with no pink in the center. You can also use rotisserie chicken here — no judgment, that's a power move.
  3. 3While the chicken cooks, combine your mayonnaise, yellow mustard, and apple cider vinegar in a large mixing bowl and stir it together. This little trio is the flavor base that makes everything pop.
  4. 4Add your finely diced celery and red onion to the bowl. The celery brings the crunch and the red onion brings a little attitude — both are essential.
  5. 5Once your chicken is cooked, remove it from the pot and let it rest for 5 minutes so it's easier to handle. Then shred it using two forks or your hands, pulling it apart into bite-sized pieces — not too fine, you want some texture in there.
  6. 6Add the shredded chicken to your bowl. Season everything with garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper. Fold it all together gently until the chicken is fully coated and everything is evenly distributed.
  7. 7Taste it and adjust — more mayo if you want it creamier, a splash more vinegar if you want more tang. This is your chicken salad, make it yours.
  8. 8For meal prep, store the chicken salad in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days. Keep your bread separate so it doesn't get soggy. When you're ready to eat, pile it onto your bread with lettuce and tomato and you are good to go.
💡 Improv Tip

The secret weapon here is the apple cider vinegar — it cuts through the richness of the mayo and makes the whole thing taste brighter and fresher. Don't skip it even if it sounds random, it's doing a lot of heavy lifting. And if your chicken salad sits in the fridge overnight, the flavors meld together and it tastes even better the next day, so this is truly one of those rare recipes that rewards patience.
