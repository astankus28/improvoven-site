Simple Batch Cooking Recipes: Brown Butter Shortbread Bars with Sea Salt

Published October 24, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you've been searching for simple batch cooking recipes that actually deliver on flavor, these brown butter shortbread bars are your answer — one pan, six pantry ingredients, and enough bars to last the whole week. They come together in about 30 minutes and get better as they sit, which makes them basically perfect for batch cooking. Think buttery, slightly nutty, melt-in-your-mouth shortbread with a flaky sea salt finish that makes people think you went to culinary school.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
16 bars

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter
  • 2/3 cup powdered sugar, sifted
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon fine salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon flaky sea salt (for topping — Maldon or any flaky salt works)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 325°F. Line an 8x8-inch or 9x9-inch square baking pan with parchment paper, leaving a little overhang on the sides so you can lift the bars out later. No parchment? Butter the pan generously — it'll still work.
  2. 2Brown your butter: add the butter to a small saucepan over medium heat. Let it melt and then keep cooking, swirling the pan occasionally, until the butter turns a deep golden amber and smells like toasted hazelnuts — about 4 to 5 minutes. Watch it closely near the end because it goes from perfect to burnt fast. Pour it immediately into a large mixing bowl and let it cool for 10 minutes. You want it warm but not hot.
  3. 3Whisk the powdered sugar and vanilla extract into the browned butter until smooth and combined. It'll look a little glossy and almost caramel-colored — that's exactly right.
  4. 4Add the flour and fine salt to the bowl. Switch to a spatula or wooden spoon and stir until a soft, slightly crumbly dough comes together. It won't look like cookie dough — it'll be more like damp sand that holds when you press it. That's perfect.
  5. 5Press the dough evenly into your prepared pan. Use the bottom of a flat measuring cup or a glass to really press it down — you want it compact and smooth on top, about 1/2 inch thick. Sprinkle the flaky sea salt evenly over the surface.
  6. 6Bake for 22 to 26 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden and the center looks set but still pale. It will feel slightly soft when you pull it out — that's okay, it firms up as it cools. Don't overbake; shortbread is supposed to be tender, not crunchy.
  7. 7Let the bars cool completely in the pan on a wire rack — at least 45 minutes. This is non-negotiable; if you cut them warm, they'll crumble. Once fully cooled, lift out using the parchment, set on a cutting board, and slice into 16 squares or fingers. Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 7 days — they get even better on day 2 and 3.
💡 Improv Tip

Brown butter is the whole game here — don't skip it or sub it for just melted butter. Those little brown bits at the bottom of the pan (called milk solids) are pure flavor, so scrape every last drop into your bowl. If you're nervous about burning it, pull it off the heat the moment it smells nutty. And for batch cooking purposes, you can double this recipe and bake in a 9x13 pan — same temp, just add 3 to 5 minutes to the bake time.
