Vegetarian Blessing Budget Recipe: Golden Lentil & Spinach Soup

Published November 8, 2026 · Improv Oven

This vegetarian blessing budget recipe is exactly what it sounds like — a warm, nourishing pot of food that makes you feel genuinely taken care of without spending more than a few dollars. Red lentils, wilted spinach, and a bloom of warm spices come together in under 40 minutes into something that tastes like it simmered all day. It's the kind of meal that makes you feel rich even when you're watching your wallet.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
30 mins
Total
40 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 4 cups vegetable broth (or water with 1 bouillon cube)
  • 3 cups fresh spinach (or 1 cup frozen, thawed and squeezed dry)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional, to taste)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon
  • Crusty bread or pita, for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a medium pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced onion with a pinch of salt and cook, stirring occasionally, for about 5 to 6 minutes until softened and lightly golden around the edges.
  2. 2Add the minced garlic, cumin, turmeric, smoked paprika, and cayenne (if using). Stir everything together and let it cook for 60 to 90 seconds — you're just waking up those spices. The smell at this point is going to be incredible.
  3. 3Pour in the diced tomatoes (with their juices) and stir to combine, scraping up any bits stuck to the bottom of the pot.
  4. 4Add the rinsed red lentils and vegetable broth. Stir well, bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to a gentle simmer. Cook uncovered for 20 to 22 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils are completely soft and starting to fall apart. The soup will naturally thicken as the lentils break down.
  5. 5Stir in the spinach and let it wilt for 2 minutes. Taste the soup and adjust salt and black pepper as needed.
  6. 6Squeeze in the lemon juice and give it one final stir. Ladle into bowls and serve hot with crusty bread or warm pita on the side for dunking.
💡 Improv Tip

Red lentils are your secret weapon here — unlike green or brown lentils, they don't need soaking and they dissolve into the broth naturally, giving you that creamy, almost silky texture with zero extra effort. If your soup gets thicker than you like after sitting (it will), just stir in a splash of water or broth when you reheat it and it'll come right back to life.
