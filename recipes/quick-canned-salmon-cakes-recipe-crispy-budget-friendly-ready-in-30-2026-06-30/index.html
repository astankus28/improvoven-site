Quick Canned Salmon Cakes Recipe (Crispy, Budget-Friendly, Ready in 30)

Published June 30, 2026 · Improv Oven

This quick canned salmon cakes recipe is the kind of thing your abuela would throw together on a Tuesday and somehow make it taste like a whole event. We're talking crispy on the outside, tender on the inside, and packed with flavor from stuff you already have in the pantry. Serve them with a squeeze of lime and some hot sauce and watch everyone act like you cooked all day.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (14.75 oz each) pink or red salmon, drained
  • 1/2 cup plain breadcrumbs (plus a little extra for coating)
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon yellow mustard
  • 1/4 cup white or yellow onion, finely diced
  • 1/4 cup green bell pepper, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil or vegetable oil for frying
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges for serving
  • Hot sauce for serving (optional but highly recommended)

Instructions

  1. 1Drain your canned salmon really well — press it against the strainer with a fork to get as much liquid out as possible. If your salmon has bones and skin, no stress, just pick out the big bones or mash the small soft ones right in (they're actually full of calcium). Flake the salmon into a large mixing bowl.
  2. 2Add the diced onion, green bell pepper, and minced garlic to the bowl. These three are your flavor foundation, so don't skip them.
  3. 3Add the egg, mayo, mustard, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Mix everything together with a fork until well combined.
  4. 4Add the breadcrumbs and fold them in. You want a mixture that holds together when you press it but isn't dry or stiff. If it feels too wet, add breadcrumbs one tablespoon at a time. If it feels too dry, add a tiny splash of water or another squeeze of mayo.
  5. 5Divide the mixture into 8 equal portions and shape each one into a patty about 3/4-inch thick. Press them gently but firmly so they hold their shape. Optional: press the outside of each patty lightly into extra breadcrumbs for an extra crispy crust.
  6. 6Heat your oil in a large skillet over medium heat. You want the oil hot but not smoking — a small piece of breadcrumb dropped in should sizzle right away.
  7. 7Cook the salmon cakes in batches, about 3 to 4 minutes per side, until they're deep golden brown and crispy. Don't crowd the pan or they'll steam instead of fry. Resist the urge to flip them early — let the crust form.
  8. 8Transfer to a plate lined with paper towels to drain for a minute. Serve immediately with lime wedges and hot sauce on the side.
💡 Improv Tip

The number one reason salmon cakes fall apart is too much moisture — drain that can like your dinner depends on it, because it does. Press, squeeze, do what you gotta do. And if you have 20 minutes to let the shaped patties chill in the fridge before frying, they'll hold together even better and get even crispier in the pan.
