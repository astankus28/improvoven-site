Buffalo Tuna Mac Budget Recipe (Creamy, Spicy, and Ready in 20 Minutes)

Published September 15, 2026 · Improv Oven

This buffalo tuna mac budget recipe is the kind of midday meal that makes you feel like you actually have your life together — and it costs next to nothing. Canned tuna gets tossed with hot sauce, a creamy buffalo-style sauce, and stirred right into tender macaroni for something that's equal parts comfort food and fridge clean-out win. Think buffalo chicken mac, but smarter, faster, and easier on your wallet.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
20 mins
Serves
3

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups dry elbow macaroni
  • 2 cans (5 oz each) tuna in water, drained
  • 3 tablespoons hot sauce (Frank's RedHot or any Louisiana-style)
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons cream cheese (about 1 oz), softened
  • ½ cup shredded cheddar or any melting cheese you have
  • ¼ cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon onion powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 stalks celery, thinly sliced (optional but highly recommended)
  • Sliced green onions or parsley for topping

Instructions

  1. 1Cook the elbow macaroni in well-salted boiling water according to package directions until just al dente — usually about 7 to 8 minutes. You want a little bite left because it'll finish cooking in the sauce. Before draining, scoop out about ¼ cup of the starchy pasta water and set it aside. Drain the pasta and set it aside too.
  2. 2While the pasta cooks, mix your buffalo tuna. In a bowl, combine the drained tuna, hot sauce, mayonnaise, and a pinch of black pepper. Stir it well and taste it — if you want more heat, add another splash of hot sauce. Set this aside.
  3. 3In the same pot you cooked the pasta (less dishes, always), melt the butter over medium-low heat. Add the cream cheese and milk and stir constantly until the cream cheese melts into a smooth, loose sauce — about 2 minutes. If it looks a little lumpy at first, just keep stirring. It'll come together.
  4. 4Add the garlic powder, onion powder, and shredded cheddar to the pot. Stir until the cheese melts fully and the sauce is creamy. If it feels too thick, splash in a little of that reserved pasta water to loosen it up.
  5. 5Add the cooked macaroni to the cheese sauce and toss to coat everything evenly. Then fold in the buffalo tuna mixture and the sliced celery if you're using it. Stir gently — you want everything combined but you're not trying to mash the tuna into oblivion.
  6. 6Taste and adjust. More hot sauce? Go for it. Need salt? Add a pinch. Want it looser? A tiny splash more milk does the trick. Serve warm, topped with sliced green onions or a little parsley, and an extra drizzle of hot sauce if you're feeling bold.
💡 Improv Tip

Don't skip the reserved pasta water — it's the secret weapon here. The starch in it helps the sauce cling to the mac and keeps everything from getting gluey as it sits. Also, celery isn't just a garnish in this one. It adds that cool, crunchy contrast that makes every bite feel like actual buffalo dip energy. If you've got a little crumbled cotija or queso fresco hanging around, sprinkle some on top — it's a Miami move that cuts the heat beautifully.
