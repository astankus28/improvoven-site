Fast Homemade Burger Recipe (Juicy, Done in 20 Minutes)

Published May 26, 2026 · Improv Oven

This fast homemade burger recipe is your answer when you want something real on the table without a whole production — no special equipment, no fancy ingredients, just a seriously good burger made from stuff you already have. We're talking juicy, seasoned beef, toasted buns, and a quick sauce that tastes like you put in way more effort than you did. Miami move: a little sofrito in the patty mix takes this from basic to 'wait, what did you put in this?'

Prep
10 mins
Cook
10 mins
Total
20 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef (80/20 is your best friend here)
  • 1 tablespoon sofrito (store-bought is totally fine)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices American or cheddar cheese
  • 1 tablespoon butter (for toasting buns)
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon ketchup
  • 1 teaspoon hot sauce (like Cholula or Tabasco)
  • 1 teaspoon pickle juice or apple cider vinegar
  • Lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles for topping (whatever you have)

Instructions

  1. 1Make your quick sauce first so the flavors have a minute to come together: stir together the mayo, ketchup, hot sauce, and pickle juice in a small bowl. Taste it, adjust the heat to your liking, and set it aside.
  2. 2In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, sofrito, garlic powder, onion powder, Worcestershire sauce, cumin, salt, and pepper. Mix gently with your hands — just enough to combine everything. Overmixing makes tough burgers, so stop as soon as it looks uniform.
  3. 3Divide the meat into 4 equal portions and shape them into patties about 3/4 inch thick. Press a slight indent in the center of each one with your thumb — this keeps them from puffing up into a meatball while they cook.
  4. 4Heat a cast iron skillet or heavy pan over medium-high heat until it's genuinely hot, about 2 minutes. You want a good sear, not a steam. Place the patties in the pan and don't touch them for 3 to 4 minutes. You're building a crust.
  5. 5Flip the patties once. For medium doneness, cook another 3 minutes. In the last minute of cooking, lay a cheese slice on each patty and cover the pan with a lid or a piece of foil to melt the cheese quickly. Remove patties and let them rest for 2 minutes.
  6. 6In the same pan, drop the heat to medium and melt the butter. Place the buns cut-side down and toast them for about 1 minute until golden. This step is non-negotiable — a toasted bun holds everything together and adds so much texture.
  7. 7Build your burgers: spread the sauce on both sides of the bun, add your patty, then pile on whatever toppings you have. Serve immediately while everything is hot and the cheese is still melty.
💡 Improv Tip

The sofrito is the secret weapon here — it adds that little something people can't quite place but definitely notice. If you don't have it, mince up a quarter of an onion, a clove of garlic, and a bit of green pepper super fine and stir it into the meat. Same energy, same result. Also, resist the urge to press down on the patties while they cook — all that juice you're squeezing out is flavor you're losing forever.
