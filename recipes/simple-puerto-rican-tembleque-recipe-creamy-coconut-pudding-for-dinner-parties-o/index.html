Simple Puerto Rican Tembleque Recipe (Creamy Coconut Pudding for Dinner Parties or Any Night)

Published August 7, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple Puerto Rican tembleque recipe is one of those magical desserts that feels fancy but costs almost nothing and comes together in about 20 minutes on the stove. Tembleque — the name literally means 'wobbly' in Spanish — is a silky, coconut milk pudding that's been showing up on Puerto Rican tables for generations, and once you make it, you'll understand why it never left. It's the kind of thing you whip up while dinner is winding down, pop in the fridge, and bring out to serious applause.

Prep
5 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
20 mins (plus 2–4 hours chilling)
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (13.5 oz each) full-fat coconut milk
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon (plus more for topping)
  • optional: 1 tablespoon shredded sweetened coconut for garnish

Instructions

  1. 1Pour both cans of coconut milk into a medium saucepan. Add the sugar, cornstarch, salt, and ground cinnamon. Before you turn on the heat, whisk everything together until the cornstarch is fully dissolved — no lumps. This step matters, so take 60 seconds and do it right.
  2. 2Set the saucepan over medium heat and cook, stirring constantly with a whisk or wooden spoon. Don't walk away — tembleque goes from liquid to set pretty quickly once it gets going, and you want to be there for it.
  3. 3After about 8 to 12 minutes, the mixture will thicken noticeably and start to pull away from the sides of the pan. It should look like a thick, glossy pudding. Stir in the vanilla extract, then remove from heat.
  4. 4Lightly grease a 9-inch round cake pan, a large mold, or individual ramekins with a tiny bit of neutral oil or cooking spray — just a swipe. Pour the warm tembleque mixture in evenly.
  5. 5Let it cool on the counter for about 15 minutes, then cover loosely and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, ideally 4. It needs that time to fully set into that iconic jiggly, sliceable texture.
  6. 6To serve, run a thin knife or offset spatula around the edges of the mold. Place a serving plate face-down on top of the pan, then flip it in one confident motion. The tembleque should release cleanly. Dust generously with ground cinnamon and add shredded coconut if you're feeling it. Slice and serve cold.
💡 Improv Tip

Full-fat coconut milk is non-negotiable here — the light stuff won't give you that rich, creamy set. If your tembleque feels like it's taking forever to thicken on the stove, bump the heat up just slightly and keep stirring. And if it cracks a little on top when you unmold it? Dust it with cinnamon and call it rustic. It'll taste exactly the same.
