Simple Quinoa Bowl Recipe with Roasted Sweet Potato and Tahini Drizzle

Published October 29, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple quinoa bowl recipe is the kind of meal that makes you feel like you really have your life together — and it comes together in about 30 minutes with stuff you probably already have. Roasted sweet potato, creamy tahini, a handful of greens, and some warm spices give this bowl a serious personality without asking much from your wallet. It works for lunch, dinner, or meal prep — honestly whenever hunger hits.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dry quinoa, rinsed
  • 2 cups water or vegetable broth
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed into 1-inch pieces
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 3 cups baby spinach or arugula
  • 1/4 cup red onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 tablespoons tahini
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice (about 1 lemon)
  • 1 tablespoon warm water
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated or minced
  • Optional: fresh parsley, red pepper flakes, or a drizzle of hot sauce for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 425°F and line a large baking sheet with foil or parchment. The higher heat is what gives you those golden, slightly caramelized edges on the sweet potato — don't skip it.
  2. 2Toss the cubed sweet potatoes and drained chickpeas together on the baking sheet with 1.5 tablespoons of olive oil, the cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Spread everything out in a single layer — crowding the pan steams instead of roasts, and we want crispy edges here.
  3. 3Roast for 22 to 25 minutes, flipping once halfway through, until the sweet potatoes are tender and starting to brown and the chickpeas are slightly crispy. Let them do their thing while you handle everything else.
  4. 4While the veggies roast, combine the rinsed quinoa and water or broth in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce to a low simmer, cover, and cook for 13 to 15 minutes until the liquid is absorbed and the little white tails have popped out of the grains. Remove from heat and let it sit covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
  5. 5Make the tahini drizzle: whisk together the tahini, lemon juice, warm water, grated garlic, and a pinch of salt in a small bowl. It'll look like it's seizing up at first — that's normal. Just keep whisking and it'll come together into a smooth, pourable sauce. Add a tiny splash more water if it's too thick.
  6. 6To build your bowls, divide the warm quinoa between four bowls as the base. Add a handful of baby spinach or arugula — the warmth from the quinoa will gently wilt it just enough. Pile on the roasted sweet potato and chickpeas, scatter some sliced red onion over the top, and finish with a generous drizzle of tahini sauce.
  7. 7Serve immediately with fresh parsley, a pinch of red pepper flakes, or a few dashes of your favorite hot sauce if you want a little kick. These bowls also pack up great for lunch the next day — just keep the tahini drizzle on the side until you're ready to eat.
💡 Improv Tip

Tahini can be stubborn — some brands are thicker than others right out of the jar. If yours is super thick, start by stirring it well inside the jar first (the oil separates and settles at the top), then build your sauce. A good tahini drizzle is the secret weapon of this bowl — don't rush it.
