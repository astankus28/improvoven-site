Simple Low Budget Meal Prep Plan: Rice, Beans, and Sofrito Chicken

Published May 9, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple low budget meal prep plan is what my abuela would call 'cooking smart' — one big cook session on Sunday and you've got flavorful, filling meals all week for under $15 total. We're talking juicy sofrito-marinated chicken thighs, fluffy rice, and creamy black beans that taste like they came from a ventanita in Little Havana. Stack it in your containers and cure that refrigerator blindness for good.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
40 mins
Total
55 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs bone-in chicken thighs (skin-on, cheapest cut at the store)
  • 2 cups long grain white rice
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil (or vegetable oil)
  • 1 tablespoon cumin
  • 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon sazon seasoning (or just a pinch of turmeric and annatto if you have it)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 4 cups chicken broth (or water with a bouillon cube)
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges for serving
  • Fresh cilantro for garnish (optional but worth it)

Instructions

  1. 1Make your quick sofrito base first: heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced onion and green bell pepper and cook for about 5 minutes until softened and starting to look a little golden. Add the minced garlic and stir for one more minute until fragrant — your kitchen should smell incredible right about now.
  2. 2Season the chicken thighs generously on both sides with cumin, garlic powder, smoked paprika, sazon, salt, and pepper. Don't be shy — this is where all your flavor is coming from.
  3. 3Push the sofrito veggies to the side of the pan and add the remaining tablespoon of oil. Sear the chicken thighs skin-side down for about 4-5 minutes until golden brown and crispy. Flip and sear the other side for 3 minutes. Remove the chicken and set aside — it doesn't need to be fully cooked yet.
  4. 4In the same pan with all those gorgeous drippings, add the diced tomatoes and stir everything together, scraping up any bits from the bottom of the pan. Add the chicken broth and bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. 5Nestle the chicken thighs back into the pan, cover, and let everything cook together on medium-low heat for 25-30 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and reads 165°F internally. The sauce will thicken up beautifully.
  6. 6While the chicken cooks, make your rice: combine 2 cups of rice with 4 cups of water (or broth for extra flavor), a pinch of salt, and a drizzle of oil in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce to the lowest heat, cover tightly, and cook for 18 minutes. Remove from heat and let it steam with the lid on for 5 more minutes. Fluff with a fork.
  7. 7Warm the black beans in a small saucepan with a splash of water, a pinch of cumin, a little garlic powder, and salt. Let them simmer for 5-10 minutes until creamy and well-seasoned. You can lightly mash a spoonful against the side of the pot to thicken the broth — this is the move.
  8. 8Now meal prep time: grab 4 airtight containers and divide everything evenly — a scoop of rice, a ladle of black beans, and one or two pieces of chicken with some of that tomato sofrito sauce spooned right on top. Add a lime wedge to each container if eating fresh, or just keep a lime in the fridge to squeeze on when you're ready to eat. Garnish with cilantro if you've got it.
  9. 9Store containers in the fridge for up to 4 days. To reheat, microwave for 2-3 minutes with a damp paper towel loosely covering the container to keep everything moist and delicious. Dinner is officially handled, friend.
💡 Improv Tip

The real secret to making this meal prep plan taste different every day? Keep a few mix-ins in your fridge — hot sauce for Tuesday, a fried egg on top for Wednesday, a spoon of sour cream for Thursday. Same base, totally different vibe each day. Your wallet and your taste buds will both thank you.
