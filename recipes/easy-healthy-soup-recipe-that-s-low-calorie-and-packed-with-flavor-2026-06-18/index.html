Easy Healthy Soup Recipe That's Low Calorie and Packed with Flavor

Published June 18, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy healthy soup recipe is low calorie but tastes like you spent all day in the kitchen — we're talking under 150 calories a bowl with zero sacrifice on flavor. It's a big pot of sofrito-kissed vegetable and white bean soup that comes together fast with pantry staples you probably already have. Miami approved, abuela would be proud.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced (or one 14oz can diced tomatoes)
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1 can (15oz) white beans or cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups chopped kale or spinach
  • 1 medium zucchini, chopped
  • 4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Juice of half a lime
  • Fresh cilantro for serving

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the diced onion and green bell pepper and cook for about 4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they soften up and start smelling amazing.
  2. 2Add the minced garlic and cook for 1 more minute — just until it turns fragrant. Don't walk away here, garlic burns fast and that bitterness will follow you through the whole pot.
  3. 3Stir in the diced tomatoes, cumin, oregano, smoked paprika, and red pepper flakes if you're using them. Let everything cook together for 2 minutes so those spices bloom in the oil and the tomatoes break down a little.
  4. 4Pour in the vegetable broth and stir to combine everything. Bring the pot up to a gentle boil over medium-high heat.
  5. 5Add the white beans and zucchini. Reduce the heat to medium and let it all simmer together for about 10 minutes until the zucchini is just tender but not mushy.
  6. 6Drop in the chopped kale or spinach and stir it in. Cook for another 2 minutes — kale needs a little longer, spinach will wilt almost immediately.
  7. 7Squeeze in the lime juice, taste it, and adjust salt and pepper as needed. That lime at the end is non-negotiable, it brightens the whole thing.
  8. 8Ladle into bowls, top with fresh cilantro, and serve hot. This soup is even better the next day once everything has had time to hang out together.
💡 Improv Tip

If your broth is looking a little thin and you want more body without adding calories, scoop out about a cup of the beans, mash them with a fork, and stir them back in — it thickens the soup naturally and you didn't add a single extra thing.
