Vietnamese Shaken Beef Budget Recipe — Served Over Eggs for Breakfast

Published August 24, 2026 · Improv Oven

This Vietnamese shaken beef budget recipe takes the bold, garlicky, umami-packed flavors of bò lúc lắc and turns them into a seriously satisfying breakfast — served over a runny fried egg instead of a restaurant salad. It sounds fancy, it costs almost nothing, and it comes together in about 20 minutes with pantry staples you probably already have. Miami mornings just got way more interesting.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • ¾ lb beef sirloin or chuck steak, cut into 1-inch cubes (or whatever cut is on sale)
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce (or hoisin sauce if that's what you've got)
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced (divided)
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil (divided)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
  • 2 slices thick toast or 1 cup cooked white rice (your call)
  • ½ small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon fish sauce (optional but really worth it)
  • Fresh cilantro or green onions for topping (optional)
  • Pinch of red pepper flakes or sliced jalapeño for heat (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1In a bowl, combine the soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, black pepper, and half the minced garlic. Add the beef cubes and toss well to coat. Let this marinate for at least 5 minutes while you get everything else ready — even a quick sit makes a real difference.
  2. 2Slice your red onion thin and toss it in a small bowl with the lime juice and a pinch of sugar. Let it quick-pickle while you cook. This little step adds brightness that wakes the whole dish up.
  3. 3Heat a cast iron skillet or heavy pan over HIGH heat until it's really hot — you want a serious sear. Add half the oil and let it shimmer. Add the beef in a single layer, don't crowd it. Let it sit for 60-90 seconds untouched so it gets a good crust.
  4. 4Now shake the pan — or toss the beef — to flip the cubes and sear the other sides. Add the remaining garlic, toss everything together, and cook another 60-90 seconds. The beef should be browned on the outside and still a little pink inside if you like medium. Pull it off the heat and drizzle with fish sauce if you're using it. Set aside.
  5. 5In a separate nonstick pan, melt the butter over medium heat. Crack your eggs in and fry them sunny-side up or over-easy — you want a runny yolk because it becomes part of the sauce when it breaks over the beef.
  6. 6Toast your bread or warm your rice. Plate it up: toast or rice on the bottom, shaken beef piled on top, fried egg laid right over the beef, pickled onions scattered over everything, and a handful of cilantro or green onions if you've got them. Hit it with red pepper flakes or jalapeño if you want heat. Eat immediately.
💡 Improv Tip

The single biggest move with shaken beef is using a screaming hot pan — don't be shy about it. If your pan isn't hot enough, the beef steams instead of sears and you lose all those crispy, caramelized edges that make this dish special. Cast iron is your best friend here. And if beef sirloin feels expensive, chuck steak or even beef stew meat cut down into cubes works beautifully at a much lower price point — just make sure you cut it small so it cooks fast.
