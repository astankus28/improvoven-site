Simple Watermelon Salad Recipe for Summer (With a Miami Twist)

Published May 21, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple watermelon salad recipe is the kind of thing Miami summers were made for — cold, juicy, a little salty, and ready in under 10 minutes. We toss sweet watermelon with cucumber, fresh mint, lime juice, and a crumble of cotija cheese for that Latin flair that takes it from basic to unforgettable. No cooking, no stress, just a bowl of pure summer.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
0 mins
Total
10 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 6 cups seedless watermelon, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1 medium cucumber, halved lengthwise and sliced
  • 1/3 cup cotija cheese, crumbled (or feta if that's what you've got)
  • 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves, roughly torn
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice (about 1 big lime)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/4 teaspoon chili powder or Tajín
  • Pinch of salt
  • Pinch of black pepper

Instructions

  1. 1Cube your watermelon into roughly 1-inch pieces and toss them into your biggest, prettiest bowl — this salad deserves to be seen.
  2. 2Add the sliced cucumber right on top of the watermelon. You want that crunch to balance the juicy sweetness.
  3. 3Drizzle the olive oil and lime juice over everything. Don't skip the lime — it's the whole vibe. Give it a gentle toss so the fruit gets coated.
  4. 4Sprinkle in the chili powder or Tajín, the pinch of salt, and the black pepper. Toss again lightly.
  5. 5Scatter the crumbled cotija cheese over the top and finish with the torn mint leaves. Serve immediately while everything is cold and fresh.
💡 Improv Tip

Cold watermelon is non-negotiable here — if yours has been sitting on the counter, throw the cubed pieces in the fridge for 20 minutes before you build the salad. And don't toss the cheese in, always add it on top so it stays crumbly and doesn't disappear into the mix.
