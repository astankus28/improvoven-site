Simple BBQ Chicken Recipe (Easy Stovetop or Grill)

Published June 30, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple BBQ chicken recipe is so easy it almost feels like cheating — we're talking pantry staples, one pan, and about 30 minutes standing between you and something seriously delicious. No fancy equipment, no marinade you have to start the night before, just bold flavor built fast the way we do it in Miami. Whether you're feeding the family on a Tuesday or throwing something together last minute, this one's got you covered.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs bone-in chicken thighs or drumsticks (cheapest cut, most flavor)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional, for heat)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 3/4 cup BBQ sauce (store-bought is totally fine)
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar or white vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon hot sauce (optional but recommended)

Instructions

  1. 1Pat your chicken pieces completely dry with paper towels — this is non-negotiable. Dry chicken browns and caramelizes beautifully; wet chicken just steams and turns sad. Take an extra 30 seconds and do this step.
  2. 2In a small bowl, mix together the garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, cumin, salt, black pepper, and cayenne if you're using it. Rub this spice blend all over every piece of chicken, getting under the skin if you can. This dry rub is your flavor foundation.
  3. 3Heat your oil in a large skillet or cast iron pan over medium-high heat. If you're grilling, get those grates hot and oiled. You want real heat here so the chicken sears and doesn't just simmer.
  4. 4Place the chicken skin-side down and let it cook undisturbed for 6 to 7 minutes until the skin is golden brown and releases easily from the pan. Flip and cook another 6 minutes on the other side. Resist the urge to move it around — let it do its thing.
  5. 5While the chicken cooks, stir together your BBQ sauce, vinegar, and hot sauce in a small bowl. The vinegar cuts through the sweetness and brightens everything up — trust the process.
  6. 6Once the chicken is nearly cooked through (internal temp around 155°F), reduce the heat to medium-low and spoon the BBQ sauce mixture generously over each piece. Let it cook another 4 to 5 minutes, flipping once and basting again, until the sauce thickens and gets sticky and the internal temperature hits 165°F.
  7. 7Pull the chicken off the heat and let it rest for 5 minutes before serving. That rest time lets the juices redistribute so every bite stays juicy instead of running all over your plate the second you cut in.
💡 Improv Tip

If your BBQ sauce is on the sweeter side — which most store-bought ones are — that vinegar and hot sauce combo is doing real work to balance it out. You can also swap in a splash of orange juice for a little Miami citrus energy. And listen, chicken thighs and drumsticks are almost always cheaper per pound than breasts AND they're harder to dry out, so they are genuinely the move here for a budget-friendly meal that actually tastes like you tried.
