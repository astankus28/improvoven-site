Simple Tahini Lemon Dressing Salad with Crispy Chickpeas

Published May 11, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple tahini lemon dressing salad is the one you're going to make on repeat — creamy, bright, a little nutty, and it comes together in about ten minutes with stuff you already have in your pantry. It's got that Miami energy where everything feels a little more alive than it needs to be, and that's exactly the point. Throw it over greens, grain bowls, roasted veggies — this dressing plays well with everything.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 large head romaine lettuce, chopped (or whatever greens you have)
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 English cucumber, sliced into half-moons
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, roughly chopped
  • 3 tablespoons tahini
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice (about 1 large lemon)
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced or grated
  • 2 tablespoons warm water, plus more to thin
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon honey or maple syrup
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 400°F. Pat the chickpeas super dry with a paper towel — and I mean really dry, because moisture is the enemy of crispy. Spread them on a baking sheet, drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle on the cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper, and toss everything together. Roast for 20 to 25 minutes, shaking the pan halfway through, until they're golden and crispy. Let them cool slightly while you build the rest of the salad.
  2. 2While the chickpeas are doing their thing in the oven, make the tahini lemon dressing. In a small bowl or jar, whisk together the tahini, fresh lemon juice, minced garlic, olive oil, honey, and a pinch of salt. It's going to look thick and a little seized up at first — that's normal, don't panic. Add the warm water one tablespoon at a time and whisk until it loosens into a smooth, pourable consistency. Taste it and adjust — more lemon if you want more brightness, more water if it's too thick.
  3. 3Chop and prep all your salad ingredients. Add the romaine to a large bowl, then layer on the cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, and fresh parsley. There's no wrong way to do this — just make it look like something you'd be excited to eat.
  4. 4Drizzle the tahini lemon dressing generously over the salad and toss everything together until the greens are nicely coated. Top with the warm crispy chickpeas right before serving so they stay crunchy. Serve immediately and watch it disappear.
💡 Improv Tip

If your tahini has been sitting in the pantry for a while and it's separated with oil on top, stir it really well before you measure it out — that thick paste at the bottom will make your dressing gummy and uneven. Also, this dressing keeps in the fridge for up to five days in a sealed jar, and it thickens up as it sits, so just add a splash of warm water and whisk it back to life.
