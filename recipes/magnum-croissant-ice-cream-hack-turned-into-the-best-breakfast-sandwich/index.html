Magnum Croissant Ice Cream Hack (Turned Into the Best Breakfast Sandwich)

Published August 16, 2026 · Improv Oven

The magnum croissant ice cream hack that broke the internet is delicious — but we flipped it into a morning meal that'll make you question every breakfast you've ever eaten. Picture a buttery, flaky croissant stuffed with a creamy coconut-vanilla egg custard filling, pan-toasted until golden, and finished with a drizzle of condensed milk and fresh mango. It's the vibe of that viral frozen treat, but warm, eggy, and totally breakfast-legal.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
15 mins
Total
25 mins
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 large croissants (store-brand or day-old is perfect)
  • 3 large eggs
  • 3 tablespoons canned coconut milk (or whole milk)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 2 tablespoons sweetened condensed milk (for drizzling)
  • 1/2 ripe mango, thinly sliced (or 1 banana, sliced)
  • Pinch of salt
  • Powdered sugar for dusting (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1Slice your croissants in half lengthwise like you're opening a book, but don't cut all the way through — leave a little hinge at the back so they stay together like a clamshell. This is what gives you that stuffed, melty pocket moment.
  2. 2In a shallow bowl or baking dish, whisk together the eggs, coconut milk, vanilla extract, sugar, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt until smooth and fully combined. This custard is the magic — it's what transforms your croissant into something that tastes like a warm, creamy dream.
  3. 3Gently press each open croissant face-down into the egg custard mixture and let it soak for about 60 to 90 seconds per side. You want the custard to soak into the layers but not turn the croissant into a soggy mess. Think French toast energy, croissant format.
  4. 4Heat a non-stick skillet or cast iron pan over medium heat and melt the butter until it starts to foam. Lay your soaked croissants cut-side down first and cook for about 2 to 3 minutes until golden and slightly crispy on the inside faces.
  5. 5Flip the whole croissant over and cook the rounded outside for another 2 minutes until deep golden brown. The layers should be puffed, caramelized, and smelling like a Miami bakery on a Saturday morning.
  6. 6Remove from heat and open each croissant gently. Tuck in your mango slices (or banana), drizzle condensed milk generously over the fruit and the warm custard inside, then close it back up.
  7. 7Dust with powdered sugar if you want to be extra about it — and honestly, be extra about it. Serve immediately while everything is still warm and the condensed milk is pooling into all the layers.
💡 Improv Tip

Day-old croissants from the bakery markdown bin are actually better here — they're drier and absorb the custard without falling apart. Fresh croissants can get a little too soft and won't hold up in the pan as well. If you can't find mango, a ripe plantain sliced thin and caramelized in a little butter for 2 minutes is an incredible swap and very Miami.
