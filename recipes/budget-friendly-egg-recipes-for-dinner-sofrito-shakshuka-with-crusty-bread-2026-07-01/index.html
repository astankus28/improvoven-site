Budget-Friendly Egg Recipes for Dinner: Sofrito Shakshuka with Crusty Bread

Published July 1, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're hunting for budget-friendly egg recipes for dinner, this sofrito shakshuka is about to become your new weeknight hero — we're talking a full, satisfying meal for under five bucks. It's a classic Middle Eastern dish done Miami-style, with a rich tomato sauce built on sofrito, cumin, and a little kick of heat. Eggs poach right in the sauce, and when you tear into it with crusty bread, it feels way fancier than what you actually spent.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
30 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup sofrito (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 loaf crusty bread or 4 slices toast, for serving
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley, a small handful for garnish

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large, deep skillet over medium heat. Once it shimmers, add the diced onion and cook for about 4 minutes until it softens and starts to go translucent — stir it around so nothing burns.
  2. 2Add the minced garlic and cook for one more minute until fragrant. Your kitchen should start smelling really good right about now.
  3. 3Stir in the sofrito and let it cook down for 2 to 3 minutes. This is the move that gives the whole dish that deep, savory Miami backbone — don't skip this step.
  4. 4Add the cumin, smoked paprika, red pepper flakes, salt, and black pepper. Stir everything together and let the spices toast in the oil for about 30 seconds.
  5. 5Pour in the crushed tomatoes and stir well to combine everything. Bring the sauce to a gentle simmer over medium-low heat and let it cook uncovered for about 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it thickens slightly.
  6. 6Taste the sauce and adjust salt or heat to your liking — this is your kitchen, make it yours.
  7. 7Use a spoon to create 6 small wells in the sauce, spaced evenly around the pan. Crack one egg into each well, being careful not to break the yolks.
  8. 8Cover the skillet with a lid and cook for 5 to 7 minutes. Check at 5 minutes — you want the whites fully set but the yolks still soft and runny. If you like firm yolks, give it the full 7 or even 8 minutes.
  9. 9Remove from heat, scatter fresh cilantro or parsley over the top, and bring the whole pan straight to the table. Serve with big torn pieces of crusty bread to scoop up every last bit of that sauce.
💡 Improv Tip

Sofrito is the secret weapon here — if you can grab a jar at any Latin grocery store or even a big supermarket, keep it stocked in your fridge at all times. It's basically a flavor bomb in a jar that makes eggs, rice, beans, and soups taste like you cooked for hours. No sofrito? Blend together half a green pepper, half an onion, a few garlic cloves, and a handful of cilantro — done.
