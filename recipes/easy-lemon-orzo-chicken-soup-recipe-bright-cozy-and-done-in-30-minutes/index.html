Easy Lemon Orzo Chicken Soup Recipe (Bright, Cozy, and Done in 30 Minutes)

Published July 23, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy lemon orzo chicken soup recipe is the kind of bowl that fixes everything — bright, savory, a little silky, and deeply comforting without being heavy. It comes together fast with pantry staples, and that hit of fresh lemon at the end makes it taste like you actually tried. Think of it as classic chicken noodle's sunnier, more interesting cousin.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
25 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 medium carrots, sliced into coins
  • 2 celery stalks, sliced
  • 6 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 cups cooked shredded chicken (rotisserie works great)
  • 3/4 cup dry orzo pasta
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice (about 1 large lemon)
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped (optional but worth it)

Instructions

  1. 1Heat the olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it softens and turns translucent. Add the garlic and cook for another 30 seconds until fragrant — just until you can smell it.
  2. 2Add the carrots and celery to the pot and stir everything together. Pour in the chicken broth and bring it up to a gentle boil over medium-high heat. This usually takes about 5 minutes.
  3. 3Once the broth is boiling, add the orzo, dried oregano, thyme, salt, and pepper. Stir well and reduce the heat to a medium simmer. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring every couple of minutes so the orzo doesn't stick to the bottom, until the pasta is just tender.
  4. 4Stir in the shredded chicken and let it warm through for about 2 minutes. Reduce the heat to low — you want the soup hot but not aggressively boiling for the next step.
  5. 5In a small bowl, whisk together the eggs and lemon juice until smooth and combined. This is your avgolemono-style moment — it thickens the broth slightly and makes it incredibly silky. Slowly ladle about a cup of hot broth from the pot into the egg mixture while whisking constantly. This tempers the eggs so they don't scramble.
  6. 6Pour the tempered egg-lemon mixture back into the pot while stirring gently. The broth will turn slightly creamy and opaque — that's exactly what you want. Add the lemon zest, taste for salt, and adjust as needed.
  7. 7Ladle into bowls, scatter fresh parsley over the top if you have it, and serve immediately with crusty bread or crackers on the side.
💡 Improv Tip

Orzo keeps absorbing liquid as it sits, so if you have leftovers, the soup will thicken up quite a bit in the fridge — that's normal. Just splash in a little extra broth when you reheat it and it comes right back. Also, don't skip the egg-tempering step — it sounds fussy but takes about 60 seconds and it's the thing that makes this soup feel restaurant-special instead of just good.
