Easy Potato Salad Recipe for BBQ Season (The One Everyone Asks For)

Published May 25, 2026 · Improv Oven

This easy potato salad recipe is the BBQ side dish that disappears first every single time — creamy, tangy, and packed with flavor without requiring a culinary degree. We keep it classic but with a little Miami soul: a touch of mustard, plenty of herbs, and that balance of richness and brightness that makes people go back for a third scoop. Make it the night before and watch it become the star of the whole cookout.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
20 mins
Total
35 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs Yukon Gold or russet potatoes, cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 1 tbsp salt (for boiling water)
  • 3/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika (plus more for garnish)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 3 hard-boiled eggs, roughly chopped
  • 3 stalks celery, diced small
  • 1/2 small red onion, diced small
  • 3 tbsp fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped
  • 2 green onions, sliced thin

Instructions

  1. 1Add your potato chunks to a large pot and cover with cold water by about an inch. Add the tablespoon of salt — don't skip this, it seasons the potatoes from the inside out. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then cook for 12 to 15 minutes until the potatoes are fork-tender but not falling apart. You want them to hold their shape in the salad.
  2. 2While the potatoes cook, whisk together the mayonnaise, yellow mustard, apple cider vinegar, sugar, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and a generous pinch of salt and black pepper in a large bowl. Taste it — it should be creamy, a little tangy, a little smoky. Adjust to your liking right now before everything goes in.
  3. 3Drain the potatoes and spread them out on a sheet pan or cutting board for about 5 minutes. You want them to steam dry and cool slightly — not stone cold, just not steaming hot. Slightly warm potatoes absorb the dressing way better than cold ones.
  4. 4Add the warm potatoes to your dressing bowl and gently fold everything together. Don't mash — use a spatula and fold with patience. Add the chopped eggs, celery, red onion, parsley or cilantro, and green onions. Fold again until everything is evenly coated.
  5. 5Taste one more time and adjust salt, pepper, or vinegar as needed. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving — 4 hours is even better. Right before putting it out, dust the top with a little extra smoked paprika and scatter some green onion slices on top so it looks as good as it tastes.
💡 Improv Tip

The real secret here is dressing the potatoes while they're still a little warm — they soak up that creamy tangy dressing instead of just being coated by it. And if your potato salad ever tastes flat after being in the fridge overnight, hit it with a small splash of apple cider vinegar and a pinch of salt before serving. Cold dulls flavor, and that little refresh wakes the whole thing back up.
