Simple Grilled Burgers Recipe for Memorial Day (That'll Make You the Hero of the Cookout)

Published May 19, 2026 · Improv Oven

This simple grilled burgers recipe for Memorial Day is the only one you need — no fancy gadgets, no expensive cuts, just real flavor that hits every single time. We're keeping it classic but punching it up with a few little tricks that'll have your neighbors asking what your secret is. Grab your tongs, fire up that grill, and let's make some magic.

Prep
10 mins
Cook
16 mins
Total
26 mins
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs 80/20 ground beef
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices American or cheddar cheese
  • 1 tbsp butter (for toasting buns)
  • Toppings of your choice: lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, ketchup, mustard, mayo

Instructions

  1. 1Take your ground beef out of the fridge about 10 minutes before cooking — cold meat straight to the grill is a rookie mistake that leads to uneven cooking. While you wait, fire up your grill to medium-high heat, around 400°F.
  2. 2In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and black pepper. Mix gently with your hands just until combined — don't overwork it or your burgers will come out tough and dense.
  3. 3Divide the mixture into 4 equal portions, about 6 oz each. Form them into patties slightly wider than your buns, because they're going to shrink a little on the grill. Press your thumb into the center of each patty to make a shallow dimple — this keeps them from puffing up into a meatball instead of lying flat.
  4. 4Place the patties on the hot grill and close the lid. Cook for 4 minutes without touching or pressing them down — pressing squeezes out all that good juice and that's not the move. Flip once, cook another 3-4 minutes for medium doneness.
  5. 5In the last minute of cooking, lay a slice of cheese on each patty and close the lid for 60 seconds so it melts perfectly. Pull the burgers off and let them rest for 2 minutes — this is non-negotiable, it keeps all the juices inside.
  6. 6While the burgers rest, butter your buns and toast them cut-side down on the grill for about 1 minute until golden. A toasted bun is the difference between a good burger and a great one.
  7. 7Build your burgers with whatever toppings make you happy and serve immediately. That's it — you just became the cookout legend.
💡 Improv Tip

The 80/20 ground beef is not negotiable here, mija. That 20% fat is where all the flavor and juiciness lives. If you go leaner trying to be healthy, you'll end up with a dry hockey puck and nobody wants that at a Memorial Day cookout. Save the lean ground beef for meatballs — burgers need that fat.
