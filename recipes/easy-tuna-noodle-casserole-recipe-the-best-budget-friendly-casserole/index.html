Easy Tuna Noodle Casserole Recipe (The Best Budget-Friendly Casserole)

Published September 19, 2026 · Improv Oven

If you're hunting for budget-friendly casserole recipes that actually taste like something you'd want to eat twice, this classic tuna noodle casserole is your answer. It's creamy, comforting, and built almost entirely from pantry staples — canned tuna, egg noodles, a simple homemade sauce, and whatever frozen veg you've got hanging around. No cream of mushroom soup can required, and it comes together in under an hour.

Prep
15 mins
Cook
35 mins
Total
50 mins
Serves
6

Ingredients

  • 12 oz wide egg noodles
  • 2 cans (5 oz each) tuna in water, drained
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1 cup frozen corn (or one more cup of peas — no stress)
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk (whole or 2% works great)
  • 1 cup chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese, divided
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs (panko or regular)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or melted butter (for the topping)

Instructions

  1. 1Preheat your oven to 375°F. Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish and set it aside.
  2. 2Cook the egg noodles in a large pot of salted boiling water until just barely al dente — about 1 to 2 minutes less than the package says. They'll finish cooking in the oven, so don't overdo it. Drain and set aside.
  3. 3While the noodles cook, melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and let it cook for about 1 minute, stirring constantly, until it smells a little nutty. This is your roux — it's what makes the sauce thick and creamy without any canned soup.
  4. 4Slowly pour in the milk and broth while whisking steadily to avoid lumps. Keep whisking and cooking over medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes, until the sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon.
  5. 5Stir in the garlic powder, onion powder, dried thyme, a good pinch of salt, and plenty of black pepper. Then add 3/4 cup of the shredded cheddar and stir until it's fully melted into the sauce.
  6. 6Remove the saucepan from the heat. Add the drained tuna, frozen peas, and frozen corn to the sauce, breaking up the tuna with a fork as you stir. Don't worry about thawing the vegetables — they'll cook perfectly in the oven.
  7. 7Add the cooked egg noodles to the saucepan and fold everything together gently until the noodles are well coated in that creamy sauce. Pour the whole mixture into your prepared baking dish and spread it into an even layer.
  8. 8In a small bowl, toss the breadcrumbs with the olive oil or melted butter and the remaining 1/4 cup of shredded cheddar. Sprinkle this mixture evenly over the top of the casserole — this is what gives you that irresistible golden crust.
  9. 9Bake uncovered for 25 to 30 minutes, until the topping is golden brown and the edges are bubbling. Let it rest for 5 minutes before serving so it sets up nicely and doesn't fall apart when you scoop it.
💡 Improv Tip

The homemade white sauce is the real magic here — once you make it a couple times, you'll realize it's basically just butter, flour, and milk, and you'll never buy a can of cream-of-anything again. If your sauce feels too thick before you add the noodles, splash in a little more broth or milk to loosen it up. The noodles will absorb liquid as they bake, so a slightly saucy mixture going into the dish is exactly what you want.
